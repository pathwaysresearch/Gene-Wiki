# Warren McCulloch and Walter Pitts

## Foundational Work
The 1943 work of Warren McCulloch and Walter Pitts is considered the first work in the field of Artificial Intelligence. They proposed a model of artificial neurons that connected brain function, logic, and computation, laying the groundwork for neural networks.

## The Artificial Neuron Model
Their model characterized each artificial neuron as being either "on" or "off." A neuron would switch to the "on" state in response to stimulation from a sufficient number of its neighbors, providing a simple but powerful computational unit.

## Intellectual Synthesis
The McCulloch-Pitts model was a significant synthesis of knowledge from three distinct sources. It drew on the basic physiology and function of neurons in the brain, the formal analysis of propositional logic by Russell and Whitehead, and Alan Turing's theory of computation.
