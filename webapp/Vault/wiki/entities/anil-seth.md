# Anil Seth

## Overview
Anil Seth is a professor at the University of Sussex, a neuroscientist, a TED speaker, and the author of the book "Being You." He is presented as a leading expert on the biological basis of consciousness and is the main guest on the "Creative Intelligence" podcast episode discussing AI and consciousness.

## Key Ideas on Consciousness
In the discussion, Seth articulates the view that our perception of the world is a "construction" created by the brain based on sensory input, but one that is closely tied to reality. He emphasizes the "hard problem of consciousness"—how physical processes give rise to subjective experience—as a core challenge in the field.

## Role in the AI Debate
Seth serves as the expert guide in the conversation, unpacking the complexities of consciousness and its relation to AI. He engages with other theories, such as those involving narrative self-modeling, and highlights the deeper philosophical question of whether consciousness is tied to its biological substrate or can be achieved through pure information processing.
