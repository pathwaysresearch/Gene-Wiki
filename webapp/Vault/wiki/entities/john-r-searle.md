# John R. Searle

## Overview
John R. Searle is an author who has published multiple works examining the philosophical foundations of mind, consciousness, and artificial intelligence.

## Key Publications
The provided bibliography lists several of his key works, including the books 'Minds, Brains and Science' (1984, Harvard University Press) and 'The Rediscovery of the Mind' (1992, MIT Press). It also cites his 1990 article in Scientific American, 'Is the brain's mind a computer program?'

## Central Philosophical Questions
The titles of his publications indicate a focus on fundamental questions regarding the relationship between the mind and the brain, and a critical stance on the computational theory of mind. His work explicitly questions whether the mind can be equated with a computer program.
