# Demis Hassabis

## Overview
Demis Hassabis is the founder of the AI lab DeepMind and is portrayed as a researcher who is not a conventional modeler but rather a strategic thinker. His background as a competitive chess player is cited as a key influence on his perspective, leading him to be described as someone who is "always a few moves ahead of the game."

## Strategic Thinking
An anecdote illustrating his forward-thinking nature involves a meeting with Elon Musk. When Musk discussed his plan to colonize Mars to escape dangers on Earth, Hassabis reportedly responded that this would only work if a superintelligent AI didn't follow humans to Mars. This comment allegedly left Musk speechless and was a factor in his decision to invest in DeepMind.

## Role at DeepMind and Google
As the leader of DeepMind, Hassabis oversaw the development of landmark AI systems like AlphaGo, which defeated the world Go champion Lee Sedol, and AlphaFold, which made breakthroughs in predicting protein structures. He and his company were eventually acquired by Google, bringing his strategic vision and research capabilities into the tech giant.
