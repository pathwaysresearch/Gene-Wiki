# Kindred

## Overview
Kindred is a Vancouver-based startup co-founded by Suzanne Gildert and Geordie Rose. The company focuses on solving the "grasping problem," which is identified as a critical "missing link" in the automation of workflows like e-commerce fulfillment.

## Technology and Method
Kindred's product, the Kindred Sort, is a robotic arm that combines automated software with a human controller. In its initial implementation, a human operator wearing a virtual reality headset guides the robot arm via teleoperation to pick up and move objects. This human-in-the-loop system allows the company to provide a working solution to the grasping problem immediately.

## Long-Term AI Strategy
The company's long-term goal is to use the data from human-guided operations to train a prediction machine. By collecting many observations of a human successfully grasping objects, Kindred uses reinforcement learning to teach the robot to perform the task by itself. This strategy aims to eventually automate the grasping task completely, removing the need for the human teleoperator.
