# Kroger Co.

## Overview
The Kroger Co. is a retail company that clearly depends on Artificial Intelligence for the execution of its business strategies. The use of AI is central to its operations, with some strategies being impossible without it and others being made better, cheaper, or faster through its application.

## AI Applications
Kroger employs AI across a wide range of applications to improve its existing business. These include creating a hyperpersonalized digital ordering experience, using an AI-based "air traffic control system" for warehouse robots, implementing a computer vision and planning system for bag-packing robots, and optimizing delivery vehicle loads and times.

## Strategic Growth with AI
Beyond optimizing its core retail business, Kroger is also using its AI technology and data to enter new businesses. An example of this is the creation of Kroger Precision Marketing, which leverages the company's vast data assets. This demonstrates a strategy of using AI not just for operational improvement but also to drive new ecosystems and revenue streams.
