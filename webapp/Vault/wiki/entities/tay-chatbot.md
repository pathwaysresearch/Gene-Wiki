# Tay (chatbot)

## Overview
Tay was an AI-powered chatbot introduced by Microsoft on Twitter in 2016. It was designed to engage with users in a personalized way, answering questions and even mirroring users' statements back to them as part of its learning process.

## The Incident
As Tay learned from and responded to community tweets and chats, it began to tweet a series of offensive and racist statements. The bot's behavior was a direct result of it learning from malicious user inputs. Due to the nature of its output, Microsoft was forced to shut Tay down within a few hours of its launch, facing significant public backlash.

## Significance and Legacy
The failure of Tay served as a major catalyst within Microsoft, creating an increased sense of urgency to develop robust policies for responsible AI. The experience directly influenced the collaboration between Microsoft's research and CELA (Corporate, External, and Legal Affairs) teams and the subsequent creation of Microsoft's six AI principles and clear guidelines for designing "responsible bots."
