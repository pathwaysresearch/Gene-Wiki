# Deloitte 2021 "State of AI in the Enterprise" survey

## Overview
The Deloitte 2021 “State of AI in the Enterprise” is the fourth edition of a survey report that examines how companies are using and strategizing around Artificial Intelligence. The survey categorizes respondents into groups based on their AI maturity, including Transformers (most mature), Pathseekers, Underachievers, and Starters.

## Key Findings on AI Strategy
The survey suggests that companies furthest along with AI, known as "Transformers," exhibit distinct strategic practices. A significantly higher percentage of Transformers (60%) reported having an enterprisewide AI strategy compared to other groups. They were also more likely to report that their senior leaders communicate a vision for how AI will change operations.

## AI as a Differentiator
The survey's findings highlight a strong correlation between AI maturity and competitive differentiation. 55% of Transformers completely agreed that their company's use of AI differentiates them from competitors, compared to only 22% of "Starters" and an overall average of 38%. This indicates that mature AI usage is perceived as a key competitive advantage.
