# Google Translate

## Role as an Example
Google Translate is presented in the text as a key example illustrating the economic impact of machine learning. The service's evolution demonstrates how this technology has dramatically reduced the cost of "quality-adjusted prediction." Language translation itself is framed as a prediction problem: predicting the correct words and phrases in a target language that match the source language.

## Economic Impact of AI
The improvements in Google Translate show that for the same cost in computational capacity, a much higher quality of translation can now be provided. This fall in the cost of a given quality of prediction is a central theme of the book, and Google Translate serves as a tangible illustration of this trend.

## Significance
The ability to perform a task like language translation so well that it seems magical is a direct result of better and cheaper prediction. The success of services like Google Translate showcases how advances in AI are enabling activities that were previously unimaginable or of much lower quality.
