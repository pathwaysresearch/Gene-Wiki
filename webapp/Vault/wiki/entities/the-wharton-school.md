# The Wharton School

## Overview
The Wharton School is the business school of the University of Pennsylvania. It is the academic home of Stefano Puntoni, who holds the title of the Sebastian S. Kresge Professor of Marketing. Professor Puntoni joined Wharton after a tenure at the Rotterdam School of Management, Erasmus University.

## Role in AI Adoption Research
Wharton has been actively involved in researching the state of AI in the enterprise. The school published a significant report on AI adoption, authored in part by Stefano Puntoni, which offered a different perspective on success rates compared to an earlier, widely-cited report from MIT. This work contributes to the broader understanding of how organizations are integrating AI technologies.

## Institutional Focus on Human-AI Interaction
The school is home to a dedicated research department focused on the intersection of human and artificial intelligence. Stefano Puntoni serves as the co-director of Wharton's Human AI research department, indicating an institutional focus on studying the complex dynamics between people and AI systems in business and society.
