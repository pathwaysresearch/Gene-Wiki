# Generative AI

## Overview

Generative AI is described in the text as a promising technology with the potential to create significant economic gains. However, it is argued that these potential benefits will remain elusive under the current development trajectory pursued by the technology industry.

## Current Development Focus

The most common generative AI models, such as Large Language Models (LLMs), are currently being developed with a priority on creating general human-like conversational tools. This focus is contrasted with an alternative approach centered on augmenting worker productivity. The industry's broader priorities shaping this development are characterized as automation and online monetization.

## Proposed Architectural Change

To unlock greater productivity gains, the text suggests a major change in the architecture of generative AI models may be necessary. The goal of this architectural shift would be to reorient the models to focus on providing reliable information that can specifically increase the marginal productivity of different kinds of workers.
