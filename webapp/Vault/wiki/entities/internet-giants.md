# Internet Giants (IGs)

## Role in Early AIT Adoption
The Internet Giants (IGs) are identified as pioneers in applying Artificial Intelligence Technologies (AITs). They developed and successfully used AIT-based systems for product/consumer matching, which served as an early, valuable, and highly profitable application of the technology.

## Discovery of Limitations
Experiments by the IGs revealed a significant limitation in the range of applications for these AIT matching systems. The systems were found to work well in low-stakes contexts, such as recommending a product or ad that a consumer could easily ignore. However, they performed poorly in high-stakes contexts where a bad recommendation might offend the consumer.

## Influence on AIT Diffusion
The success of the IGs' matching engines suggested the possibility of imitative invention in other corporate contexts. However, the high-stakes limitation they uncovered highlighted a significant barrier that could raise costs or slow the speed of AITs becoming a General-Purpose Technology (GPT) across the numerous industries not served by the IGs.
