# Fabrizio Dell'Acqua

## Overview
Fabrizio Dell'Acqua is a postdoctoral researcher affiliated with Harvard Business School and Harvard's Digital Data Design Institute. He holds a PhD in management from Columbia Business School. His research centers on the dynamics of human interaction with technology, particularly artificial intelligence.

## Research Contributions
Dell'Acqua's work examines the complexities of human-AI collaboration. He has authored a 2022 working paper on the risk of professionals "falling asleep at the wheel" when collaborating with AI in HR recruiting. He has also co-authored research on the effects of automation and skills on team performance and coordination (Dell'Acqua et al. 2025), as well as studies on how professionals can be "persuasion bombed" when trying to validate LLMs (Randazzo et al. 2025a).

## Experimental Framework
His research, such as Dell'Acqua et al. (2025), utilizes an experimental framework to evaluate the effects of AI on human and team performance. This approach is cited in the text as being comparable to the one used in the main study being discussed.
