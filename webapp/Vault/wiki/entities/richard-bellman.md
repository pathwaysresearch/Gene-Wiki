# Richard Bellman

## Overview
Richard Bellman was a mathematician whose work from 1957 is cited as foundational for the methods used in making complex, sequential decisions under uncertainty.

## Contributions
Bellman's primary contribution discussed in the text is the development of the **Bellman equation**. This equation is a cornerstone of dynamic programming and provides a recursive formulation for the utility of a state in a sequential decision problem. It connects a state's value to the immediate rewards and the values of states that can be reached from it.

## Legacy and Significance
The Bellman equation is fundamental to solving Markov Decision Processes (MDPs). It forms the theoretical basis for the value iteration algorithm, which iteratively applies the equation to find the optimal utilities of states. His work has had a lasting impact on artificial intelligence, control theory, economics, and operations research by providing a powerful mathematical tool for optimization over time.
