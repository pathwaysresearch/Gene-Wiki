# Ada

## Overview
Ada is a customer service system that uses AI to automate responses to frequently asked questions. Its purpose is to handle a high volume of simple customer interactions quickly and efficiently, reducing costs associated with human call-center operators and diminishing customer frustration.

## Operational Model
Ada's system works by predicting the nature of a customer's question. It can improve this prediction by using individual characteristics, such as the customer's technical competence, the device they are using, or their past call history. For common and straightforward questions, Ada provides an immediate answer. This is possible because the judgment—what to do in response to a specific, anticipated prediction—has been pre-specified and hard-coded into the software.

## Human-Machine Collaboration
Ada is presented as an example of effective human-machine collaboration. The machine is tasked with handling the high-volume, easy questions where judgment is codifiable. This frees up human operators to specialize in the unusual and more difficult questions that require nuanced, real-time judgment that is too complex or time-consuming to specify in advance. This division of labor allows the overall system to be both efficient and capable of handling complexity.
