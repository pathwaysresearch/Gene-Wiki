# Satya Nadella

## Taking Over Microsoft
When Satya Nadella took over as CEO, Microsoft was described as a 'tired company' that had stagnated after its period of wild growth with DOS, Windows, and Office. He initiated a major transformation to fundamentally change the core of the company.

## New Mission and Strategy
Nadella established a new mission for Microsoft: 'to empower every person and every organization on the planet to achieve more.' This gave rise to a new strategy focused on becoming the productivity platform for the age of AI, shifting to a services-based 'consumption' model backed by a cloud-based architecture. A key part of this strategy was a decision to embrace open source, including Linux, to regain relevance with developers.

## Key Transformation Decisions
One of Nadella's first major moves was to integrate the previously separate and disconnected Azure organization into the core of the company, making it central to the new strategy. He also appointed Kurt DelBene, an executive with extensive product experience, as Chief Digital Officer to transform Microsoft's internal IT organization and build its own AI Factory.
