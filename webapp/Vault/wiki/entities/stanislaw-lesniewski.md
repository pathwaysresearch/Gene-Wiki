# Stanisław Leśniewski

## Overview
Stanisław Leśniewski was a Polish logician active in the early 20th century. The text identifies him as the originator of the theory of mereology.

## Contribution to Logic and Ontology
Leśniewski's key contribution discussed in the text is the development of mereology, which he proposed in 1916. His goal was to use the part-whole relation as a foundational concept to replace mathematical set theory. This was motivated by a desire to eliminate abstract entities, such as sets, from his logical system.

## Legacy
Leśniewski's work on mereology provided an alternative framework for ontology and knowledge representation. His ideas were later expounded upon by others, such as Leonard and Goodman, and gained support from prominent philosophers like Quine, influencing subsequent work in knowledge representation within AI.
