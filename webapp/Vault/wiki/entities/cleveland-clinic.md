# Cleveland Clinic

## Overview
Cleveland Clinic is a healthcare organization recognized for providing innovative, high-quality care and is also a leader in developing and deploying AI use cases. According to Chris Donovan, its executive director of enterprise information management and analytics, AI is "popping up all over the place" within the institution. (Chunk 50)

## AI Strategy and Governance
The clinic's approach to AI involves a dual strategy of facilitating bottom-up efforts from various departments while also providing centralized governance. This work is guided by a cross-organizational community of practice anchored in its enterprise analytics, IT, and ethics departments. (Chunk 50)

## Key AI Use Cases
The primary benefit of most AI applications at Cleveland Clinic is operational—making decisions faster and with greater precision. For example, it is implementing a machine learning-based preoperative patient risk score for anesthesia, which is more automated and precise than its previous rule-based system. The clinic also uses machine learning with its financial data to better estimate financial risk and has built a predictive model to prioritize the allocation of its scarce care management resources. (Chunk 50)
