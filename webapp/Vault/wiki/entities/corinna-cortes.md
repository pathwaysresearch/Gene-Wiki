# Corinna Cortes

## Overview
Corinna Cortes is a prominent computer scientist recognized for her significant contributions to the field of machine learning.

## Contributions
Cortes is the co-author of a seminal 1995 paper with Vladimir Vapnik that introduced the soft-margin classifier for Support Vector Machines (SVMs). This development was critical for making SVMs a practical tool capable of handling noisy, real-world datasets. The importance of this work was recognized when the paper won the 2008 ACM Theory and Practice Award.
