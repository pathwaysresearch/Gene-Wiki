# Karthik Hosanagar

## Overview
Karthik Hosanagar is the John C. Hower Professor at the Wharton School, Faculty Co-Director of Wharton Human-AI Research, and a serial entrepreneur. He is a prominent voice on the intersection of technology and business, with a particular focus on artificial intelligence.

## Key Works
Hosanagar is the author of the book "A Human's Guide to Machine Intelligence," for which he attempted to co-author a chapter with AI in 2017-18. He is also the host of the "Creative Intelligence" podcast, a biweekly show where he interviews leading figures on topics at the intersection of AI, creativity, and human potential.

## Perspective on AI Development
In a reflection on AI's recent progress, Hosanagar identified the development of Transformer models, introduced in the 2017 paper "Attention is All You Need," as the key technical change that enabled the field's takeoff. He noted the significant leap in text generation capabilities from older RNN-based methods to models like GPT-2, BERT, and eventually GPT-3, which made complex writing tasks feasible for AI.
