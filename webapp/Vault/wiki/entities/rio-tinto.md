# Rio Tinto

## Overview
Rio Tinto is identified as a mining giant with highly capital-intensive iron ore operations in Australia's remote Pilbara region. The main operational challenge and cost driver in these mines is not people but downtime for the enormous hauling trucks, which are the size of two-story houses.

## Implementation of Automation
To optimize its operations and reduce downtime, Rio Tinto first employed trucks that could be controlled remotely from Perth. In 2016, it advanced this by deploying seventy-three self-driving trucks that could operate autonomously. This automation allowed the trucks to run twenty-four hours a day, overcoming the productivity limitations of human drivers, especially at night.

## Impact and Significance
The use of autonomous trucks has already saved Rio Tinto 15 percent in operating costs. This case serves as a clear example of how AI and prediction machines can be applied to automate tasks in capital-intensive industries, particularly where human deployment is costly and downtime is a major expense.
