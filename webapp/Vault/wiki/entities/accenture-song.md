# Accenture Song

## Overview

Accenture Song is identified as the creative agency of the global consulting firm Accenture. It is led by CEO David Droga, who sold his previous company, Droga5, to Accenture with the belief that technology and creativity must be woven together to meet modern business needs.

## Strategic Focus

The agency's work and philosophy are centered on the necessity of creating a unified customer experience. This involves integrating marketing with commerce, sales, and product development, ensuring a seamless and consistent brand interaction for consumers. AI is a key enabler of this strategy, providing the tools to connect these traditionally siloed functions.

## Role in the AI Transition

Through his work at Accenture Song, David Droga observes firsthand how AI is being implemented with clients. He notes that while the technology offers exciting new capabilities, it also exposes a company's operational readiness. The process of adopting AI reveals internal challenges such as fragmented data and departmental silos, highlighting the deep organizational changes required to fully leverage its potential.
