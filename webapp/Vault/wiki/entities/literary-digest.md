# Literary Digest

## Overview
The *Literary Digest* was a magazine that conducted a very large-scale poll during the 1936 US presidential election between Landon and Roosevelt.

## The 1936 Poll
The magazine sent mail to approximately 10 million people and received 2.4 million responses. Based on this sample, it predicted with high confidence that Landon would win with 57% of the votes.

## Methodological Failure
The poll's prediction was famously incorrect; Roosevelt won with 62% of the votes. The failure was due to a flawed sampling method that suffered from two major issues: the source lists used to find addresses (telephone directories, magazine subscriber lists) favored wealthier people, and a very low response rate of less than 25% introduced significant nonresponse bias.
