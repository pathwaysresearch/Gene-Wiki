# Karim R. Lakhani

## Overview
Karim R. Lakhani is the Dorothy & Michael Hintze Professor of Business Administration at Harvard Business School. He is the founding chair of the Digital, Data & Design (D^3) Institute at Harvard and also leads the Laboratory for Innovation Science. He is described as an expert on technological innovation and AI strategy.

## Key Research and Concepts
Lakhani's research focuses on conducting "clinical trials" of AI in major organizations like BCG to understand its effects on knowledge workers. He frames Generative AI as a "drug" with unknown effects and emphasizes key concepts like the "jagged technological frontier" of AI, the evolution of AI from a tool to a "teammate," and the dangerous gap between AI's exponential capability growth and organizations' linear adoption rates.

## Educational Impact
At Harvard Business School, Lakhani teaches a course on data science and AI for leaders. In his course, MBA students, even those without coding skills, have successfully created multiple machine learning algorithms and two AI agents, demonstrating the democratizing power of modern AI tools.
