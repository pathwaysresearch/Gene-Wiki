# Aurélien Géron

## Overview
Aurélien Géron is the author of "Hands-on Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras & TensorFlow". The copyright for the 2019 second edition of the book is held by him.

## Published Work
His book, published by O'Reilly Media, aims to provide the concepts, tools, and techniques needed to build intelligent systems capable of learning from data. The second edition is noted for being updated for TensorFlow 2 and for covering additional topics in unsupervised learning, deep learning, computer vision, and natural language processing.
