# Shell

## AI at Scale
Shell is an example of a company that both needs and has achieved AI at a large scale. The company is pursuing several different business objectives with AI, including improving its understanding of the subsurface more rapidly, maximizing recovery of new and existing fields, making its existing asset operations more effective and energy efficient, and offering low carbon solutions to customers.

## Predictive Maintenance Application
The maintenance process is a key area where Shell applies AI at scale, as it has hundreds of thousands of pieces of equipment across its facilities. The company uses predictive maintenance to forecast when equipment might degrade or fail, which executives believe makes equipment more reliable and maintenance more efficient compared to standard interval-based approaches.

## Data Ecosystem Leadership
To manage the vast amounts of data required for subsurface exploration, Shell initially created a subsurface data universe. Recognizing the need for collaboration, Shell and its partners launched the Open Subsurface Data Universe (OSDU), a data and algorithm-sharing ecosystem to facilitate the exchange of seismic, well, and production data among over 160 member companies.
