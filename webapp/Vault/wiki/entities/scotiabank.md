# Scotiabank

## AI Journey
Scotiabank, officially the Bank of Nova Scotia, is one of Canada's 'big five' banks. Its experience serves as an example that an organization can successfully catch up in AI capabilities even if it gets a slow start compared to competitors. The bank's initial focus was on a large-scale digital transformation, which created the necessary foundation for its subsequent data and analytics capabilities.

## Organizational Strategy
In mid-2019, Scotiabank's CEO, Brian Porter, established a new team called Customer Insights, Data, and Analytics (CID&A) to centralize and accelerate its efforts. The bank adopted what it calls a 'blue collar AI strategy,' which emphasizes a pragmatic approach focused on ensuring that AI initiatives provide tangible business value and that the vast majority are deployed into production. This strategy is primarily aimed at improving existing operations and fostering closer relationships with customers.

## Data Management Innovation
A key enabler of Scotiabank's progress was a new data delivery approach called the Reusable Authoritative Data Set (RAD). This initiative, led by Chief Data Officer Peter Serenita, shifted the data management function from a purely defensive, compliance-focused role to one that also prioritized the rapid delivery of data for customer insights and AI use cases, thereby increasing the speed and return on investment of its data projects.
