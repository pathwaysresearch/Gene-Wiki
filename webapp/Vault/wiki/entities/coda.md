# Coda

## Overview
Coda is a business productivity platform created by founder and former CEO Shishir Mehrotra. It is described as a tool that reinvented the traditional productivity suite of documents, spreadsheets, and presentations.

## Acquisition by Grammarly
Coda was acquired by Grammarly as a foundational piece of Grammarly's new strategic direction. The acquisition brought both the technology and its founder, Shishir Mehrotra, into the Grammarly organization, with Mehrotra becoming CEO of the combined entity.

## Role in the AI Superhighway
Within Grammarly's "AI Superhighway" vision, Coda functions as a key "surface." It provides the document, spreadsheet, and presentation environment where Grammarly's various AI agents can be deployed to assist users directly in their workflow.
