# SIAB (Sample of Integrated Labour Market Biographies)

## Overview
The SIAB (Sample of Integrated Labour Market Biographies) is a large-scale German administrative dataset that provides longitudinal information on workers' employment histories. The study uses SIAB data covering the period from 2010 to 2017 for its main wage analysis.

## Data Content
The SIAB dataset is the source for the study's dependent variable, the log daily wage, which is deflated to a 2015 base year. It also provides a rich set of control variables used in the regression models. These include time-varying worker controls like experience, education, and age, as well as plant controls such as the number of workers, industry (3-digit NACE), and region (NUTS 3).

## Application in the Study
The researchers leverage the SIAB to conduct a large-scale econometric analysis of the impact of AI on wages. The main analysis is restricted to full-time workers liable to social security, resulting in a sample size of over 2.4 million observations. The detailed nature of the data allows the researchers to include worker, plant, and occupation fixed effects to isolate the relationship between occupational AI exposure and individual wages more precisely.
