# Dynamic Artificial Intelligence Occupational Exposure (DAIOE) index

## Overview

The Dynamic Artificial Intelligence Occupational Exposure (DAIOE) index is a measure of AI occupational exposure developed by Engberg et al. (2024). It is a key variable in the study for linking AI advancements to changes in work content and wages.

## Methodology

Building on the work of Felten et al. (2018, 2021), the DAIOE index estimates AI exposure by mapping data on technological progress in AI to the worker abilities required for different occupations, as detailed in the O*NET database. A key feature of the DAIOE is that it is time-varying, providing estimates of AI exposure by occupation and year, which allows for a dynamic analysis of AI's impact.

## Interpretation and Use

According to the model, a high exposure score in the DAIOE suggests that AI is likely to be applicable to the tasks within an occupation. However, the index itself does not predict whether this exposure will lead to the substitution or augmentation of human labor. The paper utilizes this index in conjunction with German worker-level data to empirically investigate the relationship between increasing AI exposure and changes in tasks, skills, and individual wages.
