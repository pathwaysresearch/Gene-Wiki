# Nathan Rosenberg

## Overview
Nathan Rosenberg is an economic historian credited in the text with coining the term 'learning-by-using.'

## Key Contribution
Rosenberg's central concept discussed is 'learning-by-using,' which describes the process by which firms improve their products' design and performance by learning from real-world user interactions. His primary examples involved the aviation industry, where airplane designs became more efficient and capable as manufacturers learned from their operational history.

## Legacy and Application to AI
The authors apply Rosenberg's concept directly to the strategic dynamics of prediction machines. The idea of a learning curve, where early experience leads to product improvements, is presented as particularly relevant for AI. This process can create a powerful strategic advantage for early movers in AI-driven industries, as they can accumulate more data and learn faster than their rivals.
