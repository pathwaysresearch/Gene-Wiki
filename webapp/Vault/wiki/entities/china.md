# China

## National AI Strategy
The text identifies China as a major global player in AI, highlighting its national ambition and strategic government push to lead the field (8, 218–220). This coordinated effort is presented as a key factor in its rapid advancement.

## Data Advantage
China's most significant advantage in the global AI race is its access to vast quantities of data. The book notes that different cultural norms and government policies regarding privacy provide Chinese firms with data on a scale that is difficult to match elsewhere (219–220).

## Key Application Areas
The text points to specific areas where China is leveraging its AI capabilities. These include advanced language translation services (26–27) and the widespread deployment of identity verification technologies like facial recognition, which are both enabled by and contribute to large-scale data collection (219-220).
