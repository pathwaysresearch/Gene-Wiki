# ABSTRIPS

## Overview
The ABSTRIPS system, developed by Earl Sacerdoti in 1974, was a significant evolution in automated planning. It built upon earlier systems like STRIPS by introducing a more structured form of hierarchical reasoning.

## Key Innovation: Abstraction Hierarchy
ABSTRIPS's main contribution was the idea of an abstraction hierarchy. This technique allowed the planner to first derive the general structure of a working plan by operating at a higher level of abstraction, where it was permitted to ignore lower-level preconditions of actions.

## Influence
The concept of the abstraction hierarchy introduced in ABSTRIPS became a cornerstone of modern Hierarchical Task Network (HTN) planning. It laid the groundwork for subsequent systems that planned by refining high-level actions into more detailed, concrete steps.
