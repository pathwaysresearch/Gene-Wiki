# Genius Makers

## Overview
"Genius Makers: The Mavericks Who Brought AI to Google, Facebook, and the World" is a book authored by New York Times reporter Cade Metz. The book's central theme is telling the stories of the key researchers who championed AI, particularly deep learning, when it was an unpopular idea.

## Central Figures
The book features several central protagonists who were instrumental in the AI revolution. The podcast discussion highlights figures like Geoffrey Hinton and Demis Hassabis, whose stories, motivations, and key decisions are detailed in the book, providing a human-centric view of technological development.

## Thesis
The podcast host introduces the book in the context of the idea that technology is not neutral and reflects the philosophies and goals of its creators. "Genius Makers" provides insight into the technology of AI by exploring the personal stories, beliefs, and goals of the people who built it, arguing that understanding the creators helps in understanding the technology itself.
