# Marvin Minsky

## Overview
The bibliography cites Marvin L. Minsky as the author of seminal works in artificial intelligence. His publications listed, from 1975 and 1986, point to his significant contributions during a formative period of the field, with work originating from the MIT AI Laboratory.

## Frameworks for Knowledge
Minsky's 1975 work, "A framework for representing knowledge," is highlighted as a key contribution to the field. The citation notes that this work originated as an MIT AI Laboratory memo and that the widely cited version is an abridged one, indicating its foundational importance and widespread influence on how AI systems structure and use knowledge about the world.

## The Society of Mind
His 1986 book, "The society of mind," presents another major theoretical contribution. The title itself suggests a model of intelligence not as a monolithic process, but as the emergent behavior of a collection of simpler, interacting agents or processes. This offered a novel and influential perspective on the nature of thought, mind, and consciousness within the context of AI.
