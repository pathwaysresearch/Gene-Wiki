# Cambridge Analytica

## Overview
Cambridge Analytica was a data analytics firm that gained notoriety for its role in a major Facebook data privacy scandal. The company, through its parent company SCL Group, provided funding to harvest user data to assess the psychological attributes of individual Americans.

## Data Harvesting Method
In 2014, Cambridge Analytica's parent company, SCL Group, funded Aleksandr Kogan, a Cambridge psychology lecturer, to harvest Facebook user data. Kogan used Amazon Mechanical Turk to pay users to take a survey and download an app. This app exploited Facebook's pre-2014 terms of service, which allowed developers to collect data not only from the app's direct users but also from their entire network of Facebook friends.

## Significance of the Scandal
The incident highlighted the significant privacy risks associated with digital platforms and their APIs. It revealed how third-party developers could exploit platform policies to gather vast amounts of personal data without the explicit consent of all affected individuals, sparking a major public debate about data privacy and platform responsibility.
