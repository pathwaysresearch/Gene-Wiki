# Next Best Action system

## Overview
The Next Best Action system is an AI-based platform used within Morgan Stanley's Wealth Management business, which is described as the largest in the world. Its primary function is to serve as a "Netflix-like recommendation engine" for suggesting investment ideas to the firm's clients.

## Development and Leadership
The system's creation was a long-term project that required significant and sustained personal engagement from senior leadership. The initial idea came from Jim Rosenthal, the organization's chief operations officer at the time, who oversaw its development for over a decade until his retirement. Andy Saperstein, then head of Wealth Management and now copresident of Morgan Stanley, strongly supported the project and later managed the addition of a communications platform for client engagement.

## Significance
The development of the Next Best Action system is presented as a prime example of how a senior leader can become personally engaged with a particularly important AI use case to drive it to completion. According to Jeff McMillan, the chief data and analytics officer at Morgan Stanley, the system could not have been created without the long-term involvement of these key leaders.
