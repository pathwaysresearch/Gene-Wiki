# Isaac Asimov

## Overview
The text references Isaac Asimov in the context of legal and ethical constraints on full automation. He is presented as someone who anticipated the regulatory issues surrounding the potential for autonomous machines to cause harm, a common theme in science fiction.

## The Three Laws of Robotics
Asimov's solution to the problem of robot-inflicted harm was to propose hard-coding robots with three laws. The text describes these laws as being "cleverly designed to remove the possibility that robots harm any human." This represents a non-learning, pre-specified approach to managing robot judgment.

## Relevance to Modern AI
Asimov's work is cited as a parallel to modern discussions about machine autonomy. It highlights the long-standing concern that even if full machine autonomy becomes technically feasible and socially acceptable, the law might not allow it without built-in safeguards. His fictional laws serve as a relevant historical precedent for current ethical debates in AI.
