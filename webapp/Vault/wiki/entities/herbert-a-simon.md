# Herbert A. Simon

## Overview
Herbert A. Simon was a key founder of the field of artificial intelligence, whose work is referenced extensively throughout the text. His contributions are cited from the earliest pages discussing the foundations of AI (pages 3, 10, 17, 18) to specific technical areas.

## Contributions to AI
Simon's foundational work on search algorithms is highlighted, with citations on pages covering search theory and algorithms (109, 110, 191). He was a co-creator of early AI programs that simulated human problem-solving, with related concepts in logic and automated reasoning discussed on pages 276 and 356. His work on planning is also noted (page 393).

## Legacy and Philosophy
Simon's concept of building rational agents that are intended to do "the right thing" (page 1049) is presented as a central theme of artificial intelligence. His broader contributions to understanding decision-making, particularly the idea of bounded rationality, are referenced in the context of utility and preferences (page 639).
