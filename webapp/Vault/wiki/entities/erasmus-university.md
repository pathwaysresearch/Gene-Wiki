# Erasmus University

## Overview
Erasmus University is an institution of higher education located in the city of Rotterdam in the Netherlands.

## Role in Dr. Puntoni's Career
Dr. Stefano Puntoni spent a significant portion of his academic career at Erasmus University. It was here that he took on his first faculty position after a year in Stockholm. During his long tenure, he achieved tenure, was promoted to full professor, and also took on leadership responsibilities as a head of department.

## Significance
This institution represents a major phase in Dr. Puntoni's career in European academia before he was recruited to join the Wharton School in the United States.
