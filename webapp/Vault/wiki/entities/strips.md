# STRIPS

## Overview
STRIPS was a pioneering planning program developed in the early 1970s by Fikes et al. It was associated with the control of the robot Shakey.

## Contribution to Hierarchical Planning
The STRIPS program is credited with introducing the first mechanism for hierarchical planning through its facility for learning "macrops." These macro-operators consisted of a sequence of primitive steps that could be reused, forming an early type of generalized plan.

## Role in Online Planning
The STRIPS planner was used in conjunction with PLANEX, the first online planner with execution monitoring. This combination was used to control the Shakey robot, demonstrating an early integration of deliberative planning and reactive execution.
