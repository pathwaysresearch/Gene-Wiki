# Aristotle

## Overview

Aristotle was a philosopher in ancient Greece who placed a strong emphasis on classification and the creation of categorization schemes, with his work influencing Western thought for centuries.

## Ontological Contributions

In a treatise called *Categories*, part of a collection of his works on logic known as the *Organon*, Aristotle constructed what can be considered an early version of an upper ontology. This work attempted to create a high-level classification of fundamental concepts.

## System of Classification

He introduced the foundational concepts of **genus** and **species** as a method for lower-level classification. This approach became a cornerstone of taxonomic systems and directly influenced later biological classification systems like that of Carolus Linnaeus.
