# Morgan Stanley

## AI Adoption for Financial Advisors
Morgan Stanley is presented as a company that has successfully deployed AI to support its core business operations. The firm developed and introduced a "next-best-action" (NBA) system in 2017 to provide its financial advisors with timely and relevant financial insights to present to clients.

## The Next-Best-Action (NBA) System
The NBA system at Morgan Stanley uses machine learning to identify personalized investment offers and actions for specific clients. This technology dramatically improves efficiency, reducing the time an advisor needs to find a relevant idea from about forty-five minutes to just seconds. This is a critical advantage for advisors who typically manage two hundred or more clients.

## Augmenting Human Expertise
The text emphasizes that the NBA system is designed to augment, not replace, the human financial advisor. The system provides a slate of recommendations—perhaps twenty in a given day—but the advisor uses their judgment to decide whether to share these ideas with the client, maintaining control over the client relationship.
