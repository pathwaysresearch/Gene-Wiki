# Timothy Bresnahan

## Overview
Timothy Bresnahan is an economist affiliated with the Department of Economics at Stanford University. He is the author of the paper "What innovation paths for AI to become a GPT?", which examines the commercial adoption of artificial intelligence.

## Key Arguments
In his work, Bresnahan argues that while early commercial applications of AITs were profitable, their expansion into a General-Purpose Technology (GPT) has stalled. He posits that for AITs to achieve widespread use, new innovations are required, particularly those that involve Digital Transformation as a key step, as early successes specifically avoided this requirement.

## Analysis of AIT Adoption
Bresnahan's analysis highlights the limitations of early AIT applications, such as their poor performance in high-stakes contexts. He critiques the "technological determinism" and "fond hope" surrounding the automation of white-collar work, pointing to low visibility and high organizational and adjustment costs as major, well-documented barriers to enterprise systems development.
