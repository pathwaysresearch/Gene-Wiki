# ADP Administrative Data

## Overview
The ADP administrative data is the primary source of information for the study "Canaries in the Coal Mine?". It is described as a high-frequency dataset containing records for millions of workers each month, providing detailed information on employment status and compensation.

## Use in the Study
The researchers use this dataset to document shifts in the US labor market following the widespread adoption of generative AI. The data's granularity allows for the analysis of employment changes across different age groups, occupations, and levels of AI exposure, while also enabling controls for firm-level shocks. Overall employment in the dataset remained robust during the study period.

## Data Characteristics and Limitations
While the dataset is extensive, the paper notes that the distribution of firms using ADP services does not perfectly match the distribution of firms across the entire US economy, referencing Cajner et al. (2018) for details on these compositional differences. The data includes worker year of birth, which is used for age-based analysis.
