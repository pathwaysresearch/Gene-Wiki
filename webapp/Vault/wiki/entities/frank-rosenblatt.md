# Frank Rosenblatt

## Key Works
The provided text lists several foundational works by Frank Rosenblatt on the topic of perceptrons. These include the 1957 report 'The perceptron: A perceiving and recognizing automaton,' a 1961 report 'On the convergence of reinforcement procedures in simple perceptrons,' and the 1962 book 'Principles of Neurodynamics: Perceptrons and the Theory of Brain Mechanisms.'

## Institutional Affiliation
His 1957 and 1961 reports were produced at the Cornell Aeronautical Laboratory, under Project PARA.

## Research Focus
Based on the titles of his works, Rosenblatt's research focused on establishing the perceptron as a model for perception and recognition, analyzing its learning mechanisms (specifically 'reinforcement procedures'), and developing a broader theory of brain mechanisms based on these principles.
