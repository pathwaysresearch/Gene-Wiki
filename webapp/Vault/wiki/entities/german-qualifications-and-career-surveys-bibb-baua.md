# German Qualifications and Career Surveys (BIBB-BAUA)

## Overview

The German Qualifications and Career Surveys (BIBB-BAUA) are a series of surveys conducted in Germany, with the paper utilizing data from the 2006, 2012, and 2018 waves. They are considered highly suitable for analyzing changes in the nature of work due to their detailed and consistent data collection over time.

## Data Content

The surveys provide rich, representative worker-level data. This includes detailed information on employees and their employers, as well as specific questions about the performance of 18 time-consistently defined tasks. This granularity allows for in-depth analysis of how the content of occupations evolves.

## Role in Research

The BIBB-BAUA data is a cornerstone of the paper's descriptive analysis of task and skill changes within German occupations. The researchers use it to construct task measures based on the classification by Spitz-Oener (2006), which are then used to study the relationship between technological exposure and the changing content of work. The data's quality and detail have also made it a key resource in previous important contributions to labor economics.
