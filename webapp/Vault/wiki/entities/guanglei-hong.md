# Guanglei Hong

## Overview
Guanglei Hong is a researcher whose work on educational policy in the Chicago Public Schools is presented as a primary case study demonstrating the practical power of mediation analysis.

## Analysis of "Algebra for All"
Hong conducted a mediation analysis of Chicago's 1997 "Algebra for All" policy, which required all ninth graders to take college-prep algebra. Her analysis was crucial in explaining the policy's unimpressive results. She concluded that the implementation had an adverse side effect, mediated through the classroom environment, which undermined the intended benefits of the curricular change. Her work suggested that returning to the pre-policy classroom environment while maintaining the new curriculum would lead to better outcomes.

## Significance and Impact
Hong's analysis successfully answered the "Why?" questions surrounding the policy's limited effect. It also explained why a subsequent 2003 reform, "Double-Dose Algebra," which effectively repaired the adverse classroom environment for lower-achieving students, was more successful. The text presents her work as a success for mediation analysis, showing how it can guide future policy by providing a deeper, mechanistic understanding of past results.
