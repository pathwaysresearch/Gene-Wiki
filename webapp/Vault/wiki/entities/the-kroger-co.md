# The Kroger Co.

## AI Strategy for Operations
The Kroger Co. is presented as a prime example of a company using AI to transform its operations and make its existing strategies more successful. This approach was central to its "Restock Kroger" strategy, announced in 2017, which aimed to position the company to compete more effectively in a changing business environment.

## The "Restock Kroger" Strategy
Analytics and AI were heavily featured in the "Restock Kroger" strategy, particularly in its first major objective: "Redefine the Food and Grocery Customer Experience." This goal involved accelerating digital and e-commerce activities and leveraging customer data and personalization through AI.

## Role of the 84.51° Subsidiary
The text emphasizes the critical role of 84.51°, Kroger's in-house subsidiary for data science, insights, and media. This organization is tasked with applying its expertise in customer data and AI-driven personalization to many aspects of Kroger's business, including its digital presence and the growth of its private label product portfolio.
