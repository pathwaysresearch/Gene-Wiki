# Alibaba

## Overview
Alibaba is a major technology firm that operates in e-commerce, retail, and other sectors. The text highlights it as a "hub firm" that successfully moves into many different markets by leveraging its core assets across networks.

## Bridging Strategy in Action
Alibaba provides a clear example of the bridging strategy by connecting its e-commerce platforms, Taobao and TMall, with financial services. It leveraged its payment network, Alipay, and the vast amount of transaction and user data from its e-commerce operations to launch new services through its financial arm, Ant Financial.

## Leveraging Data Assets
The success of Alibaba's bridging strategy demonstrates how data-based assets are useful across multiple scenarios and network sides. By building a critical mass of users in e-commerce, Alibaba was able to use the data generated by that network to create and capture value in an entirely new market, financial services, by offering products like a credit system.
