# Deep Learning (Book)

## Overview
"Deep Learning" is a book authored by Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio, and Aaron Courville. The text is presented as a formal and structured treatment of the subject, as evidenced by its table of contents.

## Content and Structure
The book is organized into chapters, beginning with an "Introduction" (Chapter 1) and followed by foundational mathematical topics. Chapter 2 is dedicated entirely to "Linear Algebra," indicating its importance to the subject matter.

## Key Topics Covered
The chapter on Linear Algebra covers a range of essential concepts. The table of contents lists sections on Scalars, Vectors, Matrices and Tensors; Identity and Inverse Matrices; Linear Dependence and Span; Norms; Eigendecomposition; Singular Value Decomposition; and The Moore-Penrose Pseudoinverse.
