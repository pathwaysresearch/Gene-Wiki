# Rina Dechter

## Overview
Rina Dechter is an author of numerous influential papers and a book in the field of artificial intelligence, with publications spanning from 1985 to 2007 cited in the text. Her work focuses on formal reasoning and search techniques, often in collaboration with researchers like Judea Pearl.

## Contributions to Constraint Processing
Dechter is a leading figure in constraint processing. The text lists her 2003 book "Constraint Processing" and a 1992 encyclopedia entry on "Constraint networks." Her research in this area includes developing techniques such as "Backjump-based backtracking for constraint satisfaction problems" (2002) and heuristics like "Network-based heuristics for constraint-satisfaction problems" (1987) and "Tree clustering for constraint networks" (1989).

## Frameworks for Reasoning and Search
Beyond specific techniques, Dechter has developed unifying frameworks for reasoning. Her 1999 paper introduces "Bucket elimination: A unifying framework for reasoning." She has also worked on search algorithms, co-authoring a 1985 paper on "Generalized best-first search strategies and the optimality of A*" and a 2007 paper on "AND/OR search spaces for graphical models."
