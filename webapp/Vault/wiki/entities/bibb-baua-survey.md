# BIBB-BAuA Survey

## Overview
The BIBB-BAuA survey is a German data source that provides detailed information on the content of work and working conditions. The paper utilizes data from its 2006, 2012, and 2018 waves. It is structured as a series of representative repeated cross-sections, meaning it surveys a different representative sample of the workforce in each wave, rather than tracking the same individuals over time.

## Data Content
The survey collects granular data on the work performed by individuals. Respondents are asked about the specific tasks they perform and the skills their jobs require. Crucially for this study, the survey also includes questions about technological change within the respondent's firm, such as the recent introduction of new computer programs, new manufacturing processes, or new machines and plants.

## Application in the Study
The researchers use the BIBB-BAuA data to measure changes in the content of work. They aggregate the data to the 3-digit occupation level to compute the share of workers performing specific tasks and requiring specific skills in 2012 and 2018. By comparing firms that did and did not introduce new technology, they construct a measure of how technological adoption affects task and skill composition. This data is also used to classify occupations by their level of 'task-churning'.
