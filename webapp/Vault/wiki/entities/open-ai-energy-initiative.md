# Open AI Energy Initiative (OAEI)

## Overview and Members
The Open AI Energy Initiative (OAEI) is an AI-driven ecosystem created for the energy sector. Its founding partners include Shell, the AI platform company C3.AI, Microsoft for its cloud services, and Baker Hughes, an energy technology company. The initiative is designed for members to supply and share AI applications and capabilities.

## Collaborative Model
The OAEI operates on a "barter style" model where participation is based on contributing AI offerings through a fair value exchange. The applications run on the C3.AI platform, with the goal of creating what a Shell executive described as "an app store for the process industry." A key part of the initiative is the plan to share data, using a standardized data model based on open standards to solve difficult industry problems.

## Strategic Scope
While the initiative's initial focus was on a relatively narrow business process like maintenance, its ambitions are expanding. The text states that the OAEI plans to address broader challenges, including how energy companies can transition to sustainable energy sources, optimize the development of oil and gas fields, and reduce leakages from infrastructure.
