# Karl Pearson

## Overview
Karl Pearson was a disciple of Francis Galton who played a key role in the development of modern mathematical statistics.

## Contribution to Correlation
Following Galton's initial work, Pearson derived the mathematical formula for the correlation coefficient. This formula is still the standard method used by statisticians worldwide to measure how strongly two variables in a dataset are related.

## Views on Causation
The text notes that Pearson, in particular, was thrilled with the discovery of correlation because he saw it as a mathematically clear and precise concept that made older, "slippery" ideas of cause and effect seem outdated and unscientific. He championed the use of correlation as a more rigorous replacement for causal language.
