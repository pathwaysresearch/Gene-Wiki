# Piyush Gupta

## Overview
Piyush Gupta is the Group CEO of DBS Bank and a veteran of the conservative banking industry with almost forty years of experience. He is presented as a powerful illustration of how a senior executive leader can effectively inspire and guide a company's journey into artificial intelligence.

## Role in AI Transformation at DBS
Gupta has played a central and personal role in DBS's adoption of AI. Unusually for a CEO, he personally led the bank's substantial data transformation, drawing on his prior experience with data centers and architectures at Citi. His engagement extended to creating new data governance structures, such as a Responsible Data Use Committee, and wrestling with decisions about data storage, leading to a hybrid cloud approach.

## Leadership Style and Impact
Since his arrival as CEO in 2009, Gupta has transformed DBS from being the lowest-ranked bank in customer service in Singapore to one of the best. He is described as an "enthusiastic" leader who fosters an AI-supportive culture by encouraging the organization of events to showcase successful AI experiments. This approach helps to publicize results and build a community of data- and AI-focused people within the company.
