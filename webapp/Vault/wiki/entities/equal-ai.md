# EqualAI

## Overview
EqualAI is a consortium created to address the challenge of bias in artificial intelligence. Its particular focus is on “reducing unconscious bias in the development and use of artificial intelligence.”

## Tools and Initiatives
To achieve its goals, EqualAI has developed practical tools for practitioners. One notable example is a checklist it has created for identifying bias in AI systems during their development and deployment.

## Policy and Regulatory Focus
Beyond creating tools, EqualAI also aims to influence policy. A key goal of the consortium is to identify regulatory and legislative solutions that can help systematically reduce bias in AI across the industry.
