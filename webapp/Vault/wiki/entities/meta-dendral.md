# Meta-DENDRAL

## Overview
Meta-DENDRAL was a pioneering artificial intelligence system designed to learn predictive rules in the domain of analytical chemistry. Its specific task was to learn the rules that govern how molecules break into pieces (fragment) inside a mass spectrometer.

## Role in Machine Learning
The system holds a significant place in the history of machine learning as it was the first system to apply the pure version space algorithm. It used this algorithm to systematically search the space of possible fragmentation rules to find those that were consistent with the observed mass spectrometry data from known molecules.

## Application and Purpose
By inducing general rules from specific examples of molecular fragmentation, Meta-DENDRAL could form hypotheses about chemical structures. The learned rules could then be used by its companion expert system, DENDRAL, to help identify the structure of unknown molecules based on their mass spectrometry data.
