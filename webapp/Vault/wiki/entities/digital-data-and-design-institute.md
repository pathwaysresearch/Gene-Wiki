# Digital, Data, and Design (D^3) Institute

## Overview
The Digital, Data, and Design (D^3) Institute is a research institute launched at Harvard Business School by Karim Lakhani and his colleagues. It was founded with the involvement of more than 30 faculty members from Harvard Business School and the engineering school, structured into 12 different labs.

## Mission and Focus
The institute's founding premise is that the combination of three key technologies—digitization, data science, and the design of new business and operating models—are having an exponential effect on businesses. The mission of D^3 is to work closely with companies to solve practical problems in this domain and to conduct significant research on the topic.

## Significance
The creation of the D^3 Institute signifies a formal recognition within Harvard Business School of the profound and interconnected impact of data, digital platforms, and business model innovation. It serves as a hub for faculty and industry collaboration aimed at understanding and navigating the business transformations driven by AI and related technologies.
