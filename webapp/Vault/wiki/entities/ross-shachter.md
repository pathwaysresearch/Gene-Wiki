# Ross Shachter

## Overview
Ross Shachter is a researcher from the influence diagram community credited with a foundational contribution to the field of probabilistic reasoning in artificial intelligence. (Chunk 311)

## Contribution to Bayesian Networks
In 1986, Shachter developed what the text describes as the first complete algorithm for performing exact inference in general Bayesian networks. This was a critical development for making these powerful probabilistic models computationally practical for a wider range of problems. (Chunk 311)

## Methodology
Shachter's algorithm was based on the principle of goal-directed reduction of the network. The method works by applying a series of posterior-preserving transformations to simplify the network structure in a way that is tailored to answering a specific probabilistic query. (Chunk 311)
