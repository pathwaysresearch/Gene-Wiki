# Oz Ataman

## Role at Well
Oz Ataman holds the position of chief technology officer at the health technology company Well. In this role, he is responsible for the company's technological strategy and the development of its machine learning models.

## Contribution to AI Strategy
Ataman explained that Well's models are a combination of two distinct types of machine learning. He identified these as traditional predictive machine learning and causal effects inference.

## Model Philosophy
According to Ataman, the use of causal effects inference is critical for making counterfactual predictions under alternative scenarios. This approach is necessary for Well's goal of planning a sequence of health interventions over time to influence member behavior positively.
