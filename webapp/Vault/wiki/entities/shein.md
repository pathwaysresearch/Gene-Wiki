# Shein

## Business Model
Shein is described as an AI-generated design company and a two-sided platform. On one side, it accumulates and engages customers through a social, trend-based digital environment. On the other side, it connects with a network of 6,000 small and medium-sized manufacturers in China capable of producing goods to specification.

## Operational Scale and Speed
The company operates at a scale and speed that dwarfs traditional fast-fashion retailers like Zara or H&M. It launches approximately 4,000 new SKUs per day, totaling one to two million new designs per year. Its time from design to product is just three to five days, and it can test new designs with initial production runs of less than 100 units.

## Use of AI
AI is central to Shein's model. It is used to help design shirts and other products. Crucially, the platform functions as a signal-detection system: a new design is put on the site to see if customers show interest. If a signal is detected, the company then scales up production, enabling a highly responsive and data-driven manufacturing process.
