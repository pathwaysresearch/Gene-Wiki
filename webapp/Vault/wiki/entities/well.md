# Well

## Overview
Well is a startup company that had artificial intelligence at its core from its very beginning. It is presented as a case study to contrast the experience of an "AI-first" company with that of legacy organizations that must wrestle with existing technologies and processes to adopt AI.

## Business Focus
The company's primary focus is on using AI to change customer behavior, specifically in the area of health. It aims to influence health behaviors by multiple means, leveraging its native AI capabilities to achieve this goal.

## Strategic Significance
As an AI-first startup, Well's experience is instructive for legacy companies. It demonstrates the relative ease of building AI capabilities without the burden of existing systems. Its model might inspire larger companies to pursue strategies like creating independent AI units or acquiring similar startups to accelerate their own transformation.
