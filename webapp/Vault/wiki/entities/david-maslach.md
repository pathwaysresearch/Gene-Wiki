# David Maslach

## Overview
Dr. David Maslach is a research professor of entrepreneurship and the host of the discussion with Karim Lakhani. He is the founder of the R3ciprocity Project, which stems from his belief that there is a better way to conduct science.

## The R3ciprocity Project
Maslach created the R3ciprocity Project as a platform to revolutionize scientific collaboration. He envisioned it as a 'sharing economy peer-to-peer kind of model' where researchers could help each other with tasks like writing papers, based on a principle of reciprocity. He personally devoted significant effort to building the platform over a period of about seven years.

## Learnings and Reflections
Through his work on the R3ciprocity Project, Maslach gained surprising insights into academic collaboration. He initially believed that users would be more altruistic than they turned out to be. This observation led him to reflect on the different exchange models that drive large-scale collaborative projects, comparing his experience to that of Wikipedia, where a small core of 5,000 contributors generates value for the entire world.
