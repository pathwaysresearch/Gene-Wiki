# Kartik Hosanagar

## Overview
Kartik Hosanagar is a professor at the Wharton School and the host of the podcast "Creative Intelligence." His work focuses on the business and societal implications of artificial intelligence. He is also identified as the faculty co-director of Wharton Human-AI Research.

## Role as Host and Moderator
In the episode on AI and consciousness, Hosanagar frames the discussion, introducing the topic and the guest, Anil Seth. He poses key questions, such as how one can determine if an AI is conscious in the absence of a clear definition, and shares his own perspective on the topic.

## Personal Views on AI Consciousness
Hosanagar expresses his personal belief that current Large Language Models (LLMs) do not have consciousness and that transformer models are not on a path towards it. However, he admits profound uncertainty about whether sentient machines could eventually be built or emerge accidentally in the future. He also aligns with the view that consciousness is deeply tied to biological experience, finding the "meat-based computer" view reductive.
