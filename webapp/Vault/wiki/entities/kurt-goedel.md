# Kurt Gödel

## Overview
Kurt Gödel is the logician and mathematician identified in the text as the creator of the incompleteness theorem, a foundational result in logic.

## Major Contribution
Gödel's key contribution discussed is the proof of his incompleteness theorem. He achieved this by working within a language of first-order logic that was extended to include the mathematical induction schema, making it powerful enough to formalize the theory of numbers.

## Legacy
His work demonstrated that any formal system capable of expressing arithmetic is inherently incomplete, meaning there are true statements within that system that cannot be proven. This finding had a profound impact on the philosophy and foundations of mathematics and logic.
