# Brian Valentine

## Overview
Brian Valentine is a software executive who formerly worked at Microsoft, where he had overseen successful releases of major products including Microsoft Exchange, Windows 2000, and Windows XP. He was brought into Amazon in the early 2000s during a critical phase of its technological transformation.

## Role at Amazon
When Amazon's first platform redesign did not meet expectations, the company hired Valentine for his deep platform experience. His specific charge was to rebuild Amazon's IT infrastructure, moving it away from a collection of siloed systems.

## Significance
The hiring of a software platform leader like Valentine, rather than a traditional IT professional, was significant. It underscored Amazon's strategic goal to transform its IT infrastructure into a true software and data platform—a common set of building blocks that could drive scale and scope economies across its expanding businesses.
