# Perceptron

## Overview
The perceptron, developed by Frank Rosenblatt in the 1950s and 1960s, was a landmark model in the first wave of neural network research.

## Key Innovation
Its primary innovation was its ability to learn. The text identifies the perceptron as the first model that could learn the weights defining the categories given examples of inputs from each category. This was a significant step beyond earlier models like the McCulloch-Pitts Neuron, which required weights to be set manually.

## Historical Context
The perceptron was part of the cybernetics movement and was developed around the same time as the ADALINE model. Its development greatly influenced the future landscape of machine learning.
