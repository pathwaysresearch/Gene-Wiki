# National Institute of Science and Technology (NIST)

## Overview
The text identifies the National Institute of Science and Technology (NIST) as a United States government agency. Its role in the context of the provided excerpts is as a curator and provider of important resources for the machine learning community.

## Role in Machine Learning Research
NIST's key contribution discussed in the text is the creation and archiving of a large database of handwritten digits. This dataset has become a standard benchmark problem, widely used by researchers to compare the performance of new learning algorithms in a consistent and reproducible manner.

## The NIST Handwritten Digit Database
The text provides specific details about this influential dataset. It contains a total of 60,000 labeled examples of handwritten digits. Each example is a 20x20 pixel image, resulting in 400 features per image, with each pixel having an 8-bit grayscale value.
