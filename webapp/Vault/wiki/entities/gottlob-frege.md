# Gottlob Frege

## Overview
Gottlob Frege (1848-1925) was a German logician and mathematician who is considered one of the founders of modern logic. His work was a significant leap forward from earlier systems.

## Major Work
Frege's 1879 work, "Begriffschrift" (which translates to "Concept Writing" or "Conceptual Notation"), is cited as the first comprehensive exposition of modern propositional logic and first-order logic. This text introduced a rigorous formal system that became the basis for subsequent work in logic and philosophy.

## Significance
By providing a complete and rigorous formulation of logic, Frege's work was instrumental in the shift towards formal methods in philosophy and mathematics. His system provided the expressive and precise language necessary for the formal study of reasoning that underpins modern computer science and artificial intelligence.
