# Robbins Algebra

## Overview
The Robbins algebra is a simple set of axioms proposed by Herbert Robbins in 1933. For over 60 years, it was an open mathematical question whether these axioms were sufficient to define Boolean algebra.

## The Mathematical Challenge
Despite serious work by prominent mathematicians, including Alfred Tarski, no proof could be found to confirm the conjecture. The problem's longevity and the caliber of mathematicians who attempted it highlight its difficulty.

## Automated Proof
The conjecture was finally proven on October 10, 1996, by EQP, a version of the OTTER automated theorem prover. After eight days of computation, the program found a proof, marking a significant achievement for automated reasoning by solving a novel mathematical problem that had eluded human experts for decades.
