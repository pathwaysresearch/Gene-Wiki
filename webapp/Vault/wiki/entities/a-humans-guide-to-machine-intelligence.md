# A Human’s Guide to Machine Intelligence

## Overview
"A Human’s Guide to Machine Intelligence" is a book authored by Kartik Hosanagar. He introduces the book at the beginning of his talk, noting that its content forms the basis for his presentation.

## Core Topics
The book's primary focus is on machine decisions, exploring both the opportunities and the challenges they present. A central theme is the development of a governance framework for how individuals, companies, and regulators should manage and think about automated decisions powered by machine learning.

## Research Basis
The content of the book is informed by research that included interviews with various people to understand the real-world impact of machine intelligence. One such interview mentioned was with a 22-year-old biotech professional in Shenzhen, China, named Yuan Jen.
