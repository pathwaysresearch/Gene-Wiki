# Ant Financial

## Overview
Ant Financial originated from Alipay, a payment platform created by Alibaba in 2004 to solve the trust problem for its e-commerce marketplace. Alipay functioned as an escrow service, holding payments until transactions were fulfilled, thereby building trust between buyers and sellers. Ant Financial expanded from this foundation to offer a vast array of financial services, demonstrating how a transformed operating model can drive business model innovation.

## Key Services
Beyond its core Alipay payment platform, Ant Financial launched a wide roster of successful financial services. A key example is Yu'e Bao, an investment platform allowing Alipay users to easily invest small amounts of money into money market funds, which grew to become the largest money market fund in the world by 2017. Other offerings include Ant Fortune, a personal investment platform; Zhima Credit, a social credit scoring system; and MYbank, an internet banking service provider.

## AI-Powered Operating Model
The core of Ant Financial's operating model is a sophisticated, integrated data platform that collects information on user activities through the Alipay app. Artificial intelligence leverages this data to drive functions like personalization, revenue optimization, and product recommendations. A critical application is ensuring trust, where AI uses five layers of real-time digital checks on every transaction, analyzing account information and device data to validate legitimacy far faster than any human-centric process could.
