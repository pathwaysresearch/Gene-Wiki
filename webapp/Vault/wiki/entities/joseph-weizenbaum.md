# Joseph Weizenbaum

## Overview
Joseph Weizenbaum was the author of the early natural language processing program ELIZA. In his 1976 book, *Computer Power and Human Reason*, he pointed out some of the potential threats that artificial intelligence poses to society.

## Critique of AI
One of Weizenbaum's principal arguments was that AI research makes possible the idea that humans are automata. He believed this view results in a loss of individual autonomy and even of humanity itself.

## Societal Impact
Weizenbaum's concerns highlight the potential for AI to be a threatening force to the moral assumptions of society, much as Darwin's theory of evolution was in the 19th century. His work serves as a key early ethical critique of the field from one of its own practitioners.
