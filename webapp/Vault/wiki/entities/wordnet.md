# Wordnet

## Overview
Wordnet is a publicly available dictionary resource for the English language. It contains a large vocabulary of approximately 100,000 words and phrases, making it a significant tool for natural language processing tasks.

## Structure and Content
The database organizes its entries by categorizing words and phrases into their respective parts of speech. Its key feature is a network structure where words are interconnected through a variety of specified semantic relations.

## Semantic Relations
Wordnet explicitly defines the relationships between words. The text highlights several of these, including synonymy (words with similar meanings), antonymy (words with opposite meanings), and the part-of relation (meronymy), which links a component part to its whole.
