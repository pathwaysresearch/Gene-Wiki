# Eloundou et al. (2024) AI Exposure Measure

## Overview
The AI exposure measure developed by Eloundou et al. (2024) is one of the two primary approaches used in "Canaries in the Coal Mine?" to quantify how much an occupation is affected by generative AI. It is part of a body of recent work adapting earlier automation susceptibility methodologies for the generative AI era.

## Methodology
This approach estimates AI exposure at the level of individual tasks as defined by the O*NET database. The estimation is performed using the capabilities of ChatGPT, with the results being validated through human labeling to ensure accuracy. These task-level exposure scores are then aggregated to create an overall exposure measure for each occupation, identified by 2018 SOC codes.

## Specific Version Used
The study specifically focuses on the GPT-4 based "beta" exposure measures from the Eloundou et al. (2024) paper. This measure serves as a key input for categorizing occupations into quintiles of AI exposure to analyze employment trends.
