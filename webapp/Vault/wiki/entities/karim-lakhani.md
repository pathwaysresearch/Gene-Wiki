# Karim Lakhani

## Overview

Karim Lakhani is a faculty member at Harvard Business School (HBS), where he has taught for 17 years as of the time of the talk. He is a central figure in the university's research and discussion on artificial intelligence.

## Key Work: 'Competing in the Age of AI'

Lakhani is the author of the book 'Competing in the Age of AI,' which was co-authored with Marco Iansiti and published in January 2020. The book's title is noted as being 'ahead of its time,' aiming to make sense of the world and business in the context of advancing AI technologies.

## Role at Harvard

In addition to his teaching and research, Lakhani is involved with a newly launched AI Institute at Harvard Business School. He actively engages with alumni and the broader community to discuss the implications of AI, using his work and live demonstrations to illustrate its rapid progress and impact on business and creativity.
