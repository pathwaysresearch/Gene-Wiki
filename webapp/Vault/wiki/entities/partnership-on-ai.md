# Partnership on AI

## Overview
The Partnership on AI, formed in 2016, is a consortium that brings together a diverse group of stakeholders to guide the development of artificial intelligence. Its members include major AI and tech vendors like Amazon, Google, Facebook, IBM, and Sony, as well as academic institutions and nonprofit organizations.

## Mission and Goals
The stated mission of the Partnership on AI is “bringing diverse voices together across global sectors, disciplines, and demographics so developments in AI advance positive outcomes for people and society.” It aims to foster collaboration and open dialogue on the societal impacts of AI.

## Key Activities and Research
The staff and affiliates of the Partnership on AI have authored a variety of research and policy documents. Their work covers several critical aspects of AI ethics and governance, including algorithmic bias, the importance of diversity among AI developers, the role of documentation in machine learning ethics, and the challenge of misinformation.
