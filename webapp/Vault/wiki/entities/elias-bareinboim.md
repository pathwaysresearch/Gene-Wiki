# Elias Bareinboim

## Overview
Elias Bareinboim is a researcher who worked in Judea Pearl's lab and made significant contributions to the field of causal inference by extending its formal tools to new classes of problems.

## Contributions to Do-Calculus
In 2012, Bareinboim developed a complete algorithm for a class of problems not covered by earlier methods, specifically those involving finding a surrogate variable Z that can be subject to a do-operator in place of the original variable of interest X.

## Transportability and External Validity
Bareinboim's most notable work addresses the long-standing scientific problem of transportability, also known as external validity. This involves determining whether a causal effect found in an experimental study can be generalized to a different target environment. In 2015, he and Pearl presented a complete solution, using the rules of do-calculus to provide a systematic method for determining if and how causal effects can be transported, provided the assumptions about both environments can be expressed in a causal diagram.
