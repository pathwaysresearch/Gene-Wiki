# Anthem

## Strategic Vision
In 2020, Anthem announced its strategic goal to become a 'digital platform for health.' The company's vision is to shift the focus of care from reactive 'sick care' to proactive and preventive 'health care.' As articulated by Rajeev Ronanki, the company's president of digital platforms, the objective is to connect members, employers, and healthcare providers to create personalized health care and drive healthy behaviors.

## AI-Powered Member Engagement
Anthem utilizes AI to motivate its members to improve their own health. The company offers a 'digital concierge,' which is a centralized suite of tools to connect a member with a chronic or complex condition to their entire care team. Customer service interactions are supported by AI-based predictive modeling, which provides relevant information to consumers through voice or chat communications to encourage them to take an active role in their health.

## Digital Health Tools
Anthem has integrated several digital tools to support its members. In partnership with Hydrogen Health, it offers a symptom checker app that allows users to input symptoms and receive information on potential diagnoses and options for care. The company also partners with Lark to use AI and automated interventions to monitor members' health, teach them about healthy behaviors, and encourage the adoption of those behaviors at scale.
