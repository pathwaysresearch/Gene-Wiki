# Otto

## Overview
Otto is a German e-commerce venture presented as a real-world example of a business grappling with the consequences of uncertainty. The company's experience highlights a major barrier to online consumer purchases: uncertain delivery times, which can lead to a poor customer experience.

## The Impact of Delivery Uncertainty
Otto discovered a direct correlation between delivery delays and product returns. When deliveries took longer than a few days, the rate of returns increased significantly. The text explains that consumers, faced with uncertainty about when their online order would arrive, would often purchase the desired product from a physical store in the interim and then return the item from Otto when it finally arrived.

## Strategic Significance
Otto's case demonstrates how reducing operational uncertainty can create tangible business value. A poor delivery experience not only adds direct costs from handling returns but also damages customer loyalty, making consumers unlikely to use the site again. This illustrates the type of operational problem where a prediction machine—for example, one that could better predict logistics and delivery times—could provide a strategic advantage.
