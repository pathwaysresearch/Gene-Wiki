# J. A. Robinson

## Major Contributions
J. A. Robinson is a key figure in the field of automated logic. His 1965 paper, 'A machine-oriented logic based on the resolution principle,' published in JACM, introduced a foundational method for automated reasoning.

## Editorial Work
His influence in the field is also demonstrated by his role as a co-editor, with A. Voronkov, of the 'Handbook of Automated Reasoning' (2001), a major reference work in the discipline.

## Impact
The title of his 1965 paper emphasizes the creation of a logic specifically designed for machine implementation, highlighting his role in bridging theoretical logic and practical computer science for automated theorem proving.
