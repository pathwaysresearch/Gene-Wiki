# Annual Business Survey

## Overview
The Annual Business Survey is a large-scale data collection effort that, in 2018, covered 850,000 firms across the United States. It is conducted by the U.S. Census Bureau and provides representative data on various aspects of business operations, including technology use.

## Role in AI Research
The 2018 iteration of the survey is a key source for studying the early adoption and diffusion of artificial intelligence. It specifically documented firm-level use of five AI-related technologies: automated-guided vehicles, machine learning, machine vision, natural language processing, and voice recognition. This direct measurement of actual AI use provides a critical alternative to indirect methods like analyzing job postings or patents.

## Key Findings on AI Adoption
Data from the survey revealed that as of 2017, fewer than 6% of U.S. firms used any of the surveyed AI-related technologies. However, adoption was not uniform. Most very large firms (over 5,000 employees) reported some AI use, and the employment-weighted adoption rate was just over 18%. The leading sectors for adoption were Manufacturing and Information, each with rates of roughly 12%.
