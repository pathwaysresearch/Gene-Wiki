# Airbnb

## Overview
Airbnb is an online platform that connects people who want to rent out their homes with people who are looking for accommodations. Its business model is built on indirect network effects, where renters and vacationers value a large selection of available properties, and hosts value a large pool of potential renters.

## Network Structure
In contrast to Uber's highly clustered network, Airbnb's network is described as more global. The text implies that a user in one location is likely to care about the availability of rentals in a different location (e.g., San Francisco), making the overall scale of the network more valuable than in Uber's case.

## Strategic Challenges
Like Uber, Airbnb faces significant challenges with multihoming, as it is common for hosts to list their properties on multiple platforms due to low adoption costs. To combat this, Airbnb offers tools and advantages exclusively to its power users, which provide value and increase switching costs. Despite these efforts, multihoming remains common and limits profitability.
