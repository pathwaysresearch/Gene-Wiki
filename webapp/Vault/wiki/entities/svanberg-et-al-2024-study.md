# Svanberg et al. (2024) Study

## Overview
The Svanberg et al. (2024) study provides a practical assessment of AI automation, focusing specifically on computer vision technologies. It attempts to answer crucial questions about how many theoretically automatable tasks are likely to be feasibly and profitably automated in the near term.

## Key Finding
The study's base estimates conclude that among tasks exposed to computer vision, 23% can be feasibly and profitably automated within a 10-year timeframe. This figure serves as a critical adjustment factor to temper more optimistic or purely technical assessments of AI's potential.

## Role in Economic Forecasting
In the analyzed text, this study's 23% estimate is extrapolated beyond just computer vision to all AI-exposed tasks identified by Eloundou et al. This application allows the author to move from a measure of total technical exposure to a more grounded forecast of likely economic impact over the next decade.
