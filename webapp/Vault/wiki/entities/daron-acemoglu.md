# Daron Acemoglu

## Overview

Daron Acemoglu is an economist whose research is cited multiple times in the text to support key arguments about the current trajectory of AI development and its macroeconomic effects. His work provides a critical perspective on the priorities of the technology industry.

## On Tech Industry Priorities

The text references Acemoglu's work (specifically Acemoglu (2021), Acemoglu and Restrepo (2020b), and Acemoglu and Johnson (2023)) to argue that the tech industry is not currently focused on creating new tasks that improve productivity. Instead, his research is used to support the claim that the industry's areas of priority are automation and online monetization, such as through search or social media digital ads.

## On the Need for Institutional Change

Acemoglu's work is also cited (Acemoglu and Johnson (2023) and Acemoglu et al. (2023)) to suggest that achieving more beneficial outcomes from AI is not purely a technical challenge. The argument is made that realizing these gains may require the creation of new institutions, policies, and regulations to guide the technology's development and integration into the economy.
