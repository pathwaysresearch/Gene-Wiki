# René Descartes

## Overview
René Descartes was a French scientist and philosopher mentioned as a key intellectual influence in the 'Creative Intelligence' podcast. His work provides a historical and philosophical context for the show's central themes.

## Influence on 'Creative Intelligence'
Descartes' famous statement, "I think, therefore I am," is cited by host Kartik Hosanagar as the direct inspiration for the podcast. This principle is used to frame the core inquiry of the series: what it means to be human when thinking is no longer a uniquely human activity.

## Philosophical Relevance in the AI Era
The podcast leverages Descartes' principle to explore the modern challenge posed by AI. The fact that "thinking machines can outthink us" forces a re-evaluation of the very basis of human identity that Descartes' quote established, making his philosophy a critical starting point for discussing human potential today.
