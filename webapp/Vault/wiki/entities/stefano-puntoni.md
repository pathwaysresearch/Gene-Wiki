# Stefano Puntoni

## Overview
Stefano Puntoni is the Sebastian S. Kresge Professor of Marketing at The Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania. He is also the co-director of Wharton's Human AI research department. Before joining Penn, he was a professor and head of department at the Rotterdam School of Management, Erasmus University. He holds a PhD in marketing from London Business School and a degree in Statistics and Economics from the University of Padova in Italy.

## Research on AI and Technology
Professor Puntoni's research investigates how new technology is changing consumption and society. A significant portion of his work focuses on the psychological aspects of AI, including the effects of worker psychology on AI transformation and the mental health impacts of AI. For over a decade, he has been exploring feelings of psychological threat triggered by AI, publishing numerous papers on the topic and writing for managerial outlets like Harvard Business Review and MIT Sloan Management Review.

## Key Contributions to AI Adoption Discourse
In his analysis of AI adoption, Puntoni co-authored a Wharton report that presented a different view on adoption rates than a preceding MIT report. He argues for a more inspiring corporate narrative around AI that emphasizes augmentation over replacement and innovation over simple cost-cutting. He applies frameworks like Self-Determination Theory to diagnose and understand the complex psychological reactions of workers, such as identity threat, to AI implementation.
