# Tesla Autopilot

## Overview

Tesla Autopilot is an advanced driver-assistance system featured in the company's vehicles. The text discusses it in the context of public trust toward algorithms, particularly in high-stakes scenarios like self-driving cars.

## The Joshua Brown Incident

A tragic incident in May 2016 involving Joshua Brown, who was killed while using the Autopilot feature in his Tesla, became a focal point for public debate. His car crashed into a semi-truck, sparking widespread discussion about the safety and reliability of semi-autonomous driving technology.

## Public Debate and Trust

The crash highlighted the challenges of user trust and responsibility in automated systems. Critics pointed out that the Autopilot feature was still in a beta testing phase at the time and that Tesla's guidelines required drivers to remain engaged and ready to take control. The incident underscored the complexities of human-machine interaction and the need for clear communication about the capabilities and limitations of such technology.
