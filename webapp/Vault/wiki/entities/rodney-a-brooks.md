# Rodney A. Brooks

## Overview
Rodney A. Brooks is an author of several key papers in robotics and artificial intelligence published in the mid-1980s to early 1990s. His work addresses both the practical engineering of autonomous systems and the philosophical foundations of intelligence.

## Contributions to Mobile Robotics
Brooks's research provides concrete solutions for mobile robot control. A 1986 paper in the IEEE Journal of Robotics and Automation details "A robust layered control system for a mobile robot." In a 1985 paper with T. Lozano-Perez, he also developed "A subdivision algorithm in configuration space for find-path with rotation," addressing a fundamental problem in robot motion planning.

## The 'Intelligence without Representation' Thesis
Brooks is noted for his influential 1991 paper in the Artificial Intelligence journal titled "Intelligence without representation." This work challenges traditional symbolic AI by proposing that complex intelligent behavior can emerge without explicit, centralized world models. This idea is part of his broader "Engineering approach to building complete, intelligent beings," as described in a 1989 publication.
