# Thomas Bayes

## Overview
The Reverend Thomas Bayes is credited as the first person to discover a special case of the formula now known as Bayes' rule.

## Contributions to Probability Theory
His work laid the groundwork for what is now known as Bayesian statistics. The theorem named after him provides a mathematical means to update a belief in light of new evidence, forming a cornerstone of modern probability theory and inference.

## Legacy
Although the general version of the rule was later discovered independently by Pierre-Simon Laplace, Bayes' name is attached to this fundamental formula, and he is recognized as a key figure in the history of statistics.
