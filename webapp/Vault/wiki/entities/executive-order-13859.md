# Executive Order 13859

## Overview
Executive Order 13859, titled "Maintaining American Leadership in Artificial Intelligence," is a key policy document that formalized the US government's strategy for AI adoption and leadership.

## Key Mandate
A central requirement of the executive order was for federal agencies to create publicly available inventories of their use cases of artificial intelligence. This mandate was intended to increase transparency and track the government's progress in deploying the technology.

## Context and Impact
The order followed a study by the Administrative Conference of the United States which found that a significant portion of federal agencies were already experimenting with AI. The order helped to structure and accelerate these efforts, pushing for more coordinated and visible adoption of AI across the government's civilian and defense sectors.
