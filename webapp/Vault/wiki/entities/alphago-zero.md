# AlphaGo Zero

## Overview

AlphaGo Zero is a successor to the original AlphaGo system, also developed by Google's DeepMind. It represents a more advanced application of AI learning techniques.

## Learning Method

AlphaGo Zero is distinguished by its use of a pure reinforcement learning approach. Unlike prior versions of AlphaGo, which used data from hundreds of thousands of human games as input, the AlphaGo Zero system was given only the rules of the game. It learned to master Go by figuring out the best approaches on its own. The "Zero" in its name signifies this absence of external data.

## Performance

The system demonstrated the power of its learning method by beating the original AlphaGo at its own game, showcasing a significant leap in AI capability.
