# Cardiogram

## Overview
Cardiogram is a company that builds products to help diagnose heart disease by combining rich personal data with a prediction machine. It is cited as an example of how AI is being applied in the healthcare sector to generate valuable predictions from consumer-level data.

## How It Works
The service operates by using heart rate data collected from an individual's smartphone or wearable device as "input data." This personal data is fed into a prediction machine, specifically a deep neural network, which then predicts the unknown information of whether that person has an abnormal heart rhythm. The combination of the prediction machine and an individual's personal data is crucial for generating a personalized risk assessment.

## Performance and Impact
The text states that Cardiogram's deep neural network can detect an irregular heart rhythm, medically known as atrial fibrillation, with 97 percent accuracy. This is highly significant because such abnormalities are responsible for about a quarter of all strokes. By providing better predictions, the service enables doctors to deliver better, preventative treatment, such as prescribing stroke-preventing drugs.
