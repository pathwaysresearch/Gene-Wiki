# Equifax Data Breach (2017)

## Overview and Cause
The 2017 data breach at Equifax is presented as a significant case study in corporate security failures. According to the company's former CEO, the massive breach was precipitated by the error of a single employee, highlighting the vulnerability of complex systems to human error.

## Corporate Response and Disclosure
The company's handling of the incident drew significant criticism, particularly regarding the delayed disclosure of the hack to the public. This delay raised questions about whether the company had broken the law and damaged public trust.

## Regulatory Aftermath
The breach triggered a major response from federal agencies. The United States Accountability Office (GAO) produced a report on the incident, and Equifax faced the expectation of punishment from both the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) and the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) for its failure to protect consumer data.
