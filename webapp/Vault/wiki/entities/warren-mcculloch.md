# Warren McCulloch

## Overview
Warren McCulloch was a neurophysiologist who made foundational contributions to the fields of artificial intelligence and cybernetics.

## Key Contribution
In 1943, McCulloch co-authored the landmark paper, “A Logical Calculus of Ideas Immanent in Nervous Activity,” with mathematician Walter Pitts. This paper is credited with introducing the first artificial neural network architecture.

## The McCulloch-Pitts Neuron
The model they proposed was a simplified computational representation of how biological neurons might function together in animal brains. This "artificial neuron" had binary inputs and a single binary output and could be networked to perform complex computations using propositional logic, laying the theoretical groundwork for the entire field of neural networks.
