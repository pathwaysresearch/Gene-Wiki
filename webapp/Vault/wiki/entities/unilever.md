# Unilever

## Overview
Unilever is a company that utilizes AI in its business operations and has developed a sophisticated governance framework to manage its deployment. This framework ensures that AI applications align with the company's values and regulatory requirements.

## AI Assurance Process
Unilever has implemented a formal AI assurance process to review and approve AI use cases. This process involves risk assessment, recommendation of mitigation strategies like "human in the loop," and final approval from a senior executive board composed of legal, HR, and AI department representatives. If risks cannot be mitigated, an application may be rejected for violating company values.

## Example in Practice
A real-world example of this process involved a proposed system for sales agents to prove work attendance by sending a daily selfie. A further objective to use computer vision AI to also check if the agent's personal appearance met a required standard was evaluated through the assurance process. This prompted the project team to think more broadly than just the legality and efficacy of the app, demonstrating the process's role in fostering ethical thinking.
