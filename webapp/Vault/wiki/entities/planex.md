# PLANEX

## Overview
PLANEX was a pioneering system developed by Fikes et al. in 1972. It is recognized as the first online planner that incorporated execution monitoring.

## Functionality
The primary role of PLANEX was to oversee the execution of plans generated by another system. It monitored the progress of the plan and could initiate repairs or replanning when execution deviated from the expected course.

## Integration with STRIPS
PLANEX was designed to work in conjunction with the STRIPS planner. This combination was used to control the robot Shakey, representing one of the earliest examples of a system that could both generate a plan and adaptively manage its execution in a real-world environment.
