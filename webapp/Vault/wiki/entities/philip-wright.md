# Philip Wright

## Overview
Philip Wright was an economist who, in a 1928 monograph, conducted an analysis of the elasticity of supply for flaxseed oil. His work is highlighted for its methodological innovation in the field of economics.

## Pioneering Methodological Contributions
Wright's analysis was remarkable for being the first time an economist had used a path diagram to model a problem. This was a brave and novel approach, as the technique was completely unknown in economics at the time. He also used an instrumental variable to solve a complex causal problem involving feedback between supply, demand, and price.

## Application of Instrumental Variables
In his model, Wright deliberately introduced the variable 'Yield per Acre' of flaxseed as an instrument. He reasoned that yield directly affects supply but has no direct correlation to consumer demand. This instrument allowed him to disentangle the reciprocal effects of supply on price and price on supply, a notoriously difficult problem in econometrics.
