# Eloundou et al. (2024)

## Overview
Eloundou et al. (2024) is a key academic work cited as the source for a primary measure of occupational exposure to Generative AI. The measures from this paper are used extensively throughout the "Canariesinthecoalmine" analysis to categorize jobs into different levels of AI exposure.

## Contribution to the Analysis
The paper's main contribution is providing the "GPT-4 β measures." These measures are used to define the AI exposure quintiles that form the basis of comparison in numerous figures, including Figure 5 (on compensation) and Figures A12, A16, A23, A27, and A28 (on employment). This allows the authors to systematically compare outcomes for workers in occupations deemed more or less susceptible to AI's influence.

## Role in the Research
The reliance on Eloundou et al. (2024) positions it as a foundational source for the paper's core independent variable. The findings regarding the divergence in employment between "More exposed" and "Less exposed" groups after early 2023 are directly dependent on the validity and construction of the exposure measures from this specific study.
