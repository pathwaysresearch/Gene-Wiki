# Rahul Kapoor

## Overview
Rahul Kapoor is the chairperson of the management department at Wharton. He contributes to the AI discussion by drawing parallels between the current state of AI and the historical evolution of other transformative technologies.

## Core Concept: Dominant Design
Kapoor explains the concept of a "dominant design," using the early automotive industry as an example where the internal combustion engine won out over competing technologies. He defines a dominant design as a technology architecture that satisfies both supply-side factors (innovation goals, cost) and demand-side factors (value drivers, needs) at a mass-market scale.

## Application to AI
By introducing this concept, Kapoor provides a framework for thinking about how the currently fragmented and rapidly evolving AI landscape might eventually coalesce around a particular architecture or set of standards. His perspective suggests that a winning formula for AI will need to be viable for producers and valuable for a broad base of users.
