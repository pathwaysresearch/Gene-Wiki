# Alex Graves

## Overview
Alex Graves is a key researcher in the application and development of recurrent neural networks for complex sequence tasks, as evidenced by his numerous publications on the topic, including a 2012 book on supervised sequence labeling with RNNs.

## Major Contributions
Graves is a primary author of the 2006 paper introducing Connectionist Temporal Classification (CTC), a groundbreaking method for labeling unsegmented sequence data with RNNs. This work has been fundamental to the development of modern end-to-end recognition systems.

## Applications of RNNs
His work demonstrates the practical power of RNNs and LSTMs. He has applied them to generate sequences (2013), achieve end-to-end speech recognition (Graves and Jaitly, 2014), and perform offline handwriting recognition using multidimensional recurrent networks (Graves and Schmidhuber, 2009). His research often involves collaboration with Jürgen Schmidhuber on architectures like LSTMs.
