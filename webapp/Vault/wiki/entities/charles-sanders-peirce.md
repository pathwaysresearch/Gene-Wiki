# Charles Sanders Peirce

## Overview
Charles Sanders Peirce was a logician whose work in logic was pioneering and developed in parallel to that of Gottlob Frege.

## Contributions to Logic
Peirce conducted in-depth studies of the logic of relations, a crucial area that Aristotelian logic could not handle. Building on this work, he independently developed first-order logic in 1883, slightly after Gottlob Frege. His system, like Frege's, incorporated quantifiers and relational predicates, significantly advancing the expressive power of formal logic.

## Significance
Peirce's independent development of first-order logic underscores the importance and timeliness of this logical innovation. His focus on relations was a key step in moving beyond the one-place predicates that had limited logic since Aristotle, enabling the formal analysis of more complex statements and inferences.
