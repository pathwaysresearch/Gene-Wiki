# Claude

## Overview
Claude is identified in the text as a large language model (LLM), a type of artificial intelligence designed to understand and generate human-like text.

## Role in Research
Claude was the subject of a study by Handa et al. (2025) focused on understanding AI-human interaction. The researchers analyzed conversations between users and Claude to identify recurring patterns in how people integrate AI into their workflows.

## Significance
The analysis of interactions with Claude served as the empirical basis for Handa et al.'s classification framework. This framework, which distinguishes between Automative and Augmentative behaviors, provides a structured understanding of the different ways users leverage LLMs like Claude.
