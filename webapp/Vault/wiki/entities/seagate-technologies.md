# Seagate Technologies

## Overview
Seagate Technologies is described as the world's largest manufacturer of disk drives. The company has made significant use of its massive amounts of factory sensor data over the last five years to enhance the quality and efficiency of its manufacturing processes.

## AI for Quality Control
A primary focus of Seagate's manufacturing analytics has been the automation of visual inspection. This involves using AI to analyze images of silicon wafers, from which disk drive heads are made, and the tools used in their fabrication.

## Implementation of Deep Learning
Seagate's Minnesota factory created an automated fault detection and classification system using deep learning image recognition algorithms. Deployed in late 2017, these models can detect and classify wafer defects directly from microscope images. Other models are used to ensure the quality of the inspection process itself, for instance by detecting out-of-focus electron microscopes.
