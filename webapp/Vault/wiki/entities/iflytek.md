# iFlytek

## Overview
iFlytek is a company that has successfully commercialized AI by developing a deep learning-powered service for natural language processing. This service is used by over 500 million people in China for tasks like translation, transcription, and communication.

## Commercial Applications
The text provides several concrete examples of how iFlytek's technology is used in daily life. Landlords use it to communicate with tenants who speak other languages, hospital patients use it to get directions from robots, doctors use it to dictate a patient's medical details, and drivers use it to communicate with their vehicles.

## The Learning Cycle
A key aspect of iFlytek's AI is its ability to improve through use. The text notes that the more the AI is used, the more data it collects. This data is then used to further train the system, allowing it to learn and become better. With a massive user base, this creates a virtuous cycle of rapid improvement.
