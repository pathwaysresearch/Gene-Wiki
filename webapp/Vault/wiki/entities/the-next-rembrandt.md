# The Next Rembrandt

## Creation Process
"The Next Rembrandt" was created approximately 350 years after the artist's death using artificial intelligence. Scientists and engineers applied learning algorithms to analyze Rembrandt's portraits and identify typical characteristics. These algorithms determined the subject would be a Caucasian male, aged 30-40, with facial hair, a hat, and a white collar, facing to the right.

## Technical Execution
After algorithms assembled the components into a full composition, a 3-D printer was used to physically create the painting. The printer deposited thirteen layers of paint-based UV ink onto a canvas. This process was specifically designed to closely imitate the master's distinctive brushstrokes, giving the final work a physical texture similar to an original painting.

## Significance
The project serves as a prominent example of AI becoming a force in the creative arts. It demonstrates how AI can not only replicate existing artistic styles with high fidelity but also connect various disciplines like art history, data science, and engineering. The techniques used, such as style-transfer, are now being explored by programs like Google's AMI to expand the range of artistic possibilities.
