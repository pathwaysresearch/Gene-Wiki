# US Government

## AI Adoption Trajectory
While the US government had a slow start in adopting AI outside of military and intelligence sectors, it has "come on strong" in recent years. A study as of February 2020 found that nearly half of federal agencies (45 percent) had experimented with AI and related machine learning tools.

## Policy and Mandates
The federal government's push into AI was formalized by Executive Order 13859, "Maintaining American Leadership in Artificial Intelligence." This order required federal agencies to create and maintain publicly available inventories of their AI use cases, promoting transparency and coordination.

## Agency-Level Implementations
Various federal agencies have deployed AI for specific missions. NASA has launched RPA pilot projects for back-office functions in HR and finance, with 86 percent of some transactions completed without human intervention. NOAA has an AI strategy to improve efficiency across all its mission areas. The Social Security Administration uses AI in its adjudication work to manage high caseloads, and the Department of Veteran's Affairs established a National Artificial Intelligence Institute.
