# Open Subsurface Data Universe (OSDU)

## Overview
The Open Subsurface Data Universe (OSDU) is a collaborative ecosystem created by Shell and its business partners. It was established after Shell realized its internal subsurface data universe needed to be accessible to its many collaborators in exploration activities.

## Membership and Scope
Although only a few years old, the OSDU ecosystem is quite large, consisting of over 160 companies. Its members include a diverse range of organizations such as energy companies, technology vendors, consultants, and academic researchers.

## Purpose and Shared Assets
The primary focus of OSDU is the exchange of data across organizations, with established standards for sharing seismic, well, reservoir, and production data. Beyond data, the ecosystem also serves as a vehicle for sharing models, applications, platforms, and training materials among its members.
