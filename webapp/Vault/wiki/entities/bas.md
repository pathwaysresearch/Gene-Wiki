# Bas

## Overview
Bas is a Grammy-nominated and multi-platinum recording artist affiliated with Dreamville Records, J. Cole's label. He participated in the panel discussion to offer his perspective as a working musician and songwriter on the integration of AI into the creative process.

## Perspective on AI as a Creative Tool
Bas views AI as a natural extension of how technology has always made music creation more accessible. He explains that as a songwriter who doesn't produce, he often has musical ideas inspired by other songs. Previously, he would have to describe these ideas to a human producer, a process he now sees as essentially a text prompt that can be given to an AI to generate a starting point. He has also used AI to hear how a chorus he wrote might sound when sung by a different vocalist, demonstrating its utility for experimentation.

## Views on Industry Dynamics
Bas emphasizes the immense pressure on artists to maintain a high level of output in the modern music landscape. With thousands of new albums released every week, he notes that an album is "old news" almost immediately, and artists who don't release music annually risk becoming irrelevant. He believes AI will be a crucial tool to help artists meet this demand for a constant stream of new content, as every song is a product that generates revenue. He concludes that the future hits will come from a creator using AI, not from AI alone.
