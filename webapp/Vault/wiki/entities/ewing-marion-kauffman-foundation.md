# Ewing Marion Kauffman Foundation

## Overview

The Ewing Marion Kauffman Foundation is an organization involved in supporting entrepreneurship.

## Role in Data Collection

The foundation was a key partner in the Annual Survey of Entrepreneurs (ASEs). It worked alongside the U.S. Census Bureau and the Minority Business Development Agency to conduct this survey.

## Legacy

The ASE, which the foundation helped create, has since been consolidated into the broader Annual Business Survey (ABS), continuing the legacy of collecting data on U.S. entrepreneurship.
