# Khan Academy

## Organizational Model

Khan Academy is a nonprofit organization founded on the principle that in sectors like education, the decision-makers, payers, and beneficiaries are often different people, making a for-profit model problematic. The founder believed a nonprofit structure was the best way to build an institution that could one day educate billions of people, as it is less constrained by politics than government and by profit motives than a business.

## Educational Approach

The organization's model focuses on lowering the "activation energy" required for student engagement. It provides on-demand videos, searchable content, and digital practice exercises, often incorporating game mechanics. These tools make it easier for students to engage with material compared to traditional methods like independently working through a textbook.

## Integration of AI

Khan Academy has actively integrated AI into its platform through its product, Khanmigo. This AI tool is designed to act as a tutor by engaging students in Socratic conversations, and as a teaching assistant by identifying classroom trends and helping with tasks like lesson planning and grading.
