# Organizational Capital

## Overview
Organizational capital is a type of intangible asset that captures the value of a firm's unique combination of business processes, management techniques, and corporate culture. The study defines it specifically as the capitalized stock of selling, general, and administrative (SG&A) expenses.

## Data and Measurement
The data for organizational capital used in the analysis is sourced from the work of Peters and Taylor (2017). The authors of this paper extend these measures through 2017 using Wharton Research Data Services (WRDS) data, following the guidelines established in the source paper. 

## Role in the Dataset
Along with R&D capital, market values, and book values, organizational capital is a key variable in the firm-level panel dataset constructed for the study, which spans from 1961 to 2017. It is one of the primary forms of intangible capital considered in the broader economic context of the paper, which is merged into Compustat firm-level data for analysis.
