# Competing in the Age of AI

## Overview
"Competing in the Age of AI" (2020) by Marco Iansiti and Karim R. Lakhani of Harvard Business School (HBS) is recommended as the "best business-school text on AI strategy." The book's full title is "Competing in the Age of AI: Strategy and Leadership When Algorithms and Networks Run the World."

## Origin and Core Thesis
The book originated from a 2019 Harvard Business Review (HBR) article of the same name by the same authors. Its central focus is on how businesses can develop strategy and leadership in an environment increasingly dominated by AI, algorithms, and networks.

## Publisher and Availability
The book is published by Harvard Business Review Press. The text also notes that HBR Press offers significant quantity discounts for bulk purchases, suggesting its use in corporate training and professional development settings.
