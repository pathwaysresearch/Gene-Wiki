# Eloundou et al. (2023)

## Overview
The study by Eloundou et al. (2023) is a primary source of data for the analysis presented in the text. It provides a comprehensive list of tasks deemed to be "exposed" to recent advances in AI, particularly generative AI.

## Role in TFP Estimation
The author uses the data from Eloundou et al. as the basis for estimating the share of the economy impacted by AI. The study's list of 4,089 exposed tasks is the starting point for calculating the potential TFP and GDP gains from AI-driven cost savings.

## Use in "Easy vs. Hard" Classification
The author builds directly upon the Eloundou et al. data by developing an algorithm to classify each of the exposed tasks into "easy" or "hard" categories. This classification reveals that 74% of the wage bill-weighted exposed tasks identified in the study are considered "easy" for current AI, a crucial step in refining the overall productivity estimates.
