# Fidelity Investments

## Overview
Fidelity Investments is a major financial services company that undertook a significant initiative to integrate artificial intelligence more deeply into its business. The effort was driven by senior leadership, including Chairman and CEO Abby Johnson, who saw the strategic need for AI when technology companies like Google and Microsoft announced their "AI first" strategies.

## AI Strategy and Implementation
In 2011, Fidelity established a new AI Center of Excellence, led by executive vice president Vipin Mayar. The center's work was launched with a series of small group sessions, organized by business unit, to produce a list of key AI initiatives, use cases, and goals. The company found a wide range of potential applications for AI across every facet of its business, prompting a need to prioritize its AI strategy and build serious capabilities.

## Talent and Capabilities
To support its AI ambitions, Fidelity recognized the need to hire top data scientists and expanded its recruitment efforts to attract talent typically drawn to technology companies or Silicon Valley. The company leveraged its extensive data, unique business use cases, and corporate culture as a major draw for this talent, successfully building a world-class team to drive its AI initiatives.
