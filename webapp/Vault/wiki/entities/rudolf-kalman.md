# Rudolf Kalman

## Overview
Rudolf Kalman is the researcher for whom the Kalman filter is named. He published a paper in 1960 that described the filter, which provided a simpler, direct state-space modeling approach to filtering problems compared to previous methods.

## Key Contributions
Kalman's primary contribution was the description of the filter for forward inference in linear systems with Gaussian noise. The text notes that he met the Russian mathematician Ruslan Stratonovich, who had previously obtained similar results, in Moscow in 1960.

## Role in NASA's Apollo Program
Kalman played a crucial role in the practical application of his filter. Following a visit to NASA Ames Research Center in 1960, he identified the method's potential for tracking rocket trajectories. This insight led to the filter's implementation in the Apollo missions, cementing its importance in engineering and control systems.
