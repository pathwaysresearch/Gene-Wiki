# Abraham Wald

## Overview
The text identifies Abraham Wald as "one of the best statisticians in history." He is presented as an example of a human with a deep understanding of the mathematics of statistics and a flexible mind capable of modeling the process that generates data.

## Key Contribution: The Missing Data Problem
Wald's key insight, as described in the text, involved analyzing data on bullet holes in returning aircraft. He understood that the crucial data was missing: the bullet holes on the planes that did not return. This required an understanding of where the data came from, a problem that had not arisen before and for which there were no prior examples to draw from.

## Significance for AI
Wald's solution demonstrates a type of modeling skill that is currently beyond the abilities of prediction machines. His work highlights the danger of the "unknown knowns" trap, where one might draw incorrect conclusions from available data without considering the process that generated it. For the foreseeable future, humans with such modeling skills are needed to work with prediction machines to avoid mixing cause and effect.
