# John Koza

## Overview
John Koza is a key figure associated with the advancement and popularization of the field of genetic programming.

## Role in Genetic Programming
His work, specifically publications from 1992 and 1994, is credited with having spurred significant interest in the technique.

## Key Work and Applications
The text cites work by Koza et al. (1999) that describes experiments using genetic programming for the practical task of designing circuit devices.
