# Daniel Kahneman

## Overview
Daniel Kahneman is identified in the text as a prominent thinker who is both confident about the potential of AI and worried about its implications for the world. He is presented alongside figures like Elon Musk as someone deeply considering the future of AI.

## Contributions to AI Discourse
Kahneman is quoted offering a framework for how an advanced AI or robot would differ from humans. He proposes three main differences: superior statistical reasoning, higher emotional intelligence, and greater wisdom. He defines this wisdom as "broad framing"—the ability to see a problem in a wide context, which he contrasts with the "narrow" and "noisy" thinking of humans.

## Perspective on AI's Future
According to the text, Kahneman believes that AI will eventually be wiser than people precisely because it will be endowed with broad framing. He expresses a strong conviction that it is very easy to improve upon human thinking and that there is little that humans can do that computers will not eventually learn to do, highlighting his belief in AI's transformative potential.
