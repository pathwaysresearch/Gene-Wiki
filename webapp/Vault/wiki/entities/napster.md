# Napster

## Historical Context
Napster was a service that emerged roughly 25 years ago which made the sharing and discovery of music highly convenient for a mass audience through a peer-to-peer network. It fundamentally changed how consumers accessed music.

## The Intellectual Property Challenge
The text highlights that Napster's model disrupted the music industry by allowing widespread sharing without a viable business model to provide financial incentives back to artists and rights holders. This created a legal and economic problem that required a new solution.

## Analogy to Generative AI
The speaker uses Napster as a historical parallel for the current state of generative AI, which relies on vast amounts of existing intellectual property for its training and output. The analogy suggests that, just as iTunes and Spotify eventually created legal and monetized frameworks for digital music, a similar solution must be developed for AI to ensure creators are compensated for their work.
