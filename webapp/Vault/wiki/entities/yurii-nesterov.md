# Yurii Nesterov

## Overview
Yurii Nesterov is a researcher whose work has had a significant impact on the field of optimization for machine learning. The provided text highlights his contributions to two important optimization algorithms.

## Key Contributions
Nesterov is credited with proposing the optimization technique known as Dual Averaging, more commonly referred to as Follow The Regularized Leader (FTRL). This algorithm is particularly noted for its ability to produce highly sparse models when used with L1 regularization. Additionally, the widely used Nesterov Accelerated Gradient (NAG) or Nesterov Momentum optimization method is named after him, indicating his foundational work in this area.

## Significance
His work provides powerful tools for training machine learning models. Nesterov Accelerated Gradient is a standard technique for speeding up convergence, while FTRL offers a specialized solution for practitioners who require models that are both compact and fast at inference time due to sparsity.
