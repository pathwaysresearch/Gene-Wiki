# Creative Intelligence

## Overview
"Creative Intelligence" is a biweekly technology podcast hosted by Karthik Hosanagar, the John C. Hower Professor at the Wharton School. The show, which launched around May 2025, focuses on the intersection of artificial intelligence, creativity, and human potential, examining how AI is reshaping industries and work.

## Format and Guests
The podcast features in-depth conversations with prominent figures from various fields. Notable guests have included neuroscientist Anil Seth, discussing the possibility of machine consciousness; Accenture Song CEO David Droga, on AI's impact on the creative industry; and author Sangeet Paul Choudary, on how AI transforms the entire system of work.

## Key Themes
Episodes delve into complex questions such as the nature of consciousness in AI, the evolving role of creativity in a technologically advanced world, and the systemic changes AI brings to organizational structures and job roles. The discussions aim to move beyond simplistic narratives, such as "AI won’t take your job, but someone using AI will," to provide a more nuanced understanding of AI's impact.
