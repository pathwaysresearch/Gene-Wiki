# Handa et al. (2025)

## Overview
Handa et al. (2025) is a research paper that analyzes millions of conversations with the AI assistant Claude to determine which economic tasks are being performed with AI. The paper is cited in "Canariesinthecoalmine" as the source for an alternative, usage-based measure of AI exposure.

## Contribution to the Analysis
This paper provides a distinct method for measuring AI exposure, which is used to construct exposure quintiles. As described in the caption for Figure A6, this measure is "based on the share of queries to Claude that relate to tasks associated with an occupation." This provides a real-world, adoption-based metric as opposed to a theoretical or potential-exposure metric.

## Role in the Research
The data from Handa et al. (2025) is used in Figures A6 and A17 to conduct a parallel analysis of employment changes by age and exposure. By using this alternative measure, the authors can test the robustness of their findings. If the results are consistent across both the Eloundou et al. (2024) potential exposure measure and the Handa et al. (2025) usage-based measure, it strengthens the overall conclusion.
