# Bengio et al. (2001)

## Overview
The work by Bengio et al. in 2001 is a highly influential publication in the history of natural language processing. It is credited with returning the research focus to modeling words as the primary units in language models, moving away from character-level or other representations.

## Key Contributions
This paper is best known for introducing the concept of neural language models. A key feature of these models is that they learn and produce interpretable word embeddings as part of their architecture. This provided a powerful new method for representing words in a continuous vector space.

## Significance and Impact
The introduction of neural language models by this work yielded immediate and significant improvements in language modeling performance. The ideas and techniques presented, particularly the use of learned word embeddings, became foundational for much of the subsequent research and application of deep learning in NLP.
