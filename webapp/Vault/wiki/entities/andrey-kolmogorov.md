# Andrey Kolmogorov

## Overview
Andrey Kolmogorov was a highly influential 20th-century mathematician with wide-ranging contributions to mathematics and computer science.

## Contributions to Learning Theory
In 1965, Kolmogorov independently developed the concept now known as Kolmogorov complexity, or algorithmic complexity. This theory provides a formal, algorithmic definition for the notion of simplicity, a concept central to scientific principles like Ockham's razor and foundational to computational learning theory.
