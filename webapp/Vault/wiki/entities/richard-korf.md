# Richard Korf

## Overview
Richard Korf is an AI researcher whose work is cited in the context of online and real-time search algorithms.

## Contributions
He developed the LRTA* algorithm in 1990. This work was part of a broader investigation into search strategies for environments where an agent has a fixed, limited amount of time to decide on an action, a common constraint in domains like two-player games.

## Significance
Korf's LRTA* algorithm is noted as a special case of more general reinforcement learning algorithms for stochastic environments, providing a concrete method for agents to learn and act in real-time.
