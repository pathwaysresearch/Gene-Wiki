# Marilyn vos Savant

## Overview
Marilyn vos Savant is an American columnist who, in her "Ask Marilyn" column, addressed the famous probability puzzle known as the Monty Hall problem. Her correct answer and simple explanation generated a massive and passionate response from the public, including many academics.

## The Monty Hall Controversy
When vos Savant published her solution, correctly stating that the contestant should switch doors, she received more than 10,000 letters, most of them disagreeing with her. Many critics, including some who claimed to have PhDs in mathematics and statistics, insisted she was "utterly incorrect" and that the probability for the remaining two doors must be one-half. This widespread and often condescending criticism from supposed experts highlighted the counter-intuitive nature of the problem.

## Her Solution
The text praises vos Savant's solution for its "astounding simplicity." Instead of a complex probabilistic proof, she made a simple list or table of the three possible arrangements of the car and goats. Assuming the contestant always picks Door 1 initially, this table clearly shows that the "Stay" strategy wins in only one of the three equally likely scenarios, while the "Switch" strategy wins in the other two. This compelling, easy-to-understand demonstration proved the advantage of switching.
