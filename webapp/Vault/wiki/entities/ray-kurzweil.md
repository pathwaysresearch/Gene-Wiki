# Ray Kurzweil

## Overview
Ray Kurzweil is currently the most visible advocate for the view that a technological Singularity is approaching. He has written extensively on the topic, notably in his 2005 book, *The Singularity is Near*.

## Vision of The Singularity
Kurzweil argues that the Singularity will allow humanity to transcend the limitations of our biological bodies and brains. He predicts that by the end of the 21st century, the nonbiological portion of our intelligence will be trillions of trillions of times more powerful than unaided human intelligence, giving us power over our own mortality and allowing us to live as long as we want.

## Acknowledgment of Risks
While optimistic, Kurzweil also acknowledges the potential dangers associated with this rapid technological advancement. He notes that the Singularity will also amplify humanity's ability to act on its destructive inclinations, suggesting that its full story has not yet been written.
