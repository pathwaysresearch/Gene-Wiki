# Gemini

## Overview
Gemini is a Large Language Model (LLM) that was utilized in this research as an evaluation tool to assess the quality of written communication by customer service agents.

## Role in the Study
The researchers employed Gemini to systematically score the text from each agent conversation on two specific dimensions: 'comprehensibility' and 'native fluency'. This provided a quantitative and scalable method for measuring how the introduction of an AI assistant affected the quality and style of an agent's writing.

## Application and Findings
The study used Gemini to analyze linguistic nuances, such as when non-native English speakers used grammatically correct but uncommon phrases like 'to have a blessed day'. The scores generated by Gemini demonstrated that agents with AI assistance produced text with markedly higher comprehensibility and native fluency, suggesting the AI tool helped them align with more standard communication patterns.
