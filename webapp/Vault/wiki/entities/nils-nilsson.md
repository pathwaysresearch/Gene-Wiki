# Nils Nilsson

## Overview
Nils Nilsson was an influential figure in the field of artificial intelligence who contributed to the vision of general-purpose AI.

## Key Proposal
He proposed a measure for AI success that went beyond performance in games. He suggested that a more meaningful benchmark for achieving real human-level intelligence would be the ability for an AI to master a variety of jobs.

## Vision for General AI
Nilsson's proposal was part of a broader effort to envision the future of AI. His idea of measuring intelligence through job mastery connects AI research to real-world economic and workforce dynamics, such as the potential for automation suggested by studies showing many occupations could be automated.
