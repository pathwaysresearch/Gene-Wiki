# VisiCalc

## Overview
VisiCalc was the first electronic spreadsheet program, created by Dan Bricklin (141, 163, 164). Its introduction on personal computers dramatically lowered the cost and effort of financial calculation and forecasting.

## Role as an Analogy for AI
The book uses VisiCalc as a primary analogy for the economic impact of AI (141–142). Just as VisiCalc lowered the cost of arithmetic, AI is lowering the cost of prediction. This comparison is used to explain how a new tool doesn't just make old tasks faster but enables entirely new work flows and business strategies that were previously impractical.

## Significance for Value Capture
The story of VisiCalc is also used to illustrate a point about value capture (163, 164). While the program created immense value for its users and the broader PC industry, its creators captured only a small fraction of that value. This serves as a cautionary tale that the value of a foundational AI tool may be dwarfed by the value created in its diverse applications.
