# DBS Bank

## Overview

DBS Bank is a bank in Southeast Asia cited as a key example of a company that has effectively combined process reengineering and artificial intelligence to achieve more than just incremental performance improvements.

## AI-Driven Process Improvements

The bank used AI to enable major process improvements in its anti-money laundering (AML) efforts, successfully reducing the time required to evaluate a potential AML case by one-third. In its customer centers in India and Singapore, AI allowed the bank to manage a six-fold increase in customers and a twelve-fold increase in financial transactions without adding any staff.

## Data Strategy

As a bank, DBS operates in an industry where data is already voluminous. The company is noted for using its customer and transaction data to learn more about its customers and then turning that data back to them to help them better manage their finances.
