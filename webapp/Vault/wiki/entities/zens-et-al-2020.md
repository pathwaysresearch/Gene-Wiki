# Zens et al. (2020)

## Overview
Zens, Böck, and Zörner (2020) is a paper published in the *Journal of Economic Dynamics and Control* that examines how monetary policy heterogeneously affects the U.S. labor market. In the context of the "Canariesinthecoalmine" paper, it is cited as a source for a specific economic variable: interest rate exposure.

## Contribution to the Analysis
The data from Zens et al. (2020) is used to control for a potential confounding factor in the analysis of AI's impact. The period under study (post-2022) was characterized by both the rise of generative AI and significant changes in interest rates. To isolate the effect of AI, the authors use this data to conduct a specific sub-analysis.

## Role in the Research
The measure of interest rate exposure from this paper is used specifically in the analysis for Figure A23. This figure examines employment changes by AI exposure quintile "considering only occupations with below median interest rate exposure." This methodological step is designed to ensure that the observed effects are attributable to AI exposure and not to the contemporaneous tightening of monetary policy, which might disproportionately affect certain occupations.
