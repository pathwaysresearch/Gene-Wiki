# Core-Based Statistical Area (CBSA)

## Overview
A Core-Based Statistical Area (CBSA) is a geographical unit used for statistical purposes. The research on AI adoption focuses specifically on the largest CBSAs, defined as those with a population greater than 1,000,000, to analyze patterns of AI use among new firms.

## Application in AI Adoption Research
In the study of AI adoption by startups, CBSAs serve as the primary unit of analysis for geographic patterns. A map (Figure 5) visualizes data for each large CBSA, with bubble sizes representing the number of single-unit startups using AI and a color gradient indicating the percentile rank of the AI usage rate within that area.

## Observed Geographic Patterns
The analysis reveals distinct geographic hubs for AI use. High concentrations are found in areas known for pioneering technologies, such as Silicon Valley and Research Triangle. However, the study also identifies high shares of startups using AI in CBSAs throughout the South and the West, including the metro areas of Nashville, San Antonio, Las Vegas, New Orleans, San Diego, and Tampa.
