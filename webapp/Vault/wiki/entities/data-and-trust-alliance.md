# Data and Trust Alliance

## Overview
The Data and Trust Alliance was established in 2020 as a consortium of CEOs. It is notable for having a large proportion of non-tech employers among its members, with Deloitte being a founding member.

## Mission and Goals
The alliance is focused on advancing responsible data practices. One of its primary goals is to “develop new practices and tools to advance the responsible use of data, algorithms, and AI.”

## Inaugural Project
The first major project identified and undertaken by the Data and Trust Alliance is titled “Algorithmic Safety: Mitigating Bias in Workforce Decisions.” This project highlights the consortium's focus on practical applications of ethical AI principles in critical business functions like human resources.
