# Walter Pitts

## Overview
Walter Pitts was a mathematician and logician who made seminal contributions to computational neuroscience and the nascent field of artificial intelligence.

## Key Contribution
In 1943, Pitts co-authored the highly influential paper “A Logical Calculus of Ideas Immanent in Nervous Activity” with neurophysiologist Warren McCulloch. This work presented the first formal model of an artificial neural network.

## The McCulloch-Pitts Neuron
The model they developed, now known as the McCulloch-Pitts neuron, was a simplified computational unit with binary inputs and a binary output. Their crucial insight was demonstrating that networks of these simple neurons could perform any logical proposition, establishing for the first time that brain-like structures could be modeled as computational devices.
