# Survey of Business Owners (SBOs)

## Overview

The Survey of Business Owners (SBOs) was a data collection initiative focused on the characteristics of U.S. businesses and their owners.

## Administration

The SBO was conducted by the U.S. Census Bureau.

## Consolidation

The SBO, along with the Annual Survey of Entrepreneurs and the Business R&D and Innovation Survey for Microbusinesses, was consolidated into the more comprehensive Annual Business Survey (ABS).
