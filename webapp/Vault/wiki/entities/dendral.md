# DENDRAL

## Overview
DENDRAL is cited as the first successful knowledge-intensive system in the history of AI. Its domain of expertise was spectrum prediction in chemistry.

## Knowledge-Intensive Approach
The significance of DENDRAL was that its expertise was derived from a large number of special-purpose rules. These rules effectively mapped general theoretical knowledge ("first principles") into efficient, special-purpose forms described as "cookbook recipes."

## Influence on AI
DENDRAL was influential in establishing the methodology of separating the knowledge base (in the form of rules) from the reasoning component. This approach, which aligned with McCarthy's Advice Taker concept, became a central theme in the development of subsequent expert systems.
