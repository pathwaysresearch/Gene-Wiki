# Marco Iansiti

## Overview
Marco Iansiti is the David Sarnoff Professor of Business Administration at Harvard Business School. At the school, he also holds leadership roles as the head of the Technology and Operations Management Unit and cochair of the Digital Initiative. Additionally, Iansiti serves as the faculty codirector of the Laboratory for Innovation Science at Harvard.

## Research Focus
His research is centered on the digital transformation of companies and industries. He gives special focus to several key areas, including digital ecosystems, the development of AI-centric operating models, and analyzing the impact that AI and network effects have on corporate strategy and business models.

## Key Contributions
Iansiti is known for his research on the management of innovation and business ecosystems. His work specifically on business ecosystems and their strategic impact has been widely recognized and quoted in the field.
