# Business R&D and Innovation Survey for Microbusinesses (BRDI-Ms)

## Overview

The Business R&D and Innovation Survey for Microbusinesses (BRDI-Ms) was a survey designed to gather data on innovation and research and development activities within very small U.S. firms.

## Administration

This survey was conducted by the U.S. Census Bureau under a partnership with NCSES.

## Consolidation

The BRDI-M was one of three data collections that were consolidated to form the current Annual Business Survey (ABS).
