# Sangeet Paul Choudary

## Overview
Sangeet Paul Choudary is an author featured on the Creative Intelligence podcast. His work focuses on the systemic impact of technology, particularly AI, on the future of work and business.

## Core Contribution: The System of Work
Choudary's key contribution discussed in the text is the concept that AI's impact must be analyzed at the level of the "system of work." He argues against the simplistic view that AI merely augments or displaces tasks. Instead, he posits that AI changes the competitive dynamics of an ecosystem, which forces organizations to adapt their priorities and structures, ultimately reshaping workflows and rendering entire job roles obsolete.

## Critique of Common AI Narratives
He is critical of the common saying, "AI won’t take your job, but someone using AI will." Choudary identifies this as a dangerous oversimplification because it ignores both direct job displacement by AI and, more importantly, the larger systemic changes that AI brings about, which is where the most significant transformation occurs.
