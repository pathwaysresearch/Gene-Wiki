# Cade Metz

## Overview
Cade Metz is a technology correspondent with The New York Times, where his reporting covers artificial intelligence, robotics, and related fields. He is featured as the main guest on the "Creative Intelligence" podcast to discuss the people who created modern AI.

## Author of "Genius Makers"
Metz is the author of the book "Genius Makers: The Mavericks Who Brought AI to Google, Facebook, and the World." The book tells the stories of the researchers who believed in AI when much of the world was skeptical, and it serves as the primary basis for the podcast discussion with host Kartik Hosanagar.

## Perspective on AI
In the discussion, Metz reflects on the timing of his book, joking that it was published "two years too early," before the explosion of generative AI with ChatGPT. He also shares his personal workflow, stating that while he uses tools like ChatGPT to understand them for his reporting, he does not use them to write or edit his own work.
