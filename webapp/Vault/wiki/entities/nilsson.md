# Nilsson

## Overview
Nilsson is an AI researcher whose vision for the future of artificial intelligence is centered on practical, real-world capabilities rather than game-playing prowess.

## Vision for Employable AI
Nilsson suggested that the goal of achieving human-level AI requires creating systems capable of performing a vast range of jobs. This perspective directly contrasts with the approach of developing AI to excel at specific, isolated games.

## Implications for the Future of Work
Nilsson's vision shifts the focus of AI evaluation from gaming metrics to practical applications within the workforce. It highlights the need for AI to be developed in a way that allows it to integrate into and enhance human jobs, emphasizing its utility in the broader economy.
