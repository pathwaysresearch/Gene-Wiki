# Competing in the Age of AI

## Overview
"Competing in the Age of AI: Strategy and Leadership When Algorithms and Networks Run the World" is a book authored by Harvard Business School professors Marco Iansiti and Karim R. Lakhani. The book aims to cut through the hype and anxiety surrounding artificial intelligence to explain how it is fundamentally changing business and the nature of companies.

## Core Thesis
The central argument of the book is that by reinventing a firm around data, analytics, and AI, businesses can overcome traditional operating constraints that have limited growth for centuries. These constraints relate to scale, scope, and learning. When these are removed, business strategy becomes a "whole new game," and the book sets out to define the new rules and likely outcomes of this new competitive landscape.
