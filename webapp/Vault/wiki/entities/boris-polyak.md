# Boris Polyak

## Overview

The provided text identifies Boris Polyak as the originator of the Momentum optimization technique, a significant contribution to the field of numerical optimization.

## Key Contribution

His primary contribution discussed in the text is the proposal of Momentum optimization in 1964. This algorithm introduced the concept of using a momentum term, which accumulates an exponentially decaying moving average of past gradients, to accelerate the convergence of the gradient descent method.

## Significance

The text establishes the importance of Polyak's work by positioning Momentum optimization as one of the most popular optimization methods used in training neural networks. It is presented as a foundational technique that improves upon standard Gradient Descent and is listed among other key optimizers like AdaGrad, RMSProp, and Adam.
