# Ray Solomonoff

## Overview
Ray Solomonoff was a pioneering American researcher in machine learning and a founder of algorithmic information theory.

## Contributions to Learning Theory
In 1964, Solomonoff independently developed the concept of Kolmogorov complexity (also known as algorithmic complexity). His work was aimed at creating a formal definition for the notion of simplicity, a key idea in Ockham's razor, thereby providing a foundational concept for computational learning theory.
