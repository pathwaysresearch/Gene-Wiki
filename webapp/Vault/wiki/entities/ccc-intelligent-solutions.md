# CCC Intelligent Solutions

## Overview
CCC Intelligent Solutions, founded in 1980 as Certified Collateral Corporation, is a company that leverages its extensive data assets and business ecosystem to serve automobile insurance companies. It originally provided car valuation (collateral) information to insurers.

## Transition to an AI-Focused Business
The company's journey illustrates a transition from being a data-oriented business to an AI-oriented one. This is best exemplified by its long-term, multi-year effort to develop automated collision repair estimates based on vehicle images, a process that required significant investment and technological foresight.

## Key AI Initiative: Image-Based Estimating
Over almost a decade, under the leadership of Githesh Ramamurthy, CCC developed an AI system for image-based estimating. This involved leveraging its vast repository of billions of vehicle images and applying deep learning neural networks and GPUs to analyze photos of damaged cars, often taken by the vehicle owners themselves on smartphones.
