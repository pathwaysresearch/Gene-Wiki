# Deep Belief Network

## Overview
A deep belief network is a specific kind of neural network. It gained prominence as the model used in a pivotal 2006 research paper that helped launch the modern deep learning era.

## Role in Deep Learning History
In 2006, Geoffrey Hinton demonstrated that a deep belief network could be trained efficiently. This was significant because, at the time, training deep networks was considered a major challenge.

## Training Method
The breakthrough in training deep belief networks was achieved using a strategy called greedy layer-wise pre-training. This success was quickly replicated and extended by other research groups to other types of deep networks.
