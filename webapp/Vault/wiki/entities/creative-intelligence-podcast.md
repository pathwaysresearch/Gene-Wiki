# Creative Intelligence (Podcast)

## Overview
"Creative Intelligence" is a podcast hosted by Kartik Hosanagar, a professor at the Wharton School. The podcast is associated with the Wharton Human-AI Research initiative and is available on platforms like Spotify and Apple Podcasts.

## Focus and Content
The podcast delves into thought-provoking topics, with the featured episode focusing on the question "Can AI be Conscious?". It aims to bridge different fields, bringing together neuroscience, philosophy, and cutting-edge AI research to explore complex questions about technology and humanity.

## Featured Episode
The substantive discussion analyzed in the text is from an episode of "Creative Intelligence." This episode features neuroscientist Anil Seth as a guest, who, along with host Kartik Hosanagar, unpacks the meaning of consciousness, the "hard problem," and the ethical dilemmas of potentially sentient machines.
