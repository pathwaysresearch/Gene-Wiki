# Annual Survey of Entrepreneurs (ASEs)

## Overview

The Annual Survey of Entrepreneurs (ASEs) was a data collection focused on providing information about U.S. entrepreneurs and their businesses.

## Partnerships

The ASE was conducted by the U.S. Census Bureau in partnership with the Ewing Marion Kauffman Foundation and the Minority Business Development Agency.

## Consolidation

The data collection efforts of the ASE have been consolidated into the Annual Business Survey (ABS), which provides a broader set of business statistics.
