# Skywise

## Overview and Objective
Skywise is an open data platform launched by Airbus in 2017 in collaboration with Palantir Technologies. Its primary objective is to become the standard platform for major aviation players, enabling them to use data to improve operational performance, achieve better business results, and advance their own digital transformations.

## Data and Scale
The platform is built to handle the vast amount of data generated by modern commercial aircraft, which the text notes can exceed thirty gigabytes per day across more than forty thousand operational parameters. By 2021, the Skywise platform had connected over 9,500 aircraft from more than 140 airlines.

## AI-Powered Applications
Airbus has leveraged the aggregated data on the Skywise platform to develop a suite of AI and analytics applications. These include Skywise Health Monitoring, which analyzes real-time aircraft data; Skywise Predictive Maintenance; and Skywise Reliability. The overarching goal of these applications is to enhance fleet performance and ultimately eliminate unscheduled maintenance.
