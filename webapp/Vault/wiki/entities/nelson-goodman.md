# Nelson Goodman

## Overview
Nelson Goodman was an American philosopher whose work is cited in the context of knowledge representation and mereology.

## Contributions to Mereology
Along with Henry S. Leonard, Goodman is credited with providing a more readable exposition of Leśniewski's ideas on mereology in a 1940 paper. This helped make the theory more accessible.

## Key Works
Goodman's 1977 book, *The Structure of Appearance*, is highlighted as a significant work that applies the ideas of mereology to various problems within the field of knowledge representation. This application demonstrated the practical relevance of the theory beyond pure logic.
