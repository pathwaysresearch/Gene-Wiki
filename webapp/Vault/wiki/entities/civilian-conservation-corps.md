# Civilian Conservation Corps

## Overview
The Civilian Conservation Corps, commonly abbreviated as C.C.C., was a government program in the United States. It was established as a work relief initiative during its operational years.

According to the text, the program was a voluntary government work relief program. Its primary function was to provide employment and relief to its participants through public works projects.
