# Rich Fairbank

## Overview
Rich Fairbank is the co-founder and long-time CEO of Capital One. He, along with Nigel Morris, established the company in 1994 with a vision centered on using data for strategic advantage in the financial services industry.

## Contributions
Fairbank was a primary architect of Capital One's "information-based strategy," the belief that data and analytics could revolutionize the credit card business by enabling more precise risk assessment and customer targeting. This foundational idea guided the company's early growth and success.

## Vision for AI
As CEO, Fairbank continues to drive the company's strategic direction. He is a vocal proponent of Capital One's transformation into a "real-time decisioning company," believing that the winners in the future economy will be tech companies with banking capabilities. He has championed the company's broad emphasis on AI as a core component of this transformation.
