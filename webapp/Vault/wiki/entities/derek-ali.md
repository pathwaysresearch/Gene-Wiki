# Derek Ali

## Overview
Derek Ali is a highly respected, Grammy Award-winning sound engineer, introduced by Karim Lakhani as the recording engineer for Kendrick Lamar's acclaimed album "good kid, m.A.A.d city." He is also the founder and CEO of EngineEars, a company that provides business solutions for audio professionals.

## Role in AI Demonstration
In the "Creativity in AI with Music" session, Ali conducted a live demonstration of AI's capabilities in music production. He used text prompts to generate complete songs in various genres, including a classical piece about HBS Dean Srikant Datar and a jazz hip-hop song about Karim Lakhani's book "Competing in the Age of AI." This demonstration served to illustrate how quickly and effectively AI can create music from simple instructions, while also noting that the technology is still in its "early days" regarding mix quality.

## Views on AI's Impact
Ali views AI not as a replacement but as a powerful "co-pilot" for creators. He argues that AI can eliminate the "busy work" that consumes engineers' time, saving them money and allowing for greater efficiency. He also highlights specific benefits for different roles: for producers, AI can generate sample-able material without the legal risk of sound clearance issues; for artists, it allows for vocal cloning to test ideas; and for engineers, it provides assistive utilities through platforms like his own, EngineEars.
