# Annual Business Survey (ABS)

## Overview
The Annual Business Survey (ABS) is a key data source used in the study to measure technology use among US firms. The research specifically leverages the 2018 edition of the survey, which provided novel, detailed insights into the adoption of modern digital technologies.

## Technology Module
The 2018 ABS was distinguished by a new technology module containing three connected questions on digitization and technology use. This module specifically queried firms on their reliance on data (digitization), their use of cloud computing for IT functions, and their adoption of five specific AI-related technologies: machine learning, machine vision, natural language processing, voice recognition, and automated-guided vehicles.

## Data Linkage and Sample
For the purposes of the research, data from the ABS was linked with the Longitudinal Business Database (LBD) to create the "ABS–LBD linked sample." This linkage allowed the researchers to combine the detailed technology use data from the ABS with rich administrative data on firm characteristics such as industry, size, age, and location, enabling a comprehensive analysis of the factors associated with AI adoption.
