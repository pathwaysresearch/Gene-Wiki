# Shakey the Robot

## Overview
Shakey was a robot developed between 1966 and 1972 that is considered a major milestone in artificial intelligence and robotics. It is distinguished as the first robot to successfully combine several critical AI functions into a single, integrated system.

## Integrated Capabilities
Shakey's primary innovation was its ability to integrate perception, world-modeling, planning, and learning. This allowed it to perceive its surroundings, maintain an internal model of its world, formulate and execute plans to achieve goals, and learn from its experience. This combination of abilities enabled it to perform complex tasks that required reasoning about its actions and environment.

## Project Leadership and Significance
The Shakey project was led by Charles Rosen (1917-2002). The robot's development represented a landmark achievement, demonstrating for the first time how logical reasoning and planning could be coupled with physical action, perception, and learning in a real-world agent.
