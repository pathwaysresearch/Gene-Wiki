# Garry Kasparov

## Overview
Garry Kasparov is a world chess champion whose match against the AI computer Deep Blue is a significant event in the history of artificial intelligence.

## The Match Against Deep Blue
Kasparov was defeated by Deep Blue in a highly publicized match. This event ignited widespread discussion about AI's capabilities and its potential to match or exceed human intellect in specific domains.

## Significance of the Defeat
The defeat of a human champion of Kasparov's caliber by a machine marked a turning point in the public's understanding of AI. It prompted both initial enthusiasm for AI's potential and, later, a more critical reflection on whether game-playing ability constitutes true intelligence.
