# Vladimir Vapnik

## Overview
Vladimir Vapnik is a key figure in the development of modern machine learning theory and a primary developer of Support Vector Machines (SVMs).

## Contributions
The full development of the theory behind kernel machines, which are the basis for SVMs, is attributed to Vapnik and his colleagues. He co-authored a highly influential 1995 paper with Corinna Cortes that introduced the soft-margin classifier, a crucial innovation that made SVMs practical for handling noisy data. This paper was later recognized with the 2008 ACM Theory and Practice Award.
