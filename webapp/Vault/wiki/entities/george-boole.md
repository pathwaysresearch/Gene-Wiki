# George Boole

## Overview
George Boole was a mathematician who lived from 1815 to 1864. His work was central to the mathematical development of formal logic, transforming it into a formal science.

## Key Contribution
In his 1847 work, Boole detailed the principles of propositional logic, which has since become known as Boolean logic. This provided a formal system for reasoning with propositions that could be either true or false.

## Legacy in AI
Boole's formalization of logic was one of the three key mathematical areas, alongside computation and probability, that enabled the creation of AI as a scientific discipline. His work was later extended by Gottlob Frege to create the first-order logic used today.
