# Deep Blue

## Overview
Deep Blue was a chess-playing computer whose significance in AI research is compared to the role of fruit flies in genetics—a test bed for major ideas. It gained global fame for its match against world chess champion Garry Kasparov.

## Initial Impact of Victory
Deep Blue's victory over Kasparov initially ignited tremendous enthusiasm and speculation about the potential for AI to emulate human intellect. It was widely seen as a landmark achievement in the field.

## Evolving Critique
Over time, the perception of Deep Blue's achievement shifted. Critics began to argue that mastery of a symbolic game like chess did not encapsulate true, general intelligence. This critique suggested that such AI advancements represented a narrow focus that lacked practical applicability in complex, real-world scenarios.
