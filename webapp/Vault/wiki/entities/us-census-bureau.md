# U.S. Census Bureau

## Overview

The U.S. Census Bureau is a federal agency responsible for a wide range of statistical data collection. Its work provides foundational information about the nation's population and economy.

## Role in Business Surveys

The Census Bureau is the primary conductor of the Annual Business Survey (ABS), a key source of data on U.S. firms. It also conducted the Survey of Business Owners (SBO), one of the predecessors to the ABS.

## Partnerships

In its data collection efforts, the Census Bureau has partnered with other organizations. For the Annual Survey of Entrepreneurs (ASE), it worked with the Ewing Marion Kauffman Foundation and the Minority Business Development Agency. For the Business R&D and Innovation Survey for Microbusinesses (BRDI-M), it partnered with NCSES.
