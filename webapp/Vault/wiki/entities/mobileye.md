# Mobileye

## Overview
Mobileye is an Israeli technology company specializing in data-collection technology for autonomous vehicles. It was acquired by Intel in 2017 for over $15 billion, a transaction that highlights the immense value of its technology.

## Role as a Complement to Prediction
The book uses the Mobileye acquisition to illustrate the economic concept of complements. As AI makes the prediction component of autonomous driving cheaper, the value of its complements—like data collection—increases dramatically. Mobileye's technology enables vehicles to "effectively see objects (stop signs, people, etc.) and markings (lanes, roads)," providing the critical data needed for prediction algorithms to function.

## Economic Significance
The high price Intel paid for Mobileye serves as powerful evidence for the economic principle that cheap prediction drives up the value of its complements. The acquisition demonstrates that the market places a high premium on the data and data-collection capabilities that are essential inputs for modern AI systems, particularly in the automotive industry.
