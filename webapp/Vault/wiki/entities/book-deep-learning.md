# Deep Learning (book)

## Overview
The book "Deep Learning" (2016) by Goodfellow, Bengio, and Courville is positioned as the "technical bible for neural networks." It received a strong endorsement from Elon Musk, who called it 'the only comprehensive book' on the subject.

## Content and Audience
This book is intended for practitioners and data engineers who require a deep, technical understanding of what is "under the hood" of neural networks. Its focus is on the foundational principles and mechanics of deep learning.

## Accessibility
A key feature highlighted in the text is its accessibility. The book is available to read for free online at deeplearningbook.org, making it a widely available resource for anyone looking to master the technical details of deep learning.
