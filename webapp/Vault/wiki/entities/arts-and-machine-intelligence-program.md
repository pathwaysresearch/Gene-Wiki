# Arts and Machine Intelligence (AMI) program

## Overview
The Arts and Machine Intelligence (AMI) program is an initiative by Google designed to explore the intersection of art and AI. Its primary goal is to organize a community of artists and engineers to investigate how creative practices are being transformed by new technologies.

## Activities and Methods
The AMI community applies AI techniques, such as the style-transfer methods used in "The Next Rembrandt," across a wide variety of subjects and media. This exploration is not limited to visual art but also extends to other creative fields like film and music. The program serves as a hub for experimentation with AI in the arts.

## Creative Goals
Beyond simply replicating existing styles, the AMI program and similar initiatives are pushing AI further into the realm of creation. The endeavor aims to use AI to generate completely new works of art. This transforms not only the method for crafting art but also the fundamental organization of creative work.
