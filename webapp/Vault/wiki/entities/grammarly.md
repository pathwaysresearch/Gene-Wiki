# Grammarly

## Overview
Grammarly is a technology company, led by CEO Shishir Mehrotra, that is undergoing a significant strategic reinvention. While traditionally known as a tool for correcting grammar and spelling, the company is now positioning itself as a comprehensive AI productivity platform.

## The "AI Superhighway" Strategy
The company's new vision is to build an "AI Superhighway," a platform designed to bring various specialized AI agents directly into the applications where people work. The original grammar functionality is viewed as just the first "car" on this highway, with many more to come.

## Strategic Acquisitions
To accelerate its vision, Grammarly has made key acquisitions. It acquired Coda, a productivity platform for documents and spreadsheets, to serve as a core "surface" for its AI agents. It also acquired Superhuman to build out its presence on the email "surface," expanding the reach of its AI-powered ecosystem.
