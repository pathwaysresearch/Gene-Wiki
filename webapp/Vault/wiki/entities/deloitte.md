# Deloitte

## AI Transformation Strategy
Deloitte is presented as a case study of a company transforming itself from an exclusively people-oriented professional services firm to one where smart humans work alongside smart machines. This transformation involves significant collaboration across its business areas, such as the audit and tax practices working together on common tools for client data analysis. (Chunk 54, 55)

## AI Talent and Services
To build internal capabilities and serve clients, Deloitte has established several AI-focused initiatives. Its consulting practice created the AI Foundry, a dedicated group of AI services and professionals. To upskill its broader workforce, Deloitte launched the AI Academy in 2021, which teaches employees about AI in the context of client business processes and strategies. (Chunk 55)

## AI in Audit and Assurance
Deloitte's audit and assurance practice has been a pioneer in AI adoption within the firm, beginning development work in 2014. This effort led to the creation of Omnia, a global AI platform used to support the audit business. Omnia automates audit transactions, prioritizes reviews for human auditors, and generates business insights for clients. (Chunk 55)
