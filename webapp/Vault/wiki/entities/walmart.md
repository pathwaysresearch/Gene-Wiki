# Walmart

## Background and Traditional Strengths
Founded by Sam Walton in 1962, Walmart has historically been a leader in leveraging technology within a traditional retail model. For decades, it set the standard in supply chain technology with its Retail Link system and early adoption of EDI and RFID technologies. A data-rich supply chain has always been a key part of its operating model and scale.

## The Challenge from Digital Models
Despite its historical success, Walmart's traditional operating model is not strong enough to confront the onslaught from digitally native companies like Amazon. The competitive pressure from Amazon's digital capabilities has forced Walmart to undertake a substantial transformation to avoid the fate of other bankrupt retailers like Sears.

## Digital Transformation Strategy
To compete effectively, Walmart is rearchitecting its operating model on a digital and AI-enabled foundation. This involves replacing traditional siloed enterprise software with an integrated, cloud-based architecture. The goal is to expose Walmart's unique data assets to powerful new applications, enabling a growing number of operating tasks to be enhanced or automated by AI.
