# ChatGPT

## Demonstrated Capabilities
The text provides several examples of ChatGPT's advanced capabilities in a business education context. It shows the model can understand the implicit rules of the MBA case method, generate insightful comments for a student who has not read the case, create 2x2 analytical frameworks for a specific business decision, and explain the axes of these frameworks.

## Impact on Education
The speaker characterizes the emergence of tools like ChatGPT as a "covet level crisis" for education. It fundamentally changes the nature of assignments like case preparation, as students are already using it for homework. The text demonstrates its meta-awareness by having it generate a pros-and-cons list for its own use by MBA students.
