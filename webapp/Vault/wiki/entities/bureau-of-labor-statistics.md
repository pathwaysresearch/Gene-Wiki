# Bureau of Labor Statistics

## Overview
The Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) is a U.S. government agency responsible for collecting and disseminating a wide range of data on the labor market, economy, and prices.

## Data Provided for the Study
For the analysis of LLM labor market impact, researchers obtained crucial employment and wage data from the BLS. Specifically, they used the 2020 and 2021 Occupational Employment series. This dataset provided information on occupational titles, the number of workers in each occupation, employment projections, and the typical education and on-the-job training required for various jobs.

## Data Integration
The BLS data was essential for contextualizing the task-level exposure findings. To link the wage and employment statistics to the task data, the study used a crosswalk recommended by the BLS to connect its occupational data with the O*NET database. The study also used the BLS Labor Force Demographics, derived from the Current Population Survey (CPS), for demographic information.
