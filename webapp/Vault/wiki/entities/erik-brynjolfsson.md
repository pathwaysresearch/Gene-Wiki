# Erik Brynjolfsson

## Overview
Erik Brynjolfsson is an economist affiliated with Stanford University and the National Bureau of Economic Research (NBER). He is the lead author of the 2025 paper "Canaries in the Coal Mine? Six Facts about the Recent Employment Effects of Artificial Intelligence."

## Research Contributions
Based on his cited works, Brynjolfsson's research focuses on the intersection of technology, particularly AI and machine learning, and its implications for the workforce, productivity, and the economy. His work includes establishing methodologies for estimating which occupations are susceptible to automation and studying the effects of generative AI in specific firm settings.

## Key Works Cited
The text cites several of his influential papers that form part of the foundational literature for measuring the employment effects of AI. These include "What Can Machine Learning Do? Workforce Implications" (2017), "What Can Machines Learn, and What Does It Mean for Occupations and the Economy?" (2018), and "Generative AI at Work" (2025).
