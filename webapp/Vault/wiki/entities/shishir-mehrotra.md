# Shishir Mehrotra

## Overview
Shishir Mehrotra is the CEO of Grammarly. His professional background includes founding and leading the business productivity platform Coda, which was later acquired by Grammarly. He was also formerly the head of product and engineering at YouTube and began his career as a developer on the Microsoft Outlook team.

## Vision for AI at Work
Mehrotra is the chief architect of Grammarly's new strategic vision, which he calls the "AI Superhighway." This vision aims to transform Grammarly from a single-purpose grammar tool into a ubiquitous platform that brings an "army of agents" to assist users across all their digital workspaces. He also frames the strategic challenge for companies and individuals as learning how to "play offense and defense with AI."

## Perspective on Human-AI Collaboration
Mehrotra expresses a very positive outlook on the future role of humans in the workplace. He uses the analogy that employees will become "managers of agents," where their primary role will be to direct and orchestrate AI tools to achieve goals, rather than being replaced by them. This view emphasizes the continued importance of human judgment and strategic thinking.
