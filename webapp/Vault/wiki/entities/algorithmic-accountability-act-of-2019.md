# Algorithmic Accountability Act of 2019

## Overview
The Algorithmic Accountability Act of 2019 is a proposed piece of legislation in the United States designed to regulate the use of artificial intelligence and automated systems by companies. It is presented in the text as a formal, regulatory approach to mitigating the negative consequences of AI, particularly those related to algorithmic bias.

## Key Provisions
As described in the excerpt, the primary requirement of the act would be for companies to conduct assessments of their AI systems. The goal of these assessments is to proactively identify potential risks. Specifically, companies would have to evaluate their systems for the possibility of producing "'inaccurate, unfair, biased, or discriminatory decisions'" and then take reasonable steps to address the findings of their assessments.

## Significance in Consumer Context
The act is cited as a direct response to the problems inherent in the AI Classification Experience, where algorithmic biases can lead to discriminatory outcomes for consumers. The authors suggest that firms should not wait for such regulations to be enacted but should instead proactively collaborate to learn about and root out the specific biases present in their own algorithms and processes, thereby taking responsibility for the societal impact of their technology.
