# Ant Group

## Overview
Ant Group is presented as a prime example of a company that has reimagined business operations at scale, achieving exponential growth to serve 1.2 billion users. It is highlighted as a leader in applying AI to its core business in a systematic way.

## Role of the AI Factory
The core of Ant Group's success is attributed to its implementation of what is called an "AI Factory." This system industrializes the entire data and prediction process, allowing the company to operate effectively at an immense scale that would be impossible with traditional, craft-based approaches to analytics.

## Organizational Focus
Ant Group's organizational structure reflects its AI Factory model. The company employs only 500 AI engineers, with the majority of its technical staff focused on data engineering. This emphasis on industrializing the data process, rather than just focusing on algorithms, is identified as a key differentiator and a critical lesson for other companies.
