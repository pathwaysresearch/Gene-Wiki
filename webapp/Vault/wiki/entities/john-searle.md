# John Searle

## Overview
John Searle is a philosopher who has mounted a strong challenge to the theory of functionalism in the philosophy of mind. He is also noted as being associated with the brain replacement thought experiment.

## Biological Naturalism
Searle proposed the theory of biological naturalism, which posits that mental states are high-level emergent features caused by specific low-level physical processes in neurons. This view holds that the particular, unspecified properties of biological neurons are essential for consciousness, and thus mental states cannot be duplicated simply by replicating a system's functional organization in a different medium, like a computer program.

## The Chinese Room Argument
To support his position against functionalism, Searle developed the Chinese Room argument. The purpose of this argument is to provide intuition for his axiom that 'programs are not sufficient for minds.' The argument has been highly influential, acting as what Daniel Dennett calls an 'intuition pump' that tends to reinforce the prior beliefs of both functionalists and their opponents, stirring up combatants but changing few opinions.
