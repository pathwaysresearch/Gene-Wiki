# Boston Consulting Group (BCG)

## Overview
The Boston Consulting Group (BCG) is a global management consulting firm. The text highlights it as a workplace where many business school graduates perform tasks that are both creative and analytical, making it a suitable environment for studying the impact of AI on high-value knowledge work.

## Role in AI Research
BCG collaborated with researchers from Harvard, led by Karim Lakhani, on a significant study of AI's impact in the workplace. This study was structured as a "clinical trial" involving 758 of its employees, who were divided into groups with and without access to AI.

## Significance of the Study
The research conducted at BCG provided key evidence for the concept of AI's "jagged technological frontier." The trial demonstrated that AI significantly boosted performance on certain tasks but was detrimental on others, highlighting the need for careful, task-specific AI implementation.
