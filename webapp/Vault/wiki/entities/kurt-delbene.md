# Kurt DelBene

## Background and Appointment
Kurt DelBene, an executive with extensive product experience, was convinced by Satya Nadella to return to Microsoft in 2015. He was appointed to run corporate strategy and also took over the IT and operations organizations, which were grouped into a new unit called Core Services Engineering and Operations, making him the company's Chief Digital Officer.

## Mission: Transforming Internal IT
DelBene's primary task was to build Microsoft's own AI Factory and establish a new data- and software-centric operating model. This required a fundamental transformation of Microsoft's internal IT department, which had traditionally operated in a reactive mode, focusing on deploying and maintaining systems like CRM, working the help desk, and keeping the network secure.

## Shift to a Proactive Model
Under DelBene's leadership, Microsoft IT shifted from a reactive to a proactive mode, led by a clear vision. The integration of IT with operations and strategy emphasized its new, fundamental role in how the company operates. The goal was for IT to build and deploy the software foundations of a new operating model, with DelBene stating, 'Our product is the process.'
