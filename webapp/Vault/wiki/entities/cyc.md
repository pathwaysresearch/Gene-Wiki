# Cyc

## Overview
Cyc is one of the most famous projects based on the knowledge base approach to artificial intelligence. The project includes an inference engine and a database of statements intended to capture common-sense knowledge.

## Implementation
The knowledge in Cyc's database is encoded in a formal language called CycL. This knowledge is entered into the system by a staff of human supervisors, a process described as unwieldy.

## Limitations and Failures
The text uses Cyc as an example of the limitations of the knowledge base approach. It notes that the project struggled to devise formal rules with enough complexity to accurately describe the world, citing a specific failure where Cyc could not understand a story about a person named Fred shaving in the morning.
