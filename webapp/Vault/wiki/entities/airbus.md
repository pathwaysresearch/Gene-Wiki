# Airbus

## Overview

Airbus is a European aviation giant that embarked on a broad digital transformation in the mid-2010s. The company recognized the need to adapt and improve operational efficiency in an aviation industry that was being heavily disrupted by digitization.

## Strategic Approach to AI

AI and data are front and center in Airbus's transformation strategy. The company's website states its belief that AI is a key competitive advantage that enables it to capitalize on the value of its data. As part of its program, Airbus has invested in new technology and has begun to retrain employees to use AI.

## AI Applications

Airbus applies its AI capabilities across the entire breadth of its global organization, including its commercial airplane business as well as its Helicopters, Defense, and Space divisions. AI technology has been foundational for many of its offerings, including the OneAtlas imagery service and the ATTOL (vision-based navigation for autonomous taxi, takeoff, and landing) demonstrator.
