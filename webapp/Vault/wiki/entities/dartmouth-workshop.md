# Dartmouth Workshop

## Overview
The Dartmouth workshop, based on a 1955 proposal by McCarthy and others, was a pivotal event in the history of AI. While it did not lead to any immediate new breakthroughs, its primary significance was in introducing all the major figures of the nascent field to each other.

## Establishing AI as a Field
The workshop was instrumental in establishing AI as a separate field of study. The proposal argued for this separation by highlighting AI's unique goal of duplicating human faculties such as creativity, self-improvement, and language use, which distinguished it from existing fields like control theory, operations research, or mathematics.

## Lasting Impact
For the two decades following the workshop, the field of AI was dominated by the event's attendees and their students and colleagues at key institutions. These included MIT, Carnegie Mellon University (CMU), Stanford, and IBM, which became the epicenters of AI research.
