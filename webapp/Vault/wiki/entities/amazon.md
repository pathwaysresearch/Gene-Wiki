# Amazon

## Role in Digital Competition
Amazon poses a formidable challenge to traditional firms, especially in the retail industry, due to its convenience, low prices, and software-enabled logistics. Its success is a key driver for the transformation of competitors like Walmart and has contributed to the bankruptcy of numerous long-standing retailers. Amazon's digital operating model allows it to overwhelm traditional competitors.

## Use of Collaborative Filtering
Amazon's website uses a powerful recommendation engine based on collaborative filtering algorithms. This system ingests vast amounts of data on customer purchases and behavior to suggest new products. The engine continuously learns and improves, becoming more effective with more data, scale, and product diversity. This AI-driven personalization is a core part of its value proposition.

## Scalability and Scope
The AI engines used by Amazon are highly scalable and do not suffer from the human complexity costs that limit traditional systems. The learning is also transferable across different product categories; for example, insights from book purchases can inform video or clothing recommendations. This demonstrates the immense scalability and scope amplification characteristic of a digital operating model.
