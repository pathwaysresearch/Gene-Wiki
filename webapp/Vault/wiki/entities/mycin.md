# MYCIN

## Overview
Developed by Feigenbaum, Buchanan, and Dr. Edward Shortliffe as part of the Heuristic Programming Project, MYCIN was a major expert system designed for the medical diagnosis of blood infections.

## Performance and Structure
With approximately 450 rules, MYCIN was able to perform as well as some human experts and considerably better than junior doctors in its specific domain.

## Key Innovations
MYCIN differed from its predecessor DENDRAL in two significant ways. First, its rules were not deduced from a general theoretical model but had to be acquired through extensive interviewing of experts. Second, the rules were designed to reflect the uncertainty inherent in medical diagnosis, a crucial feature for real-world application.
