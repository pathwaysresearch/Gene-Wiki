# Omnia

## Overview
Omnia is the global AI platform developed and used by Deloitte to support its audit business in member firms around the world. The platform is the result of AI development work that began within the firm's audit and assurance practice in 2014. (Chunk 55)

## Core Functions
Omnia is a set of tools and methods designed to enhance the external audit process. Its key functions include the automation of certain audit transactions, the prioritization of items for review by human auditors, and the generation of valuable insights for clients about their businesses and risks. (Chunk 55)

## Strategic Importance
The platform represents a significant step in Deloitte's strategy to have AI perform key tasks in external audits. Omnia is designed to be an evolving system, built with a best-of-breed technology approach, and allows for local customization by member firms to meet specific needs. (Chunk 55)
