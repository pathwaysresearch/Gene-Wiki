# Atomwise

## Overview
Atomwise is a company that builds AI tools for enterprise customers, specifically focusing on a prediction task within the drug discovery workflow. Their business is predicated on providing a tool to deliver a specific, high-value prediction.

## Role in the AI Canvas Framework
The text uses Atomwise as the primary case study to demonstrate how to use the AI Canvas to deconstruct a task. The authors fill in each of the seven elements of the canvas to analyze Atomwise's molecule selection process, showing how the framework can be applied in a business context.

## Deconstruction of the Task
According to the text's analysis, Atomwise's task involves the **Action** of testing molecules to cure disease. The core **Prediction** is the binding affinity of potential molecules and proteins. **Judgment** is exercised by Atomwise and its customers in setting criteria for targeting a disease versus the costs of side effects. The **Input** data is the characteristics of disease proteins, while **Training** data is known binding affinities. The **Outcome** is the test result, and this **Feedback** is used to improve future predictions.
