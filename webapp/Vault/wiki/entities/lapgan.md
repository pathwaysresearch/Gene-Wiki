# LAPGAN

## Overview
The LAPGAN model (Denton et al., 2015) is a generative model for images that is capable of producing high-quality results. Its name is derived from its use of a Laplacian pyramid structure in the generation process.

## Architecture and Method
LAPGAN is built upon a series of conditional Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs). The generation process is incremental. First, a GAN generates a very low-resolution version of an image. Then, a sequence of conditional GANs is used to progressively add higher-frequency details to the image, with each network conditioned on the output of the previous one.

## Performance
The images generated by LAPGAN generators have proven to be highly realistic. The model is able to fool not only discriminator networks but also human observers. In experiments, human subjects identified up to 40% of the images generated by the network as being real data.
