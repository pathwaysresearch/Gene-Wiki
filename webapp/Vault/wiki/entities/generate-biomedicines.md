# Generate Biomedicines

## Overview
Generate Biomedicines is a company that operates at the intersection of artificial intelligence and biology. It is mentioned by Professor Karim Lakhani, who advises the company, as a prime example of AI's transformative potential in the R&D process.

## Core Mission and Technology
The company's stated view is that it is creating a platform capable of generating any protein based on desired characteristics. This includes proteins that have not previously existed in the natural world. This work is based on generative AI technologies, such as diffusion models, which are applied to the field of protein engineering.

## Significance
Generate Biomedicines serves as a concrete illustration of how AI is changing the fundamental R&D process in the life sciences. It represents a shift from a paradigm of discovering existing biological molecules to one of generating new ones with specific functions, fundamentally altering how new therapeutics and other biological products can be developed.
