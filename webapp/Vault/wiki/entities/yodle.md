# Yodle

## Overview
Yodle was a marketing platform designed to help small, hyperlocal businesses advertise on Google and other digital platforms. [Chunk 2] The company was co-founded by Kartik Hosanagar, who developed the bidding algorithms, while his co-founders handled sales and operations. [Chunk 2]

## The Scaling Challenge
In its early days, Yodle struggled to secure venture capital funding because investors viewed the business as a "good lifestyle business" that was not scalable. [Chunk 2] The prevailing belief among the first couple dozen VCs was that the model, which was working in Philadelphia, was not repeatable or replicable across the United States. [Chunk 2]

## Growth and Outcome
After eventually receiving an investment from Bessemer, Yodle proved its scalability. At the time of investment, the company had a $1 million run rate and six employees. [Chunk 2] Roughly ten years later, the company was sold, at which point it had grown to about 1200 employees and had a run rate of a little over $200 million. This trajectory serves as a key example of a company successfully scaling its operations. [Chunk 2]
