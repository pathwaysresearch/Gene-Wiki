# Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach

## Overview
"Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach" is a comprehensive textbook on the subject of AI. The provided excerpts are from the 3rd edition, published in 2010 by Prentice Hall.

## Authors
The book is co-authored by Stuart Russell and Peter Norvig, both prominent computer scientists in the field of artificial intelligence.

## Content from Excerpts
Chapter 18, titled "Learning from Examples," delves into core machine learning concepts. It covers the theoretical foundations of learning, including computational learning theory, generalization loss, and empirical loss. The chapter also provides algorithmic details for methods like cross-validation, decision list learning, and multivariate linear regression using gradient descent.
