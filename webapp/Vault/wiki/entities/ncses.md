# NCSES

## Overview

NCSES is an organization involved in the collection and analysis of data related to science and engineering.

## Partnership on BRDI-M

NCSES partnered with the U.S. Census Bureau to conduct the Business R&D and Innovation Survey for Microbusinesses (BRDI-M). This survey was specifically designed to capture innovation activities in very small firms.

## Survey Evolution

The BRDI-M survey, developed through this partnership, has since been integrated into the larger Annual Business Survey (ABS).
