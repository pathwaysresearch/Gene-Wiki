# OpenCyc

## Overview

The OPENCYC project, developed by researchers including Lenat and Guha, represents a significant effort in the creation of large-scale ontologies for artificial intelligence. It is part of a growing interest in developing comprehensive knowledge bases.

## The Knowledge Base

The project has publicly released a substantial ontology containing approximately 150,000 concepts. This makes a vast amount of structured, common-sense and domain-specific knowledge available for AI applications.

## Content and Structure

The OPENCYC ontology includes a detailed upper ontology, similar in principle to the general-purpose hierarchies used in AI. It also contains a multitude of specific concepts, such as "OLED Display" and "iPhone," with the latter being classified as a type of "cellular phone."
