# Microsoft

## The Need for Transformation
Before the transformation led by CEO Satya Nadella, Microsoft was considered a 'tired company' whose growth had stalled. Its Windows developer ecosystem had been in a steady decline since the 1990s, while the most innovative companies were being built on open source foundations.

## Strategic Shifts
Under Nadella, Microsoft adopted a new mission and a strategy to become the 'productivity platform for the age of AI.' This involved a fundamental shift to a cloud-based architecture, a services-based 'consumption' business model, and a significant embrace of open source technology like Linux to regain relevance with developers.

## Organizational and Technical Rearchitecting
A key part of the transformation was changing the company's core. The Azure cloud platform was moved from the fringe to the center of the company and redesigned for compatibility and ease of use. Internally, the traditional, reactive IT department was transformed into a proactive 'Core Services Engineering and Operations' group, led by a Chief Digital Officer, tasked with building the company's own AI Factory and data-centric operating model.
