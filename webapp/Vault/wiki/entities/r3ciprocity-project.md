# R3ciprocity Project

## Overview
The R3ciprocity Project is a software platform developed by Dr. David Maslach. It was born from the idea that there was a 'better way for us to actually do science' by fostering direct, mutual support among researchers.

## Operating Model
The platform is designed to function as a 'sharing economy peer-to-peer kind of model.' The core principle is reciprocity, where scientists and graduate students can help each other out, with an initial focus on the process of writing and reviewing academic papers. The goal is to create a collaborative ecosystem that operates outside of traditional institutional structures.

## Founder's Insights
David Maslach's experience building the project revealed that the dynamics of participation were different from his initial expectations. He was surprised to find that users were less altruistic than he had anticipated. This led him to analyze the project's exchange model in comparison to other large-scale collaborations like Wikipedia, where a small number of active contributors support a vast number of users.
