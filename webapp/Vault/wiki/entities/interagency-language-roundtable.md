# Interagency Language Roundtable (ILR)

## Overview
The Interagency Language Roundtable (ILR) is an organization composed of various U.S. federal government agencies. Its purpose is to coordinate and share information regarding foreign language activities and proficiency standards.

## Functionally Native Proficiency Standard
The ILR scale for language proficiency is a widely recognized standard. The study specifically references the highest level on this scale, 'Functionally Native Proficiency.' This level describes an individual who can communicate with complete fluency, precision, and cultural understanding equivalent to a well-educated native speaker, including the ability to counsel, persuade, and negotiate effectively.

## Application in the Study
The research adapted the ILR's 'functionally native' standard to create criteria for assessing the written English fluency of contact center agents. A large language model, Gemini Pro, was then used to score agent text based on these adapted criteria, providing a quantitative measure of language comprehensibility and fluency.
