# Novartis

## Overview
Novartis is a pharmaceutical company that is notably public about its various AI initiatives. A key component of its strategy is the AI Innovation Lab, which it operates in partnership with Microsoft to address complex challenges in medicine. (Chunk 51)

## AI Innovation Lab Initiatives
The AI Innovation Lab is focused on several advanced use cases. These include the intelligent design of more effective and efficient molecules, personalizing viruses to turn the body's own T-cells into cancer-fighting agents, and developing precision dosing for treatments like those for age-related macular degeneration. (Chunk 51)

## Data-Driven Research Strategy
Beyond specific lab projects, Novartis is using AI to extract findings from real-world data. The insights generated from this analysis are then used to identify and suggest new opportunities for the company's research and development pipeline, making its R&D process more data-driven. (Chunk 51)
