# Dutch East India Company

## Overview

Founded in 1602, the Dutch East India Company is presented as possibly the first modern corporation. It was formed from the consolidation of seven rival trading companies to achieve economies of scale and manage the significant risks of individual voyages.

## Operating Architecture

To manage its extensive global operations, the company developed a multi-unit structure. It subdivided the organization into a number of specialized, geographically separate, and largely autonomous units. This "siloed operating architecture" and flexible management approach allowed it to handle multinational operations without being overwhelmed by communication delays and complexity.

## Scale and Success

This organizational structure enabled the company to grow into an economic powerhouse, dominating global trade in spices, silk, cotton, and textiles. By 1670, the text suggests it was possibly the richest company the world had ever seen, employing over fifty thousand people and deploying a large fleet of ships.
