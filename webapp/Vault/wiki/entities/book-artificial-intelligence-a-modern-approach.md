# Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach

## Overview
"Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach" by Russell & Norvig (4th ed., 2020) is described as the definitive university AI textbook. It is noted for being used in virtually every top-tier AI course around the world.

## Content and Scope
The book provides comprehensive coverage of key AI subjects. The text specifies that its topics include problem-solving, knowledge representation, machine learning (ML), natural language processing (NLP), and robotics.

## Target Audience
While the book is technical in nature, it is also described as being accessible to analytically minded business professionals. This suggests it serves as both a foundational academic text for students and a detailed reference for professionals seeking a deep understanding of AI principles.
