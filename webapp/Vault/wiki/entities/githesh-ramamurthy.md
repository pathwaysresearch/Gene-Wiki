# Githesh Ramamurthy

## Overview
Githesh Ramamurthy is the leader of CCC Intelligent Solutions and the key visionary behind the company's transition into an AI-focused business in the auto insurance ecosystem.

## Vision for Image-Based Estimating
Almost a decade ago, Ramamurthy foresaw a future where vehicle owners could take photos of their damaged cars with smartphones to start the claims process. He noticed the rapid improvement in amateur cameras and initiated research into the feasibility of using these images for collision damage assessment, engaging with university professors to explore the issue.

## Role in AI Adoption
Ramamurthy guided CCC's long-term technology bet on automated image recognition. When he began to read about deep learning neural networks, he recognized their potential for image analysis. He led the company to invest in the necessary technology, such as GPUs from Nvidia, and to leverage its massive image database to train the AI systems, ultimately realizing his vision.
