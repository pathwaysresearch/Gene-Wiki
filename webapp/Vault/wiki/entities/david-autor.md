# David Autor

## Overview
David Autor is the author of the NBER Working Paper 32140, "Applying AI to Rebuild Middle Class Jobs." In this work, he analyzes the transformative potential of Artificial Intelligence on the labor market, focusing specifically on the nature of expertise and the structure of jobs. The views expressed in the paper are his own, though he acknowledges influence from and collaboration with numerous colleagues and support from several foundations.

## Core Argument on AI and Expertise
Autor argues that AI represents a new technological epoch that will reshape which forms of human expertise are valuable. Unlike previous technologies that automated routine, rule-based tasks, AI is beginning to master "expert judgment"—the ability to improvise and make decisions based on experience. He posits that AI's primary role will be to augment human experts, making their judgment more effective and accessible, rather than simply replacing them.

## Historical Parallels and Future Outlook
In his analysis, Autor draws parallels between AI's impact and previous technological shifts, such as how mass production decimated artisanal expertise. However, he argues the parallel with AI runs counter to the past, suggesting AI could empower a broader set of workers with expert capabilities, potentially helping to rebuild middle-class jobs. He remains skeptical about the imminent replacement of jobs involving physical dexterity in unpredictable environments, citing the persistent challenges of autonomous driving as a key example.
