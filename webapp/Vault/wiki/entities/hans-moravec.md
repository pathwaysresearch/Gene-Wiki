# Hans Moravec

## Overview
Hans Moravec is a roboticist and futurist known for his contributions to philosophical discussions about artificial intelligence and the future of humanity.

## Role in the Brain Replacement Experiment
The brain replacement thought experiment, which explores the implications of functionalism by imagining the gradual replacement of neurons with electronic devices, is most commonly associated with Moravec (1988).

## Transhumanist Views
Moravec is a proponent of transhumanism, a movement that looks forward to a future where humans are merged with or replaced by technology. He encourages humanity to give every advantage to our 'mind children'—the intelligent robots we create—which he believes may surpass us in intelligence.
