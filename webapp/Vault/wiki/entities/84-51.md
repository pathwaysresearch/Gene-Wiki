# 84.51°

## Overview
84.51° is the data science group for The Kroger Co. and is central to the company's AI-driven strategic initiatives. The organization's current approach to machine learning emerged from an initiative called *embedded machine learning* (EML) that began in 2015 under the leadership of data science manager Scott Crawford.

## Adoption of AutoML
A key part of 84.51°'s strategy is the extensive use of AutoML tools and processes. This adoption is seen as a logical progression from ad hoc modeling to automated processes that improve efficiency and accuracy. The tools are used to improve the productivity of professional data scientists and to enable frequent model retraining, such as the nightly updates to sales forecasting models based on the most recent data.

## Empowering New Roles
84.51° uses AutoML to expand the number of people who can apply machine learning. The unit hires "insights specialists" who have high business acumen but less formal data science training. Aided by AutoML, these specialists can perform many traditional model development activities, allowing experienced data scientists to focus on more complex problems and on training others. The final component of their approach is model deployment into production systems at scale.
