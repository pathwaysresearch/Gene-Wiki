# Ocado

## Automation Strategy

Ocado's core technological journey has been the continuous automation of its operating processes, particularly within its highly automated warehouses. While human labor is still used for tasks that bots find difficult, such as picking certain grocery items, the company's strategy is to move labor off the critical path as much as possible to improve the scalability and reliability of its fulfillment process.

## Dual Business Models

Ocado operates with two distinct business models enabled by its deep AI and digital capabilities. The first is its own UK-based online retail business. The second involves offering its proprietary technology platform, the Ocado Smart Platform, to power third-party retail and delivery services for other companies.

## Global Partnerships

Leveraging its technology platform, Ocado is expanding internationally by partnering with major retailers. It works with companies like Marks & Spencer in the UK, Sobeys in Canada, and Kroger in the United States to set up and operate warehouses and customer fulfillment centers. As part of its partnership, Kroger increased its investment stake in Ocado to over 6 percent.
