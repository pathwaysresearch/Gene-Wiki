# Minority Business Development Agency

## Overview

The Minority Business Development Agency (MBDA) is an agency focused on the growth and competitiveness of minority-owned businesses.

## Role in Data Collection

The agency partnered with the U.S. Census Bureau and the Ewing Marion Kauffman Foundation to conduct the Annual Survey of Entrepreneurs (ASEs). This survey provided key data on the state of entrepreneurship in the United States.

## Survey Consolidation

The ASE, a product of this partnership, was later consolidated into the Annual Business Survey (ABS) to create a more comprehensive data source on U.S. businesses.
