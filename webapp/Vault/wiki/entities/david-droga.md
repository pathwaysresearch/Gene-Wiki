# David Droga

## Overview

David Droga is a prominent figure in the advertising and creative industries, identified as the founder of the "legendary agency" Droga5 and the current CEO of Accenture Song. He appears as a guest on the Creative Intelligence podcast to discuss the transformative effects of artificial intelligence on creative work, agencies, and the role of marketing leaders.

## Views on AI and Creativity

Droga expresses a bullish and optimistic perspective on AI's integration into the creative world. Rather than viewing it as a threat that will eliminate jobs, he sees it as a historical inevitability that will move the industry forward, similar to past technological shifts. He argues that the ultimate impact of AI depends on how creative minds use, interpret, and even misinterpret the technology. He emphasizes that AI is a tool that still requires human strategy, taste, and insight to create work that truly motivates and connects with people.

## Perspective on Creative Agencies

Droga repeatedly advises creative agencies to fundamentally change their business models to remain relevant. He believes the old structures are broken but is highly optimistic about the future for creative people, stating that the need for their skills has never been greater. He encourages creatives to be more assertive, push into new areas, and recognize their value in asking different questions, not just providing different answers. He advocates for new collaborative structures, such as pairing designers and engineers as equal partners, to adapt to the new technological landscape.
