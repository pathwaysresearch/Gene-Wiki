# Warren Abstract Machine (WAM)

## Overview
The Warren Abstract Machine, or WAM, is the most popular intermediate language used for compiling Prolog. It is named after its creator, David H. D. Warren.

## Role in Compilation
The semantics of Prolog do not map well to the instruction sets of typical computers. The WAM serves as a compilation target that bridges this gap, providing a more suitable abstract instruction set for executing logic programs, which can then be interpreted or further compiled to native machine code.

## Creator
The WAM is named after David H. D. Warren, who is credited with its development.
