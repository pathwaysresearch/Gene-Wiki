# Digital, Data & Design (D^3) Institute at Harvard

## Overview
The Digital, Data & Design (D^3) Institute at Harvard is a research center founded and chaired by Professor Karim R. Lakhani. It comprises over 65 researchers focused on understanding the effects of digital technologies and AI on business and society.

## Research Focus
The institute's primary research methodology for studying AI, as described in the text, is conducting "clinical trials of AI in the workplace." This approach aims to rigorously measure AI's efficacy, side effects, and overall impact on knowledge workers and organizations.

## Mission and Contribution
The institute's work is framed as a response to the rapid, widespread introduction of Generative AI, which Lakhani compares to a "drug" with unknown properties. By performing these studies in organizations like BCG, the D^3 Institute aims to provide evidence-based guidance for leaders on how to strategically implement AI.
