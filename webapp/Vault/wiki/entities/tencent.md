# Tencent

## Overview and Origins
Founded in Shenzhen in 1998, Tencent is one of the companies behind the major "collisions" between digital technology and traditional businesses. The company initially entered the market with a PC-based internet instant messaging service for Chinese users, similar to the early web service ICQ.

## Role in Digital Ecosystems
Tencent is the creator of the highly influential WeChat app. Through applications like WeChat, Tencent has become a central player in reshaping businesses, professions, and daily life, particularly in Asia. The company's focus on connecting users and capturing data has allowed it to build a powerful digital ecosystem.

## Impact on Commerce
Through its WeChat app and its payment functionalities, Tencent has been instrumental in the shift towards app-based digital payments. This has driven a wave of new applications that leverage the resulting data, analytics, and AI, fundamentally changing how commerce is conducted in markets where it is dominant.
