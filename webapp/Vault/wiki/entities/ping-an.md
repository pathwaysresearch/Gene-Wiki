# Ping An

## Overview
Ping An is a company based in China that was founded in 1988 as an insurance provider. It has since transformed into an integrated financial services platform, offering products and services in insurance, banking, and investment. The company operates through a series of lifestyle ecosystems focused on financial services, healthcare, auto services, and smart cities.

## AI-Fueled Strategy
The company is highlighted as having an exceptionally strong commitment to an AI-fueled business strategy. Ping An's primary focus is on using AI to create entirely new business models, strategies, ecosystems, and processes, rather than merely using it for incremental improvements to existing operations. This forward-looking strategy was highly effective during the period of dramatic economic growth in China in the late twentieth and early twenty-first centuries.

## Leadership and Engagement
Senior management at Ping An is deeply engaged with the company's AI initiatives. The founder and chairman, Peter Ma Mingzhe, is described as being closely involved with the data science team and personally driving new developments in AI and related technologies. When he conceives of a new application for AI within the business, he actively finds the right team to execute the vision.
