# Pfizer

## Overview
Pfizer is a pharmaceutical company that leverages its strengths in sales and marketing by applying AI to support those functions. The company is also making strategic investments to use AI more aggressively in its core business of drug discovery and development. (Chunk 51)

## AI in Commercial Operations
Many of Pfizer's AI applications are focused on sales and marketing. These include use cases for identifying physicians most likely to assist patients with prescribed medications and informing them about appropriate product use. Its Australian business unit uses an AI platform to simulate the impact of different sales and marketing strategies. AI is also used to personalize communications with patients participating in clinical trials. (Chunk 51)

## AI in Research and Development
Pfizer notably used AI methods to accelerate the clinical trials for its record-setting Covid-19 vaccine. To further its R&D capabilities, the company is creating a scientific data cloud to develop algorithms for improving compound prediction. To ensure its workforce is prepared, Pfizer is training employees across the company in AI methods through a series of boot camps. (Chunk 51)
