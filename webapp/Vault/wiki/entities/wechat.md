# WeChat

## Functionality and Reach
WeChat is an app created by Tencent that allows users to transfer money instantly, digitally, and securely. Its use is widespread in many Asian cities, where it has become the preferred mode of payment in various settings, from luxury malls to restaurants and even for transactions with street performers and panhandlers.

## Impact on Payments
The app, along with competitors like Alipay, is driving a rapid shift away from cash. Western visitors are often surprised to find that app-based digital systems like WeChat Pay are preferred over cash or even credit cards. This widespread adoption has made cash virtually useless in many contexts.

## Enabling New Business Models
By digitizing payments and daily interactions, WeChat is a key driver of new business applications. The platform generates vast amounts of data that can be leveraged for analytics and AI, reshaping all kinds of businesses and professions. It exemplifies how digital technology is colliding with and transforming the traditional economy.
