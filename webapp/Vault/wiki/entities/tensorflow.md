# TensorFlow

## Overview
TensorFlow is described as a "now almost ubiquitous machine learning framework." It is presented as a key product that is powered by the open source software approach.

## Origins and Governance
The framework was originally built by Google. It has since been released into the open domain, making it a part of the global open source ecosystem.

## Role in Technology
As a popular machine learning framework, TensorFlow is an important tool in the development of AI applications. Its open source nature contributes to its widespread adoption and the collaborative improvement of its capabilities.
