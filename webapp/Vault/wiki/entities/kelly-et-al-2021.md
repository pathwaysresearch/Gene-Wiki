# Kelly et al. (2021)

## Overview
Kelly et al. (2021) is an academic paper cited as the source for the study's measure of breakthrough innovations. This work provides a methodology for identifying novel and impactful patents using natural language processing tools.

## Contribution to the Study
The primary contribution of Kelly et al. (2021) to this research is its quantitative measure of a patent's breakthrough status. The measure is based on comparing a patent's textual similarity to patents that came before it (backward-similarity) and after it (forward-similarity). A high ratio of forward- to backward-similarity indicates a breakthrough.

## Significance
This measure is critical for the paper's instrumental variables strategy. By providing an objective way to identify major, unanticipated innovations, the Kelly et al. (2021) methodology allows the researchers to construct an instrument to estimate the causal effect of technology on the creation of new work.
