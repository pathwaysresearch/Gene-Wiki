# The EFF (AI Progress Measurement)

## Overview
The EFF is an organization that, as one of its activities, collects and maintains statistics about the progress of AI across separate artificial intelligence applications. This initiative, referred to as AI Progress Measurement, produces metrics that have been cited in various computer science outlets.

## Data Curation and Sourcing
The EFF monitors progress in AI by drawing on data from multiple sources, including academic literature, review articles, blog posts, and websites focused on artificial intelligence. A key aspect of its methodology is that it only includes data from verified sources that document proof of their findings, ensuring a high standard of evidence.

## Role in AIOE Construction
The researchers creating the AI Occupational Exposure (AIOE) measure used the EFF's categorization to identify key applications of AI. They specifically focused on 10 AI applications for which the EFF had recorded measured scientific activity and progress from 2010 onward, selecting these as the applications experiencing the fastest growth and most likely to be used in the medium-term.
