# Toyota Research Institute (TRI)

## Overview and Mission
The Toyota Research Institute (TRI) is described as having a mission to improve the quality of human life through advances in artificial intelligence, automated driving, robotics, and materials science. Its overarching goal is to build a world of "mobility for all," where everyone can live in harmony with technology, regardless of age or ability.

## Key Automated Driving Projects
TRI is actively developing two major automated driving systems, "Guardian" and "Chauffeur." The Guardian mode is a safety system designed to prevent crashes by protecting drivers from making lethal mistakes. The Chauffeur mode is designed to provide continuous driving assistance for those who need it, such as older drivers.

## Broader Research Goals
Beyond its work on Guardian and Chauffeur, TRI's objectives include developing technology for vehicles and robots to enhance personal independence and mobility. The institute also aims to accelerate the market introduction of advanced mobility technology and to discover new materials to make batteries and hydrogen fuel cells smaller, lighter, and more powerful.
