# DAIOE Index

## Overview
The DAIOE index is a quantitative measure designed to capture the exposure of different occupations to Artificial Intelligence. It was developed by Engberg et al. (2024), the same authors as the paper under analysis. It serves as the primary instrument for measuring the paper's main independent variable, AI exposure.

## Characteristics
A key feature of the DAIOE index is that it provides yearly information on AI exposure, distinguishing it from some time-invariant measures of technology exposure. For their analysis of wages, the authors use the first year of data available for the index, which is 2010.

## Use in the Study
The DAIOE index is the basis for the 'AI-Exposure' variable used in all the wage regressions presented in the paper's tables. The study correlates this occupational-level AI exposure measure with the log daily wages of individual workers from the SIAB dataset to estimate the effect of AI on earnings, controlling for a host of other factors.
