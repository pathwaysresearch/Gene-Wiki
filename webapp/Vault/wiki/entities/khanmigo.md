# Khanmigo

## Role as a Student Tutor

For students, Khanmigo acts as a personal tutor that engages them in a "Socratic conversation" when they have a question. It understands the context of the student's work and guides them toward the answer. The tool also has a memory, allowing it to personalize interactions by referencing a student's stated interests, such as basketball, to make learning more relatable.

## Features for Teachers

Khanmigo offers a suite of tools for teachers. It can analyze student interactions across a classroom to report on trends, such as which students are struggling and which are excelling. This allows teachers to provide targeted support. The AI can also collaborate with teachers on developing lesson plans based on these classroom insights.

## Support for Parents and Grading

The tool surfaces information to parents, allowing them to have conversations with the AI about their child's activities and progress. Additionally, Khanmigo is designed to assist with the time-consuming task of grading. It can provide consistent feedback on student essays, such as those on "The Great Gatsby," significantly reducing the workload for teachers while keeping them in the loop.
