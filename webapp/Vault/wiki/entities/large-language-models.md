# Large Language Models

## Overview
Large Language Models (LLMs) are described as the most popular and well-studied class of generative AI models. Their primary function is to generate text outputs in response to user-provided text prompts.

## Training Method
LLMs are trained using a hybrid learning approach known as self-supervised learning. This method combines elements from both unsupervised learning, where the model detects underlying patterns in huge data corpuses without human guidance, and supervised learning, where it generates its own training examples from those data.

## Ideation Capabilities
The functionality of LLMs in creative ideation is noted to mirror two distinct pathways of human creativity. Their ability to generate a high volume of ideas is analogous to "persistence," while their capacity for "semantic breadth"—connecting disparate concepts—is analogous to "flexibility."
