# Peloton

## Overview
Peloton is a fitness company that provides a high-end stationary bike featuring a large HD touchscreen. This screen allows users to stream thousands of live and on-demand workout classes from studios in New York and London, effectively bringing the group fitness experience into the home.

## Business Model Innovation
Peloton's business model is presented as a collision with the traditional fitness industry. It transforms a conventional "analog" product—a piece of home exercise equipment—by integrating digital content, data, analytics, and connectivity. This model contrasts sharply with traditional gyms, which rely on capital-intensive facilities and subscription fees, and with typical home equipment manufacturers, who simply sell a physical product.

## Value Creation
The core value creation for Peloton lies in providing the benefit and convenience of an in-home fitness experience without sacrificing access to high-quality instructors and the motivating community of a fitness studio. By bringing the studio experience directly to the customer's home, Peloton addresses the desire for both convenience and a guided, engaging workout, enhanced by access to an unlimited number of classes.
