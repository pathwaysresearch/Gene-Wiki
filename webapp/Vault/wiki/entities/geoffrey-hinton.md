# Geoffrey Hinton

## Overview
Geoffrey Hinton is presented as a central protagonist in the story of modern AI and a key figure in Cade Metz's book "Genius Makers." He is recognized for his unwavering belief in neural networks at a time when the idea was deeply unpopular in the research community. His persistence is highlighted as a defining characteristic.

## The 2012 Auction
A significant event detailed in the text is the auction for Hinton and his two-person research team. Over two days, companies including Microsoft and Google engaged in a bidding war, with offers escalating from $10 million to over $40 million. Ultimately, the bidding reached $44 million, at which point Hinton ended the auction himself because he specifically wanted to work for Google, and he and his team accepted their offer.

## Research Philosophy and Contributions
Hinton's philosophy is captured in his quote: "If it's a good idea, you keep trying for 20 years... it doesn't stop being a good idea because it doesn't work the first time you try it." This commitment was validated when his team's neural network broke records in image recognition, which not only beat existing systems but also proved that the neural network approach still held immense promise and could deliver significant value.
