# Rodney Brooks

## Overview
Rodney Brooks is an AI researcher known for his critique of the prevailing focus of AI development on symbolic games.

## Critique of AI's Focus on Games
Brooks emphasized the importance of shifting AI development away from a narrow focus on games like chess. He argued that success in such constrained, symbolic environments was not a true measure of intelligence.

## Vision for Real-World AI
According to Brooks, true intelligence should encompass a broader understanding and responsiveness to the physical realities of daily tasks. His perspective called for developing AI that operates effectively in complex, real-world situations, rather than in the artificial confines of a game.
