# Dana Mackenzie

## Overview
Dana Mackenzie is an award-winning science writer who co-authored "The Book of Why." He resides in Santa Cruz, California.

## Key Works
In addition to his work on causality with Judea Pearl, Mackenzie is the author of several other books. His works mentioned in the text include "The Big Splat, or How Our Moon Came to Be," "The Universe in Zero Words," and several volumes of "What's Happening in the Mathematical Sciences."

## Role
As a science writer, Mackenzie's role is to make complex scientific topics, such as causality and mathematics, accessible to a broader audience.
