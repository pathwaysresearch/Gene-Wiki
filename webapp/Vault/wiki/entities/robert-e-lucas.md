# Robert E. Lucas

## Overview
Robert E. Lucas is identified in the text through his seminal 1967 publication in the Journal of Political Economy, titled “Adjustment Costs and the Theory of Supply.” This work is cited as a key theoretical antecedent for the model being developed in the excerpts.

## Contribution to the Model
The authors of the text explicitly state that they modify their firm-level production equation to incorporate adjustment costs by "following Lucas (1967)." This indicates that Lucas's work provides the formal economic structure for representing the costs that firms incur when they change their level of capital investment. His framework is essential for moving beyond a frictionless view of investment.

## Significance
The citation of Lucas is not a passing reference but a cornerstone for a significant feature of the analytical model. By building on his work, the authors are able to construct a more realistic framework that can simultaneously analyze the impacts of adjustment costs, multiple varieties of capital, and unmeasured intangible investments on firm behavior and production.
