# Adherence to AI Recommendations

## Definition and Measurement
Adherence is defined as the proportion of AI suggestions an agent typically adopts. In the context of the study, an agent is considered to have adhered when they either directly copy the AI's proposed text or manually enter highly similar content. This metric is crucial because the AI tool makes suggestions, but agents are ultimately responsible for what they say and can choose to ignore the guidance.

## Relationship with Skill and Productivity
The study documents the association between adherence and the returns to AI adoption. It finds that agents, particularly those initially more skeptical, come to value and adhere to AI recommendations over time. Interestingly, high-skill agents increase their adherence as quickly as their lower-skill peers, even though their productivity gains are smaller.

## Potential for Over-reliance
While adherence can facilitate learning, the study notes a potential for over-reliance on AI recommendations. The fact that high-skill agents increase adherence despite smaller gains suggests they may be using the AI for convenience rather than performance improvement. This could be suboptimal in the long run, as top agents may see little value in providing high-quality inputs when an adequate AI suggestion is available, potentially reducing the quality and diversity of solutions used for future AI training.
