# Deskilling (from AI)

## Definition
Deskilling is the process by which reliance on technology to perform certain tasks leads to the loss of the human ability to perform those same tasks. The text presents this not as a new phenomenon specific to AI, but as a consistent historical consequence of technological advancement in general.

## Historical Context and Examples
Technology has always been a source of deskilling. The text provides several examples to illustrate this point, including the loss of skills such as driving a car with a manual gear shift, navigating without GPS, or remembering phone numbers. These examples show that as technology takes over a function, human proficiency in that function naturally declines.

## Relevance to Generative AI
In the age of Generative AI, understanding deskilling is particularly important, especially for students and professionals entering the workforce. It is crucial to be thoughtful and intentional in the use of GenAI to avoid the unintended loss of critical skills. This involves balancing the use of AI for productivity with the deliberate practice of core competencies, and understanding which skills are becoming obsolete versus which are fundamental and should be retained.
