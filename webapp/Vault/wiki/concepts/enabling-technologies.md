# Enabling Technologies

## Definition and Role
Enabling technologies are foundational innovations that support the diffusion and impact of other, more advanced technologies. The text argues that studying frontier technologies like AI in isolation from these enablers could obscure important interdependencies that shape their adoption. The research focuses on data availability and cloud use as key enabling technologies for AI, motivated by recent academic attention to their interconnected roles.

## Key Examples in AI Diffusion
The study identifies two primary enabling technologies for AI: digitization and cloud computing. Digitization, defined as having at least one type of company information in digital format, is a widespread precursor, reported by over 65% of firms. Cloud computing, or purchasing cloud services for IT functions, is shown to be a powerful predictor of AI use, with firms using cloud services being 5 percentage points more likely to also use AI.

## Technological Hierarchy
A key finding is that these technologies form an apparent hierarchy or progression. A Sankey diagram illustrates that firms typically adopt digitization first, followed by cloud computing, and then AI. This suggests that firms build up digital capabilities sequentially, with reliance on data and cloud services acting as crucial steps that enable the eventual adoption of AI.
