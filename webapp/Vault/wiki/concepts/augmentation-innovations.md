# Augmentation Innovations

## Definition
Augmentation innovations are technological advancements that complement the outputs of occupations, as opposed to substituting for worker inputs. In the theoretical framework presented, these innovations are supplied by entrepreneurs to augment workers, which leads to the creation of new tasks. The study makes a crucial empirical distinction between these innovations and those that automate labor.

## Measurement
The study measures an occupation's exposure to augmentation innovations by analyzing the full corpus of U.S. utility patents issued between 1940 and 2018. The methodology identifies overlaps between the textual content of patents and the content of occupations. For augmentation, the occupational content is characterized using the text from the Census Alphabetical Index of Occupations and Industries (CAI), which describes job outputs and specializations.

## Economic Impact
The research finds a strong, positive relationship between augmentation innovations and the creation of new work. Occupations that are more exposed to augmentation innovations are the primary locus of new job title emergence over time. Consequently, these occupations also experience growth in both employment and total wage bills, highlighting the role of these innovations in boosting labor demand.
