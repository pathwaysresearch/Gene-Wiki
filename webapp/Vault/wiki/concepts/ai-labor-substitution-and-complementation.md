# AI-Labor Substitution and Complementation

## The Core Debate

A fundamental question in the study of AI's economic impact is whether it will primarily substitute for human labor, leading to job displacement, or complement it, creating new roles and increasing productivity. The text notes that while public discussion often focuses on the threat AI poses to workers, there is very little systematic evidence on its actual impact on labor demand.

## A Measurement Challenge

Studying this issue requires measures that do not presuppose the outcome. The text highlights that the AIOE (AI Occupational Exposure) measure is specifically designed to be agnostic on this point. It quantifies an occupation's exposure to AI technologies without assuming whether that exposure will ultimately lead to substitution or complementation.

## Research Framework

By being neutral on the effect of AI on labor demand, the AIOE measure provides a tool for researchers to empirically investigate the circumstances in which each effect dominates. It allows for a more nuanced understanding beyond the simple question of substitution, enabling study of how AI interacts with human capital and affects labor productivity. This contrasts with traditional capital-labor substitution, which was often driven by highly specialized technologies.
