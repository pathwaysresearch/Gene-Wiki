# Human Impact of AI

## Definition
The human impact of AI is an area of study that analyzes the consequences of deploying artificial intelligence technologies from a behavioral science perspective. It contrasts with purely 'algorithmic work' by focusing on how AI affects people and social structures.

## Scope of Analysis
This field examines the effects of AI on three distinct levels. It considers the impact on individuals in their daily lives and decision-making, on organizations in terms of operations and strategy, and on society as a whole.

## Academic Application
This concept is central to the work of Dr. Stefano Puntoni at the Wharton School. He teaches a course titled 'AI in our lives' dedicated to this topic, and his expertise in this 'impact work' was a key reason for his recruitment by the institution as it sought to expand its AI footprint.
