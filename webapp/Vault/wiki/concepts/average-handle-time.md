# Average Handle Time (AHT)

## Definition
Average Handle Time (AHT) is defined in the text as "the average duration of each technical support chat" (Notes for Figure A.3). It is a standard operational metric used to measure the efficiency of customer service agents, with lower AHT generally indicating higher efficiency.

## Impact of AI Assistance
The study investigates the effect of AI deployment on AHT as a key outcome variable. The event study in Figure A.3 (Panel A) visually demonstrates a slight decrease in AHT in the months following the AI's introduction. This finding is confirmed with greater precision in the Randomized Controlled Trial (RCT) analysis in Table A.1, which reports a statistically significant decrease of 3.713 in AHT for treated agents.

## Role in Overall Analysis
AHT is one of several metrics used to build a comprehensive picture of the AI's impact on agent performance. It is analyzed alongside other outcomes like chats per hour and resolution rate. The negative effect on AHT (shorter calls) combined with a positive effect on chats per hour contributes to the overall increase in the main productivity metric, resolutions per hour.
