# Task Complementarities

## Role in AI's Economic Impact

Task complementarity is a key channel through which AI can boost productivity, working alongside automation in the paper's task-based framework. Rather than substituting for labor, AI in this role acts as a tool that enhances the efficiency, quality, or speed of human workers, thereby improving their productivity in the tasks they perform.

## Theoretical Importance

The paper's model incorporates the effects of both automation and task complementarities to provide a comprehensive analysis of AI's potential impact on the economy. The potential for strong complementarities between AI and human skills is a source of optimism for some experts regarding AI's ability to boost wages and overall productivity without causing widespread job displacement.

## Empirical Challenges

The text points out that it is empirically challenging to disentangle the effects of task complementarities from automation. It cites the work of Eloundou et al. (2023) as an example where the methodology used to measure AI's impact on tasks does not fully distinguish whether the impact will take the form of automation or task complementarities.
