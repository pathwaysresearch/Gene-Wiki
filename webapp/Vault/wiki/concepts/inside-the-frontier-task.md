# Inside-the-Frontier Task

## Definition
In the context of the Ssrn 4573321 study, an "inside-the-frontier" task is an experimental activity designed to be within the high-capability range of the AI model, GPT-4. These are tasks where AI is expected to function as a performance "booster" for knowledge workers (Chunk 14).

## Performance Effects on Productivity
The study found that for inside-the-frontier tasks, the introduction of GPT-4 had a significant positive impact on productivity. Table 5 shows that subjects in the AI treatment groups had a statistically significant increase in the percentage of subtasks completed. Table 6 demonstrates that subjects using AI took significantly less time, measured in seconds, to complete the task compared to the control group (Chunk 8, 9).

## Replication and Robustness
The findings for inside-the-frontier tasks were supported by robustness checks. A replication study involving shoe design experts evaluated creativity on an inside-the-frontier task. The results confirmed that both AI treatment groups had higher creativity scores than the control group, although the magnitude of the effect was smaller than in the main analysis. This convergence across different evaluators provides evidence against systematic bias (Chunk 13).
