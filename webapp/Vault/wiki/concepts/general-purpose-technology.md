# General Purpose Technology

## Definition
A General Purpose Technology (GPT) is a technology that has the power to change the world and humanity forever. Unlike specialized tools, GPTs have a wide-ranging and profound impact across many sectors of the economy and society. Historical examples include the steam engine, electricity, and the internet.

## AI as a General Purpose Technology
Artificial intelligence is considered to be a GPT that will be fundamentally transformative for business. Its impact is expected to be on a similar scale to the internet, which enabled society to navigate challenges like the pandemic through remote work and communication. The early applications of AI, such as ChatGPT, are just the beginning of a much larger societal and economic pivot.

## Business Implications
Companies that fail to recognize AI as a GPT and treat it as just another buzzword risk being left behind. Those that are quick to backtrack after early failures or who wait to follow rather than lead will be at a significant disadvantage. The transformative nature of this technology requires a strategic, long-term approach to adoption and integration, similar to how businesses had to adapt to the rise of the internet.
