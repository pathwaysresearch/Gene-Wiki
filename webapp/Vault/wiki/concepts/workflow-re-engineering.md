# Workflow Re-engineering

## Definition
Workflow re-engineering is the practice of fundamentally rethinking and redesigning business processes in light of AI capabilities, rather than simply equipping existing roles with AI tools. The text contrasts this with the more common approach of augmenting a single job, suggesting that re-engineering the entire workflow may lead to the elimination or complete transformation of pre-existing roles.

## A More Advanced Approach
This concept is presented as a strategy employed by more forward-thinking and "advanced companies." Instead of focusing narrowly on how a specific role can adopt generative AI, these organizations step back to analyze and re-engineer the entire value chain, asking how the work itself should be restructured.

## Implementation Challenges
A significant barrier to workflow re-engineering is a skills gap at the operational level. The employees who are intimately familiar with current workflows often lack a deep understanding of what AI, particularly concepts like agents, can do. The text argues that this re-engineering cannot be successfully dictated top-down by executives; it requires upskilling the people involved in the day-to-day processes so they have the comfort and exposure to AI necessary to contribute to the redesign.
