# Inductive Learning

## Definition
Inductive learning is the process of finding a hypothesis that is consistent with and generalizes from a set of examples. The primary focus of the text's chapter on learning from examples is on the inductive learning of functions.

## Guiding Principle
The principle of Ockham's razor is a key guide in inductive learning, suggesting that the simplest hypothesis that is consistent with the examples should be chosen. The difficulty of the learning task is highly dependent on the chosen representation for the hypotheses.

## Forms of Learning
Learning can take many forms, depending on the agent, the component being improved, and the available feedback. Supervised learning is a specific and common type of inductive learning where correct answers are provided for example inputs.
