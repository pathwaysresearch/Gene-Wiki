# Cyborg Collaboration Pattern

## Definition
The Cyborg pattern is a model for human-AI collaboration where the human and the AI system are enmeshed in the process together. This approach involves a tight, continuous integration of human and machine capabilities.

## How It Works
Unlike the clean handoffs of the Centaur model, a Cyborg workflow involves the human and AI working in a constant feedback loop. The AI might provide real-time suggestions, analysis, or data, which the human integrates into their ongoing work, creating a fluid, combined intelligence.

## Context of Use
This pattern is contrasted with the Centaur model to provide a spectrum of options for designing AI-assisted workflows. The Cyborg approach is suitable for tasks where continuous cognitive partnership is more effective than a sequential division of labor.
