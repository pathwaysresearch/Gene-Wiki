# Natural Language Processing

## Overview
Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a technique within AI used to make sense of unstructured data. According to Stefano Puntoni, it enables the automated analysis of human language. These tools have been available for some time and are continuously improving in their capabilities.

## Application in Customer Analysis
A primary application discussed is the analysis of customer reviews. By using NLP, a company can automatically process a large volume of text-based feedback to extract sentiment and understand what issues customers are facing and why. This forms the basis of customer sentiment analysis tools.

## Distinction from Generative AI
Puntoni explicitly distinguishes NLP-based analysis from the "generative part" of AI. While NLP is used to analyze and extract insights from existing content (e.g., determining sentiment from a review), Generative AI is focused on the creation of new content (e.g., summarizing those reviews in a new, free-form way).
