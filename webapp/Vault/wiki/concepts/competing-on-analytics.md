# Competing on Analytics

## Overview
Competing on analytics is a strategic approach where organizations build a strong foundation in data analysis as a competitive differentiator. The text presents this as a common and recommended pathway for established companies before they move into artificial intelligence.

## Role as a Precursor to AI
The text notes that most of the profiled companies, including Deloitte and Capital One, had significant initiatives in analytics before they moved "headlong into AI." This suggests that building analytical capabilities is a foundational step for a successful AI journey. Well is noted as an exception, being an AI startup from its inception.

## Key Proponents
Gary Loveman, the CEO of Well, is identified as a strong advocate of this strategy from his time leading Harrah's and Caesars Entertainment. The concept is also associated with author Thomas H. Davenport, who wrote about it in an earlier book and a Harvard Business Review article.
