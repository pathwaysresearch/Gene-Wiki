# Physicalism

## Definition
Physicalism, also referred to as the monist theory of mind, asserts that the mind is not separate from the body. It posits that mental states, such as being in pain or knowing one is riding a horse, are identical to physical states, specifically the molecular configurations and electrochemical processes of the brain.

## Relation to Dualism
Physicalism stands in direct opposition to dualism by avoiding the mind-body interaction problem. Instead of trying to explain how two separate realms connect, it denies their separation altogether. Most modern philosophers of mind are physicalists of one form or another.

## Implications for AI
The physicalist view is significant for artificial intelligence because it allows, at least in principle, for the possibility of strong AI. The central problem for physicalists is to explain precisely how physical brain states can simultaneously be mental states.
