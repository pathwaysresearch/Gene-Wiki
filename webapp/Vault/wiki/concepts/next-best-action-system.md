# Next-Best-Action (NBA) System

## Definition
A Next-Best-Action (NBA) system is an application of AI that provides users with tailored insights and recommendations. As implemented at Morgan Stanley, the system uses machine learning to identify relevant investments, actions of interest, and personalized ideas for a financial advisor to present to a specific client.

## How It Works
The system analyzes client data to generate a list of potential ideas, such as recommending an alternative bond after a rating downgrade, suggesting investment ideas for a new cash deposit, or proposing tax planning considerations near year-end. The text notes that the system might generate twenty or so ideas per day for a client, but the human advisor makes the final decision on whether to act on them.

## Impact on Efficiency
The implementation of the NBA system at Morgan Stanley dramatically improved advisor productivity. It reduced the time required to identify a personalized investing idea for a client from approximately forty-five minutes to just a few seconds. This efficiency gain is crucial for advisors who typically manage portfolios for two hundred or more clients.
