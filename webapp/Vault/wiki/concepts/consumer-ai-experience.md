# Consumer AI Experience

## Definition
The Consumer AI Experience is a customer-centric framework for understanding artificial intelligence. It shifts the focus from the technical aspects of AI—such as its efficiency and accuracy—to how AI's capabilities are actually experienced by consumers. This perspective encompasses multiple dimensions of interaction, including the emotional, cognitive, behavioral, sensorial, and social aspects of the customer journey with AI-powered products and services.

## Core Components
The framework conceptualizes AI as an ecosystem comprising data collection, statistical techniques, and output systems, which manifest as capabilities like listening, predicting, and producing. The Consumer AI Experience is built upon four key experiential domains that correspond to these capabilities: the Data Capture Experience (from listening), the Classification Experience (from predicting), and the Delegation Experience (from producing). Each of these domains is characterized by a central psychological tension that defines the consumer's relationship with the technology.

## Key Psychological Tensions
A central tenet of the framework is that each interaction with an AI capability creates a psychological tension for the consumer. For instance, in the Data Capture Experience, consumers feel a tension between being served by personalization and being exploited by data collection. Similarly, the Classification Experience creates a tension between feeling understood by relevant recommendations and feeling misunderstood by inaccurate or discriminatory categorization. These tensions highlight the social and individual challenges that arise when AI is deployed in consumer contexts.
