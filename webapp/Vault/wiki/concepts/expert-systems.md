# Expert Systems

## Overview
Expert systems represent an early approach in artificial intelligence. These systems were designed to be effective for narrow tasks by reasoning through bodies of knowledge represented as pre-programmed rules.

## Core Limitations
The primary limitation of expert systems was their reliance on explicitly coded knowledge. This made them struggle with complexity and adaptability, as they could not learn or reason beyond their programmed instruction set.

## Brittleness
A key failure mode of expert systems was their "brittleness." This meant they could not handle unforeseen situations or inputs that fell outside their meticulously defined rules. This inability to cope with the nuanced and unpredictable nature of real-world problems showcased the inadequacies of these early AI methods when compared to human reasoning.
