# AI-Augmented Customer Service

## First-Line Automation
A common and established application of AI in customer service is the use of chatbots on company websites. These automated systems serve as the first line of support, popping up to offer assistance and handle basic customer queries without the need for immediate human intervention.

## Augmenting Human Agents
A more advanced and exciting application is using AI to augment the capabilities of human customer service representatives. The text describes a system where an AI interprets an incoming customer query from any channel, drafts an appropriate response, and presents it to the human agent. The agent then reviews the AI's suggestion for accuracy and tone before sending it, combining the efficiency of AI with the judgment of a human expert.

## Proactive Service Improvement
The text posits that the most ideal use of AI in customer service is to prevent the need for it in the first place. By leveraging learning algorithms, companies can make better predictions about customer needs and identify potential points of failure in the customer experience. This allows them to proactively address issues before a customer has to call and complain, ultimately shrinking the number of service requests and improving satisfaction.
