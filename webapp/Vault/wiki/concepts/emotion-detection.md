# Emotion Detection

## Definition
Emotion detection is an AI capability that leverages deep learning models to detect human emotions with an increasing degree of accuracy. The technology analyzes data, such as facial expressions, to infer a person's emotional state. (Chunk 53)

## Applications
The purposes for using emotion detection are diverse. The text provides several examples, including detecting audience responses to advertisements, identifying signs of road rage in drivers, and noticing fear or anxiety among travelers in airports for security purposes. (Chunk 53)

## Limitations and Criticisms
Despite its growing accuracy, the technique faces criticism. Critics point out that facial recognition can be an unreliable guide to human emotions on its own. To improve accuracy, the text suggests that other physiological factors may need to be assessed concurrently with facial analysis. (Chunk 53)
