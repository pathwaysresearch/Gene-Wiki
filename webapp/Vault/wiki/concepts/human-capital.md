# Human Capital

## Overview

Human capital represents the skills, knowledge, and experience possessed by an individual or population, viewed in terms of their value to an organization or economy. The text discusses human capital in the context of how its value might be altered by the emergence of Artificial Intelligence, a topic of interest in both the public press and academic community.

## Interaction with Technology

The text raises the question of whether and how AI will alter the value of human capital within firms. This is contrasted with previous literature on equipment automation, which suggested that automation did not substitute for the value of human capital in certain industries. The emergence of AI presents a new dynamic compared to traditional, highly specialized technologies that drove capital-labor substitution in the past, making its impact on human capital an open question.

## Research Applications

The AIOE (AI Occupational Exposure) measure is presented as a valuable tool for studying the relationship between AI, human capital, and labor productivity. By quantifying an occupation's exposure to AI, researchers can systematically investigate how this exposure impacts the demand for and value of the human capital associated with that occupation. This allows for empirical study of whether AI complements existing skills or renders them obsolete.
