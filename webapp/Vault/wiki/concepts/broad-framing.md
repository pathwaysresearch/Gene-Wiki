# Broad Framing

## Definition
As articulated by Daniel Kahneman, broad framing is the essence of wisdom. It is defined as the ability to avoid having too narrow a view when reasoning about a problem. This is contrasted with the narrow framing that Kahneman argues is characteristic of human thinking, which he describes as often being "noisy" and limited in scope.

## AI vs. Human Cognition
Kahneman proposes that a key difference between future advanced AI and humans will be the AI's superior ability for broad framing. He suggests that a sufficiently advanced robot or AI will be endowed with this capability, allowing it to be wiser than humans. This, combined with superior statistical reasoning, would allow an AI to easily improve upon human decision-making.

## Implications for AI's Potential
This perspective suggests that AI's potential extends beyond mere calculation to encompass a form of wisdom. By overcoming the cognitive limitations of narrow framing that affect humans, AI could make more robust and insightful decisions. Kahneman expresses confidence that because of abilities like this, there is very little that humans can do that computers will not eventually learn to do.
