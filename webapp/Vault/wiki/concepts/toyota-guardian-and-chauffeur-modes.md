# Toyota Guardian and Chauffeur Modes

## Overview
"Guardian" and "Chauffeur" are two key AI-driven projects under development at the Toyota Research Institute (TRI). They represent distinct approaches to automated driving, one focused on safety intervention and the other on continuous assistance, as part of TRI's broader mission of achieving "mobility for all."

## Guardian Mode
The "Guardian" mode is designed as a safety system to protect drivers from making lethal mistakes. The text highlights its potential to reduce teenage driving fatalities, noting that while auto accidents cause less than 1% of adult deaths in the U.S., they account for 35% of teenage deaths. The goal of this mode is to develop vehicles that are incapable of causing a crash, regardless of the driver's actions.

## Chauffeur Mode
The "Chauffeur" mode is being developed to provide continuous driving assistance for those who need it, with a particular focus on older drivers. The text notes the importance of this technology in Japan, which has a rapidly aging population. This mode aims to provide a higher level of automation to help people enjoy new levels of independence and mobility.
