# Algorithmic Dehumanization

## Core Ethical Concern
Algorithmic dehumanization is an ethical issue that emerges when algorithms are used for making significant decisions about people's lives, such as in hiring or medical diagnoses. The central concern is that this process reduces complex individuals to a collection of mere data points.

## Psychological Impact
This reduction to data can create a sense of dehumanization, making people uncomfortable with critical life decisions being made purely by algorithms. This feeling stems from the absence of nuanced understanding and empathy typically associated with human judgment.

## Preference for Human Advice
The discomfort with algorithmic decision-making is especially pronounced in subjective and emotionally significant domains. Individuals typically prefer human advice for choices related to relationships or career paths, where empathy is seen as vital. This contrasts with more data-driven areas like financial investments or movie recommendations, where algorithms are often more trusted.
