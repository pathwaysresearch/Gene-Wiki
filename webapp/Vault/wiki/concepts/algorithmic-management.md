# Algorithmic Management

## Definition
Algorithmic management refers to the deployment of algorithms and automated systems to direct, monitor, and evaluate employees. This represents a shift from traditional human-led management to a more data-driven, automated approach.

## Negative Consequences
Research indicates that algorithmic management can have significant unintended consequences. It can be perceived by employees as objectifying, leading to a reduction in prosocial behaviors. For example, in one study, workers managed by an algorithm were less likely to help their colleagues, as measured by their willingness to type out helpful tips for future workers.

## Organizational Implications
Organizations considering the deployment of algorithmic management tools are cautioned to be careful about the potential negative impacts on corporate culture and employee behavior. The efficiency gains from such systems might be offset by a decline in collaboration, helpfulness, and overall morale, which can lead to psychological threats that employees must then cope with.
