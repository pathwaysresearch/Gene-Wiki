# Automated Reasoning

## Overview
Automated reasoning is a major area of study, formalized in comprehensive works such as the 'Handbook of Automated Reasoning' (2001), edited by A. Robinson and A. Voronkov.

## Foundational Principles
A foundational technique in this field is the resolution principle, introduced by J. A. Robinson in his 1965 paper 'A machine-oriented logic based on the resolution principle.' This principle provides a basis for automated theorem proving.

## Systems and Implementations
The field has produced practical systems and technologies. The text references M. E. Stickel's 1992 work on 'A prolog technology theorem prover,' which describes an implementation in Prolog. Another example is the SPASS system, which, according to a 2001 paper by C. Weidenbach, combines techniques like superposition, sorts, and splitting.
