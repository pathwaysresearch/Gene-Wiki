# User Control in Algorithmic Systems

## Balancing Control and Performance

The author argues for a necessary balance between user control and the efficiency of algorithmic performance. Giving users a sense of control is crucial for building trust, especially as automation becomes more prevalent in daily life. The goal is to enhance trust without significantly diminishing the algorithm's performance.

## Mechanisms for Control

The text identifies several types of control that can be implemented to improve user experience and trust. These include behavioral control (the ability to intervene, like an elevator's 'stop' button), decisional control (providing meaningful choices regarding algorithmic outputs), and cognitive transparency (offering clear information about how the algorithm functions).

## Historical Precedent and User Experience

Historical examples, such as the societal adjustment to automated elevators and aviation autopilot systems, show that initial user resistance to automation can be overcome. Incorporating design elements that provide a sense of control was key to their adoption. By giving users meaningful choices and maintaining transparency, platforms can foster a better user experience and ensure continued engagement with their technology.
