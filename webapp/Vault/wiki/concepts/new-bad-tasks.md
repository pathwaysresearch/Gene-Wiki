# New Bad Tasks

## Definition and Examples

The paper introduces the concept of "new bad tasks" to account for the potential creation of negative social value by AI, even as it boosts productivity in other areas. These are new tasks that are manipulative or socially harmful. Specific examples given include the creation of deepfakes, misleading digital advertisements, addictive social media algorithms, and AI-powered malicious computer attacks.

## Divergence of GDP and Welfare

A key characteristic of new bad tasks is their potential to cause a divergence between measured economic output (GDP) and actual social welfare. While these activities can generate significant revenue for firms and thus contribute positively to GDP, their overall impact on society, once negative externalities are considered, may be detrimental.

## A Speculative Quantification

The paper provides a speculative calculation to illustrate the potential magnitude of this effect. Using research on the negative welfare effects of social media as an analogue, the author posits a scenario where the intensive use of AI for such tasks could appear to increase GDP by 2% while simultaneously reducing overall social welfare by an equivalent of 0.72% of GDP.
