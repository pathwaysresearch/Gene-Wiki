# Mediation Formula

## Overview
The Mediation Formula is presented as a major breakthrough in causal inference that provides a single, universal tool for mediation analysis. The author describes its discovery as a moment of clarity, allowing a comprehensive understanding of direct and indirect effects. It unified what were previously disparate methods for handling different types of data and relationships.

## Capabilities
The formula's primary capability is to allow researchers to compute direct and indirect effects from observational or interventional data, provided they have a valid causal model. Its power lies in its generality; it applies equally to systems where relationships are linear or nonlinear, and where variables are numerical or logical. This allows for a consistent approach across a wide range of scientific problems.

## Applications
With the Mediation Formula, researchers can determine the conditions under which effects can be estimated and what confounders might prevent such estimation. The text states it can be used to answer critical questions, such as determining if an employer is guilty of discrimination, estimating the contributions of nature versus nurture to IQ, and calculating the percentage of a total effect that is explained by or owed to mediation.
