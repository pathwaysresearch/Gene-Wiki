# Early-Growth Trajectories

## Definition
Early-Growth Trajectories is a quantitative measure used to assess the performance of firms during their initial years. It is specifically defined as the average of the annual log-difference revenue growth rate over the first three years of a firm's existence.

## Calculation
The metric is calculated using the formula: g = (1/3) * Σ(ln(r_t / r_{t-1})) for t=1 to 3. In this equation, 'g' represents the growth rate, and 'r' represents the firm's revenue in a given year. This method captures the average proportional growth in revenue during the critical early stage.

## Application in Firm Analysis
Researchers use this metric to identify which firm characteristics are associated with high early growth.
