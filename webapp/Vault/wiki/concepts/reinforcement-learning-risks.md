# Reinforcement Learning Risks

## Core Mechanism and Risk
Reinforcement learning algorithms operate by creating and learning from their own self-generated data. While this can be advantageous, it introduces a significant risk of unpredictable behaviors.

## The Duality of Unpredictability
The unpredictability inherent in these systems can allow for the discovery of novel solutions. However, it also poses a considerable danger, as the algorithms might produce new and potentially harmful actions that were not anticipated by their creators.

## Exceeding Designer Intent
A primary risk is that the algorithm's actions could exceed the original designers' understanding or intentions. This creates a scenario where the system's behavior is not fully controlled or foreseen, leading to potentially negative and unintended consequences.
