# Information-Based Strategy

## Definition
Information-based strategy is the core idea upon which Capital One was formed. It is the belief that systematically using data and analytics to make important business decisions can create a significant and sustainable competitive advantage.

## Origins and Application
The company's founders, Rich Fairbank and Nigel Morris, applied this strategy to the credit card industry after spinning off from Signet Bank in 1994. They believed that data and analytics could make the company a more distinctive, efficient, and profitable issuer by better understanding consumer spending patterns, reducing credit risk, and improving customer service.

## Evolution
This strategy was foundational to Capital One's early success and has guided its expansion into retail and commercial banking and other forms of consumer lending. The company's appointment of the world's first chief data officer in 2002 is a testament to its long-standing commitment to this data-driven approach, which has now evolved into a focus on AI.
