# AI-Powered Coaching

## Definition
AI-powered coaching is a method for delivering personalized guidance and support to learners at scale. It leverages AI to handle many components of the coaching process, thereby dramatically reducing the cost and making the benefits of one-on-one interaction more accessible to a wider audience.

## Theoretical Basis
The concept is explicitly linked to the findings of Bloom's Two Sigma Problem, which demonstrated that students receiving one-on-one tutoring achieve significantly better learning outcomes. The goal of AI-powered coaching is to solve the scalability issue of traditional tutoring, making these proven educational gains available to everyone.

## Application in Workforce Development
The text presents this approach as a key innovation for navigating the workforce transitions caused by AI. It is proposed as a tool to provide "exposure and coaching at scale," helping individuals upskill and adapt to new job requirements in a highly effective and personalized manner. While the text notes that such tools are not yet fully mature, it suggests their development is imminent.
