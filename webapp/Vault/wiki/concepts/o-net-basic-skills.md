# O*NET Basic Skills

## Definition
O*NET Basic Skills are defined as developed capacities that facilitate learning or the more rapid acquisition of knowledge. They represent foundational abilities necessary for effective performance across a variety of work domains.

## Content Skills
Content skills are described as the background structures needed to work with and acquire more specific skills in a variety of different domains. This category includes fundamental abilities such as Reading Comprehension (understanding written work-related documents), Active Listening (giving full attention to others), Writing (communicating effectively in writing), Speaking (conveying information effectively), Mathematics (using math to solve problems), and Science (using scientific rules and methods to solve problems).

## Process Skills
Process skills are procedures that contribute to the more rapid acquisition of knowledge and skill across different domains. This category focuses on cognitive and metacognitive abilities. Key process skills identified in the text include Critical Thinking (using logic and reasoning to evaluate solutions), Active Learning (understanding the implications of new information for problem-solving), and Learning Strategies (selecting and using appropriate instructional methods when learning or teaching).
