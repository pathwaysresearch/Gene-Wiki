# Job Zones

## Definition
Job Zones are a classification system that groups similar occupations based on the level of preparation needed to perform the jobs within that zone. The classification criteria include the required level of education, prior experience, and the extent of on-the-job training.

## Classification Levels
The system consists of five distinct zones. Job Zone 1 requires little to no preparation (0-3 months) and often no high school diploma (e.g., dishwashers, food preparation workers). At the other end, Job Zone 5 requires extensive preparation (4+ years), typically a Master's degree or higher (e.g., lawyers, pharmacists, astronomers).

## Exposure and Economic Characteristics
The study finds a clear trend in LLM exposure across Job Zones, with exposure generally increasing with the job zone number. For instance, the Human β exposure measure rises from 0.06 in Job Zone 1 to 0.47 in Zone 4. This aligns with economic data, as median income also rises significantly with each successive job zone, from a median of $30,230 in Zone 1 to $81,980 in Zone 5.
