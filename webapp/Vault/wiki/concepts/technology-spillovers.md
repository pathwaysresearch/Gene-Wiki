# Technology Spillovers

## Definition

Technology spillovers refer to the diffusion of knowledge, particularly tacit knowledge, which benefits from local interactions. This process is a key factor in regional innovation and the formation of industry clusters, as the transfer and diffusion of knowledge is often geographically localized.

## Mechanism of Action

The text posits that technology spillovers are often geographically localized because the transfer of tacit knowledge is facilitated by close-proximity interactions. These interactions can take the form of direct communication, collaboration, or the movement of skilled human capital within a local area. Regional capabilities, such as infrastructure like roads and railways, institutions, universities, and a skilled labor pool, can govern the innovation process and enhance the diffusion of technological knowledge among local inventors.

## Relevance to AI

The concept is applied to understand how a region's exposure to AI, as measured by the AIGE, might create location-based advantages. It is suggested that a region's AI exposure could generate tacit knowledge about how to effectively use and benefit from AI technologies. This localized, tacit knowledge can then become a regional capability upon which future innovation is built, creating a virtuous cycle of technological development.
