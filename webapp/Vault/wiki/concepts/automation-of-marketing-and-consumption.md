# Automation of Marketing and Consumption

## Overview
The automation of marketing and consumption is a central theme, described as a growing diffusion of automation in the marketplace that affects consumers in two primary ways. It has evolved from clunky telephone menus and clumsy chatbots to more sophisticated algorithms and AI that enable fully automated personalization of customer interactions.

## Automation of Marketing
This aspect involves the automation of core marketing activities. The text highlights that AI allows marketers to automate decisions related to the 4Ps (product, price, place, promotion) and other consumer-firm interactions. For example, prediction algorithms can personalize prices, optimize shipping, and maximize response rates to promotional activities.

## Automation of Consumption
This refers to the automation of activities performed by the consumer. Examples given include users automating writing tasks with large language models (LLMs), outsourcing product search to recommender systems, or adopting autonomous features in products like self-driving cars. This automation promises efficiency but also raises concerns about its psychological impact on consumers.
