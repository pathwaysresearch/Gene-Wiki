# Artificial Intelligence Technologies (AITs)

## Overview
Artificial Intelligence Technologies (AITs) are a class of information and communication technology (ICT) that have seen dramatic improvements, an explosion of entrepreneurship, and terrific buzz among both technologists and businesspeople. Despite significant advancements, their path to becoming a General-Purpose Technology (GPT) has faced challenges and is not following a simple path of adoption.

## Early Applications and Limitations
Early commercial applications of AITs were narrow but extremely profitable, particularly in product/consumer matching systems pioneered by Internet Giants. These applications succeeded in low-stakes contexts where consumers could easily ignore a bad recommendation. However, they performed poorly in high-stakes situations where a poor recommendation could offend the consumer, limiting their initial range of application. A striking feature of early AIT use was that it occurred where Digital Transformation was not required for it to succeed.

## Future Innovation Paths
For AITs to achieve widespread use, future innovation paths must address key bottlenecks. These paths require moving beyond simple automation of white-collar work, which is hampered by low visibility and high adjustment costs. Instead, progress is being seen in augmenting workers with digital tools and in making AITs more trustworthy and explainable to users. All significant future paths are described as requiring Digital Transformation as a key step.
