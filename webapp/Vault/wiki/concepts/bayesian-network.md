# Bayesian Network

## Definition and Purpose
The Bayesian network formalism was invented to allow for the efficient representation of uncertain knowledge and to enable rigorous reasoning with it. Its development followed a resurgence of interest in probability in AI during the 1980s.

## Impact on AI
This approach largely overcame many of the problems that plagued probabilistic reasoning systems of the 1960s and 1970s. It has since become the dominant approach in AI research concerning uncertain reasoning and expert systems.

## Key Features
The Bayesian network approach allows for learning from experience. It is considered to combine the best aspects of classical AI and neural networks, and it provides the foundation for normative expert systems that act rationally based on decision theory.
