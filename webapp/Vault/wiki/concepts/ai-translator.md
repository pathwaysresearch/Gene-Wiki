# AI Translator

## Definition
The AI Translator is a role designed to mediate between business stakeholders and AI developers. Individuals in this role are described as being quantitatively oriented but are distinct from data scientists. Their primary function is to facilitate effective collaboration and ensure business alignment on AI projects.

## Responsibilities and Impact
The translator ensures that the actual business problem is being specifically addressed by the AI development team. This collaboration allows data scientists more freedom to be experimental with their modeling, confident that the translator is keeping the project aligned with business objectives. The text notes this is an important role that has been fairly widely discussed but not widely implemented.

## Implementation at DBS Bank
DBS Bank is cited as a company that has formally created and staffed the translator role. The bank's chief analytics officer stated that when the two roles collaborate, data scientists can be more experimental. Demonstrating a significant commitment to the role, DBS decided to staff its AI projects with a ratio of one translator for every two data scientists.
