# Age/Career Stage Groups

## Definition
The Age/Career Stage Groups are a classification system used throughout the analysis to segment workers into six distinct cohorts based on their age. This framework facilitates the study of how economic phenomena, such as the adoption of AI, affect individuals at different points in their professional lives.

## Classification Structure
The framework, consistently labeled in the charts (e.g., Figure 1, A29), divides the workforce into the following six specific groups: Early Career 1 (ages 22-25), Early Career 2 (ages 26-30), Developing (ages 31-34), Mid-Career 1 (ages 35-40), Mid-Career 2 (ages 41-49), and Senior (ages 50+). This detailed segmentation allows for a more nuanced analysis than a simple "young vs. old" dichotomy.

## Analytical Use
This classification is a fundamental dimension of the paper's analysis, with nearly every multi-panel chart being a 3x2 grid where each panel corresponds to one of these six age groups (e.g., Figures A12, A17, A23, A28). By examining employment headcount and compensation trends within each group, the research investigates whether the labor market effects of AI are concentrated in specific career stages. For example, Figure 1 explicitly compares normalized headcount trends for these six groups within the "Software Developers" and "Customer Service" occupations.
