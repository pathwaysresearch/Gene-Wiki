# Latent Dirichlet Allocation

## Definition
Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA) is a probabilistic model for analyzing text corpora. It operates on the principle that each document can be viewed as a mixture of multiple underlying topics. In this model, each topic is defined by its own distinct probability distribution over a vocabulary of words.

## Relationship to Other Models
The text describes LDA as an extension and a rationalization of the earlier Latent Semantic Indexing (LSI) model. It is also noted as being related to the multiple-cause mixture model of Sahami et al. (1996).

## Purpose
By modeling documents as combinations of topics, LDA provides a method for discovering the thematic structure within a large collection of texts. This can be achieved without requiring any pre-existing labels or annotations for the documents, making it an unsupervised learning technique.
