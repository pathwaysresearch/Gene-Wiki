# Science of Science

## Overview
The 'science of science' is a research field that analyzes how science gets done. Karim Lakhani's work in this area explores the underlying dynamics of scientific communities, treating them as systems that can be studied and understood. This involves looking at how scientists work together while also vying for resources and recognition.

## Duality of Collaboration and Competition
A core insight from Lakhani's research is the dual nature of scientific work. He describes science as a community endeavor where researchers collaborate, but also as a competitive environment where they compete for grants, lab space, and priority in discoveries. This simultaneous competition and collaboration is a key dynamic that shapes scientific progress.

## Research Application
Lakhani applied frameworks from his PhD thesis, which studied how online communities and contests solve innovation problems, to the scientific establishment at the Harvard Medical School. By observing these researchers, he identified the same patterns of collaboration and competition he had seen in online software developers, demonstrating that this framework was a powerful tool for understanding knowledge creation in different domains.
