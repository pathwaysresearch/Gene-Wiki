# Bayesian Probability

## Definition
Bayesian probability is an interpretation of probability used to represent a "degree of belief" in a proposition. This is in contrast to the frequentist view, which relies on the frequency of repeatable events.

## Core Principle
In this framework, a probability of 1 indicates absolute certainty that a proposition is true, and 0 indicates absolute certainty that it is false. Values between 0 and 1 represent intermediate levels of confidence.

## Applicability
This approach is particularly useful for reasoning about propositions that are not repeatable. For example, a doctor stating a patient has a 40% chance of having the flu is expressing a degree of belief, as the specific patient and their condition cannot be replicated infinitely.
