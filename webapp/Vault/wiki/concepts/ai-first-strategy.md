# AI First Strategy

## Overview

An "AI First" strategy represents a fundamental strategic shift for a company, moving Artificial Intelligence from a peripheral or experimental role to the absolute center of the organization's operating model. It signifies that AI will become the common foundation across virtually every operating process, rather than just being used for a few new products or pilot projects.

## Google's Adoption

Google's CEO, Sundar Pichai, announced the company's shift to an "AI first" strategy at the Google I/O conference on May 17, 2017. This announcement was significant because Google's business had always been driven by data and software, and it had already invested heavily in AI. The declaration signaled a deeper integration of AI into the core of all its operations, capping two decades of investment in software algorithms and AI technologies.

## Core Principle

The central idea of an AI First strategy is to make AI the primary lens through which the company views its operations and future growth. It involves more than just developing AI-inspired products; it means re-architecting the company's processes and models to leverage AI's predictive and learning capabilities at every level, making it the core of how the company functions.
