# Dynamic Nature of the Firm

## Challenging the Stable Firm Assumption
The text critiques the common view of the firm as a stable entity into which AI is simply integrated to perform tasks more efficiently. It argues that this is a fallacy because the organization itself exists within a competitive ecosystem. The firm's structure, purpose, and basis for existence are not fixed but are a response to the rules of that ecosystem.

## AI as an Ecosystem Disruptor
AI is presented as a force that fundamentally changes the "rules of competition" within the business ecosystem. By enabling new business models, creating new competitive tensions (like tool-provider competition), and altering the architecture of work, AI reshapes the entire landscape. When the external environment changes so profoundly, the firm cannot remain stable; its very reason for being must adapt in response.

## The Firm's Evolving Purpose
Because AI alters the competitive ecosystem, the basis of what an organization has to accomplish also changes. The idea of a stable firm becomes nonsensical when the foundational logic of its industry is being rewritten. Therefore, understanding the impact of AI requires an external-in perspective, analyzing how the entire competitive field is shifting and then determining how the firm must evolve to survive and thrive in the new environment.
