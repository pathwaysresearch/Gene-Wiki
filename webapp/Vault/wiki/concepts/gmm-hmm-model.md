# GMM-HMM Model

## Overview
The GMM-HMM model family was the dominant approach for state-of-the-art speech recognition systems from the 1980s until approximately 2009-2012. It combines two distinct statistical models to handle different aspects of the speech signal.

## Component Roles
In this architecture, Gaussian Mixture Models (GMMs) are used to model the association between acoustic features and phonemes (the basic units of sound). The Hidden Markov Models (HMMs) are then used to model the temporal sequence of these phonemes and their discrete sub-phonemic states.

## Generative Process
The GMM-HMM framework treats acoustic waveforms as being generated by a two-stage process. First, an HMM generates a sequence of phonemes and sub-phonemic states. Then, for each state in the sequence, a GMM generates the corresponding acoustic features.
