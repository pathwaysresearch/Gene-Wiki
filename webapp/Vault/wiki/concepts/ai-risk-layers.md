# AI Risk Layers

## Overview
This concept provides a structured map for understanding the complex risk and regulatory landscape for AI in financial services. The framework's goal is to enable pattern recognition across various overlapping regulatory regimes rather than memorization of any single one.

## The Four Layers of Risk
The framework categorizes AI risk into four distinct layers. The first is 'model' risk, concerning the performance and integrity of the AI model itself. The second is 'operational' risk, related to the implementation and functioning of the AI system within business processes. The third is 'compliance' risk, which covers adherence to laws and regulations. The final layer is 'strategic/reputational' risk, which involves potential harm to the firm's brand and market position.

## Application in Governance
This four-layer risk framework is a foundational tool for designing an effective AI governance operating model. By categorizing and assessing risks across these layers, an organization can determine the appropriate approval paths, evidence standards, and escalation architecture for a given AI deployment.
