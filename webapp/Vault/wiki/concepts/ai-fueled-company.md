# AI-Fueled Company

## Overview

An AI-fueled company represents the end state for an organization that has aggressively integrated artificial intelligence into its business. The text describes four alternative paths to becoming AI-fueled, illustrated by examples like Deloitte, CCC Intelligent Solutions, Capital One, and Well.

## Key Characteristics

AI-fueled organizations are defined by several key attributes. They successfully move beyond pilots and proofs of concept to get many AI systems into production deployment, generating economic value. This is a significant challenge, as many companies struggle to deploy any systems at all. Furthermore, being AI-fueled is as much about people as technology; these companies have a high percentage of executives and employees who are fluent in AI and understand how it can be applied to the business.

## The Role of Data

Data is the fuel for AI, and AI-fueled companies are serious about its management—collecting, integrating, storing, and making it broadly accessible. They often undertake major data management projects and modernize their data infrastructure for AI. A critical differentiator for these companies is the possession of unique or proprietary data, which allows them to build machine learning models and achieve outcomes that competitors with access to the same data cannot replicate.
