# Experimentation and Sandboxing for AI

## Definition

Experimentation and sandboxing is a proactive strategy for corporate AI adoption recommended by Karim Lakhani. It involves creating controlled environments ("sandboxes") where employees can freely experiment with AI tools like generative AI to discover practical applications for their work without risking core business operations.

## Recommended Actions

The strategy consists of several concrete actions for business leaders. These include running internal "boot camps" to educate staff, conducting use case analyses to identify where AI can be helpful versus where it might "hallucinate" or produce errors, and providing broad access to AI tools rather than banning them.

## Target Audience and Goal

A key aspect of this approach is its inclusivity. Lakhani stresses that these initiatives should not be limited to technology workers but must be extended to *all* employees. The ultimate goal is to demystify AI, foster a culture of learning and adaptation, and empower the entire workforce to find value in these new technologies, thereby preparing the organization for the "humans with AI" future.
