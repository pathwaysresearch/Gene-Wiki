# Ensemble Methods

## Definition
Ensemble Methods are a way to fine-tune a machine learning system by combining several models that perform best. The core idea is that a group, or "ensemble," of models will often perform better than the best individual model within the group.

## Key Principle
The effectiveness of an ensemble method is particularly high when the individual models make very different types of errors. By combining their predictions, the errors of one model can be offset by the correct predictions of others, leading to a more robust overall system.

## Example
The text provides Random Forests as an example of an ensemble method. A Random Forest model performs better than the individual Decision Trees it is composed of, illustrating the power of combining multiple models to achieve superior performance.
