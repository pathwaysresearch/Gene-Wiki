# AI Industry Reorientation

## The Need for Reorientation

The text argues that realizing the significant potential productivity gains from generative AI requires a fundamental reorientation of the industry. The current priorities of the tech industry are identified as automation and online monetization, such as through search or social media digital ads. This focus is seen as a barrier to achieving more beneficial macroeconomic outcomes, a view supported by the work of Daron Acemoglu.

## Proposed New Focus

A reoriented industry would prioritize developing AI that provides reliable information to increase the marginal productivity of various types of workers. This stands in contrast to the current emphasis on creating general human-like conversational tools. The shift would involve focusing on new tasks that can augment human labor rather than simply automating existing ones.

## Architectural and Institutional Changes

This reorientation may necessitate major architectural changes to common generative AI models, like Large Language Models (LLMs), to better support worker productivity. Furthermore, the text suggests that achieving more beneficial outcomes from AI will also require the development of new institutions, policies, and regulations to guide the technology's deployment and impact.
