# Customer Sentiment Analysis

## Overview
Customer sentiment analysis is a tool used to understand customer opinions from unstructured data. It automates the process of sifting through materials like customer reviews to determine the sentiment expressed, helping a company understand what is going wrong and why from the customer's perspective.

## How It Works
This analysis is powered by natural language processing. The tools process large volumes of text to extract sentiment. Stefano Puntoni notes that these tools have been around for a while and are already very good, with ongoing improvements.

## Purpose in Business
The primary purpose is to distill complex customer feedback into an understandable form, such as a numerical customer sentiment score. This allows businesses to quickly gauge customer attitudes from large datasets. This analytical output can be complemented by generative AI, which can provide more free-flowing summaries of the same data.
