# Mindful Friction

## Definition and Purpose
Mindful friction is the concept of consciously choosing to perform tasks manually rather than outsourcing everything to AI. The primary purpose is to prevent the atrophy of human skills and to keep people aware of what is happening in a process. This ensures that when an AI system fails or encounters an unexpected situation, humans are capable of taking back control effectively. (Chunk 6)

## The Autopilot Analogy
The concept is illustrated by the FAA's guidance to pilots regarding the use of autopilot. The FAA asked pilots to reduce their reliance on automation not because it was unsafe, but out of concern that their core flying skills were degrading. Similarly, society will need to establish guidelines for using AI to get things done while periodically engaging in the tasks ourselves to stay sharp. (Chunk 6)

## Counteracting Total Outsourcing
This approach serves as a direct strategy to mitigate the risks of total outsourcing to machines. It addresses concerns about AI diminishing human moral agency, whether in professional contexts like a judge's decision-making or in personal life skills. By periodically adding this friction, individuals and professionals can maintain their capabilities and agency in an increasingly automated world. (Chunk 6)
