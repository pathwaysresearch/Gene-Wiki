# Automated Visual Inspection

## Overview
Automated visual inspection is an application of AI used in high-tech manufacturing to ensure and improve the quality and efficiency of production processes. At Seagate Technologies, it has been a primary focus of manufacturing analytics, using massive amounts of sensor data to automate quality control.

## Technology and Implementation
The system is based on deep learning image recognition algorithms. In the manufacturing of disk drives at Seagate, multiple microscope images are taken throughout wafer fabrication. These images are fed into auto defect classification models that can detect and classify wafer defects directly from the image. Other models are used to monitor the health of the manufacturing toolsets themselves, for example by detecting out-of-focus electron microscopes to ensure defects are actual and not just out-of-focus images.

## Impact and Deployment
These AI-driven systems have been successfully deployed to improve manufacturing quality. At Seagate, the auto defect classification models were first deployed in late 2017. Since then, the scale and scope of these applications have grown, demonstrating the effectiveness of using AI to automate quality control in complex manufacturing environments.
