# AutoML (Automated Machine Learning)

## Definition and Purpose
AutoML, or Automated Machine Learning, is an approach that automates parts of the machine learning workflow. As implemented at 84.51°, it is part of a redesigned process and culture of machine learning, representing a logical progression from ad hoc modeling to automated processes that generate value through efficiency and improved accuracy. It is used within a broader context that also includes redesigned processes and culture.

## Key Benefits
AutoML provides several benefits to a business's machine learning capabilities. Its initial focus at 84.51° was to improve the productivity of professional data scientists, allowing them to focus on aspects requiring deeper expertise. It also enables the frequent retraining of models based on new data, such as the nightly updates to sales forecasting models that drive inventory management.

## Democratization of Machine Learning
A significant impact of AutoML is the expansion of who can create and apply machine learning models. At 84.51°, AutoML tools allow "insights specialists"—individuals with strong business acumen but less traditional data science training—to perform activities like use case identification and exploratory analyses, and even create models. This democratizes machine learning within the organization.
