# AI Agents

## Definition
AI agents are described as a form of artificial intelligence that can act autonomously to perform tasks. They represent a more advanced stage of AI, moving beyond simple tools to become active participants in work processes and are seen as the next evolution of AI as a "teammate" for knowledge workers.

## Emergence and Creation
A key development highlighted is the democratization of agent creation. The text notes that MBA students at Harvard Business School, with no coding knowledge, were able to use AI to create their own machine learning algorithms and AI agents. This demonstrates that specialized AI tools can be developed and deployed by non-technical users.

## Future Role in Organizations
The text projects that agents will become integral to the workplace, with employees bringing their own custom agents to their jobs. It also speculates on more advanced configurations, including the possibility of an "agent as the boss," where an AI system takes on management and oversight roles.
