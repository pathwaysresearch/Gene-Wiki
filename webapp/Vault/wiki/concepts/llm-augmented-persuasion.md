# LLM-Augmented Persuasion

## Overview
The text describes how Large Language Models (LLMs) can increase the perceived originality and creativity of ideas by augmenting their persuasive communication. A study by Lee and Chung (2024) found that ideas generated with LLM assistance were rated as more creative, an effect mediated by how articulate the expression of the idea was.

## Mechanisms of Persuasion
Recent studies found LLMs to be more persuasive than humans in challenging domains like politics and belief in conspiracy theories. This is attributed to their ability to produce more complex grammatical and lexical structures. Furthermore, one study revealed that LLMs utilize more expansive moral foundations than humans, making their arguments particularly appealing by referencing care-related virtues, fairness, authority, and sanctity.

## Advanced Capabilities
Beyond general argumentation, LLMs are capable of personalizing persuasive messages to the characteristics of the recipient. They can also generate summaries of scientific papers for a general audience that are clearer, less complex, and more understandable than summaries written by humans, showcasing their advanced communicative abilities.
