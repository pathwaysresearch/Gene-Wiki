# AI Strategy

## Importance for AI Leaders
According to the Deloitte 2021 “State of AI in the Enterprise” survey, having a formal AI strategy is a typical characteristic of companies that are most advanced with AI. These leading companies, categorized as "Transformers," are significantly more likely to have an enterprisewide AI strategy compared to less mature organizations, with 60% of them agreeing they have one.

## Key Components
A strong AI strategy involves senior leaders articulating and communicating a clear vision for how AI will fundamentally change business operations. This leadership vision is crucial for aligning initiatives and driving adoption across the enterprise, and it is another area where AI leaders significantly outperform their peers.

## Role in Competitive Differentiation
The presence of a clear AI strategy is closely linked to competitive advantage. The Deloitte survey found that companies with mature AI practices were far more likely to agree that their use of AI differentiates them from their competitors. This suggests that a deliberate, enterprise-level strategy is key to translating AI capabilities into a sustainable competitive edge.
