# Team Rituals

## Overview
Team rituals are unique, structured processes that great teams employ to foster a specific and effective culture. The concept is the subject of a forthcoming book, "Rituals of Great Teams," which aims to codify these practices for broader adoption. The interest in this topic is described as a long-standing "obsession" that originated from conversations about what makes high-performing teams successful.

## Purpose and Importance
The primary purpose of team rituals is to address common dysfunctions in team dynamics. These include meetings where one person's voice dominates the discussion or decision-making processes that are easily swayed by social bias and groupthink. By implementing structured rituals, teams can ensure that discussions are focused on topics of the most interest to the group and that decisions reflect the independent, unbiased views of all members.

## Cultural Significance
Effective rituals become deeply embedded in a company's culture and identity. A particularly powerful process can become known as a "golden ritual" that employees discuss with excitement. This indicates that the ritual is not just a mechanical process but a valued part of the organization's way of working, reinforcing its culture and principles.
