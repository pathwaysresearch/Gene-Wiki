# Perception as a Construction

## Core Idea
Neuroscientist Anil Seth explains that the world we experience is a form of "construction" created by the brain. He clarifies that this does not mean it is arbitrary, but rather that it is a model or representation that is "tied very closely to real things happening in the world and in our bodies." The brain builds this experience from the sensory information it receives.

## Avoiding Misconceptions
The text cautions against taking metaphors like an "inner projector screen" or a "picture" in the head too literally. The process of how the brain creates this experience of a world and a self is more complex than a simple internal representation.

## Relation to Consciousness
This concept is presented as part of the wider question of consciousness. It addresses the "contents" of consciousness—how the brain generates our experience of a world and a self—which is a distinct but related problem to the "hard problem" of why we have subjective experience at all.
