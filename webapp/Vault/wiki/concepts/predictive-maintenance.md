# Predictive Maintenance

## Definition
Predictive maintenance is a technique for predicting when equipment will degrade in performance or break down. This approach contrasts with traditional methods like performing maintenance at standard, fixed intervals or reactive maintenance, which involves waiting for equipment to fail before taking action.

## Application at Shell
The energy company Shell employs predictive maintenance as a key AI application to manage its vast array of equipment. Given that Shell has hundreds of thousands of pieces of equipment across its global facilities, this technique is crucial for achieving impact at scale in its maintenance processes.

## Business Impact
Shell executives are convinced that using predictive maintenance can make their equipment more reliable and maintenance processes more efficient. By anticipating failures, the company can perform maintenance more effectively, which helps to avoid costly downtime and improve overall operational effectiveness.
