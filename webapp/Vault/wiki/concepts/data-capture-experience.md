# Data Capture Experience

## Definition
The Data Capture Experience is a core component of the broader Consumer AI Experience framework. It pertains to consumer interactions with the data collection and storage elements of AI systems, which correspond to the AI's capability of "listening" to or gathering information from its environment, such as through product sensors or wearable devices.

## Core Psychological Tension
This experience is defined by an underlying tension between two opposing feelings. On one hand, consumers recognize that data capture allows AI to serve them effectively through customization and personalized services. On the other hand, the inherent lack of transparency in how AI systems collect and use data can make consumers feel exploited. This feeling is fueled by a perceived or actual loss of personal control over their information.

## Psychological Consequences
The feeling of being exploited due to a loss of personal control can have significant psychological consequences. The text notes that these can include negative affect, which may escalate into demotivation and helplessness. As being in control is a fundamental human need, this loss can also trigger moral outrage. The article provides examples, such as a sex worker whose identity was threatened by Facebook's recommendations and a consumer who received another person's Alexa voice command transcripts, to illustrate how privacy invasion can be frightening and damaging.
