# Association Rule Mining

## Definition

Association rule mining is a broad category of unsupervised learning where algorithms look for the frequency and probability of co-occurrence among any set of items. Based on this analysis, the algorithms create associations that are likely to occur between various types of products or items.

## How It Works

The technique analyzes datasets, such as the contents of an online shopping cart, to identify strong relationships. For example, it can determine that customers who buy one item are also likely to buy another specific item.

## Applications and Examples

The text highlights Amazon's scientific use of association rule mining for product recommendations. Another key example is Ocado, which learned from its data about a strong relationship between diapers and beer, leading to a profitable recommendation strategy that also increased customer satisfaction among new parents.
