# AI for Ideation and Creativity

## Core Principle
This application of AI centers on leveraging the technology to improve the "effectiveness piece" of work. The goal is to create "better work," "better products," and "better ideas," rather than focusing solely on cost-cutting measures that produce "cheaper work." It represents a strategic shift towards using AI for value creation and innovation.

## Application in Brainstorming
According to Stefano Puntoni, a key use case for AI in this domain is for brainstorming and ideation. The fact that people are already using AI for these tasks suggests a widespread recognition of its potential to serve as a creative partner. It can help individuals and teams come up with new concepts and push the boundaries of their thinking.

## Strategic Significance
Using AI for creativity serves as a powerful example of the "AI as a Complementary Technology" framework. It showcases how AI can augment high-level human cognitive functions, fostering a positive narrative around AI adoption. This application is a focus in business education, such as in Puntoni's course at Wharton, to prepare future leaders to think about AI beyond simple automation.
