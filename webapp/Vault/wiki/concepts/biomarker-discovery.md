# Biomarker Discovery

## Overview
Biomarker discovery is the process of finding biomarkers, which are defined as detectable substances that indicate the presence of a disease or medical condition. The text characterizes this search as a "fishing expedition," which can be challenging due to the vast amount of data involved. (Chunk 49)

## Role of Machine Learning
Machine learning plays a crucial role in modern biomarker discovery by allowing researchers to find individual and in-combination biomarkers much more quickly and easily. In medical fields like cancer research, where voluminous data is available, machine learning algorithms can efficiently sift through the data to identify potential biomarkers that might otherwise be missed. (Chunk 49)

## Future Developments
AI is expected to further advance this field. The text suggests that new AI algorithms capable of predicting patterns of protein folding are likely to be used to generate entirely new types of biomarkers, expanding the potential for early disease detection and diagnosis. (Chunk 49)
