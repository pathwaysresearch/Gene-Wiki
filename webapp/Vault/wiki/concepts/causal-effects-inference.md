# Causal Effects Inference

## Definition
Causal effects inference is a type of machine learning model focused on making counterfactual predictions. As described by Well's CTO Oz Ataman, it analyzes what might happen under alternative scenarios, going beyond traditional predictive modeling.

## Application at Well
The company Well employs a mix of traditional predictive machine learning and causal effects inference. This approach is necessary because the company makes recommendations for multiple health interventions over time and needs to plan a sequence of actions to influence member behavior.

## Purpose
The primary purpose of using causal effects inference at Well is to plan a series of clinical content messages, such as recommendations, relevant articles, and "twenty-one-day journeys." The models help determine the sequence of interventions most likely to produce a desired behavioral change in a member.
