# AI Adoption

## Overview
AI adoption is the integration of artificial intelligence technologies to reshape how organizations operate, compete, and create value. It impacts areas from automation and data analysis to strategy and decision-making. The provided text emphasizes that leaders should experiment, build AI fluency, and take action to integrate these tools, as waiting is not a viable strategy.

## Risks of Ignoring Adoption
According to Professor Karim Lakhani, organizations that resist or delay AI adoption risk being left behind, similar to historical instances of resistance to technological change. This failure to adopt can lead to missed opportunities to improve how teams work, collaborate, and innovate. Over time, this inaction widens the competitive gap, stalling innovation, weakening competitive advantage, and limiting an organization's long-term growth.

## Importance for Leaders
It is presented as critical for leaders to understand AI tools and the potential they can unlock. Failing to do so prevents an organization from realizing the full potential of emerging technologies. Leaders are urged to take action to build AI fluency within their organizations to stay competitive.
