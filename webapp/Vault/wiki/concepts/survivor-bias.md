# Survivor Bias

## Definition
Survivor bias is a statistical issue that can arise in longitudinal studies of entities like firms. It occurs when the sample under analysis excludes entities that did not survive the entire period of observation, leading to a dataset that is skewed towards successful or resilient examples.

## Context in Firm Growth Studies
The text identifies survivor bias as a potential sampling pressure when extending the analysis to understand later-stage firm growth. This is because the sample for this part of the study includes firms with available owner and revenue data, which may inherently exclude firms that have since ceased operations.

## Implications for Research Findings
The acknowledgment of potential survivor bias serves as a crucial caveat for the study's conclusions.
