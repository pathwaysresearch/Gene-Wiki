# Reinforcement Learning

## Definition

Reinforcement learning is an approach to AI where a software agent learns by interacting with an environment. The agent is programmed to figure out the best approaches to a problem on its own, rather than being explicitly programmed or fed labeled data.

## Key Example: AlphaGo Zero

The text highlights AlphaGo Zero as a prime example of the reinforcement learning approach. Unlike its predecessor AlphaGo, which was trained on data from hundreds of thousands of human games, AlphaGo Zero was essentially given only the rules of the game of Go and learned to master it by playing against itself. The "Zero" in its name stands for this lack of external data.

## Significance and Impact

The success of reinforcement learning, particularly through AlphaGo's victories, has captured public attention. According to computer scientist Kai-Fu Lee, these results were so stunning that they prompted the Chinese government to declare a "Sputnik moment" and make achieving world-class leadership in AI a national priority.
