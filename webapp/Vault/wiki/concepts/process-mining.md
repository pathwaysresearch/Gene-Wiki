# Process Mining

## Definition

Process mining is a new technology that employs artificial intelligence to facilitate dramatic improvements in business processes. It is designed to analyze data from enterprise transactional systems to gain a clear understanding of how a process is actually being performed within an organization.

## How It Works

After analyzing transactional data to map out the existing process, process mining technology uses AI to make recommendations for improvement. This approach takes a lot of the detailed, manual work out of traditional process improvement efforts.

## Business Application

Process mining is described as catching on rapidly in many process-oriented companies. It is a key enabler for companies seeking to move beyond incremental performance gains and achieve dramatic improvements by reengineering business processes with AI.
