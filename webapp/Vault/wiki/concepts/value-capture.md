# Value Capture

## Overview
Value capture refers to the ability of a business to appropriate a portion of the value it creates for its user network. In the digital economy, options for value capture have expanded dramatically due to technologies that allow for sophisticated pricing, usage metering, and outcome-based models. Optimizing value capture involves a combination of economic analysis, strategic thinking, and technological capability.

## Key Considerations
The appropriability of value is a function of several factors, including the existence of competitive solutions and the customer's willingness to pay. On multisided platforms, a common strategy is to adjust pricing to charge the side of the network that has the least competition and the highest willingness to pay. This is why search engines charge advertisers rather than end users.

## Role of Network Effects
Network effects open new types of value capture options. By building a critical mass of users on one network, a firm can leverage that user base and the associated data to capture value on new and different networks, a strategy known as bridging. This is a fundamental reason why hub firms like Amazon and Alibaba expand into many different markets.
