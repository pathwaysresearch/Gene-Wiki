# Image-Based Estimating

## Overview
Image-based estimating represents a major technological shift in the auto insurance industry, moving from manual damage assessment by human adjusters to an AI-powered analysis of photos. CCC Intelligent Solutions has pioneered this capability, illustrating the company's transition from a data-oriented to an AI-oriented business.

## Technological Evolution
The journey to this technology was a long-term bet. It began with a vision from CCC's leader, Githesh Ramamurthy, who noticed the rapid improvement in amateur and smartphone cameras. He envisioned a time when vehicle owners could take their own photos to initiate a claim, a departure from the historical reliance on professional adjusters with specialized cameras.

## AI Implementation
The breakthrough came with the application of deep learning neural networks for image analysis. CCC leveraged its historical database of billions of images to train these models. The company invested in graphics processing units (GPUs) to accelerate the analysis, eventually developing a system capable of equaling or exceeding human capabilities in damage assessment.
