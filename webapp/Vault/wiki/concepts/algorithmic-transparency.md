# Algorithmic Transparency

## Role in Building Trust
Transparency plays a crucial role in building trust between users and algorithms. It involves informing users about what an algorithm is doing and, importantly, why it is making certain decisions or taking specific actions.

## Impact on User Engagement
When users are aware of an algorithm's processes, they are more likely to engage with the system positively. Understanding the rationale behind algorithmic actions helps to demystify the technology and fosters a better, more trusting user-system relationship.

## Growing Significance
As artificial intelligence gains a more influential role in decision-making across various fields, the need for transparency becomes increasingly vital. It is essential for users to understand the logic behind algorithmic decisions that affect their lives.
