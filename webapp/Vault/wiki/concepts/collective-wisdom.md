# Collective Wisdom

## The Need for Collective Wisdom
In the age of AI, new operating models are binding firms together across industries, countries, and markets, creating complex interdependencies. The text argues that these interdependencies have become much too important to ignore and are motivating the need for a new kind of collective wisdom.

## The Role of Digital Firms
As digital firms use AI to reduce human friction and remove traditional internal bottlenecks, the complex interrelationships across communities and organizations become more crucial. This shift makes collective dynamics and their impact on society increasingly important to the performance of these firms.

## Risks of Collective Failure
The text highlights that a new kind of sudden, collective failure can be the main constraint left on digital firms. Examples cited include the destruction of value from fake news and privacy crises at Facebook and Twitter, and massive data breaches at Equifax and Yahoo!, which demonstrate the broad impact these firms have on society.
