# Work Flows

## Deconstruction and Analysis
The book advocates for deconstructing work flows into their component tasks to identify opportunities for AI integration (123–131). This task-level analysis (125–131) allows organizations to pinpoint which specific parts of a job are prediction tasks and can therefore be augmented or automated by AI.

## Impact of AI Tools
The introduction of AI prediction tools has a direct impact on established work flows (126–129). Rather than automating an entire job, AI tools typically automate a specific prediction task within a larger process, requiring the rest of the work flow to be adapted around this new capability.

## Job and Process Redesign
This task-level impact necessitates the redesign of jobs and processes (142–145). The text uses the design of the iPhone keyboard as an example of how a tool can fundamentally alter a common task (typing) within a broader work flow (communication), leading to new behaviors and capabilities (129–130).
