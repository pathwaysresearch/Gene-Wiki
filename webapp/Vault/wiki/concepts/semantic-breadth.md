# Semantic Breadth

## Definition
Semantic breadth refers to the ability of Large Language Models (LLMs) to generate a diverse range of ideas by connecting disparate knowledge categories. This capability is presented as being akin to "flexibility" in human creativity, a process where connecting distinct knowledge areas promotes originality.

## Mechanism for Creativity
According to the text, originality is often perceived when conceptually distant ideas are combined, such as creating a cufflink from bike chain parts. This process relies on surveying a broad range of content categories, switching between them, and harnessing associations between remote ideas. LLMs achieve this by leveraging their vast training data to make these novel connections.

## Enhancing Semantic Breadth
Researchers can increase an LLM's semantic breadth through techniques like prompt variation. For example, prompting an LLM to think like a specific creative personality, such as Steve Jobs, was found to produce more diverse product ideas than a base prompt alone. Adjusting the model's "temperature" parameter is another method, though it can also increase factual inaccuracies.
