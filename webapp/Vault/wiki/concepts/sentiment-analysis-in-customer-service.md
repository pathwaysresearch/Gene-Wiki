# Sentiment Analysis in Customer Service

## Definition and Application
Sentiment analysis is used in this context to quantify the emotional tone of customer and agent interactions. It serves as a key performance indicator to measure the impact of deploying a new AI assistance model on the quality of the work experience for both customers and employees.

## Measurement Methodology
The text specifies that sentiment is measured using SIEBERT, which is a fine-tuned checkpoint of a ROBERTA English language transformer model. This indicates a sophisticated, model-based approach to capturing sentiment from chat logs or other text-based interactions.

## Key Findings
The analysis reveals that the AI model's deployment has a measurable impact on both average customer sentiment and average agent sentiment. These impacts are not uniform and are further analyzed by breaking them down according to agent characteristics like pre-existing productivity and tenure, showing how the technology's effects can differ across a workforce.
