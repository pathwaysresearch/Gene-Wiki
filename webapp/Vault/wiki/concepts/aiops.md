# AIOps

## Definition
AIOps, a term that has more recently replaced IT autonomics, involves the application of artificial intelligence to IT operations. It utilizes software and IT device data to identify potential problem areas and to automate various aspects of IT management and maintenance.

## How It Works
The core function of AIOps is to leverage AI and automation to proactively manage IT infrastructure. These systems can predict and diagnose problems within networks and servers before they cause significant disruptions. Following a diagnosis, automation programs can be triggered to restore the affected systems to a healthy state, shortening repair times and preventing outages.

## Role and Impact
For organizations heavily dependent on their IT and digital capabilities, AIOps has become a critical function for ensuring resource availability. While it has not entirely replaced human IT operators, it has been instrumental in managing the increasing complexity and scale of IT environments, helping to limit the growth of IT operations jobs to a reasonable level. The aerospace company Airbus, for example, uses AIOps to monitor its hundreds of thousands of IT devices to predict and prevent equipment outages that could interrupt production.
