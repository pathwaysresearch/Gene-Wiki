# Missing Links in Automation

## Definition
Missing links in automation are small but critical tasks within a workflow that are fundamentally difficult to automate. The failure to automate even one such task can derail the entire effort to fully automate a job or process, analogous to how a single failed O-ring seal caused the Space Shuttle Challenger disaster. AI tools are unlikely to catalyze full automation on their own because of these unavoidable steps.

## Impact on Job Reformulation
The existence of these missing links fundamentally constrains how jobs can be reformulated. To automate a task completely, every step must be considered, and these small, difficult tasks can prevent the full automation of an entire job. Consequently, AI tools that successfully address one of these missing links can have a substantive and disproportionate effect on a workflow.

## Example in Fulfillment
The text presents the "grasping problem" in the e-commerce fulfillment industry as a key example of a missing link. While many aspects of fulfillment have been automated, the task of a robot reliably picking diverse items from a bin remains a challenge. This single task prevents the end-to-end automation of the fulfillment workflow.
