# Knowledge Representation

## Definition
Knowledge representation is the study of how to put knowledge into a form that a computer can use to reason. It is a foundational aspect of building intelligent systems that can understand and operate in the world.

## Connection to Language
Much of the early work in knowledge representation was closely tied to the field of computational linguistics and natural language processing. The challenge of understanding language revealed that it requires not just understanding sentence structure, but also having knowledge of the subject matter and context.

## Intellectual Roots
Research in knowledge representation was informed by work in linguistics, which in turn was connected to decades of work on the philosophical analysis of language. This highlights the interdisciplinary nature of the problem.
