# Explainability

## The Need for Explainability

The unpredictability inherent in advanced, resilient AI systems necessitates a focus on explainability. As these algorithms are increasingly used to make critical life decisions in fields like healthcare, it becomes crucial for users and stakeholders to understand how and why they arrive at their conclusions to build trust and ensure accountability.

## Connection to AI Design Paradoxes

Explainability is a direct challenge posed by the resilient side of the Predictability-Resilience Paradox. While machine learning models like AlphaGo can achieve incredible results, their complex, non-linear nature makes their decision-making processes opaque. This contrasts with simpler, rule-based systems that are inherently more transparent but less powerful.

## Fostering Trust in Black Box Systems

The text emphasizes that effective communication about how algorithms work is essential for fostering public trust. Achieving transparency is a key component of explainability and is presented as a way to look "Inside the Black Box." Without it, trusting these complex systems becomes a significant challenge for society.
