# AI Assistance in the Workplace

## Overview
The text analyzes an AI tool that provides real-time suggestions to customer service agents during live chats. This assistance is designed to help agents resolve customer issues more effectively by identifying patterns in data to replicate the behaviors of various workers, including those in non-routine or knowledge-based tasks. The AI functions as a form of real-time coaching, offering specific and actionable suggestions that may address limitations of traditional management coaching.

## Impact on Productivity
The deployment of the AI model leads to significant productivity gains. The study finds a 3.7 minute (8.5%) decrease in average chat duration and a 0.37 unit (15%) increase in the number of chats an agent can handle per hour. These improvements suggest the AI enables agents to both speed up individual chats and multitask more effectively. These gains are achieved without a significant negative impact on customer satisfaction or problem resolution rates, which remain high.

## Mechanism of Action
The AI assistance helps workers, particularly lower-skilled ones, learn and adopt best practices derived from the communication patterns of higher-skilled and more experienced agents. By analyzing conversation text, the study finds that the similarity of conversations between high- and low-skill agents increases after AI adoption. The greatest productivity gains occur not necessarily where the AI is most capable in absolute terms (e.g., routine problems), but where its capabilities most effectively complement or exceed those of the human workers.
