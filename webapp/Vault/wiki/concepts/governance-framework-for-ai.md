# Governance Framework for AI

## Definition
A governance framework for AI, as described in the text, is a structured approach for overseeing automated decisions to address the risks and challenges arising from machine learning. Kartik Hosanagar states that this framework is a central theme of his book and his presentation.

## Target Audiences
The framework is designed for multiple stakeholders. It provides guidance for individuals navigating a world with automated decisions, for companies on how to responsibly deploy these systems, and for regulators seeking to create appropriate oversight.

## Purpose
The primary purpose of the governance framework is to manage the risks associated with machine decisions. It is presented as a necessary tool for ensuring that the opportunities of AI are realized while mitigating potential negative consequences for consumers, businesses, and society at large.
