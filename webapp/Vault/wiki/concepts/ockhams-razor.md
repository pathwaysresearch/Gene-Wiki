# Ockham's Razor

## Definition
Ockham's razor is a principle that suggests choosing the simplest hypothesis that is consistent with the available examples. This principle is a cornerstone of inductive learning.

## Application in Machine Learning
In machine learning, Ockham's razor guides algorithms to prefer simpler models to avoid overfitting and improve generalization. For instance, the information-gain heuristic used in constructing decision trees is an efficient method for finding a simple, consistent tree that adheres to this principle.

## Formalization
The study of Kolmogorov complexity, or algorithmic complexity, developed by Ray Solomonoff and Andrey Kolmogorov, is an attempt to provide a formal, mathematical definition for the notion of simplicity that is central to Ockham's razor.
