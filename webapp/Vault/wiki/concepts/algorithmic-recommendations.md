# Algorithmic Recommendations

## Definition
Algorithmic recommendations are suggestions generated by automated systems to influence user choices. The text provides the example of e-commerce sites showing messages like "people who viewed this eventually bought that product" or "people who bought this also bought these other products" to guide purchasing decisions.

## Mechanism of Influence
These recommendations are presented as a common and long-standing example of how algorithms drive decisions in everyday life. They work by subtly influencing the choices people make, shaping their behavior and purchasing habits on platforms where they are deployed.

## Quantified Impact
The text highlights the significant power of these systems by citing a study related to Amazon.com. According to this study, over one-third of all purchasing decisions on the platform originate from its algorithmic recommendations, demonstrating their substantial and measurable impact on consumer behavior.
