# Durable Skills

## Definition
Durable skills are abilities that are not easily automated and do not quickly become obsolete, in contrast to perishable technical skills. The text identifies them as capabilities that humans can uniquely perform, such as trust, team building, and strategic thinking.

## Corporate Demand
The excerpts highlight a significant and growing demand for durable skills from large corporations, particularly Fortune 500 companies. This demand exists alongside the need for technical AI skills, indicating that companies are seeking a workforce with a balanced skill set that combines technical proficiency with uniquely human strengths.

## Application in the Modern Workforce
As AI takes over more content-based and technical tasks, durable skills become more critical for navigating complex business challenges. The ability to apply strategic thinking, foster collaboration, and build trust within teams are presented as essential for workers to remain relevant and effective in an AI-integrated workplace.
