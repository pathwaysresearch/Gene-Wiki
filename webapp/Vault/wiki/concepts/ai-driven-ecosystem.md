# AI-driven Ecosystem

## Definition
An AI-driven ecosystem is a business model where companies, including those in legacy industries, collaborate to create platforms for growth, data gathering, and customer acquisition. These ecosystems are characterized by data sharing, integration, and the joint development of AI applications to analyze the collective data.

## Operating Model
These ecosystems often operate on collaborative platforms. The Open AI Energy Initiative, for example, uses a "barter style" arrangement where partners contribute AI applications in a fair value exchange. Data sharing is a core component, often facilitated by standardized data models to solve complex industry problems. The goal is often to create a shared resource, described in one case as an "app store for the process industry."

## Examples
The text provides several examples of companies pursuing this model. Airbus launched the Skywise platform with Palantir Technologies for the aviation industry. Shell, C3.AI, Microsoft, and Baker Hughes are partners in the Open AI Energy Initiative. These examples show that companies are in the process of exploring the business and revenue models for these collaborative ecosystems.
