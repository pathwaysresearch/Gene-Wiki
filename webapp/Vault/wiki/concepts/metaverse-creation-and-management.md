# Metaverse Creation and Management

## Definition
The metaverse is described as an immersive virtual environment designed for a wide range of activities, including entertainment, gaming, education, and simulation. The text notes that many companies are beginning to pursue the development of such environments. (Chunk 53)

## The Role of AI
AI is positioned to play an important and multifaceted role in both the creation and ongoing management of the metaverse. Its functions will be integral to building the complex systems that make up these virtual worlds. (Chunk 53)

## Key AI Functions
Specific roles for AI in the metaverse include the automated construction of visual images, video, and language. Beyond content generation, AI will be used for critical functions such as identity determination and the prediction of action and movement within the virtual space. Meta (formerly Facebook) has described many of these AI-driven components. (Chunk 53)
