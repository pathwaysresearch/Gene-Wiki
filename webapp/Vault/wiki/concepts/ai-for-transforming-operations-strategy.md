# AI for Transforming Operations (Strategy)

## Definition
Presented as a "Strategy Archetype," transforming operations with AI involves using artificial intelligence to make existing and well-defined business strategies much more successful. This approach focuses on enhancing current processes rather than creating entirely new business models or entering new markets.

## Application Areas
The text suggests that this strategy can be applied across various business functions. Examples include using AI to help supply chain managers ensure on-time delivery, marketers to better persuade customers to buy, salespeople to identify willing customers, and HR managers to hire the right types of people.

## Example: The Kroger Co.
The Kroger Co. is provided as a key example of this strategy in action. Through its "Restock Kroger" initiative, the company heavily utilized analytics and AI via its 84.51° subsidiary to "Redefine the Food and Grocery Customer Experience." This demonstrates a focus on using AI to make its core grocery business more efficient and effective.
