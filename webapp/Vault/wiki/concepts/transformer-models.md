# Transformer Models

## Definition
Transformer models are a type of neural network architecture that has become the foundation for most modern large language models. According to Karthik Hosanagar, their introduction was the key technical change that led to the recent significant advancements and takeoff in AI capabilities.

## Key Innovation: Attention Layers
The core innovation of the Transformer architecture is its use of "attention layers." Hosanagar explains that the fundamental idea behind attention is that the meaning of a word is heavily dependent on its surrounding context. For example, the word "model" has a different meaning when preceded by "fashion" versus "mathematical." Attention mechanisms allow the model to weigh the influence of different words in the input text when processing any given word, which was a significant improvement over previous sequential methods like RNNs.

## Impact and Evolution
The architecture was introduced in the 2017 paper "Attention is All You Need." Its application in models like BERT and the GPT series (e.g., GPT-2, GPT-3) led to a dramatic improvement in text generation and understanding compared to the RNN-based methods that were popular before. This shift made complex tasks, such as writing a coherent book chapter, possible for AI systems for the first time around 2020.
