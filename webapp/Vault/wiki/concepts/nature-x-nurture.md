# Nature × Nurture

## Definition
The concept of Nature × Nurture represents the scientific consensus that neither genetics (nature) nor our environment (nurture) is solely responsible for our traits. It moves beyond a simple "nature vs. nurture" debate.

## Interactional Framework
This framework emphasizes that traits are a product not just of both factors, but of the complex interactions between the two. This integrated understanding is crucial for fields that study human behavior.

## Interdisciplinary Relevance
The text brings up this concept in the context of understanding human behavior, suggesting that solving complex problems related to technology and society is an interdisciplinary problem. It requires insights from psychologists and social scientists, who are grounded in concepts like Nature × Nurture.
