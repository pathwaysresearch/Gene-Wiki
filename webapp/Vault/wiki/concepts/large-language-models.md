# Large Language Models

## Definition and Function
Large Language Models (LLMs) are a form of artificial intelligence that users can employ to automate elements of various tasks, such as writing. They are a key component in the increasing automation of consumption activities and are integrated into productivity tools like ChatGPT.

## Educational Resources
The provided text highlights Andrej Karpathy's YouTube video, 'Let's Build GPT from Scratch', as the most cited technical explainer of LLMs. This resource is noted for demystifying the models at the level of matrix math and gradient descent, making it a transformative learning tool for data engineers. The DeepLearning.AI YouTube channel also offers short courses on LLMs.

## Impact on Digital Consumption
The integration of LLMs into the digital consumption landscape is viewed as a significant development with both positive and negative implications. While they can enhance productivity, their use is also associated with the risk of narrowing human experience and imposing experiential constraints on consumers, as explored through the concept of "possibility space."
