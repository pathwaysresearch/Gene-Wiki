# Collaborative Learning Model

## Definition
As described by Professor Karim Lakhani, the collaborative participants in a learning model is an educational approach where students are not passive recipients of information. Instead, they are considered to have a wealth of experience that is integral to the learning process, and they are expected to bring what they know into the classroom.

## How It Works
In this model, the course is designed to facilitate students bringing their own knowledge into the classroom. This is achieved through various forms of engagement where students are prompted to share what is happening in their industries and what different perspectives they are seeing. The instructor presents ideas, and students are encouraged to challenge them based on their practical experience, creating a dynamic exchange.

## Key Benefits
This model allows for the rapid updating of knowledge within the classroom, as students' current industry experiences can inform and challenge established academic concepts. Professor Lakhani notes that his best experiences have come from this dynamic, where students provide critical feedback on why an idea might not work in practice, which he finds highly valuable for the learning environment.
