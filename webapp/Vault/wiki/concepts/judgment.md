# Judgment

## Definition
In the context of decision making, judgment is defined as "the skill used to determine a payoff, utility, reward, or profit." It is the component of a decision that assigns value to various outcomes, a task distinct from the predictive process of estimating the likelihood of those outcomes.

## Relationship with Prediction
Judgment is presented as a crucial complement to prediction in the decision-making process. While prediction reduces uncertainty by providing information, judgment is required to weigh the consequences of actions based on that prediction. The authors note that the AI community has historically neglected the importance of judgment.

## Increasing Economic Value
The book argues that one of the most significant implications of prediction machines is that they increase the value of human judgment. As AI automates and cheapens the task of prediction, the uniquely human ability to determine what to do with those predictions—to assess payoffs and define objectives—becomes more critical and economically valuable.
