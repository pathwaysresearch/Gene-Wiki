# Changing Role of the CMO

## From Brand Growth to Company Growth

The role of the Chief Marketing Officer (CMO) has been undergoing a significant transformation, a trend that predates the recent surge in AI. The responsibilities have expanded beyond the traditional silo of brand management to encompass the broader goal of driving overall company growth. This shift has elevated the CMO's position, giving their voice more weight within the C-suite and making their strategic input essential to the entire enterprise.

## AI as an Accelerator and Unifier

AI is acting as a powerful catalyst in this evolution. It provides the technological means to create a unified customer experience across every facet of the business, breaking down the silos between marketing, commerce, sales, and product development. For a CMO, AI offers the tools to orchestrate a cohesive brand presence that is consistent at every touchpoint, which is a modern business necessity.

## Exposing Organizational Challenges

While there is great enthusiasm for leveraging AI, its implementation also serves as a diagnostic tool that exposes an organization's readiness for change. Conversations about AI quickly reveal internal challenges, such as whether a company's data is clean and accessible, or if its departments remain fragmented and siloed. A key part of the modern CMO's role is to address these foundational issues to unlock the full potential of AI and achieve a truly integrated customer strategy.
