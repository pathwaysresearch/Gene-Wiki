# Possibility Space

## Definition
The concept of "possibility space" is central to the discussion by Valenzuela and colleagues (2024) on the psychological tensions consumers face with AI. It refers to the scope of human experience, which the pervasive use of AI could inadvertently narrow by imposing experiential constraints.

## Mechanisms of Constraint
The text outlines three specific mechanisms through which AI can constrain a consumer's possibility space. The first is **agency transference**, where AI's role in decision-making restricts a person's exercise of their own agency. The second is **parametric reductionism**, which involves AI reducing human complexity into quantifiable metrics. The third is **regulated expression**, referring to the limits on human expression that arise from the design choices and technological limitations of human-AI interactions.

## Research Implications
The authors who introduce this concept call for a multidisciplinary and critical examination of how AI technologies interact with consumers. They emphasize the need to ensure that AI empowers consumers rather than limiting their personal development and expressive range, highlighting this as a less-explored but significant risk of AI integration.
