# Human-AI Interaction Models

## The "Human OR Machine" Mindset
This approach has historically dominated the discussion around AI, focusing on how to take the human out of the equation. In this model, machine learning algorithms are often designed to mimic a human decision process so that the task can be done instead of by the human. The development of self-driving cars, where an algorithm learns by recording and copying a human driver, exemplifies this replacement-oriented mindset.

## The "Human AND Machine" Mindset
This alternative mindset advocates for a shift in focus towards collaboration. The goal is not to replace the human, but to leverage the unique and complementary capabilities of both algorithms and humans to improve business processes. This approach seeks to make the human more effective, inspired, and productive by using AI as a tool for augmentation rather than substitution.

## Implications for Business and Society
Adopting the "Human AND Machine" framework requires a change in how AI is developed and deployed. Instead of focusing solely on automation for the sake of removing human labor, the emphasis should be on creating synergies. This collaborative approach aims to enhance human work, making it more effective and meaningful, thereby improving overall business outcomes and processes.
