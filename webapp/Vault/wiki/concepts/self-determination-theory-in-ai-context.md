# Self-Determination Theory (in AI Context)

## Definition
Self-Determination Theory is a classic psychology framework which posits that human well-being emerges from three fundamental psychological experiences: competence, autonomy, and connectedness. In the context of artificial intelligence, Professor Stefano Puntoni and his colleagues leverage this theory to structure their research and create a framework for understanding how workers react to the integration of generative AI into their professional lives.

## Application to Generative AI
The framework is applied to analyze how generative AI can serve as either a boost or a detriment to each of the three core sources of well-being. The theory provides a lens to explore the multifaceted psychological impact of AI on employees, moving beyond simple metrics of productivity to consider deeper effects on their sense of self and professional satisfaction. Puntoni's research uses this model to tackle different streams of inquiry corresponding to each of the three pillars.

## Impact on Worker Competence
A key application of the theory is in exploring how workers react when an AI system can competently perform significant parts of a job they have invested their identity and career in developing. This directly challenges an individual's sense of competence, a core component of well-being according to the theory. This challenge can manifest as a significant psychological or even an identity threat, forcing individuals to reconsider the value of their hard-won expertise.
