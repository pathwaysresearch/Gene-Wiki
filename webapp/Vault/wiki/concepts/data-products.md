# Data Products

## Definition
Data products are offerings that combine data with analytical or AI models. They represent a strategic shift in focus within the data supply chain, moving beyond the traditional emphasis on data capture, collection, and storage to focus on the consumption and application of data.

## Purpose and Use
These products are created for consumption by either internal users within a company or by external customers. They package data and intelligence together in a single offering to deliver a specific outcome, insight, or capability, rather than just providing raw data.

## Strategic Context
The emphasis on creating data products is a characteristic of modern data strategies for AI-fueled organizations. It reflects a move towards the latter steps of the data supply chain that involve direct value creation and consumption, marking a maturation in how companies leverage their data assets.
