# Normative Expert System

## Definition
A normative expert system is defined as one that acts rationally according to the laws of decision theory. This approach focuses on achieving optimal outcomes rather than mimicking the potentially flawed or non-optimal reasoning steps of human experts.

## Intellectual Origins
The idea was promoted by researchers such as Judea Pearl, Eric Horvitz, and David Heckerman in the mid-1980s. It represented a shift away from trying to imitate human thought and toward building systems based on formal principles of rationality.

## Practical Applications
This approach has been successfully implemented in real-world systems. The text notes that the Windows operating system includes several normative diagnostic expert systems designed to correct problems.
