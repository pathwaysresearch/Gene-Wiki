# Neural Networks

## Overview
Neural networks are a class of models used in Machine Learning, particularly in the subfield of Deep Learning. The text discusses training and running "very large neural networks."

## Training and Execution
Libraries like TensorFlow and Keras are presented as key tools for working with neural networks. TensorFlow is designed to train and run them efficiently, even on a distributed scale across potentially hundreds of multi-GPU servers. Keras is a high-level API that simplifies the process of building and training them.

## Hyperparameter Tuning
A key aspect of working with neural networks is the fine-tuning of their hyperparameters. The table of contents indicates that important considerations include the number of hidden layers, the number of neurons per hidden layer, the learning rate, and the batch size.
