# Weak AI

## Definition
Weak AI refers to artificial intelligence that is not a perfect replica of a human being but is specialized to perform specific tasks effectively. It contrasts with the notion of a general, human-like intelligence. The text emphasizes that this "imperfect" form of AI is already powerful enough to cause significant transformation.

## Applications
Weak AI has a wide range of practical applications that are already changing how businesses operate. Examples cited in the text include prioritizing content on a social network, making a perfect cappuccino, analyzing customer behavior, and setting optimal prices for products. It has also been successfully applied in the creative arts, such as painting in the style of Rembrandt.

## Significance
The key insight is that we do not need to achieve perfect human-level AI to witness profound changes in the economy and the nature of firms. Weak AI is sufficient to transform how companies operate and compete. Its proliferation over the past ten years has been a primary driver of the new age where algorithms are integrated into the core of business.
