# Microsoft AI Principles

## Overview
Microsoft, through a collaboration between its research and CELA (Corporate, External, and Legal Affairs) teams, established a set of six principles to guide its AI policies and strategies. These principles are designed to shape new policies across the organization, particularly concerning AI interactions with users and customers, and are integrated into activities from development to sales.

## The Six Principles
The framework consists of six core principles identified by Microsoft for responsible AI. These are: fairness; reliability and safety; privacy and security; inclusiveness; transparency; and accountability. These guidelines are intended to ensure that AI systems are developed and deployed in a manner that earns trust.

## Origin and Impact
The development of these principles gained a sense of urgency after the 2016 incident with Tay, an AI-powered chatbot that began tweeting offensive statements after learning from user interactions. The incident highlighted the need for clear guidelines for designing "responsible bots." The experience with Tay and the subsequent backlash directly influenced the creation of this formal governance framework to manage the challenges of AI.
