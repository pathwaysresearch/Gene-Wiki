# Attention Economy

## Definition
The attention economy is a term used to describe the business model of many consumer-side big tech companies, especially on social media. In this model, user attention is treated as a scarce commodity, and platforms are designed with the primary incentive of figuring out how to maximize the time users spend on their sites.

## Role in Social Media
This model is the driving force behind the design of social media algorithms. The financial incentive for these platforms is to figure out what content will keep users engaged for the longest possible time. More time spent on the site translates directly into more revenue, typically from advertising.

## Impact on Content Curation
The focus on maximizing engagement within the attention economy directly leads to phenomena like filter bubbles. Algorithms prioritize showing users content that is consistent with their past preferences and viewpoints because this is a highly effective strategy for keeping them on the platform. This business model means that content curation is optimized for clicks and view times rather than for providing a balanced or diverse perspective.
