# Bounded Rationality

## Core Concept
Proposed by Herbert Simon, the theory of bounded rationality posits that human problem-solving capacity is very small compared to the complexity of real-world problems. It challenges the idea of perfect, objective rationality by arguing that decision-makers operate within the constraints of their limited mental resources.

## Mechanism of Action
The text states that bounded rationality works primarily through "satisficing." This means that instead of conducting an exhaustive search for the single best (optimal) solution, an agent deliberates only long enough to find an option that is "good enough" to meet a certain aspiration level.

## Role in AI and Human Modeling
Bounded rationality is described as a useful model for explaining human behavior in many situations. However, the text notes its limitation as a formal specification for AI agents because the theory itself does not formally define what constitutes a "good enough" solution. It is one of several strategies for coping with limited computational resources.
