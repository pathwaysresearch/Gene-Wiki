# Autonomous Transactional Processes

## Definition
Autonomous transactional processes are end-to-end business workflows, often involving multiple transactional systems, that are designed to operate with substantial automation and minimal human intervention. Deloitte has launched a business venture to develop these processes and offer them to clients on a subscription basis, moving beyond traditional system implementation services.

## How It Works
These processes are broken down into discrete transactions. Algorithms are then built to make intelligent decisions within each transaction. A key feature is that the system is designed to learn continuously from the data flowing through it, which allows it to improve over time and trigger autonomous actions as needed.

## Architecture and Delivery
The underlying algorithms are packaged into discrete microservices. This architectural approach allows clients to subscribe to specific automated process components as needed, offering a flexible and scalable solution. This model contrasts with large, monolithic ERP implementations and represents a new way of delivering process automation.
