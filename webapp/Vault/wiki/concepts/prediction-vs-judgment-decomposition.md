# Prediction-vs-Judgment Decomposition

## Definition
Prediction-vs-Judgment Decomposition is an analytical method used to understand the impact of AI on work. It operates by breaking down roles, tasks, and processes into two core components: prediction, which involves using information to generate new information, and judgment, which involves determining the value or consequence of different outcomes.

## Application in Role Analysis
At the individual level, this decomposition is used to map a professional role onto an 'automate / augment / innovate / leave-alone' framework. By identifying the prediction-heavy tasks, organizations can determine what AI can substitute for versus what it merely complements, thereby clarifying the capability boundary of AI for a specific role.

## Application in Process Redesign
This framework is also applied at the process level to differentiate between simple process automation and genuine process redesign. Analyzing a process through this lens reveals the demands of an 'AI factory model,' such as the need for data pipelines and algorithmic decision loops, and highlights how new forms of work emerge from a fundamental redesign based on AI's predictive capabilities.
