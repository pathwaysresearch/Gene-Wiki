# Cognitive Flexibility

## Definition
Cognitive flexibility is one of two primary pathways to creativity identified in a well-established model from social psychology by Carsten De Dreu and colleagues. It is characterized as the ability to draw connections between two concepts that are very distant from each other, often resulting in a 'flash of insight'.

## Role in Creativity
This concept represents the aspect of creativity that involves novel and surprising linkages between disparate ideas. It stands in contrast to the other identified pathway, cognitive persistence. Understanding these distinct pathways helps to deconstruct the creative process in humans.

## Application to AI
Professor Stefano Puntoni uses the concept of cognitive flexibility as an analogy to understand how and why a large language model might be considered creative. He argues that an effective user of AI must understand its capabilities, and by relating AI's potential to known human creative pathways, a user can better direct a model through prompt engineering to tap into its potential for making novel connections within its training data.
