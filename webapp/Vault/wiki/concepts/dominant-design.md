# Dominant Design

## Definition
As explained by Rahul Kapoor, a dominant design is a specific technology architecture that achieves widespread market dominance. It is the configuration that emerges from a period of competition between different technologies to become the accepted standard for a mass market, not just a niche.

## Key Criteria
A technology achieves dominant design status by meeting critical needs on both the supply and demand sides. On the supply side, it must align with the industry's innovation goals and cost structures. On the demand side, it must successfully address the core value drivers, needs, and preferences of consumers at scale.

## Historical Example
The text cites the early automotive industry as a classic example. During that period, multiple technologies, such as steam, electric, and internal combustion, competed for dominance. Ultimately, the internal combustion engine emerged as the dominant design that shaped the industry for decades.
