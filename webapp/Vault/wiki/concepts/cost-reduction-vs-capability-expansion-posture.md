# Cost-Reduction vs. Capability-Expansion Posture

## The Core Distinction
When AI technology dramatically collapses the cost of a core input like prediction, organizations face a strategic choice. They can adopt a 'cost-reduction posture,' using AI to perform existing tasks more cheaply, or a 'capability-expansion posture,' using the low-cost prediction to enhance quality, increase volume, or create entirely new products and services.

## Economic Implications
The choice of posture directly impacts the unit economics of any process built on prediction. The text highlights that the shift driven by AI affects not just cost, but also volume, quality, and overall capability, fundamentally altering the economic landscape of the function.

## Long-Term Performance
The text makes a clear judgment on the strategic value of these two postures. It states that the cost-reduction posture 'underperforms over time.' This implies that focusing solely on efficiency gains misses the larger opportunity for AI to create sustainable, structural advantage through capability expansion.
