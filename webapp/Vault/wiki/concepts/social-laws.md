# Social Laws

## Definition
Social laws are defined as conventions that are widespread within a community or environment. They serve as established constraints on how agents select joint plans to solve coordination problems.

## Function
The primary function of social laws is to solve coordination problems among multiple agents. For example, the convention of driving on a particular side of the road is a social law that prevents collisions. In the absence of a convention, agents must use communication to achieve a common plan.

## Analogy to Language
The development of human language serves as a strong analogy for social laws. The critical factor for a community is not the specific language spoken, but the shared agreement to use the same language, which enables communication and coordination.
