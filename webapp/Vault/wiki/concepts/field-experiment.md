# Field Experiment

## Overview of the Method
The study employed a field experiment to investigate the effects of artificial intelligence on the performance of knowledge workers. This method was chosen to understand the impact of generative AI on real-world task assignments within a professional organization, providing more realistic insights than a laboratory setting.

## Experimental Design
The experiment involved 758 consultants from Boston Consulting Group who were randomly assigned to one of three conditions: a control group with no AI access, a treatment group with access to GPT-4 ("GPT only"), and a second treatment group with access to GPT-4 plus an overview of its strengths and weaknesses ("GPT + overview"). Participants were given realistic tasks designed by senior firm members, and their performance was measured on metrics like quality, completion time, and correctness.

## Key Features
A key feature of this field experiment was its scale and realism. The participants were elite professionals performing tasks that mirrored their daily work and were critical for career progression. The study also included two distinct arms, each with a unique type of task (one "inside" and one "outside" the AI frontier), allowing for a nuanced analysis of AI's impact under different circumstances. The quality of the output was evaluated by both human graders and GPT-4 itself.
