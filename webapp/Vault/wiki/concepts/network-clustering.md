# Network Clustering

## Definition
Network clustering is a phenomenon that, along with multihoming, can serve as a check on the power of dominant platform firms. It describes a situation where competition can be effective even at a local level, allowing smaller services to become viable alternatives to large, global platforms.

## Role in Competition
The text suggests that network clustering makes competition more effective in certain markets. In the ride-hailing industry, for instance, any local taxi or ride-sharing service that can achieve sufficient scale within a specific geographic area (a cluster) can become an effective competitor to larger firms like Uber or Lyft.

## Interaction with Multihoming
Network clustering works in concert with multihoming to limit the market power of large platforms. While multihoming allows users to switch between existing large platforms, clustering allows for the emergence of new, localized competitors that can further fragment the market and increase competitive pressure.
