# Socratic Conversation

## Definition

A Socratic conversation is a dialogue-based teaching method that guides a student toward understanding through a series of questions. Rather than directly providing information, this approach encourages the student to think critically and arrive at the solution through their own reasoning.

## Application in AI Tutors

The AI tool Khanmigo is designed to engage students in a "nice Socratic conversation" when they have a question about a video or an exercise. The AI understands the context of what the student is working on and uses a conversational style to help them, rather than simply giving them the answer.

## Personalization

This conversational method is enhanced by the AI's ability to personalize the interaction. The text notes that Khanmigo has a sense of memory and can incorporate a student's stated interests, such as basketball, into the conversation to make the learning experience more relevant and engaging.
