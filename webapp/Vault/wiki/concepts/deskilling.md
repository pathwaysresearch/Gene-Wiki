# Deskilling

## Definition
Deskilling is a phenomenon where the introduction of a new technology leads to the atrophy of skills that were previously necessary for a given task. As Stefano Puntoni states, "technology is always deskilling us," meaning this is a continuous historical process where each generation loses certain abilities that the previous one possessed because technology has made them redundant.

## Historical Examples
The text provides several everyday examples to illustrate the concept of deskilling. These include the loss of skills such as driving a car with a manual gear shift, navigating without GPS, or remembering phone numbers without the aid of a contacts list on a phone. These examples show how reliance on technology makes once-common skills obsolete.

## Relevance to Generative AI
The concept of deskilling is particularly important in the context of Generative AI. For professionals and students, it is crucial to be thoughtful about how they use Gen AI to avoid losing valuable skills. Puntoni emphasizes that college students, in particular, must understand both the new skills required to succeed in a world with AI and the existing skills they might lose if they rely on the technology uncritically.
