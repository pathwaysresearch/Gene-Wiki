# Resolution Principle

## Definition and Purpose
The resolution principle is the foundation for what is described as a 'machine-oriented logic,' as stated in the title of J. A. Robinson's 1965 paper. Its purpose is to enable automated logical deduction by computer systems.

## Origin
This principle was introduced by J. A. Robinson in his seminal 1965 paper titled 'A machine-oriented logic based on the resolution principle,' which was published in the Journal of the ACM (JACM), Volume 12, pages 23-41.

## Application Context
The context provided by the paper's title suggests that the resolution principle was developed specifically to enable the automation of logical deduction on computers, forming a cornerstone of the field of automated reasoning.
