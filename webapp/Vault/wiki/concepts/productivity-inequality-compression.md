# Productivity Inequality Compression

## Definition
Productivity inequality compression is the phenomenon where the introduction of a new tool or technology narrows the distribution of productivity across a workforce. This occurs when the technology provides a larger relative benefit to lower-skilled or less productive workers, allowing them to "catch up" to their higher-skilled peers.

## Evidence from the ChatGPT Experiment
The study by Noy and Zhang (2023) identified this as a key effect of providing access to ChatGPT for professional writing tasks. The abstract and discussion explicitly state that ChatGPT "compresses the productivity distribution by benefiting low-ability workers more" and "substantially compresses the productivity distribution, reducing inequality."

## Mechanism
The compression occurs because the generative AI tool significantly increases the output quality of low-ability workers while also reducing the time they spend. In contrast, high-ability workers primarily benefit from a reduction in time spent while maintaining their already high quality standards. The net effect is a smaller gap in output quality and overall productivity between the two groups.
