# Generative AI in R&D

## Core Capability
The same generative AI technologies, specifically diffusion models, that are used for creative content generation like personalized advertisements can be applied to scientific domains. In biology, these models can be used to generate proteins, including ones that have never existed in nature before. This capability represents a fundamental shift in the R&D process from discovery to generation.

## Transforming the R&D Function
The ability to generate any desired protein on demand massively augments the R&D function, a domain traditionally thought to rely on human creativity and genius. Instead of a slow process of discovery, R&D can become a process of generation and engineering, where scientists can specify desired properties and have AI models create candidate molecules. This dramatically changes the speed and scope of scientific exploration in fields like biomedicine.

## Commercial Application Example
The company Generate Biomedicines is cited as a real-world example of this concept in action. The company is building a platform with the goal of being able to generate any protein based on these AI technologies. This illustrates the commercial viability and transformative potential of applying generative AI to the core R&D processes in the life sciences industry.
