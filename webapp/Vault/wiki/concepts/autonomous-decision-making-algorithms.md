# Autonomous Decision-Making Algorithms

## Definition
Autonomous decision-making algorithms are systems, particularly in AI and machine learning, that operate and make choices independently, without direct human intervention or oversight for each decision.

## Associated Risks
The risks associated with these algorithms escalate significantly when they operate autonomously. They can make critical errors or perpetuate biased choices without a human in the loop to correct them, leading to potentially dangerous consequences.

## Impact in Critical Fields
The consequences of errors from autonomous algorithms can be particularly severe in high-stakes fields. For example, flawed autonomous decisions in healthcare could negatively impact patient outcomes, while errors in autonomous transportation systems could lead to accidents.
