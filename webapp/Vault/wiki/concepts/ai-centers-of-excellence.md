# AI Centers of Excellence

## Strategic Importance
The text highlights that AI-focused companies realize they need considerable talent to be successful in their AI journeys. Developing and staffing centers of excellence is presented as a key lesson for organizations adopting AI.

## Required Talent
These centers require a concentration of specialized talent. The text explicitly names talent in AI, data engineering, and data science as critical roles to staff within these units to ensure success.

## Implementation Examples
Each of the profiled companies—Deloitte, Capital One, CCC, and Well—developed these talent resources. Deloitte built them for both internal use and consulting clients, Capital One has a large collection of data scientists and ML engineers, CCC has an interconnected data science and engineering team, and Well's data scientists are critical to its model development.
