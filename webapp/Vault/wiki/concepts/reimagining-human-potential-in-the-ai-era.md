# Reimagining Human Potential in the AI Era

## Definition
This concept, as explored in the 'Creative Intelligence' podcast, addresses the fundamental shift in understanding human potential and identity when faced with advanced AI or "thinking machines" that can outperform human intellect. It is rooted in the idea that when creativity is no longer the exclusive domain of human intellect, a re-evaluation of our role is necessary.

## Core Questions
The concept is driven by critical questions about what it means to be human in the modern era. It prompts an examination of how society must adapt its methods of learning, creating, and building innovative products and companies in a world where machines can outthink people.

## Implications
The primary implication is the necessity to "unleash human potential" in new ways. This involves moving beyond traditional definitions of intelligence and creativity to find new purposes and modes of operation for humanity, potentially in collaboration with or in response to artificial intelligence.
