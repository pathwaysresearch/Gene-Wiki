# Jagged Frontier

## Concept Overview
The Jagged Frontier describes the unpredictable and inconsistent capability boundary of modern AI systems. The text emphasizes that this boundary is often unclear, and making an incorrect assumption about AI's capabilities by crossing the frontier in the wrong direction can be more costly than not using AI at all.

## The 'Jagged Frontier' Exercise
To make this concept tangible, the curriculum includes an exercise where participants can directly observe the frontier. This practical application allows them to see firsthand where AI excels and where it fails, moving beyond abstract discussions of AI capabilities.

## Key Insights from the Concept
Working with the Jagged Frontier helps participants understand the critical difference between passive use of AI and a more cognitively engaged approach. It highlights the need for human oversight and collaboration to navigate AI's limitations effectively and informs the choice between different human-AI collaboration patterns, such as Centaur or Cyborg.
