# Centaur Collaboration Pattern

## Definition
The Centaur pattern is a model for human-AI collaboration defined by clean handoffs between the human and the AI system. In this approach, each party operates distinctly within its own domain of strength.

## How It Works
In a Centaur workflow, the AI might perform a specific, well-defined task (like data analysis or prediction), and then pass its output to a human. The human then takes over for a subsequent task that requires judgment, context, or other uniquely human skills. The interaction is sequential and the roles are clearly separated during task execution.

## Context of Use
This pattern is presented as one of two primary models for structuring human-AI interaction, the other being the Cyborg pattern. The choice between them depends on the nature of the task, the reliability of the AI, and the insights gained from understanding the 'Jagged Frontier' of AI capabilities.
