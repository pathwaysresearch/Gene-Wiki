# Organizational and Adjustment Costs (OACs)

## Definition
Organizational and Adjustment Costs (OACs) are the difficult problems and non-technology costs that firms encounter when changing their internal processes and structures to accommodate new technologies. These are described as a well-documented phenomenon in the empirical literature on enterprise systems development.

## Role as a Barrier to AIT
The text identifies OACs, along with problems of visibility, as a key factor that can block or significantly delay the invention of applications for new technologies like Artificial Intelligence Technologies (AITs). This friction slows the process of a technology becoming a General-Purpose Technology (GPT).

## Overcoming OACs
According to the text, the path of simple job automation using AITs is unpromising because it fails to address these fundamental adjustment costs. Instead, changing enterprise production systems to a more digital form through Digital Transformation is presented as the necessary approach to overcome, rather than evade, the problems of OACs.
