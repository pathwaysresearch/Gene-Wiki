# AI Model Deployment in Customer Service

## Overview of Impact
The deployment of an AI model in a customer service setting has a demonstrable effect on several aspects of the work experience. The text analyzes its impact on both customer and agent sentiment, the frequency of requests for managerial assistance, and rates of employee attrition.

## Impact on Sentiment and Assistance Requests
The AI model's deployment affects customer sentiment, with variations observed based on the agent's pre-existing productivity and tenure. Similarly, the technology influences how often customers request to speak with a manager, with these effects also differing based on the agent's initial skill level and tenure. These impacts are measured at the agent-month level using regression analysis.

## Impact on Employee Attrition
Analysis shows that the introduction of the AI model is associated with a significant reduction in employee attrition. Table A.11 indicates that agents in the "Post AI" condition are less likely to leave their jobs. This effect is observed across different tenure and skill levels, though the magnitude varies, with the reduction in attrition being statistically significant for most groups.
