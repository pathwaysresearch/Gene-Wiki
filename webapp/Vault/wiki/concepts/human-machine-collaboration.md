# Human-Machine Collaboration

## Core Principle
The book posits that the primary impact of AI on labor will be collaboration, not just replacement (65–67, 212). This involves redesigning jobs to create a new division of labor where humans and machines work as complements, each focusing on their respective strengths.

## Division of Labor
In this collaborative model, machines are tasked with prediction, while humans are responsible for applying judgment, managing complex context, and handling exceptions. The text illustrates this with the historical relationship between bank tellers and ATMs, where technology handled routine transactions, allowing tellers to focus on more complex customer service roles (171–173).

## Application in Professional Fields
This collaborative approach is particularly relevant in professional fields. The book discusses its application in medical imaging, where an AI might perform an initial scan or pattern recognition, with a human radiologist providing the final diagnosis and judgment (146–147).
