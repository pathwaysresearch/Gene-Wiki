# Artificial Intelligence

## Overview
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a term now commonly used to refer to the automation of interactions with customers and the management of their experiences, though the text notes that not all such automation is arguably AI. It is identified as one of the fastest-moving skills in the Nagarro portfolio, reflecting its rapidly growing importance and the need for frequent updates to related educational content. The discourse around AI is also causing a cultural shift in the concept of human exceptionalism.

## Applications in Marketing and Consumption
AI is increasingly used by marketers to automate decisions around the 4Ps (product, price, place, and promotion) and consumer-firm interactions. Prediction algorithms trained on large data sets can be used to maximize consumer response rates, personalize prices, and optimize distribution. For consumers, AI is used in tools like recommender systems and large language models to automate aspects of consumption, such as product search and writing tasks, promising more efficient and tailored experiences.

## Impact on Consumer Psychology
The diffusion of AI is presented as a double-edged sword for consumers. While it promises to enhance life by making decisions more efficient, it also poses risks beyond privacy and discrimination. Research highlights psychological tensions, such as how AI might limit an individual's expressive range, choices, and personal development by narrowing their "possibility space" through mechanisms like agency transference and parametric reductionism.
