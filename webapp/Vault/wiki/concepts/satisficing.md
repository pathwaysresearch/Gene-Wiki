# Satisficing

## Definition
Satisficing is defined in the text as the strategy of "deliberating only long enough to come up with an answer that is 'good enough.'" It involves searching through available alternatives until an acceptability threshold is met, at which point the search is terminated.

## Role in Bounded Rationality
This concept is presented as the primary way agents cope with bounded rationality. It is a practical heuristic that allows for decision-making in complex situations where finding the absolute best solution would be impossible or too costly in terms of time and effort.

## Context and Origin
The concept was developed by Herbert Simon to provide a more realistic model of human and organizational decision-making. It stands in contrast to the classical economic model of the perfectly rational agent who always optimizes to maximize utility.
