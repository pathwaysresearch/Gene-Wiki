# Skill Mismatch

## Definition
Skill mismatch describes a condition in the labor market where the skills held by workers do not align with the skills demanded by employers for their specific jobs. The text discusses this phenomenon in the context of rapid technological change, where the tasks required within an occupation can evolve quickly.

## Amplification by AI Exposure
The paper proposes that AI exposure can amplify skill mismatches, particularly under specific conditions. This effect is most pronounced in high-churn occupations, where the nature of tasks changes substantially over time. If workers in these occupations are also immobile—meaning they do not or cannot change jobs, firms, or retrain—the gap between their existing skills and new job requirements widens.

## Consequence for Wages
The study identifies skill mismatch as a key mechanism through which AI exposure can lead to negative wage outcomes. For the specific group of immobile workers in high-churn occupations, the inability to adapt to AI-driven task changes leads to them being increasingly substituted by the technology. This substitution effect puts downward pressure on their wages, resulting in a negative association between AI exposure and earnings for this cohort.
