# Exponential vs. Linear Adoption Gap

## Definition
The Exponential vs. Linear Adoption Gap is a strategic challenge highlighted by Karim Lakhani, describing the growing disparity between the exponential rate of improvement in AI model capabilities and the typically linear rate at which most organizations can absorb and integrate new technologies.

## Key Properties
The text states that AI model performance is increasing exponentially, with significant leaps happening every six to nine months. In contrast, organizational absorption—which includes training, process redesign, and cultural change—is a much slower, linear process. This creates a constantly widening gap where organizations fall further and further behind the state-of-the-art.

## Strategic Implications
This gap poses a significant competitive threat. Deferring the decision to adopt AI is described as a "real disaster" because competitors who manage to stay on the exponential curve will gain an insurmountable advantage. The change management process becomes progressively harder the longer an organization waits, as the leap required to catch up grows exponentially larger.
