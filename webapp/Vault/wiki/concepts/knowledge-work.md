# Knowledge Work

## Definition in Context
As examined in this study, knowledge work consists of a set of interdependent tasks that require highly skilled professionals to apply their expertise. The research focused on tasks representative of the daily activities of elite consultants, including creative product conceptualization, analytical problem-solving, and persuasive writing. These tasks are used by firms like Boston Consulting Group to screen job applicants from top academic backgrounds.

## Experimental Tasks
The field experiment operationalized knowledge work through two distinct types of task assignments. One set of tasks involved conceptualizing and developing new products, which required creativity, analytical skills, persuasiveness, and writing. The other set focused on business problem-solving using quantitative data, customer and company interviews, and a persuasive writing component. These tasks were designed by senior professionals to be realistic representations of consulting work.

## Impact of AI
The study investigated how the integration of generative AI impacts the productivity and quality of knowledge work. The findings show that for certain knowledge tasks (those inside the AI's capability frontier), AI can significantly augment human abilities, leading to higher quality and faster completion. However, for other tasks, AI integration can lead to negative outcomes, demonstrating a complex and uneven effect on the nature of knowledge work.
