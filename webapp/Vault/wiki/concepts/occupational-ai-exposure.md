# Occupational AI Exposure

## Definition
Occupational AI Exposure is a measure that estimates the potential impact of AI on the tasks that constitute a job. It is used to identify which occupations are most likely to be affected by the adoption of AI technologies. The paper uses this metric to analyze differential employment trends by categorizing workers into exposure quintiles.

## Measurement Approaches
The research utilizes two primary methods to measure AI exposure. The first is based on the work of Eloundou et al. (2024), which uses the GPT-4 model to estimate exposure for O*NET tasks, subsequently aggregated to the occupational level. The second approach uses the Anthropic Economic Index, which analyzes millions of user conversations with the AI model Claude to determine the share of queries related to specific O*NET tasks.

## Key Findings
Analysis using this metric reveals that young workers (ages 22-25) in the highest AI-exposure quintiles experienced significant relative employment declines compared to those in the least exposed quintiles. The paper also finds that occupational AI exposure is negatively correlated with occupational interest rate exposure, suggesting that interest rate sensitivity does not drive the main results.
