# Information Theory

## Definition
Information theory is a framework for quantifying the amount of uncertainty associated with a probability distribution.

## Role in Machine Learning
It complements probability theory by providing a precise measure of uncertainty, which is a key concept in designing and understanding machine learning models.

## Relationship to Probability Theory
Information theory builds upon probability theory. One must first have a probability distribution before one can measure the amount of information or uncertainty it contains, making it a fundamental tool for many disciplines of science and engineering.
