# Ideation Roles for LLMs

## Definition
"Ideation roles" are introduced as metaphors that clarify the functions Large Language Models (LLMs) can perform in creative tasks. This concept provides an intuitive way to understand LLM capabilities, as opposed to the difficulty of keeping track of the vast range of specific tasks they can perform, which depend on their architecture, training regimens, and databases.

## Purpose and Application
These roles are designed to be useful for both existing LLM users, as a way to organize their current practices, and for new users, as a way to activate their imagination about what is possible. The text suggests that each ideation role can be utilized to enhance both productivity and semantic breadth in idea generation, as ideas are ultimately judged on their outcome rather than the process.

## Framework Approach
The text presents these roles not as an exhaustive list, but as a "case study” identifying specific functions LLMs can play. The goal is to provide a practical approach for humans to co-create with LLMs, particularly for generating "big ideas" where the human may need to assume the role of the key ideator in the process.
