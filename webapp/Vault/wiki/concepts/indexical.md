# Indexical

## Definition
An indexical is a phrase that refers directly to the current situation in which an utterance is made. The meaning of an indexical cannot be determined from the grammar alone; it requires pragmatic information from the context.

## Examples
Common examples of indexicals include pronouns like "I" and temporal or spatial adverbs like "today." In the sentence "I am in Boston today," the referent of "I" is the person speaking, and the referent of "today" is the day on which the sentence is uttered.

## Role in Pragmatics
Resolving the meaning of indexicals is a fundamental problem in pragmatics. The grammar might represent "I" with a fluent like `Speaker`, but it is the task of the pragmatic interpretation component to use the context of the situation to ground this fluent to a specific individual.
