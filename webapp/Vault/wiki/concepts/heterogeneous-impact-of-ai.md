# Heterogeneous Impact of AI

## Overview
The study investigates the distributional consequences of AI adoption, finding that its impact on productivity is highly dependent on the worker's pre-existing skill and tenure. Unlike earlier information technologies that often complemented high-skilled workers, the analyzed generative AI provides the largest benefits to novice and less-skilled employees, helping them improve their performance disproportionately.

## Impact by Skill Level
The AI assistance significantly improves the performance of less-skilled workers, helping them converge towards their higher-skill peers. Analysis of conversation text shows that the communication patterns of lower-skill workers shift more significantly after AI adoption. This suggests the AI helps them learn and adopt best practices gathered from more effective agents. In contrast, high-skill agents see smaller gains and, in some cases, negative effects on quality measures.

## Impact by Worker Tenure
The largest gains in efficiency, measured by average handle times and chats per hour, are observed among the newest workers. The AI assistance appears to help them move down the experience curve more rapidly. For the most experienced workers, the AI has modest positive effects on efficiency but can have small, statistically significant negative effects on measures of call quality and customer satisfaction, suggesting a different value proposition for them.
