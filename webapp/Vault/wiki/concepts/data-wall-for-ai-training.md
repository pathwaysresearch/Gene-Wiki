# Data Wall (for AI Training)

## The Problem
The "data wall" is a concept describing the imminent obstacle for training large AI models, which are predicted to have processed the entire internet's text by 2026 or sooner. This exhaustion of new, high-quality text data poses a significant challenge for the continued improvement of these models through conventional training methods.

## Solution: Multimodal Data
One of the primary strategies to overcome the data wall is to expand training to include multimodal data. The text notes that most human learning is not from text but from other senses. Therefore, AI development is shifting to incorporate other data streams like audio and video, which are available in vast quantities, to provide new sources for model training.

## Solution: Advanced Reasoning
Another approach to progress beyond the data wall is to focus on improving the intelligence of models in ways that are not dependent on ingesting more data. This involves developing their capacity for reasoning and other cognitive functions, allowing them to grow in capability without simply consuming more information.
