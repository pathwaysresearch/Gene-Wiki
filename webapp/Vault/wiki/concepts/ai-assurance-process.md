# AI Assurance Process

## Definition
An AI assurance process is a structured methodology for reviewing proposed AI applications to ensure they comply with relevant regulations and organizational values. As implemented by companies like Unilever, it serves as a governance mechanism to assess risks and determine whether an AI system should be approved for use.

## How It Works
The process involves a formal review where if a system is judged to be risky, a mitigation approach is communicated and recommended. A key example of mitigation is requiring a "human in the loop" to make final decisions, particularly in automated systems like a resume checker. If serious risks cannot be mitigated, the process can lead to the rejection of the AI application on the grounds that it violates the organization's values.

## Governance and Decision-Making
Final decisions on AI use cases under this process are typically made by a senior executive board. As seen at Unilever, such a board may include representatives from legal, human resources, and AI departments, ensuring a multi-disciplinary evaluation. This governance structure helps organizations think more broadly than just the legality and efficacy of an application, incorporating wider ethical considerations, as demonstrated in the evaluation of a proposed computer vision app for sales agents.
