# Metaphorical Ideation Roles

## Definition
Metaphorical Ideation Roles is a framework for thinking about different ways to use Generative AI in the ideation process. It uses metaphors to distinguish between AI generating ideas itself versus AI helping to pull ideas out of a human user. The framework is designed to help users conceptualize and direct AI for different creative purposes.

## Roles for Idea Extraction
The framework identifies two specific roles for when the goal is to have the AI pull ideas out of a person, which Puntoni refers to as the 'key generator' case. The first is the 'interviewer,' where the LLM acts like a Socratic teacher, asking questions to help the user sharpen their arguments, clarify their thinking, and potentially identify new opportunities without giving direct answers.

## The 'Actor' Role
The second role for idea extraction is the 'actor.' In this role, the LLM is prompted to impersonate a specific stakeholder relevant to the user's problem. This allows a researcher, for example, to interact with a simulated version of a person they are trying to help or understand, facilitating a deeper exploration of the problem space from a different perspective.
