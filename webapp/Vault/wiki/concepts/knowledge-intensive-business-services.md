# Knowledge-Intensive Business Services (KIBS)

## Definition

Knowledge-Intensive Business Services (KIBS) refer to a category of work performed by professionals such as accountants, architects, and software developers. As defined by Miles et al. (1995), this type of work is distinguished by its high intensity in cognitive skills and broader human capital.

## Characteristics

KIBS work is fundamentally based on the application of specialized knowledge and expertise. The tasks involved are typically complex, non-routine, and require significant analytical and problem-solving abilities. This makes the skill profile of KIBS workers distinct from that of blue-collar or other non-professional workers.

## Relevance to AI Exposure

The study highlights KIBS as a particularly relevant group for analyzing the impact of Artificial Intelligence. Due to the cognitive and knowledge-based nature of their work, KIBS workers are hypothesized to be particularly exposed to AI technologies. Recent experimental studies have indicated that AI could have significant effects, either through substitution or augmentation, on the tasks performed by this occupational group.
