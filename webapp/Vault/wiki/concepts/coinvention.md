# Coinvention

## Definition
Coinvention refers to the necessary inventive work that must occur alongside the adoption of a new core technology to make it truly valuable. This process involves creating complementary applications, redesigning business processes, and making other organizational adjustments to fully leverage the new technology's potential.

## Role in Digital Transformation
Coinvention is identified as the most significant bottleneck hindering widespread Digital Transformation. While a core technology like AIT may advance rapidly, its broad commercial application is constrained by the difficulty and scarcity of the required coinventions needed to integrate it effectively into business operations.

## Economic Significance
The high costs associated with the coinvention process are the primary reason why Digital Transformation is a difficult and slow-moving phenomenon. These costs act as a major barrier to the widespread and deep use of new technologies like AITs, thereby limiting their potential to generate large gains for firms and overall economic growth.
