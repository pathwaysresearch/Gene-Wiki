# Continuous Learning in AI

## Definition
Continuous learning is a process where an organization's AI models are continuously updated and improved. This is achieved by retraining the models with new data when their predictive accuracy begins to decline, a phenomenon known as model drift. The outcome is a more valuable model that remains fit for purpose as the world changes.

## How It Works
AI-fueled companies actively monitor the performance of their predictive models, often using technologies associated with machine learning operations. If a model's predictions are found to be no longer accurate, the company uses new data to retrain it and improve its predictive power. This cycle of monitoring and retraining ensures that the models remain aligned with the current state of the business environment.

## Significance for Business
The text presents continuous learning as a hallmark of a "true learning machine company." It signifies that the company views its models as valuable business assets worthy of ongoing monitoring and improvement. This capability is considered essential for an AI-powered company to adapt to changing environments and maintain the value of its AI investments.
