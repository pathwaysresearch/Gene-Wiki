# Manipulator

## Overview
A manipulator, or robot arm, is one of the primary categories of modern robots. They are characterized by being physically anchored to their workplace, such as in a factory assembly line or on the International Space Station.

## How It Works
The motion of a manipulator typically involves a chain of controllable joints. This mechanical design enables such robots to place their effectors in any position within their designated workspace, providing precise control over object interaction.

## Applications and Prevalence
Manipulators are by far the most common type of industrial robot, with approximately one million units installed worldwide. They are critical in industries like car manufacturing, where few manufacturers could survive without them. Other applications include assisting surgeons in hospitals (as mobile manipulators) and even generating original artwork.
