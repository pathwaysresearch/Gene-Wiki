# Smart Factory

## Core Components
A smart factory is characterized by the extensive use of sensors on the factory floor, which generate massive amounts of data at each step of the manufacturing process. This constant stream of data provides the raw material for operational intelligence and process optimization.

## Operational Intelligence
The key to making a factory "smart" is the application of algorithms to assimilate and analyze the sensor data. This allows for the continuous improvement of manufacturing processes, moving beyond static operational plans to a dynamic, data-driven environment.

## Autonomous Operation
The concept is further enhanced by layering in real-time monitoring and adjustment capabilities, for example, through the use of smart cameras. The ultimate goal is a system that not only manufactures products but also operates largely autonomously and is capable of self-improvement, representing a significant leap in manufacturing efficiency and capability.
