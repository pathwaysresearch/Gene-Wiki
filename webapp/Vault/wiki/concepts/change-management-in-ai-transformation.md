# Change Management in AI Transformation

## The 70/30 Principle
According to analysis by Karim Lakhani, a successful AI-driven digital transformation is composed of two distinct parts. The technology stack and data science represent only about 30% of the overall challenge. The remaining, and more difficult, 70% consists of change management and the necessary organizational restructuring. Many leaders and boards incorrectly index on the 30% technology portion, neglecting the more critical human and process elements.

## The Leadership Mandate
This organizational transformation is described as a "total rewiring" that cannot be outsourced to vendors or delegated solely to a specific department. C-suite executives must personally lead and embody the change. Technology executives, in particular, are advised that they must also become HR specialists and change management leaders to drive the deep organizational shifts required for AI to be effective.

## Common Failure Mode
A common reason for the failure of digital transformation initiatives is an excessive focus on buying technology without addressing the underlying organizational structures and processes. When the 70% of change management is ignored, the new systems and capabilities fail to be adopted and integrated, or they "never took inside the company." This renders the technological investment ineffective and prevents the organization from realizing the benefits of AI.
