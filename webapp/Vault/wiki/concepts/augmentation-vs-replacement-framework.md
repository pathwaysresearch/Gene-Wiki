# Augmentation vs. Replacement Framework

## The Replacement Play
This perspective frames AI primarily as a tool for efficiency and cost-saving. In this view, which Stefano Puntoni describes as "uniquely uninspiring," the main goal is to automate existing work to make it cheaper and faster. He notes that in corporate discourse, especially communications aimed at investors, "productivity" often serves as a code word for cost-saving and headcount reduction. The central question of the replacement play is how to automate the work currently being done.

## The Augmentation Play
In contrast, the augmentation play focuses on using AI to achieve more than just efficiency gains. This perspective shifts the central question to how technology can be used to automate work that is not currently being done at all. The goal is to produce better and different products and services, innovating beyond current capabilities rather than simply optimizing existing processes. This approach represents a more inspiring vision for AI's role in the enterprise.

## The Need for a New Narrative
Puntoni argues that corporate conversations about AI are too heavily skewed towards the replacement narrative. He calls for a more inspiring dialogue that emphasizes augmentation, focusing on how AI can help companies innovate and create new forms of value. This shift in framing is crucial for motivating employees and unlocking the full potential of the technology beyond incremental productivity increases.
