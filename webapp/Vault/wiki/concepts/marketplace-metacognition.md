# Marketplace Metacognition

## Definition

Marketplace metacognition is a theoretical lens for understanding how consumers perceive and interact with intelligent agents in the marketplace. It focuses on the 'theories consumers have about how AI "thinks",' including its perceived intentions, strategies, and operational logic.

## Application to AI

The text proposes leveraging this theory as consumers increasingly come to understand AI as an 'independent intelligence operating in the marketplace.' Understanding consumers' lay theories about AI is crucial for predicting how they will respond to AI systems to which they delegate tasks or with whom they interact socially.

## Research Significance

The text suggests that applying marketplace metacognition can help researchers and AI designers 'form a more holistic understanding of consumers' interaction with AI.' It provides a framework for exploring how a consumer's beliefs about an AI's mind influence their behavior and responses within the marketplace.
