# AI Factories

## Core Components
An AI Factory is a business model structured around several key components. The text identifies these as a robust data pipeline for ingesting and processing information, a capability for algorithm development, and dedicated experimentation platforms to test and refine models. These are supported by the underlying software, connectivity, and infrastructure necessary for their operation.

## The Virtuous Cycle
A central principle of the AI Factory is the creation of a virtuous cycle. This feedback loop, mentioned as a key characteristic, implies that as the factory produces more AI-driven insights and products, it gathers more data, which in turn improves the algorithms, leading to a self-reinforcing process of enhancement.

## Implementation and Transformation
The text outlines that building an AI Factory is a transformative process for a company. It describes distinct stages of transformation that an enterprise undergoes to adopt this model. The case of Fidelity is presented as an example of a company that has implemented an AI Factory, demonstrating its real-world application.
