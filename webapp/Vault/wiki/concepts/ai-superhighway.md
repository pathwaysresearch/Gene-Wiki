# AI Superhighway

## Overview
The "AI Superhighway" is a strategic concept that reframes Grammarly's mission from being a grammar-focused tool to a comprehensive platform for bringing AI to where people work. The vision is to create an infrastructure that allows multiple specialized AI agents to assist users across different applications and workflows.

## Analogy and Components
In this analogy, the superhighway is the underlying platform that Grammarly is building. The "cars" on this highway are the various AI agents, with the original grammar checker being just the first one. The "surfaces" are the applications where work happens, such as documents, spreadsheets, and email. The goal is to bring an "army of agents" to every surface a user interacts with.

## Strategic Implementation
Grammarly is executing this vision through both internal development and strategic acquisitions. The acquisition of Coda provides the document, spreadsheet, and presentation "surface," while the acquisition of Superhuman adds a reinvented email "surface." These components are integrated into the superhighway to expand the reach and utility of Grammarly's AI agents.
