# New Task Creation

## Definition
New Task Creation is a type of technological innovation that involves the introduction of new, previously non-existent tasks into a sector's production process. In the model presented, it is represented by the variable N and is contrasted with automation, which focuses on replacing existing tasks. The incentives for new task creation are denoted by V_j^N.

## Relationship with Demand
Proposition 2 establishes a direct and positive relationship between consumer demand and new task creation. A demand shift towards a specific sector unambiguously increases the rate of new task creation relative to automation in that sector. This occurs because the demand shift increases both output and prices, strengthening the incentives for firms to innovate by creating new tasks.

## Economic Impact
The rate of new task creation, alongside automation, is a key determinant of a sector's employment share and wages. The text suggests that innovations which augment labor, such as new task creation, are associated with an expansion of the wagebill and employment in the occupations they affect. This process is part of a framework that endogenizes the supply of technologies based on economic incentives.
