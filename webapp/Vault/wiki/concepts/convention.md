# Convention

## Definition
A convention is any constraint on the selection of joint plans that agents agree to follow before engaging in a joint activity. The purpose is to coordinate actions effectively, especially in multiagent planning scenarios.

## Examples
A common example is the convention for drivers to stay on a specific side of the road (e.g., the right side in most countries) to avoid collisions. This works as long as all agents in the environment agree on the same convention. In sports, like doubles tennis, a convention like "stick to your side of the court" helps partners decide who should handle the ball, ruling out plans where both go for it.

## Relation to Social Laws
When conventions become widespread within a community, they are referred to as social laws. The development of human language is an example, where the key is that a community agrees to speak the same language, rather than which specific language is chosen, to enable communication.
