# Jagged Technological Frontier

## Definition
The "jagged technological frontier" is a term used to characterize the performance profile of AI systems. It signifies that an AI's capabilities are not uniform; instead, they are "jagged," with high peaks of excellence in certain areas and deep valleys of incompetence in others, even for tasks that appear closely related.

## Evidence from Research
Research, such as the clinical trial conducted at Boston Consulting Group, revealed this phenomenon in practice. When employees used AI for tasks it was well-suited for, their performance increased dramatically. However, when they used the same AI for tasks that fell outside its capability frontier, their performance was negatively impacted.

## Strategic Implications
Understanding this concept is critical for leaders implementing AI. It means that simply deploying AI across the board is a flawed strategy. Instead, organizations must carefully identify the specific tasks where AI provides a genuine advantage and avoid misapplying it in areas where it is weak, which could lead to poor outcomes.
