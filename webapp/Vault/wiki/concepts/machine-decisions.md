# Machine Decisions

## Definition
Machine decisions, also referred to as automated decisions, are choices and judgments made by AI and machine learning algorithms without direct human intervention. The text identifies this as the central topic of Kartik Hosanagar's book, "A Human's Guide to Machine Intelligence," which explores the opportunities and challenges surrounding them.

## Impact and Scope
These decisions are described as having a significant and growing role in our lives, with the text noting that the "stakes couldn't be any higher." They subtly influence choices in various domains, from e-commerce purchases driven by algorithmic recommendations to other critical areas. The text emphasizes that a large and increasing number of our decisions are driven by these algorithms.

## Governance and Risks
The text highlights the existence of risks associated with machine decisions, which necessitates a structured approach to managing them. This has led to the development of a governance framework to guide how individuals, companies, and regulators should think about and control these automated processes to mitigate potential harm.
