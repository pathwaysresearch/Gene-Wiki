# Known Knowns

## Definition
In the context of prediction, "known knowns" are situations where there is rich data available, leading to the knowledge that good predictions are possible. This corresponds to Donald Rumsfeld's category of "things we know we know."

## Role for Prediction Machines
This is the ideal environment for prediction machines to operate. With rich data, the machine effectively "knows the situation" and can work well, making accurate predictions.

## Part of a Larger Framework
This concept is one of four categories in a taxonomy used to understand the conditions under which prediction machines succeed or falter. The other categories are known unknowns, unknown unknowns, and unknown knowns.
