# Human Flourishing (AI Paradigm)

## Definition
Proposed by Stefano Puntoni, the "human flourishing" paradigm is an alternative to the "human replacement" mindset. It reframes the goal of AI integration as a collaborative "human and AI" relationship, focusing on how the technology can be used within organizations, workflows, and business functions to enhance human work and overall productivity.

## Required Perspective
Successfully implementing this paradigm requires more than a purely technical viewpoint. The text argues that a deep understanding of business is essential to see beyond simple task automation and identify opportunities for synergistic integration. This business-centric approach allows for a shift in the conversation from replacement to augmentation.

## Goal
The ultimate aim of the human flourishing paradigm is to change the narrative around AI in the workplace. Instead of viewing AI as a direct substitute for people, this approach seeks to leverage technology to create better outcomes and enable human potential within an organizational context.
