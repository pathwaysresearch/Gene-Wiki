# Age-Consumption Profile Estimation

## Objective
The primary goal of this method is to create age-consumption profiles that model how consumption of different product categories varies with the age composition of a household. This is used to understand the economic impact of demographic shifts, such as the aging of the Baby Boom generation.

## Data and Preparation
The method utilizes data from the Consumer Expenditure (CE) surveys from 2002–2018. The first step involves annualizing consumption for each household (consumption unit or CU) by scaling its recorded consumption by the number of months it appears in the survey. The second step is to pool this annualized data across all years to create an average annual consumption figure for each household and product category.

## Estimation Model
The age-consumption profile is estimated using a regression model. The average annual consumption for a household in a specific product category (c_{ik}) is regressed on variables representing the household's structure (H_{ij}, S_{ij}, O_{ij}). The coefficients from this regression (β_{jk}, γ_{jk}, δ_{jk}) quantify the relationship between the presence of household members of different ages and consumption levels for that product category.
