# Computational Neuroscience

## Primary Goal
The main objective of computational neuroscience is to understand how the brain works on an algorithmic level. The field is primarily concerned with building more accurate models of actual brain function.

## Distinction from Deep Learning
While deep learning may draw inspiration from the brain, its primary goal is to build computer systems that can successfully solve tasks requiring intelligence. In contrast, computational neuroscience's main concern is modeling the brain itself, regardless of immediate engineering applications. The text emphasizes that deep learning should not be viewed as an attempt to simulate the brain.

## Interdisciplinary Nature
Computational neuroscience is described as a separate field of study from deep learning. However, the text notes that it is common for researchers to move back and forth between the two fields, indicating a shared interest in the principles of information processing and intelligence.
