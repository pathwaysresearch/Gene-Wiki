# Identity Threat (from AI)

## Definition
An identity threat from AI is a specific form of psychological threat that occurs when a worker perceives that an artificial intelligence system can skillfully perform important parts of their job. This feeling is particularly acute when the tasks in question are ones in which the individual has invested a significant amount of time, effort, and personal identity to master. It challenges their sense of uniqueness and professional value.

## Connection to Competence
This concept is closely linked to the psychological need for competence, as outlined in Self-Determination Theory. When an AI demonstrates high capability in a domain that was previously a source of a worker's professional pride and identity, it can directly undermine that person's feeling of being competent. Accepting that a machine can replicate skills developed over a lifetime can be a profound psychological challenge.

## Examples and Reactions
This threat is not abstract and can be felt personally. The podcast host recounts his own experience of feeling this threat when people suggest an AI product could generate a podcast from a document, thereby encroaching on his role. Such threats can provoke negative coping responses, such as denial, if not managed properly within an organization undergoing an AI transformation.
