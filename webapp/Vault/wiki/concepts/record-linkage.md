# Record Linkage

## Definition
Record linkage is the task of identifying data records that refer to the same real-world entity, particularly when the records lack a standard unique identifier. It is a form of probabilistic reasoning designed to handle identity uncertainty.

## The Core Problem
The challenge arises from inconsistencies and variations in data. For example, citations for a book might list the author as “Stuart Russell,” “S. J. Russell,” or even contain a misspelling like “Stewart Russle.” Record linkage techniques are used to determine that these different strings likely refer to the same person.

## Applications
Record linkage is a significant and widespread problem with major commercial applications. Hundreds of companies specialize in solving record linkage problems across various domains, including financial, medical, and census data, where clean and consistent entity identifiers are often unavailable.
