# Digital Twins

## Definition in Market Research
Within the context of market research, digital twins are described as a specific and advanced form of simulation. The text notes that clients are actively exploring their use to simulate scenarios in ways that are not easily done today.

## Key Distinction
The primary characteristic that distinguishes a true digital twin from a more general synthetic persona is its foundation in real-world data. The text explicitly states that digital twins are "built from data on real individuals," implying a high-fidelity model of a specific person or entity.

## Application Context
The concept is raised in the context of simulating scenarios that are otherwise difficult to test. However, the conversation also implies a risk of organizations jumping to use the technology without first defining the specific problem it is meant to solve.
