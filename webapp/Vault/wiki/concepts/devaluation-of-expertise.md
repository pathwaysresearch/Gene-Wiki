# Devaluation of Expertise

## Definition
The devaluation of expertise is a concept presented as the primary risk that Artificial Intelligence poses to labor markets. It challenges the common narrative of a "technologically jobless future," suggesting instead that the core threat is the erosion of the economic value of specialized human knowledge and skills.

## Mechanism
This process occurs in a future where AI makes high-level skills and knowledge universally available, effectively making everyone an expert. When expertise is no longer a scarce human attribute, individual workers who supply only generic, undifferentiated labor become easily replaceable and disposable. The unique value proposition of human experts is eliminated.

## Socioeconomic Implications
In a world characterized by the devaluation of expertise, the economic structure would be radically altered. Most wealth would become concentrated in the hands of the owners of AI patents, as they would control the means of production of expertise itself. The text warns that the political contours of such a world would be a "hellscape," leading to extreme inequality and social instability.
