# Cost of Cognition

## Definition
The "cost of cognition" is a concept articulated by Karim Lakhani to describe the economic and operational resources required for thinking, analysis, and problem-solving. He posits that Artificial Intelligence's most significant contribution will be to drastically reduce this cost.

## Analogy to Information Transmission
Lakhani frames this concept by drawing a direct parallel to the internet's historical impact on the cost of information transmission. Just as the internet revolutionized business and society by making the movement of information nearly free, AI is set to do the same for the process of cognition itself.

## Implications for Business
The reduction in the cost of cognition implies that tasks requiring analysis, prediction, and problem-solving can be performed more cheaply, quickly, and at a greater scale. This forces business leaders to identify areas where thinking is applied within their organizations and to develop use cases for AI to maintain a competitive edge.
