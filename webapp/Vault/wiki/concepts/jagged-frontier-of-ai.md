# Jagged Frontier of AI

## Definition
The "jagged frontier" refers to the uneven nature of AI's capabilities in knowledge-intensive workflows. This means that AI's performance is not uniformly superior or inferior to humans but rather straddles both sides, excelling in some areas while failing in others. This creates a complex landscape where it is difficult for users to know whether they are operating on a task where AI is competent or not.

## Implications for Knowledge Work
The jagged nature of the knowledge frontier poses significant challenges for human-AI collaboration. Knowledge workers may find themselves in situations where they are unaware of their position relative to AI's capabilities for a given task. This can lead to performance degradation, particularly when professionals become overly reliant on AI in areas where its output is flawed, a risk described as "falling asleep at the wheel."

## Industry Recognition
The concept has gained recognition from industry leaders. For instance, Sundar Pichai, the CEO of Google, acknowledged the idea in an interview, coining the term "AJI, the artificial jagged intelligence" to describe the feeling that AI's progress and capabilities are uneven.
