# Semantic Breadth (in LLM Ideation)

## Definition
Semantic breadth is an ideation strategy that aims to generate ideas spanning more diverse semantic categories. This approach prioritizes variety and originality over sheer volume within a single category.

## Recommended Techniques
To increase semantic breadth, the text recommends several methods. These include prompt variation (varying prompts to enhance originality, such as via persona modifiers), hybrid prompting (generating smaller idea pools using different prompts and then combining them), and chain of thought prompting (asking the LLM to follow distinct, ordered steps). Another technique is to increase the LLM's temperature parameter, which dials up the stochasticity of its responses to produce more diverse and unpredictable ideas.

## Key Objective
The primary goal of pursuing semantic breadth is to increase the originality of generated ideas without the cost of decreasing the overall diversity of the collective idea pool. This approach is presented as a way to avoid the originality plateau that can result from a pure productivity focus.
