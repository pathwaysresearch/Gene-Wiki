# Visibility (in enterprise systems development)

## Definition
In the context of enterprise systems development, visibility refers to the degree to which the current structure of jobs and organizations is understood by anyone within the firm. Low visibility is presented as a significant obstacle to technology implementation projects, particularly those aimed at automation.

## Impact on AIT Adoption
The text identifies low visibility as a major problem that can block or delay the adoption of Artificial Intelligence Technologies (AITs). When there is low ex ante visibility, the predicted value proposition of a new technology is often based more on a "fond hope than a market reality." This is particularly true for the automation of white-collar work, where the complexity of existing jobs is often poorly understood.

## Relation to Digital Transformation
The difficulty of AIT-based job automation is compounded by low visibility, as it is hard to develop a system to replace a job that is not well understood. The text suggests that overcoming this problem requires the difficult work of Digital Transformation, rather than attempting to evade it.
