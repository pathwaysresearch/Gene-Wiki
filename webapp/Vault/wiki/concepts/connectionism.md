# Connectionism

## Overview
Connectionist models, also referred to as neural networks, were seen by some as direct competitors to the symbolic models promoted by Newell and Simon and the logicist approach of McCarthy.

## The Symbolic Debate
The rise of connectionism sparked a debate about the fundamental role of symbol manipulation in detailed models of cognition. While the most ardent connectionists questioned its explanatory power, the current consensus is that connectionist and symbolic approaches are complementary, not competing.

## Modern Bifurcation
Modern research in this area has split into two fields. One branch focuses on creating effective network architectures and algorithms and understanding their mathematical properties. The other branch is concerned with the careful modeling of the empirical properties of actual biological neurons and neural ensembles.
