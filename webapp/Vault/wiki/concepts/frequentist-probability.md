# Frequentist Probability

## Definition
Frequentist probability is an interpretation of probability that is directly tied to the rates at which events occur. It was the original motivation for the development of probability theory.

## Core Principle
In this view, stating that an outcome has a probability 'p' means that if the experiment were repeated an infinite number of times, that specific outcome would be observed in a proportion 'p' of the repetitions.

## Applicability
This reasoning is most directly applicable to propositions about events that are repeatable, such as analyzing the outcomes of drawing cards in a game of poker. It is less suited for propositions that cannot be repeated.
