# Automation of Repetitive Tasks

## Core Principle
The text posits that a primary function of AI will be to free humans from repetitive and uninteresting tasks, allowing them to focus on more uniquely human activities. This automation is targeted at objective tasks that do not require subjective human judgment, with the ultimate goal of allowing people to pursue more interesting work.

## Key Application Areas
The excerpts identify several industries and roles as prime candidates for automation due to their repetitive nature and low job satisfaction. These include customer support, where agents often answer the same questions repeatedly, and the recruiting and hiring process, which is described as an unpleasant experience for 95% of candidates and hiring managers. Other examples include managing production software operations and tasks performed by field workers.

## Societal Impact
The speaker suggests a positive societal shift resulting from this automation, comparing it to the historical transition from an agrarian society to the present day. The argument is that while jobs in areas like customer support may be eliminated, new, more interesting jobs will be created for humans, continuing a long-term trend of societal and economic evolution where technology takes over mundane labor.
