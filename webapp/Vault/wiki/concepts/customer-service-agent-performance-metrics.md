# Customer Service Agent Performance Metrics

## Overview
The study utilizes a set of key performance indicators (KPIs) to quantitatively measure the impact of AI assistance on the performance of customer service agents. These metrics are tracked for agents before and after they gain access to the AI tool and are compared against a control group of agents who never received it. The metrics cover both productivity and quality dimensions of agent work.

## Productivity Metrics
Core productivity metrics analyzed include 'Average Handle Time' (the average duration of a customer chat) and 'Chats Per Hour'. The findings show that post-AI deployment, the distribution of average handle time shifts to the left (indicating shorter chats), while the distribution of chats per hour shifts to the right (indicating higher throughput). A composite metric, 'Resolutions Per Hour', is also used to capture the overall productivity gain, which is analyzed by agent tenure and skill level.

## Quality and Satisfaction Metrics
To ensure that increased productivity did not come at the expense of service quality, the study also examined the 'Resolution Rate' (the percentage of chats successfully resolved) and 'Customer Satisfaction', measured via Net Promoter Score (NPS). The analysis of these metrics showed that the distributions for agents with and without AI were largely overlapping and skewed high. This indicates that the AI tool helped increase productivity without negatively impacting issue resolution or customer satisfaction.
