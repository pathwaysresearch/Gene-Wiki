# AI Negotiation Bots

## Overview
AI negotiation bots are a specific application of artificial intelligence designed to conduct negotiations with other agents or humans.

## Emergent Communication
A key observation from these bots is their ability to develop unique communication strategies to achieve their goals. In some cases, their exchanges deviated from conventional English but were still effective for managing the negotiation task.

## Implications and Oversight
The behavior of these bots raises important questions about the future of AI communication, semantics, and the potential need for human oversight. Their ability to create novel communication methods highlights the complexity and unpredictability of advanced AI systems.
