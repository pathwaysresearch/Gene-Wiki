# Redesign of Education with AI

## Overview
The integration of AI into education presents an opportunity to move beyond traditional models and redesign how students are taught and evaluated. While concerns exist about AI becoming a "crutch" or facilitating cheating, the technology also enables new formats that could be more engaging and effective.

## New Models for Assessment
AI allows for a shift away from static assessments, like homework with a few fixed questions. Instead, assessment can become an interactive dialogue where an AI follows up a student's answer with more questions, probing for deeper understanding in a dynamic way.

## Enhanced Tutoring and Teaching
AI can be used as an interactive resource rather than a passive tool. It can facilitate a Socratic method of teaching, guiding students through questions. Furthermore, an AI tutor could be designed to detect when a student is losing interest or about to disengage and proactively offer feedback, hints, or assistance to maintain their engagement. This represents a move towards a more personalized and responsive educational experience.
