# Keystone Strategy

## Definition
A keystone strategy is a business approach centered on aligning a company's internal operations and external relationships to maintain the health and stability of its dependent networks. The text presents this not just as an ethical responsibility but as a crucial method for ensuring the long-term preservation of a networked business.

## Application and Examples
The text provides examples of this strategy in action, such as when Google invests in technology to remove bias from its algorithms or when Facebook removes harmful videos from its platform. These actions are framed as efforts to sustain the business network upon which these companies rely, thereby preserving their business for the long term.

## Relationship to Information Fiduciary
The keystone concept is explicitly related to the idea of an information fiduciary. By acting to preserve the health of their networks, firms that are hubs of information, like Google and Facebook, are fulfilling some of the responsibilities associated with being a trustworthy steward of user data and community interaction.
