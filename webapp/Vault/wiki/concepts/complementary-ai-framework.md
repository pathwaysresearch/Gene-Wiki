# Complementary AI Framework

## Core Principle
The central tenet of the Complementary AI Framework is to view artificial intelligence as a technology that enhances, rather than replaces, human capabilities. According to Stefano Puntoni, the goal should not be to design AI that can merely mimic what a human expert does. Instead, the focus should be on designing AI systems that make the human expert "maximally productive," leveraging the unique knowledge and experience that people possess to make them "maximally useful."

## Strategic Framing
This framework offers a more "inspiring and productive framing" for AI's potential, shifting the objective from cost-cutting through workforce reduction to value creation through human augmentation. It encourages organizations to focus on the "effectiveness piece" and how to produce "better work." This positive framing is crucial for internal adoption, as it directly counters the prevalent, often negative, societal narratives surrounding AI.

## Implementation and Communication
Successful implementation requires strong, top-down leadership with a clear vision. Leaders must be mindful of the "dark tinged" public discourse on AI, often exemplified by references to "Terminator and the Matrix." To secure employee buy-in, it is essential to communicate with an "authentic voice," clearly explaining not just the company's objectives but also the direct benefits for employees who engage with the new technology.
