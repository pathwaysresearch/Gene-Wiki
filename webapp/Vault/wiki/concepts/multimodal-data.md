# Multimodal Data

## Definition
Multimodal data refers to datasets that incorporate multiple forms of information beyond just text. As described in the text, this includes sensory inputs like audio and video, reflecting the fact that much of human learning and perception is not text-based.

## Role in AI Development
The shift towards multimodal data is a key strategic direction for AI models. It is presented as a direct solution to the problem of the "data wall," where models are running out of new text from the internet to train on.

## Strategic Importance
By enabling models to learn from video, audio, and other data streams, researchers can tap into a much larger pool of information. This not only addresses the scarcity of new text but also aims to build more robust and comprehensive intelligence by mimicking the multi-sensory way humans learn about the world.
