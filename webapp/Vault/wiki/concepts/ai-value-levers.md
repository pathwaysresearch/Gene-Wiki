# AI Value Levers

## Overview
The text presents a framework of six "value levers" that all-in-on-AI companies use to achieve value. Companies wishing to succeed with AI are advised to employ as many of these levers as possible, and to strive to achieve multiple levers with individual use cases.

## The Six Levers
The framework consists of six distinct methods for applying AI to generate value. These include: **Speed to execution** to accelerate business results by minimizing decision latency; **Cost reduction** through intelligent automation of processes; **Comprehension of complexity** by using AI to decipher patterns in complex data; **Transformed engagement** to change how customers and employees interact with smart systems; **Fueled innovation** by generating insights for new products and business models; and **Fortified trust** by using AI to protect the brand from risks like fraud and cyberintrusion.

## Application and Measurement
Organizations are encouraged to apply multiple levers simultaneously to maximize the value achieved from AI initiatives. The text notes that some of the levers, such as cost reduction, are relatively easy to measure, implying that others may be more challenging to quantify.
