# AI Delegation Experience

## Definition

A 'delegation experience' is one in which consumers involve an AI solution in a production process to perform tasks they would have otherwise performed themselves. This shifts the consumer's role from direct execution to management or oversight of an AI agent.

## Types of Delegated Tasks

The text identifies three types of tasks that can be delegated to AI: decisions, digital actions, and physical actions. Examples provided include Google Assistant booking an appointment (a decision), Smart Compose helping to write an email (a digital action), and the Nest Thermostat programming a home's temperature based on learned preferences (a physical action).

## Psychological Impact

Delegation experiences have a dual psychological impact, as they can make consumers feel empowered while also raising concerns about being replaced. The perception of this experience is influenced by several factors, including whether consumption is motivated by instrumental or symbolic needs and whether the consumer values the process of a task over its outcome. The text also notes that delegation experiences share a theoretical foundation with data capture experiences through their mutual emphasis on concerns about personal control.
