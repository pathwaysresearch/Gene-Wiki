# Humans with AI Thesis

## Definition

The "Humans with AI" thesis, as articulated by Karim Lakhani, posits that the primary threat to employment is not artificial intelligence itself, but a skills gap. It argues that individuals who learn to leverage AI tools, particularly generative AI, will gain a significant productivity and capability advantage, ultimately displacing workers who fail to adapt to these new technologies.

## Rationale

This thesis is grounded in the observation that generative AI tools are already widely accessible and in use, with over 100 million users for platforms like ChatGPT. Banning these tools within companies is seen as a misguided and futile effort. Instead, the focus should be on augmentation, where AI enhances human capabilities, allowing for tasks to be completed more efficiently and effectively, as demonstrated by Lakhani's own experience using AI for complex travel planning.

## Implications for Companies and Workers

For workers, the implication is a continuous learning mandate to acquire skills in using AI. For companies, the thesis calls for a proactive strategy of embracing AI. This includes running internal boot camps and creating sandboxes to help all employees, not just technical staff, experiment with and find productive use cases for AI tools within their specific work contexts.
