# Homogenization Risk

## Definition
Homogenization risk is a specific, systemic risk associated with the broad adoption of AI. It is defined as the potential for AI-assisted organizations to produce less diverse judgment at scale, as decision-making processes converge around similar models, data, and logic.

## Strategic Context
This risk is discussed in the context of how AI can shift a firm from mere operational improvement to a position of structural advantage. While AI offers significant benefits, it also introduces new, structural-level risks like homogenization that must be managed.

## Implications for Business Models
The emergence of homogenization risk highlights a potential downside to AI-driven efficiency. If all competitors in an industry use similar AI tools, it could lead to groupthink, correlated errors, and a reduction in market diversity and innovation, ultimately eroding the competitive advantage that AI was intended to create.
