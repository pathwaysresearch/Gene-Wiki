# Notifications and Gamification

## Definition
Notifications and gamification are popular design approaches used by product designers to increase user engagement with digital products and services.

## Mechanism of Action
These approaches work by exploiting and amplifying inherent human vulnerabilities. For example, they tap into the human need for social approval or our inability to resist immediate gratification, even when we recognize it comes with long-term costs.

## Consequences
The use of these techniques can lead to behaviors where user actions contradict their stated words or intentions. The design encourages compulsive engagement, which can be detrimental to users' well-being or long-term goals.
