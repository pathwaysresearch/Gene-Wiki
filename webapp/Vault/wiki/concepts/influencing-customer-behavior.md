# Influencing Customer Behavior

## Overview
Influencing customer behavior is identified as one of the newest strategic objectives for AI. This archetype involves the deliberate use of AI systems to change how customers act, including their patterns of purchasing, socializing, consuming information, and sharing information.

## Examples and Commercial Impact
Companies such as Google, Facebook, and TikTok are cited as having achieved astounding commercial and behavioral impact using this AI strategy. The intended behavioral changes they have driven have made these companies enormously successful and fast-growing from a financial standpoint.

## Unintended Consequences
While financially successful, the application of AI to influence behavior has also led to significant unintended negative outcomes. These consequences, which have attracted the attention of legislators and other observers, include political and social polarization, the widespread sharing of misinformation, cyberbullying, and adverse mental health effects like distraction, insecurity, and depression.
