# Augmentative Behaviors

## Definition
Augmentative Behaviors describe a collaborative mode of AI-human interaction where the AI's role is to enhance human capabilities rather than to execute tasks autonomously. This approach emphasizes partnership and co-creation between the user and the AI.

## Interaction Patterns
Based on an analysis of conversations with the LLM Claude, Handa et al. (2025) identified three patterns of augmentative behavior. **Task Iteration** involves a collaborative refinement process, such as a user asking the AI to draft a marketing strategy and then providing feedback to improve it. **Learning** refers to using the AI for knowledge acquisition and understanding, exemplified by a user asking, "Can you explain how neural networks work?" Finally, **Validation** involves using the AI to verify and improve work, such as asking it to check the logic of an SQL query and suggest improvements.

## Context of Study
This concept is a core component of a classification framework from Handa et al. (2025), which categorizes user interactions with LLMs. It stands in contrast to Automative Behaviors and highlights the collaborative potential of AI in professional workflows.
