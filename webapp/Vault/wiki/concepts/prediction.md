# Prediction

## Definition
Prediction is the process of filling in missing information. It takes information you have, often called “data,” and uses it to generate information you don't have. The text emphasizes that prediction is not just about generating information about the future, but can also be used to generate information about the present or the past.

## Role in Decision Making
Prediction is described as a foundational input for decision-making. The text argues that better prediction leads to better information, which in turn enables better decision-making and improved outcomes. It frames machine prediction as "artificially generated useful information," comparing it to "intelligence" in the espionage sense of obtaining useful information. Examples like fraud detection and language translation illustrate how improved prediction enhances activities.

## Economic Impact
A central theme is that recent advances in AI have dramatically reduced the cost of quality-adjusted prediction. As prediction becomes cheaper, its usefulness is being discovered for a remarkably broad range of activities, enabling new applications like machine language translation that were previously unimaginable. This economic shift is expected to usher in dramatic changes to business and personal lives, similar to the transformation brought about by cheap arithmetic from computers.
