# Synthetic Data

## Definition and Purpose
Synthetic data is presented as a key tool enabled by Generative AI for market research. Its primary purpose is to serve as an alternative to "no data," allowing researchers to conduct analyses and generate insights in situations where collecting real-world data is impractical or impossible.

## Key Applications
The text highlights its application in reaching audiences that are either hard-to-sample due to their niche characteristics or are restricted from direct research. By generating synthetic data that models these populations, researchers can explore their potential beliefs and behaviors.

## Distinction from Digital Twins
A crucial distinction is made between general synthetic personas and "true digital twins." While both are forms of synthetic data, the text specifies that digital twins are more advanced models built from data on real, specific individuals, whereas synthetic personas may be more generalized.
