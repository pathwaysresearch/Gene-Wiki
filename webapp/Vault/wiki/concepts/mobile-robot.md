# Mobile Robot

## Overview
The mobile robot constitutes a primary category of robots, distinguished from anchored manipulators by its ability to move through its environment. Their function is defined by their mobility.

## Locomotion
Mobile robots achieve movement using a variety of mechanisms, most commonly wheels or legs. This allows them to traverse their surroundings to perform tasks that are not confined to a single, fixed location.

## Types and Applications
Mobile robots are used for tasks such as delivering food in hospitals and moving containers at loading docks. The category includes several sub-types, such as Unmanned Ground Vehicles (UGVs) that drive autonomously, planetary rovers used for space exploration on planets like Mars, and Unmanned Air Vehicles (UAVs) used for surveillance and crop-spraying.
