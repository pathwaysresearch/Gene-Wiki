# Transformer Architecture

## Definition
The Transformer is a novel neural network architecture that relies on a self-attention mechanism to process input data. The text refers to it as the catalyst for the "transformer revolution" that underpins modern AI systems like ChatGPT.

## Origins
The text explicitly states that the foundational paper that started this revolution, titled "Attention is All You Need," was written by researchers at Google. This highlights that the breakthrough originated within the company that was then the undisputed leader in the AI field.

## Significance
This architecture is presented as the key technological leap that enabled the current wave of generative AI. The text points out the irony that despite Google researchers creating it, the company found itself "playing defense" against competitors like OpenAI that successfully capitalized on the technology to build products like ChatGPT.
