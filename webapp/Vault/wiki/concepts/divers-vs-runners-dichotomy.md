# Divers vs. Runners Dichotomy

## Overview
Stefano Puntoni introduces the 'Divers vs. Runners' dichotomy as a model to explain the sharp divide often observed between two types of professionals involved in analytics. While a simplification, it captures the different motivations and orientations of those who work with data versus those who use its outputs to make business decisions. This difference becomes even more stark when dealing with more complicated data.

## The 'Divers'
This profile represents the analysts. 'Divers' are individuals who are intrigued by data, viewing it as a puzzle they want to solve. They are excited by the process of opening spreadsheets, creating cross-tabs, plotting information, and running models. Their core motivation is the understanding that good evidence is essential for making good decisions, and they are comfortable immersing themselves in the technical details of the data.

## The 'Runners'
This profile represents the decision-makers and managers. 'Runners' are focused on the overarching business objective and are driven to make the best possible decision and move on, recognizing that the clock is ticking. They operate with limited time and resources, and their primary concern is making a tangible difference and achieving an outcome. Their orientation is towards action and impact, rather than the analytical process itself.
