# Human Exceptionalism

## A Shifting Discourse
The text describes a recent and fascinating shift in the discourse surrounding human exceptionalism in both popular and high culture. The central argument is that the reference point for comparison has changed, which in turn has changed the perceived basis of human uniqueness.

## From Cognition to Emotion
Historically, when humans were compared to other animals, emphasis was placed on cognitive abilities as the key differentiator. However, with the rise of AI, the reference comparison category has become artificial intelligence. As machines begin to match high-order human cognitive abilities, discussions of human exceptionalism have pivoted toward the realm of emotions and social intelligence, as this is what is seen to differentiate humans from AI.

## Implications for Value and Well-being
This shift is expected to have far-reaching implications for choices, well-being, and self-concept. The text predicts that in both consumption and work situations, people will increasingly seek refuge in and place higher value on the experiential and relational aspects of their inner and social lives.
