# Jagged Capability Frontier

## Definition
The concept of a "jagged capability frontier" is used to describe the performance of AI in high-end knowledge work. It posits that an AI's utility is not uniform across all types of tasks; instead, it functions as a powerful tool for tasks within its capabilities but can negatively impact performance on tasks that lie beyond its frontier (Chunk 14).

## Dual Role of AI
This jagged frontier results in a dual role for AI. For tasks within its frontier, AI acts as a "booster," enhancing efficiency and productivity. For tasks outside its frontier, it can act as a "disruptor," negatively impacting performance. The Ssrn 4573321 study was designed to test this dual role by observing AI's impact on different types of tasks (Chunk 14).

## Experimental Validation
The study operationalized this concept by designing two types of tasks for knowledge workers: "inside-the-frontier" tasks where AI was expected to excel, and "outside-the-frontier" tasks where it was not. The differing results on these tasks, such as increased speed on the former and decreased correctness on the latter, provide empirical support for the jagged capability frontier model (Chunk 8, 10, 14).
