# AI Adoption Imperative

## The Inevitability of Adoption

Karim Lakhani argues that for most companies, adopting AI and digital technologies into their core functions is not a choice but a necessity. He suggests that the business world must catch up to the personal lives of its executives and employees, who are already living in an "AI age" through their daily interactions with consumer technology like smartphones and apps.

## The Consumer-Enterprise Disconnect

A key driver of this imperative is the stark contrast between consumer expectations and enterprise performance. In their personal lives, people expect near-instantaneous service, such as a ride-sharing car arriving in minutes or a transaction dispute being resolved automatically. Lakhani points out the disconnect when the same executives are satisfied with internal company processes where a customer service interaction might take two weeks or onboarding a new vendor could take six months.

## Overcoming Resistance

The imperative requires companies to move beyond a mindset of resistance, such as banning tools like ChatGPT. Instead, leaders must actively embrace the technology. The recommended path is to initiate experimentation, run use case analyses, and provide training to the entire workforce to integrate AI effectively and bridge the gap between consumer-grade experiences and internal business processes.
