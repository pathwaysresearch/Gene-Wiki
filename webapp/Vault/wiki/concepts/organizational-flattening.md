# Organizational Flattening

## Definition
Organizational flattening is a process where a company's hierarchical structure becomes less complex, typically involving the removal of layers of middle management. This results in a 'flatter' organization with fewer levels between entry-level employees and top executives.

## Link to AI Investment
The text connects organizational flattening to increased investment in Artificial Intelligence, citing the findings of Babina et al. (2022). The study's own results, which show wage declines for professionals (a group that can include managers) and wage increases for support workers, are presented as being consistent with this trend.

## Mechanism
The underlying mechanism for AI-driven organizational flattening is the empowerment of workers. As technology like AI makes workers more adept at solving problems independently, the need for direct supervision and coordination from middle managers decreases. This increased worker autonomy facilitates a shift towards a more streamlined and less hierarchical organizational structure.
