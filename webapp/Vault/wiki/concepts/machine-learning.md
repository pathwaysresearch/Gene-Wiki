# Machine Learning

## Definition
Machine Learning (ML) is a branch of artificial intelligence (AI) where systems use algorithms to learn from data, identify patterns, and make decisions or predictions without being explicitly programmed for the task. This approach contrasts with traditional programming, or "Software 1.0," where engineers formally specify problems and design explicit algorithms.

## How It Works
ML systems are trained on data, often generated by human workers. By observing many examples of a task being performed, such as making sales pitches or diagnosing a patient, these models can implicitly learn the specific behaviors and patterns associated with successful outcomes. The process involves amassing training data and feeding it into an ML algorithm, which then builds its own operational model.

## Key Applications
Large Language Models (LLMs) and the tools built around them, such as ChatGPT, are cited as an increasingly important application of machine learning. These models, which generate new content, are a form of generative AI and represent a significant advancement in the field.
