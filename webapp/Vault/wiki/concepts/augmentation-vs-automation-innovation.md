# Augmentation vs. Automation Innovation

## Definition
The text presents a framework that categorizes technological innovations, specifically those documented in patents, into two types: augmentation and automation. Augmentation innovations are those that enhance human capabilities and lead to the creation of new work, while automation innovations replace human labor in existing tasks.

## Impact on Employment
The analysis shows a clear divergence in the labor market impact of these two types of innovation. Exposure to augmentation patents is positively correlated with employment growth and the emergence of new occupational titles. Conversely, exposure to automation patents is strongly and negatively correlated with employment growth. A regression for the 1980-2018 period quantifies this, showing a positive coefficient for augmentation exposure (AugX) and a negative one for automation exposure (AutomX) on employment change.

## Measurement and Evidence
The study measures exposure to these innovations by linking patent data to specific industries and occupations. The relationship between exposure to augmentation patents and the creation of new job titles is visualized in scatter plots (Figure 6), which generally show a positive correlation. The differential impact on employment growth is further illustrated by bin scatters in Chunk 30, which plot the conditional correlation between employment growth and exposure to each type of innovation.
