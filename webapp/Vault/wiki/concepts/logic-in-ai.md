# Logic (in AI)

## Role in Knowledge-Based Agents
In artificial intelligence, logic serves as a general class of representations that form the foundation for knowledge-based agents. It allows an agent to move beyond simple state representations, like the variable/value assignments in CSPs, to a more powerful system where information can be combined and recombined to suit a wide variety of purposes.

## Key Capabilities
Agents built on a logical foundation can achieve competence quickly by being told or learning new knowledge about their environment. They are adaptable, as they can accept new tasks in the form of explicitly described goals and can respond to changes in the environment by updating the relevant knowledge in their knowledge base.

## Types of Logic
The text introduces logic as a broad category of representation. It specifically mentions propositional logic as a foundational type that illustrates the basic concepts of logic and is supported by well-developed inference technologies. It also notes that other formalisms, such as first-order logic, are more expressive.
