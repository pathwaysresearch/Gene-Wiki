# AI Capability Maturity Model

## Overview
An AI Capability Maturity Model is a structured approach used to evaluate an organization's progress in adopting and leveraging artificial intelligence. Similar to maturity models for other business capabilities, it provides a path for advancement, which is often organized into five distinct levels of maturity.

## Assessment Factors
An organization's maturity in AI is determined by a variety of factors. Key considerations include the breadth of AI use cases implemented across the enterprise and the diversity of different AI technologies being employed. The level of engagement and sponsorship from senior leaders is also a critical factor in assessing maturity.

## Key Indicators of Maturity
Further indicators of a high level of maturity include the central role of data in all enterprise decision-making and the extent of available AI resources, such as data, skilled people, and technology. The model also evaluates the proportion of AI initiatives that are in full production deployment versus those that are still pilots or experiments. Finally, it assesses the degree to which AI is linked to the transformation of the core business strategy and whether formal policies are in place to ensure the ethical use of AI.
