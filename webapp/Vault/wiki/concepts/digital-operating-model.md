# Digital Operating Model

## Overview
A digital operating model represents a new kind of firm architecture designed to release the full potential of digital networks, data, algorithms, and AI. Its emergence is transforming competition, overwhelming traditional companies, and changing how industries function. This model involves rearchitecting how a firm works, from gathering and using data to making and executing operating decisions.

## Key Characteristics
Digital operating models possess intrinsic potential for scalability, scope amplification, and learning that analog processes lack. Increasing levels of digitization and AI can dramatically improve the scalability of a business, allowing its value to increase rapidly with the number of users or their engagement. These models can learn and improve themselves in ways that are not limited by the traditional operating architectures of organizations.

## Competitive Impact
When a firm with a digital operating model collides with a traditional company, it can overwhelm the status quo. Traditional players that cannot adapt are often the first losers, as seen in the case of Kodak, which was disrupted not by a direct competitor but by smartphone and social network firms. Companies like Amazon, Facebook, and Tencent exemplify the power of this model to create and capture immense value.
