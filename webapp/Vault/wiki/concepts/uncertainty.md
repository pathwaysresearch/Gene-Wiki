# Uncertainty

## Role in Business Strategy
The text identifies managing uncertainty as a core function of business strategy (3, 103–110, 165). Businesses develop strategies and structures, such as contracts (170–171), to operate effectively in the face of uncertainty. The ability to reduce uncertainty is therefore highly valuable.

## AI's Impact on Uncertainty
Artificial intelligence, by making prediction cheap and accessible, provides a powerful new tool for reducing uncertainty. This has profound implications for business boundaries and operations (168–170). By turning uncertain variables into predictable probabilities, AI allows for more efficient and automated processes.

## Industry Examples
The book illustrates the management of uncertainty with several examples. These include the airline industry's handling of weather-related disruptions (168–169), the challenge of unknown delivery times in e-commerce (157–158), and the use of airport lounges to manage the uncertainty of passenger arrivals (105–106).
