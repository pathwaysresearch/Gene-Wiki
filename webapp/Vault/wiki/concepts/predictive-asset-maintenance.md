# Predictive Asset Maintenance

## Overview
Predictive asset maintenance was one of the first AI use cases in industrial companies and remains the most popular in the energy, resources, and industrials segment. The technique involves using AI models to forecast the need for maintenance on equipment before a failure occurs, enabling proactive intervention.

## How It Works
The system relies on data from sensors that indicate the early signs of a potential failure or conditions that could lead to one. AI algorithms analyze this stream of sensor data to predict when maintenance will be required, allowing companies to schedule repairs before a breakdown happens.

## Scale of Application
Leading companies in the energy and industrial sectors are applying this technique on a massive scale. For example, Shell already monitors ten thousand pieces of machinery for signs of trouble using this AI-powered approach and is planning to expand this number significantly.
