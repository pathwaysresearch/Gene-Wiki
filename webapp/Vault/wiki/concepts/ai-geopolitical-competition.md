# AI Geopolitical Competition

## The Premise
AI geopolitical competition is fueled by the belief that leadership in artificial intelligence is critical for future global influence. This idea is captured in a 2017 assertion by Russian president Vladimir Putin: "Whoever becomes the leader in this sphere will become the ruler of the world." This perspective frames AI not just as a technology but as a source of colossal opportunities and national power.

## National Strategies
Countries can leverage national policies to gain an advantage in the global AI race. These strategies include designing a favorable regulatory environment to encourage innovation and directing government expenditure towards AI research and development. Such targeted policies aim to give a country's domestic businesses an advantage in developing and deploying AI.

## The Global Landscape
At the time of writing, the text identifies the United States as the leader in both university research and commercial application of AI. The U.S. government under the Obama administration also demonstrated a significant focus on AI, with multiple agencies preparing for the technology's commercial-grade arrival. This indicates that major world powers are actively positioning themselves to compete for leadership in the AI era.
