# Real-Time Streaming Data Environment

## Definition
A real-time streaming data environment is an infrastructure that processes and analyzes vast amounts of data as it is generated, rather than in batches at later intervals. Capital One is increasingly moving toward this model to power its operations and decision-making processes.

## Contrast with Batch Processing
This approach marks a significant shift from the bank's earlier analytics-focused period, which, according to Capital One CIO Mike Eason, relied on batch data processed monthly, weekly, or nightly at best. The modern environment must handle high-velocity streaming data from sources like web and mobile transactions, ATMs, and card purchases.

## Strategic Importance
The move to a streaming environment is central to Capital One's goal of becoming a "real-time decisioning company." Analyzing data as it comes in is critical for applications such as fraud prevention and meeting immediate customer needs. While a data lake is still used for storage, the emphasis has shifted to in-stream analysis.
