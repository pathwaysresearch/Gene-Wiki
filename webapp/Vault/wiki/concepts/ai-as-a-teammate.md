# AI as a Teammate

## Definition
The concept of "AI as a Teammate" describes the evolution of artificial intelligence's role in the workplace from a simple tool to a collaborative partner. Instead of just executing commands, AI in this role actively assists, provides coordination, and offers cross-disciplinary knowledge, transforming the nature of work.

## How It Works
The text explains that just as a human team provides access to diverse skills and coordination, AI tools are beginning to fulfill the same function for an individual user. This transformation allows a single person to access a breadth of expertise that would have previously required a full team of human specialists, effectively lowering the cost of expertise.

## Significance
This shift has profound implications for organizational structure and individual productivity. It suggests new configurations of human-AI collaboration will become central to value creation. The emergence of AI agents that can be brought into the workplace by employees is a direct manifestation of this trend, where individuals are empowered with personalized AI teammates.
