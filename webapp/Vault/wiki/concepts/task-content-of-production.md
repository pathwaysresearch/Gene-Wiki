# Task Content of Production

## Definition

The task content of production refers to the specific allocation of all tasks required in the production process to the different factors of production, namely capital and labor. It is a central concept in the task-based framework, which emphasizes that technology's primary impact is on this allocation, rather than just on the general productivity of capital or labor.

## How Technology Affects It

Technology can alter the task content of production in two fundamental ways. First, automation shifts the task content against labor by enabling capital to perform tasks previously done by workers. Second, the introduction of new, labor-intensive tasks shifts the task content in favor of labor by creating new activities for which workers are essential.

## Significance

Changes in the task content of production are a primary determinant of how technology affects labor demand and the labor share of income. The displacement effect of automation, for example, is a direct result of a shift in the task content against labor. Understanding these shifts is crucial for analyzing the broader consequences of technological change for employment and wages.
