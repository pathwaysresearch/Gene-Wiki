# Generative AI

## Definition
Generative AI (genAI) refers to advanced algorithms designed to create new content, including text, images, audio, or video. This capability defies the traditional expectation of algorithms, which were originally designed to automate repetitive and routine tasks.

## Application in Ideation
In the context of consumer research, genAI is increasingly used for creative ideation. Its role is underexplored but promising, with its effectiveness stemming from its productivity and semantic breadth. The text proposes that genAI can function both as a primary ideator and as a catalyst to prompt and enhance human creativity, facilitating the discovery of radical insights through human-AI collaboration.

## Key Ideation Capabilities
The text highlights two core characteristics that make genAI, particularly Large Language Models, effective for ideation. The first is productivity, which is analogous to the human creativity pathway of persistence. The second is semantic breadth, the capacity to generate ideas spanning widely different concepts, which is analogous to the human pathway of flexibility.
