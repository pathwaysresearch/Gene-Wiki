# Access Technology

## Definition
An access technology is defined as a complement to existing systems that permits those systems to be used more effectively. This can manifest as making the system easier, more convenient, or safer to use, or by expanding its use to a broader set of users.

## Mechanism of Impact
Access technologies function by lowering the cost of system access and increasing its convenience. For example, voice-based user interfaces in environments like kitchens or automobiles reduce the inconvenience or danger of using traditional UIs, thereby increasing the use of the underlying system. This lowering of access costs is a key driver of their value.

## Conditions for Rapid Impact
Historically, access technologies have made a large and rapid impact when the business community had already clearly identified the areas where existing systems needed better or broader access. This high degree of 'visibility' for the problem allows the new access technology to be applied quickly and effectively. The widespread adoption of the Internet for commerce and GUI-based PCs are cited as major successes that met these initial conditions.
