# AI as a Complementary Technology

## Core Principle
This approach advocates for viewing and designing AI not as a tool to mimic or replace human experts, but as a technology that complements their existing skills and knowledge. The central idea is to focus on how AI can make a human expert maximally productive and their accumulated experience maximally useful, rather than on automating their tasks away.

## Strategic Framing
Adopting this perspective provides a more inspiring and productive framing for AI's potential within an organization. Instead of focusing solely on cost-cutting, it shifts the conversation toward effectiveness and creating better work. This helps to mitigate the fear-based narratives, often referencing science fiction like "Terminator," that can dominate discussions about AI.

## Implementation Guidance
To implement this strategy, leadership must communicate with an authentic voice, explaining not just the company's goals but also the direct benefits for employees. This involves showing workers how AI can be a positive force for them. Success requires a combination of top-down vision from leadership and the mobilization of bottom-up energy from the employees who actually perform the work.
