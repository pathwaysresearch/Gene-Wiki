# Epistemological Commitment

## Definition
The epistemological commitment of a formal language defines the possible states of knowledge an agent can have concerning the truth of its sentences. It specifies what an agent is able to believe about a given statement.

## Comparison Across Languages
Languages vary in their epistemological commitments. Propositional logic, first-order logic, and temporal logic share a commitment where sentences can be considered true, false, or unknown. In contrast, probability theory's commitment is to a degree of belief between 0 and 1, while fuzzy logic commits to facts having a degree of truth within the same range.

## Role in Reasoning
This commitment is crucial as it dictates the nature of belief and reasoning for an agent using the language. It determines whether knowledge is treated as categorical (true/false) or requires handling degrees of uncertainty or truth.
