# Multisided Platform Business Model

## Overview
The text describes multisided platforms as a business model where companies manage relationships between distinct groups of participants, such as buyers and sellers. This model has been a key driver of growth and profitability for digital native companies over the last two decades.

## Role of AI
AI is presented as a critical enabler for platform business models. Machine learning is used to analyze data from all platform participants to effectively match customers with the products and services they need or want. AI also facilitates the personalization of customer offerings and powers efficient customer service through tools like intelligent agents and chatbots.

## Business Significance
According to research cited in the text, multisided platforms achieve the highest valuations of any business model, with annual revenue multiples that can be more than four times those of legacy business models. The text identifies leading platform companies like Facebook, Airbnb, Amazon, Google, and Uber as also being world leaders in the application of AI.
