# AI as a Booster and Disruptor

## Definition
This framework, proposed in the Ssrn 4573321 paper, posits that AI has a dual role in high-end knowledge work. It acts as a "booster" by enhancing efficiency and productivity on tasks within its capability frontier, and as a "disruptor" by negatively impacting performance on tasks beyond its frontier (Chunk 14).

## The Booster Role in Practice
The study's experiment demonstrated the booster effect on "inside-the-frontier" tasks. Subjects using GPT-4 completed a higher percentage of subtasks and finished significantly faster than the control group. This supports the view of AI as a productivity-enhancing tool for appropriate tasks (Chunk 8, 9, 14).

## The Disruptor Role in Practice
The disruptor effect was observed in "outside-the-frontier" tasks. Here, AI assistance led to a significant decrease in the correctness of subjects' recommendations compared to the control group. This finding highlights the performance risks of applying AI to tasks for which it is not suited, confirming its potential as a disruptor (Chunk 10, 14).
