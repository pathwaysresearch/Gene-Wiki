# Mass Production

## Core Principles

Mass production is built on concepts like specialization, focus, and standardization. These principles allow manufacturing operating models to enjoy increasing economies of scale, where efficiencies and quality greatly increase with the volume of the operation. High production volumes also enable learning, which further increases production efficiencies.

## Impact on Industries

In the twentieth century, mass production took off rapidly in manufacturing in the United States and Europe, virtually wiping out traditional crafts in sectors from weapons to textiles. The text notes that these concepts also spread widely across service industries.

## Examples in Services

The text provides several examples of mass production principles in the service sector. Supermarkets rely on process standardization and economies of scale in purchasing and delivery. Fast-food franchises like McDonald's depend on highly repeatable work and scale efficiencies in supply chain and food preparation. These principles also brought efficiencies to hotel chains, banks, hospitals, and airlines.
