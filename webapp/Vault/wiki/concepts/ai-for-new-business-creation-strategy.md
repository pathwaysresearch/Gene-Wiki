# AI for New Business Creation (Strategy)

## Definition
This strategy involves using AI to facilitate new business creation and entry into new markets. Companies employing this approach build on their existing strengths with AI to offer new types of products and services or to deliver existing ones in more effective ways.

## Characteristics of Adopters
A 2021 survey mentioned in the text found that high-achieving organizations (termed "transformers and pathseekers") were more likely to emphasize such growth-oriented goals. This contrasts with lower-achieving organizations that tended to focus more on efficiency or cost-reduction goals.

## Business Impact
The text cites an MIT Sloan Management Review analysis which found that companies using AI primarily to explore and create new forms of business value are 2.7 times more likely to improve their ability to compete with AI than those who use it mainly to improve existing processes. Loblaw's use of AI to expand into the health-care industry is given as an example.
