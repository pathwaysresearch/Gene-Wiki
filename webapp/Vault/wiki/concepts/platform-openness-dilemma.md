# Platform Openness Dilemma

## The Core Dilemma
Platform companies must balance the competing needs for openness and control. An open platform can foster a large ecosystem of innovators and developers, creating value for users. However, this openness also creates vulnerabilities, making the platform susceptible to harm, such as the spread of malicious applications.

## Open vs. Closed Models
The contrast between Apple's and Google's mobile ecosystems illustrates this dilemma. Apple maintains a relatively closed platform for iOS and the App Store, with strict rules and a formal approval process for apps, which results in better security and fewer malicious apps. In contrast, the more open Google Android and Google Play Store have been found to distribute significantly more malware, often unbeknownst to Google, infecting millions of users.

## Complications with Ad Platforms
The dilemma is further complicated for platforms that share third-party assets like consumer data, particularly those with advertising models. Ad platforms like Google Ads and Facebook ads use sophisticated APIs and data to help advertisers target consumers. While this can provide value by showing relevant ads, it raises difficult questions about where to draw the line between offering relevance and violating user privacy.
