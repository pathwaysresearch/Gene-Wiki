# AI Automation vs. Augmentation

## Definition
The distinction between AI automation and augmentation categorizes AI applications based on their primary effect on human tasks. Automative AI substitutes for labor by performing tasks previously done by humans, while augmentative AI complements labor, enhancing worker productivity and capabilities.

## Impact on Employment
The paper finds this distinction is critical for understanding AI's labor market effects, particularly for entry-level workers. Occupations where AI primarily automates work experienced significant declines in employment for young workers. In contrast, occupations where AI's use is most augmentative saw employment growth, suggesting that augmentative uses do not substitute for labor in the same way.

## Empirical Measurement
The study empirically distinguishes between these two uses by analyzing queries to the large language model Claude. The extent to which these queries substitute for or complement the tasks within an occupation is used to classify the AI application as primarily automative or augmentative. This provides a data-driven basis for linking the type of AI use to observed employment changes.
