# AI Adoption in U.S. Firms

## Overview
AI adoption refers to the use of artificial intelligence technologies by business enterprises. Research based on the Annual Business Survey investigates the characteristics of firms that adopt AI, revealing several significant correlations with firm performance, financing, innovation strategy, and owner demographics.

## Correlation with Firm Growth and Financing
Statistical analysis shows a strong positive correlation between AI adoption and firm performance. Firms that use AI tend to exhibit higher revenue growth, both in their first three years and in their most recent three years. Furthermore, AI adoption is significantly more likely in firms that are funded by venture capital and those with higher levels of startup capitalization, particularly those capitalized with over $25,000 and even more so for those over $1 million.

## Correlation with Innovation and IP Strategy
AI adoption is closely linked to a firm's innovation strategy. There is a significant positive correlation between AI use and both process innovation and product innovation. Additionally, firms that own or have pending patents, and those that rate their intellectual property as very important, are more likely to be AI adopters.

## Correlation with Owner Characteristics
The characteristics of a firm's owner also correlate with AI adoption. Owners with an advanced degree or prior business experience are more likely to lead firms that use AI. Conversely, firms with older owners (55+) show a negative correlation with AI adoption, suggesting younger entrepreneurs may be more inclined to use these technologies.
