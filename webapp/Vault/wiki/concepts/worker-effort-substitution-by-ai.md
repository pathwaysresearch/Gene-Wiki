# Worker Effort Substitution (by AI)

## Definition
Worker effort substitution is a process where an AI technology, such as ChatGPT, increases productivity primarily by taking over tasks previously performed by a human worker. This is contrasted with a complementarity model, where the AI would enhance a worker's existing skills. In the substitution model, the AI produces output of a satisfactory quality that the worker can use directly, thus reducing their own effort.

## Experimental Evidence
The study by Noy and Zhang (2023) found strong evidence for this mechanism. It was observed that 68% of participants in the treatment group reported submitting the initial output from ChatGPT without making any edits. Furthermore, on average, these participants were only active on the task for three minutes after pasting in a large block of text, presumably from the AI, indicating minimal human refinement.

## Impact on Task Structure
This substitution effect was observed to restructure the nature of the work. For workers using ChatGPT, the proportion of time spent on "Rough-Drafting" fell dramatically from approximately 55% to 30%, while time spent on "Editing" increased from approximately 25% to 40%. The AI effectively automated the initial draft creation, shifting the human's role towards idea generation and refinement.

## Implications
The paper suggests that if AI primarily substitutes for worker effort, it could potentially lead to a decrease in the demand for workers. This could have adverse distributional effects as capital owners who deploy the technology may gain at the expense of workers whose labor is substituted.
