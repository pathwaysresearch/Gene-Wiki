# Agentic Era

## Core Concept
The Agentic Era describes a fundamental shift in organizational structure and management driven by AI. This new model challenges traditional corporate structures by introducing AI agents, both digital and physical, as core operational participants. This shift necessitates rethinking established principles of leadership, teamwork, and organization design.

## New Team Structures
In the Agentic Era, teams are no longer exclusively human. The text describes a future with new interaction dynamics, including human-agent teams and agent-agent teams. It is also suggested that the human component of these teams may become leaner or smaller, as agents take on more responsibilities.

## Implications for Business and Education
This paradigm could enable new types of companies, such as the "one-person company," to operate at a large scale. For educators, particularly in business schools, the Agentic Era raises critical questions about curriculum. It challenges how to teach subjects like leadership and organizational management when the primary interactions may no longer be human-to-human.
