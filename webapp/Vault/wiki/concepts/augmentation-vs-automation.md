# Augmentation vs. Automation

## Framework Overview
The concepts of augmentation and automation describe a potential pathway for the integration of Large Language Models (LLMs) into the workforce. Citing Huang and Rust (2018), the paper posits that the initial focus of LLM adoption will be on augmentation, where the technology assists and enhances human workers, before a later transition to full automation of tasks.

## Adoption Dynamics
The adoption of LLMs is influenced by several factors beyond pure technological capability. These include the level of human trust in model outputs, the cost and flexibility of the technology, and the preferences of workers and firms. The paper suggests that for many tasks, time savings and seamless application may be more important drivers of adoption than improvements in quality.

## Potential Trajectory
A possible scenario for this transition involves an initial augmentation phase where jobs may become more precarious, for example, with writers increasingly working as freelancers using LLMs as tools. This phase could then be followed by a shift towards full automation of those same tasks as the technology matures and trust in it grows.
