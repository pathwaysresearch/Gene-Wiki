# Generative AI Assistance in Customer Service

## Overview
Generative AI assistance in this context refers to a system that provides real-time, AI-generated suggestions to customer service agents during their text-based conversations with customers. The study analyzes the deployment of such a tool to understand its impact on agent productivity, communication quality, and overall worker experience.

## Impact on Agent Performance
The implementation of the AI assistant led to significant improvements in agent productivity. Key metrics like Average Handle Time decreased, while Chats Per Hour increased. This culminated in a notable rise in overall resolutions per hour. The study found that these productivity gains were most pronounced for newer and lower-skilled agents, suggesting that the AI helps them learn and perform more like experienced agents.

## Impact on Communication Quality
The study used another LLM, Gemini, to score the quality of agent communication on metrics of 'comprehensibility' and 'native fluency'. Access to the AI assistant resulted in a marked and significant improvement in these scores. The improvement was particularly large for workers based in the Philippines, indicating the tool helps non-native speakers adopt more standard communication patterns.

## Impact on Worker Experience
Beyond productivity, the research examined the AI's effect on the work environment by analyzing worker attrition as a proxy for job satisfaction. Access to AI assistance was associated with a strong reduction in attrition rates, especially for agents with less than six months of experience. This suggests that the tool may improve the work experience, particularly for new employees, by reducing stress and increasing their effectiveness.
