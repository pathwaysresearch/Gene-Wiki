# AI Industry Exposure (AIIE)

## Definition
The AI Industry Exposure (AIIE) is a measure designed to quantify the exposure of an entire industry to developments in artificial intelligence. It aggregates occupational-level exposure to provide a broader view of how AI might impact different sectors of the economy.

## Construction
The AIIE is constructed directly using the AI Occupational Exposure (AIOE) measure. The AIIE represents the industry-level composite of the AIOE scores for the occupations that constitute that industry's workforce.

## Application in Research
The AIIE measure is intended for use by management, organization, and strategy scholars. It can be applied to study how artificial intelligence may alter industry trajectories and to analyze the strategic implications of AI adoption at the industry level.
