# Decision Theory

## Definition
Decision theory is a formal and complete framework for making decisions, whether economic or otherwise, under uncertainty. It operates in cases where outcomes can be described probabilistically.

## Theoretical Foundations
The framework is a synthesis of two other fields. It incorporates utility theory, which provides a mathematical treatment of "preferred outcomes," and combines it with probability theory to handle the uncertainty associated with those outcomes.

## Application
Decision theory provides a structured way to analyze choices and maximize payoff in situations where the results are not guaranteed. It is a foundational concept for agents that need to act rationally in uncertain environments.
