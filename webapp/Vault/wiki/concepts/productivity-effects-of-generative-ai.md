# Productivity Effects of Generative AI

## Definition
The productivity effects of generative AI refer to the measurable changes in worker efficiency and output quality resulting from the use of AI tools in professional tasks. In the context of the Noy and Zhang (2023) study, this was examined through an experiment on mid-level professional writing tasks assigned to 444 college-educated professionals.

## Key Findings
The study found that providing access to ChatGPT led to substantial and positive productivity effects. On average, the time required to complete a writing task decreased by 0.8 standard deviations, while the quality of the output, as graded by professional evaluators, increased by 0.4 standard deviations. These effects were observed across various occupations, including marketers, grant writers, and consultants.

## Impact on Inequality
A significant finding was that generative AI compresses the productivity distribution among workers. The tool was found to provide greater benefits to low-ability workers, raising their output quality and reducing their time spent, thereby decreasing the inequality between the highest and lowest-performing professionals in the experiment.

## Subjective Outcomes
Exposure to the generative AI tool was also found to have subjective effects on workers. In the context of the experiment, it increased participants' job satisfaction and self-efficacy. It also heightened both their concern about being replaced by automation and their excitement about being enhanced by it.
