# AI for Ideation

## Core Function
AI for Ideation involves using artificial intelligence systems as a collaborative partner in creative processes like brainstorming to help people generate "better ideas." This application leverages AI's capacity to process information and generate novel combinations to augment human creativity, rather than simply automating a routine task. It is presented as an exciting and high-value use case for modern AI.

## Strategic Importance
This application is highlighted as a powerful example of focusing on the "effectiveness piece" of AI implementation, as opposed to focusing exclusively on cost-cutting. By enabling the creation of better work and better products, AI for ideation demonstrates a path to value creation that is not solely about efficiency gains. The organic adoption of AI for brainstorming suggests that users intuitively see its potential to improve the quality and innovation of their work.

## Relation to Human-AI Collaboration
Using AI for ideation perfectly embodies the principle of AI as a complementary tool for human experts. It is not about producing "cheaper work" but about fostering a partnership that leads to superior outcomes. This approach aligns with the broader strategy of making human knowledge and experience more productive and valuable with the help of technology.
