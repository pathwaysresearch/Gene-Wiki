# Transhumanism

## Core Idea

The text defines transhumanist ideas as those which 'emphasize technological progress as an unstoppable force that alters human experience.' These ideas are described as being deeply inscribed in contemporary AI experiences, from the promise that a Roomba vacuum could perform tasks more effectively than humans to the idea that 23andMe could aid in creating genetically optimized offspring.

## Systemic Consequences

The text argues that the 'transhumanist preoccupation with Promethean aims' underlying many AI experiences can lead to negative societal outcomes. A key consequence is 'systemic dehumanization,' where human perception of mastery over the environment is undermined by being subject to unilaterally imposed specifications from technology.

## Economic and Social Impact

According to the text, transhumanist ideals can endorse a glorification of capitalism's endless creativity while treating 'destructiveness and human replacement as normal costs of doing business.' An economic obsession with 'perfection,' 'progress,' and 'efficiency' is predicted to promote the rise of the 'useless class'—individuals whose skills are no longer developed or demanded—which in turn fundamentally erodes democracy and social justice.
