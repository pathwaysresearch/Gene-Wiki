# AI's Impact on Human Identity

## Overview

The development and diffusion of AI raises fundamental questions about how humans perceive themselves. Beyond the instrumental use of technology, AI's growing capabilities can impact our sense of identity, which is often constructed through the activities we perform, the hobbies we pursue, and the passions we hold. As machines become capable of performing these tasks, the personas we build for ourselves and others can be threatened.

## Identity in Specific Domains

The impact of AI on identity is particularly relevant in specific life domains such as consumption and work. Many activities in these areas are not performed solely for instrumental reasons to get a job done; they are also expressions of who we are. Technology and automation can challenge these identities as more of these defining activities can be performed by machines.

## A Shift in Focus

Instead of only asking what people think of AI or how to improve its acceptance, a more interesting question is how AI changes the way we think about ourselves. This perspective shifts the focus from the technology itself to its deeper psychological and sociological effects on human self-concept and the construction of personal and professional personas.
