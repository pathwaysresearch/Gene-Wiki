# Employee Attrition Reduction

## Core Finding
The analysis presented in Table A.11 demonstrates a statistically significant reduction in employee attrition after the deployment of an AI model. The "Post AI X Ever Treated" variable shows a negative coefficient of -0.0868, indicating that agents assisted by the AI are less likely to leave their jobs in a given month.

## Variation by Tenure
The reduction in attrition is most pronounced for newer employees. The effect is strongest for agents with 1-2 months of tenure (-0.121) and 0 months of tenure (-0.0952). The impact gradually diminishes as tenure increases, becoming statistically insignificant for employees with more than 12 months of experience.

## Variation by Skill Level
The AI's impact on attrition is observed across all skill quintiles, from the lowest (Q1) to the highest (Q5). The effect is statistically significant for all groups, with the largest reduction seen in the fourth quintile (Q4, -0.0936), suggesting the AI's benefits in retaining employees are widespread but not perfectly uniform across different skill levels.
