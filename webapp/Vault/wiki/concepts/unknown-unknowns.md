# Unknown Unknowns

## Definition
"Unknown unknowns" are events that are possible but are not represented in the available historical data or past experience. Because they are outside the realm of what has been seen before, their possibility is not even considered, making prediction difficult.

## Failure of Prediction
Both prediction machines and humans are relatively bad at predicting unknown unknowns. Since machines learn from past data, they are fundamentally unable to predict events for which there is no precedent in their training set. Faced with unknown unknowns, both humans and machines fail.

## Rumsfeld's Framework Context
This category, which Donald Rumsfeld described as "the ones we don't know we don't know," is identified in the text as being particularly difficult for prediction.
