# Productive Struggle

## Definition

Productive struggle is a learning process where a student is encouraged to actively work through a difficult problem on their own rather than being given the solution immediately. A thoughtful tutor, whether human or AI, will prompt the student to "struggle a little bit" and keep working on it, reinforcing the learning process through effort and engagement.

## Role in Tutoring

A key role of an effective tutor is to facilitate productive struggle. Instead of giving away the answer, the tutor provides a "little hint" or a "little nudge" only when they see that the student is truly stuck or about to disengage. This targeted support helps the student move forward without undermining the learning that comes from the struggle itself.

## Contrast with Over-Assistance

This approach is contrasted with "overenabling" or "over-assistance," where a tutor or an AI tool does the work for the student or helps them get through problem sets as quickly as possible. The text cites a study where students who used AI for quick answers performed the worst, suggesting that avoiding the struggle is detrimental to learning.
