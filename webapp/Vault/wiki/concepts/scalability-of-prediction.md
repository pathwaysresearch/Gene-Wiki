# Scalability of Prediction

## Machine Scalability
Prediction machines are highly scalable. As the frequency of predictions increases, the unit cost per prediction falls significantly. This makes them well-suited for high-volume prediction tasks.

## Human Scalability
In contrast, human prediction does not scale in the same manner. The cost and effort for a human to make a prediction does not decrease dramatically with volume.

## The Role of Data and Cognitive Models
This difference in scalability is linked to data requirements. Machines scale well with rich data for frequent predictions. Humans, however, have an advantage with thin data because they can use their cognitive models of how the world works to make predictions from small amounts of information.
