# Transformers (AI Adoption Category)

## Definition

In a recent enterprise AI survey, "transformers" are the label given to the companies with the most AI capabilities and achievements. This group comprised 28 percent of the survey sample and is considered to be well along in its AI journey, with a goal of business transformation.

## State of AI Deployment

On average, the companies in the transformers category had about six full-scale deployments of AI use cases in production. They had also achieved about seven business outcomes from their AI initiatives. This level of deployment places them in the most aggressive survey category.

## Limitations

Despite their impressive achievements, very few of the respondents in the transformers category are yet considered AI-fueled. The text notes that the number of AI-fueled companies is too small to be picked up in a broad survey. The achievements of transformers are described as being short of what a truly AI-fueled company would have, highlighting the difficulty of reaching the highest level of AI maturity.
