# Filter Bubbles

## Definition
A filter bubble is described as a significant concern related to AI, where the system filters out viewpoints that a user is not expected to agree with from their online experience. This creates a personalized information ecosystem where an individual is primarily exposed to content that reinforces their existing beliefs, such as those based on their political leanings.

## Algorithmic Mechanism
These bubbles are created by algorithms on platforms like social media that provide personalized suggestions. The text explains that because of these tailored recommendations, two individuals can have entirely different points of view and online experiences, as the content they see is curated to match their specific profiles and preferences.

## Societal Impact
The text directly links the phenomenon of filter bubbles to rising societal polarization, particularly in the United States. While acknowledging that multiple factors contribute to this trend, it suggests that AI-driven filter bubbles fuel this divisiveness by algorithmically separating people and reducing their exposure to diverse perspectives, making society feel more ideologically divided than it was decades ago.
