# Prediction (in AI)

## Definition
Prediction is defined as the process of filling in missing information. It takes information you have, referred to as “data,” and uses it to generate information you don't have. The text clarifies that this concept is not limited to forecasting the future; it can also be about the present, such as determining if a credit card transaction is fraudulent, or even the past.

## Economic Significance
The central thesis of the book is that AI's economic importance stems from its ability to make prediction dramatically cheaper. This drop in cost is analogous to how the internet lowered the cost of search, distribution, and communication. As prediction becomes cheap, it will be used more frequently and will emerge in surprising new applications, fundamentally altering business models and strategies.

## Role in Decision Making
Prediction is a critical input into decision making because it reduces uncertainty. As prediction machines become cheaper and more accurate, they can improve the quality of decisions. At a certain point, a prediction machine may become so reliable that it moves beyond simply enhancing productivity to fundamentally changing an organization's strategy.
