# Greedy Layer-Wise Pre-training

## Definition
Greedy layer-wise pre-training is a strategy used to efficiently train deep neural networks. It was presented as a breakthrough technique that addressed the long-standing difficulty of training deep networks.

## Historical Context
The strategy was introduced in 2006 by Geoffrey Hinton and his collaborators for training deep belief networks. This development is considered the start of the third wave of neural networks research, which popularized the term "deep learning." Prior to this, deep networks were generally believed to be very difficult to train.

## Impact
The introduction of greedy layer-wise pre-training was a pivotal moment. It not only enabled the training of deep belief networks but was also quickly shown by other research groups to be applicable to many other kinds of deep networks. This led to improved generalization on test examples and helped deep neural networks outperform other competing AI systems.
