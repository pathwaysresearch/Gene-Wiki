# Expert Judgment

## Definition
Expert judgment is defined as the capacity to depart from a script and improvise based on accumulated training and experience. Until the recent advent of AI, this capability was considered the exclusive province of elite human experts. The text argues that Artificial Intelligence is developing this as a "superpower," enabling it to engage in sophisticated decision-making.

## AI's Role and Applications
The paper posits that AI's primary role in the near future will be to advise, coach, and alert human decision-makers as they apply their own expert judgment. Early, low-stakes examples are already present in everyday life, such as an email application completing a sentence, a smartwatch detecting a fall, or a car's lane-assist feature. These actions involve AI interpreting intentions and guiding actions based on its training.

## Future Implications
As AI's facility in expert judgment becomes more reliable and accessible, it is expected to become a near-ubiquitous presence in working life, taking on higher-value assignments. The text suggests AI could supplement the judgment of professionals like Nurse Practitioners in a broader scope of medical tasks or help radiologists become faster and more accurate. This advance in machine capability is presented as an epochal shift with significant implications for the future of human expertise.
