# Human Replacement (AI Mindset)

## Definition
A mindset for AI development, described by Stefano Puntoni as prevalent in the tech sector, is that of "human replacement." This approach is characterized by observing a human performing a task, understanding how they do it, and then building a machine to perform that same task. This creates a competitive dynamic framed as "human or AI."

## Origin
The text suggests this way of thinking is the natural or default perspective for engineers and computer scientists. It is a direct, task-oriented approach to automation that focuses on replicating and substituting human labor with technology.

## Alternative Paradigm
This mindset is explicitly contrasted with the concept of "human flourishing," which proposes a collaborative "human and AI" model. The text argues that a business school perspective is needed to shift the conversation away from simple replacement and towards understanding how AI can be integrated into organizational workflows to augment human capabilities.
