# All-in on AI

## Definition

All-in on AI is a perspective on artificial intelligence at its most extreme, representing the most aggressive adoption, the best integration with strategy and operations, the highest business value, and the best implementation. It involves understanding what the leading adopters of AI are doing to transform their businesses.

## Implications for Business

Adopting an all-in-on-AI approach has broad implications for an organization, affecting its core strategy, business processes, technology infrastructure, corporate culture, and talent management. It requires a holistic view of how AI can be integrated into the business.

## Scope of Application

Companies that are all-in on AI eventually develop use cases across a wide variety of functions and processes, decisions, and products or services. While each individual application may not be transformative on its own, the broad collection of them can fundamentally change the company.
