# Homophily

## Definition
Homophily is a principle describing the social tendency for individuals to be drawn to and form connections with others who are similar to themselves.

## Relation to Recommendation Engines
This social tendency is mirrored in the design and function of recommendation engines. These algorithms operate on a similar principle, identifying links and relationships between products or people to predict user preferences and enhance product discovery.

## Application
By understanding and leveraging the concept of homophily, recommendation algorithms can better predict what a user might like based on the preferences of similar users or the properties of similar items. This parallels how social connections form in the real world and is a key mechanism for enhancing online discovery.
