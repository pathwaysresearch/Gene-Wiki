# The Singularity

## Overview
The Singularity is a concept referring to a future point where nonbiological intelligence will become vastly more powerful than unaided human intelligence. This event is seen by some as a moment when humans will transcend the limitations of their biological bodies and brains.

## Proponents and Vision
Advocates like Ray Kurzweil, in his book *The Singularity is Near*, view this event as a means for humanity to gain power over its own fate and mortality. He predicts that by the end of the 21st century, the nonbiological portion of our intelligence will be trillions of trillions of times more powerful than human intelligence. The transhumanist movement actively looks forward to this future.

## Potential Dangers
While proponents are optimistic, the concept also acknowledges significant risks. Kurzweil himself notes that the Singularity will also amplify humanity's ability to act on its destructive inclinations. The possibility of ultraintelligent machines poses a challenge for moral theorists and raises concerns about the end of the human race if such machines are not aligned with human values.
