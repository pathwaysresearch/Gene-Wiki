# Data Lake

## Definition
A data lake is a type of data storage architecture used by firms undertaking substantial data transformations for AI. It serves as a modern alternative to traditional data warehouses, designed to handle the large and diverse datasets required for advanced analytics.

## Key Properties
According to the text, data lakes offer two primary advantages over traditional data warehouses. They are described as being "much cheaper" and "better suited to less-structured data." This flexibility makes them a more appropriate environment for aggressive AI initiatives that rely on a wide variety of data types beyond what is typically stored in a structured warehouse.

## Example of Adoption
DBS Bank is cited as an example of a large enterprise that moved much of its data from traditional data warehouses to data lakes. This migration was a critical component of the bank's broader data transformation strategy, which was necessary to make its data environment suitable for its AI ambitions.
