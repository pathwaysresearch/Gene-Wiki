# Voice UIs

## Overview
Voice UIs (User Interfaces) are a form of AIT-based interface that originated from Internet Giants (IGs) seeking to improve consumer access to their services. They function as a key access technology, enabling easier and more convenient interaction with digital systems.

## As an Access Technology
Voice UIs exemplify the concept of an access technology by lowering the cost and increasing the convenience of using a system. They are particularly valuable in situations where traditional UIs, such as screens and keyboards, are inconvenient or unsafe to use, thereby expanding the contexts in which a system can be accessed.

## Applications and Benefits
The value of voice UIs is demonstrated in consumer environments like kitchens or automobiles, where hands-free operation is a significant benefit. This same principle has driven their adoption into professional settings, such as in health care, where voice UIs for communication systems can improve safety and efficiency by reducing the need for frequent handwashing between tasks.
