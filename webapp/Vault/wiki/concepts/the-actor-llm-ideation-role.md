# The Actor (LLM Ideation Role)

## Role Definition
"The Actor" is a specific ideation role where an LLM is used to imitate a consumer, allowing a researcher to "interview" it for ideas. The text emphasizes that this should not be treated as an actual simulation of a consumer but as a tool to gain an empathetic grasp of consumer psychology and behavior.

## How It Works
A researcher provides the LLM with a prompt describing a consumer with specific characteristics, such as an American who often attends Friendsgiving parties. The model's most probable response conforms to this description, drawing on the enormous information about consumer behavior and decision-making contained in its training corpus (e.g., social media posts).

## Applications in Research
This technique can be used to find new consumer samples worth incorporating into a study, correct errors in a study design before initiating it with real human participants, or gain ideas for specific questions to include in a qualitative interview with real consumers. The hope is that engaging with an LLM in this role may offer researchers insights about new hypotheses and phenomena to investigate.
