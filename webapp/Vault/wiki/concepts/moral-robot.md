# Moral Robot

## A Causal Approach to Morality
The concept of a moral robot is approached not through prescriptive, rule-based systems, but through a causal framework. Instead of being programmed with absolute laws, the machine would be equipped with the same cognitive abilities that humans use for moral reasoning.

## Necessary Cognitive Abilities
The text suggests that a moral robot should be endowed with abilities such as empathy, the capacity for long-term prediction of its actions' consequences, and self-restraint. With these tools, the machine would be allowed to make its own decisions, rather than rigidly following a pre-programmed ethical code.

## Contrast with Asimov's Laws
This approach is explicitly contrasted with Isaac Asimov's laws of robotics. The text argues that rule-based systems like Asimov's inevitably lead to contradictions and are not a viable path to creating a truly moral machine. The failure of such systems suggests that a more flexible, cognitive, and causal approach is necessary.
