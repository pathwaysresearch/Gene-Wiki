# Self-Determination Theory

## Framework Overview
Self-Determination Theory is a classic psychology framework which posits that human well-being emerges from three fundamental experiences: competence, autonomy, and connectedness. It provides a structured way to understand the psychological needs that contribute to a person's sense of fulfillment and motivation.

## Application to AI Adoption
In the context of artificial intelligence, this theory is leveraged to analyze the impact of generative AI on the workforce. Researcher Stefano Puntoni uses this framework to argue that generative AI can either be a boost or a detriment to these three core sources of well-being for workers. It serves as a model for understanding and anticipating the psychological threats that AI can pose in an organizational setting.

## Key Components and AI's Impact
The theory's three pillars are directly affected by AI integration. The text highlights the impact on competence, where an AI system that can perform key job functions can create a profound psychological or even an identity threat. This challenges an individual's sense of skill and self-worth, which they have invested years in developing.
