# Business Model Transformation with AI

## Definition
A business model consists of two core components: the way a company creates value for customers and the way it captures that value to make money. Business Model Transformation with AI involves a strategic analysis of how artificial intelligence and digital technologies can fundamentally enhance both of these components, requiring a deliberate rethinking of the entire model rather than just incremental improvements.

## Enhancing Value Creation
AI and digital technologies enable companies to create more value for customers by offering greater personalization, improved scalability, and a wider scope of products and services. The process involves mapping out customer journeys and identifying opportunities where digital interactions and algorithms can enhance the experience and utility for the customer. This allows companies to serve customers in ways that were previously not feasible.

## Innovating Value Capture
Beyond simply charging a fee for value created, AI allows for more creative, automated, and scalable value capture mechanisms. The tech industry provides a key example, where value is often created for users for free (e.g., search or social media), while value is captured from a different party, such as advertisers. This separation and automation of value capture is a key feature of modern digital business models that AI can enable.
