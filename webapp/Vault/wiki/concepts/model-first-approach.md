# Model-First Approach

## Definition
A model-first approach to building products is a strategy where the design process begins with and is centered on the AI model itself. Instead of fitting a model into a pre-existing product concept, developers first and foremost consider the model's capability set and its potential evolution, making that the core of the product.

## Impact on Product Design
This approach makes the AI model's functionality front and center in the product's design. It requires a deep understanding of what the model can do and where its development is heading. This focus can fundamentally change how products are conceived and built compared to traditional software development.

## Influence on User Interfaces
Adopting a model-first approach may necessitate redesigning user interfaces to better align with how the AI model interacts. The text highlights the growing interest and emphasis on chat-based interfaces as an example. Such interfaces can feel more natural for interacting with certain AI models and may become a new norm in product design as a result of this strategy.
