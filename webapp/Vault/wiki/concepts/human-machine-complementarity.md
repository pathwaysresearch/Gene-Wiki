# Human-Machine Complementarity

## Definition
Human-machine complementarity describes a scenario where an AI tool like ChatGPT increases worker productivity by augmenting or complementing the worker's skills. In this model, the combination of human and AI is synergistic, producing more than the sum of their parts. This could happen, for example, if the AI aids in brainstorming or produces a rough draft that a human then significantly edits and improves upon.

## Hypothesized Evidence
The study outlined two potential forms of evidence that would support a complementarity story. The first would be observing participants spending significant time editing or repeatedly prompting the AI to refine its output in an effort to earn higher grades. The second would be if the final submissions from the treatment group received significantly higher grades than ChatGPT's raw, unedited output, indicating that human input added substantial value.

## Experimental Findings
The experiment found little to no evidence supporting the complementarity mechanism in the context of the assigned writing tasks. The majority of users did not edit the AI's output, and there was no correlation between the time spent after pasting the AI text and the final grade. The study concluded that ChatGPT was primarily substituting for worker effort rather than complementing their skills.
