# Synthetic Control Arms

## Definition
AI-based synthetic control arms are a method used in clinical trials where individuals who are not officially enrolled in the trial serve as the control group. This is made possible through the use of digital platforms that allow for AI-based analysis and automation of key trial phases. (Chunk 48)

## How It Works
Instead of randomly assigning a portion of trial participants to a placebo or standard treatment group, this approach uses AI to create a comparable control group from external data sources. This allows for a higher percentage of trial participants to receive the experimental therapy being tested. (Chunk 48)

## Applications and Benefits
This method is part of a broader trend toward digital data flow for clinical trials. Its use can provide significant economic value and accelerate the speed to market for new drug compounds. By helping to integrate and reconcile trial data, AI-based synthetic control arms can contribute to speeding up the overall trial process. (Chunk 48)
