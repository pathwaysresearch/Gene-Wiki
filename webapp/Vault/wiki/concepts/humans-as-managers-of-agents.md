# Humans as Managers of Agents

## The Core Analogy
Proposed by Shishir Mehrotra, this concept suggests that the role of humans in an AI-driven workplace will evolve to be analogous to that of a manager. Instead of being replaced, employees will direct and leverage AI agents, effectively managing a team of digital assistants to perform their work.

## A Positive Vision for Human-AI Collaboration
This framework offers a positive perspective on the future of work, countering the narrative of mass job replacement. It emphasizes that human skills in judgment, strategy, and direction become more valuable. The human is kept firmly in the loop, not as a passive observer, but as an active director of AI capabilities.

## Application to Specific Roles
This idea can be applied to nearly every discipline. For example, a software developer's role might shift from writing every line of code to directing AI coding tools to generate, test, and debug code. The developer's value lies in their architectural vision and problem-solving skills, managing the AI agents to execute that vision.
