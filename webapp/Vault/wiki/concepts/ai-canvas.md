# AI Canvas

## Definition
The AI Canvas is a tool developed to aid in the contemplation, building, and assessment of AI tools. It provides a disciplined and structured process for decomposing tasks and decisions into their core elements to understand the potential role of a prediction machine.

## Key Components
The canvas breaks a decision into seven distinct elements: prediction (what you need to know to make the decision), input (the data needed to run the predictive algorithm), judgment (how different outcomes and errors are valued), training (the data needed to train the algorithm), action (the task you are trying to accomplish), outcome (the metrics for task success), and feedback (how outcomes can be used to improve the algorithm).

## Application
The AI Canvas is intended to be filled out for every decision or task being considered for AI implementation. It is used by companies to deconstruct enterprise workflows, identify a task with a prediction element, and position their AI offering. The text uses the company Atomwise's molecule selection process as a detailed example of how to apply the canvas to a real-world business problem.
