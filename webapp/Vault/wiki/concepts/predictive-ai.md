# Predictive AI

## Definition
Predictive AI is a form of artificial intelligence that makes predictions and is then plugged into various tasks. This type of AI has been in use for approximately the last ten years and is distinct from the more recent wave of generative AI.

## How It Works
Predictive AI models are trained on historical data to identify patterns and make forecasts about future events. These predictions are then used to automate or support decision-making in specific business processes.

## Applications
Common applications of predictive AI include predicting whether a credit card transaction is fraudulent or forecasting if an email should be classified as spam. These applications are typically narrow and task-specific, designed to optimize a particular function by providing a probabilistic assessment.
