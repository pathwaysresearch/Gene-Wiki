# Data Preparation

## The Challenge of Raw Data

Before data can be used to build an effective AI factory, it must be gathered and prepared, which is a challenging process. Data assets are frequently plagued by various issues, including biases, plain errors, inaccuracies, and inconsistencies. A significant investment of work is required to carefully check and correct these problems to ensure the data's integrity.

## Key Steps

The primary steps in data preparation are cleaning, normalizing, and integrating the data. As various streams of data are combined to feed complex analysis, they must be normalized to be compatible. This includes ensuring that units are consistent, redundancies are eliminated, and variables are compatible with one another. These tasks, while sounding simple, become very complex with large datasets.

## Importance for AI

Data preparation is described as the first order of business for executives aiming to build an AI factory. It is essential for ensuring that any insight derived from analyzing an integrated dataset is accurate. This is particularly crucial when integrating different kinds of data, such as financial and operational data, to ensure they are used in a consistent and proper way.
