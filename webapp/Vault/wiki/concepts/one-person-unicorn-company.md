# One-Person Unicorn Company

## Concept Overview
The one-person unicorn company is a forward-looking concept describing a new type of company that venture capital investors are beginning to look for. It posits that a single individual, augmented by AI, could perform the functions of a much larger traditional organization and achieve massive scale and valuation.

## Enabling Technologies
The feasibility of this concept is attributed to AI tools that significantly lower the barriers to entry in technical fields. The text specifically mentions AI-powered coding tools that reduce the difficulty of becoming an engineer and break down the traditional silos between specialized roles like front-end, back-end, DevOps, and data engineering.

## Skill Convergence
This new company structure is supported by the idea that a single person can become more versatile than ever before. The text suggests that the skills and knowledge of a technology expert and a subject matter expert will converge more and more into the same individuals, allowing one person to have a much broader and deeper impact.
