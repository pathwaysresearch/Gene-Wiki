# Technology Complementarities

## Overview
The text references a large and influential literature on the complementarities between organizational features and technology use. This concept posits that technology investments should not be analyzed in isolation, as their effectiveness and adoption are intertwined with other aspects of the firm, such as its strategies and organizational structure. These factors are often considered codetermined.

## Application to AI in Startups
The research applies this framework to understand the relationship between AI use and the innovation and business strategies of startups. It recognizes that a startup's strategic choices and its decision to adopt AI may be mutually influential. This approach seeks to disentangle factors like financing and firm "quality" from strategic decisions when analyzing AI adoption.

## Contrast with Incumbent Studies
The text notes that much of the existing literature on technology complementarities focuses on large, established firms (incumbents). In those studies, organizational characteristics are often treated as quasi-fixed compared to IT investments. By focusing on startups, this research explores these dynamics in a context where organizational features and strategies are more fluid.
