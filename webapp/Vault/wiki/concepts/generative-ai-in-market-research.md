# Generative AI in Market Research

## Reframing Value Beyond Productivity
The text argues that the conversation around GenAI in market research should shift from a narrow focus on efficiency and productivity to its potential for effectiveness. This involves using the technology to achieve things that were previously difficult or impossible, described as doing "better things," rather than just doing existing work faster.

## New Research Frontiers
GenAI opens up new frontiers for market research by enabling novel capabilities. These include achieving "just-in-time insights" to act on trends faster, using synthetic data as a viable alternative when real data is unavailable, and reaching hard-to-sample or restricted audiences that traditional methods cannot.

## Applications and Limitations
The text suggests that Large Language Models (LLMs) are particularly well-suited for certain tasks like "belief estimation," where they can effectively replicate survey data on beliefs. However, they may struggle with more complex, context-driven experimental responses, highlighting the need for hybrid approaches that combine human and synthetic inputs for optimal results.
