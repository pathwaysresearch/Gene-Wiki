# AI Exposure

## Definition
AI Exposure is a concept that quantifies the extent to which a specific occupation is likely to be impacted by advancements in Artificial Intelligence. It is used as a key independent variable in the study to analyze the relationship between AI progress and changes in work content and wages. The primary measure used is the DAIOE index.

## Impact on Wages
The study finds a nuanced relationship between AI exposure and worker wages. The effect is not uniform across all occupations. For instance, technicians, clerical support staff, and sales agents experience a wage increase when their occupation has higher AI exposure. In contrast, professionals and operators see a decline in wages. This suggests that AI can be both a complement and a substitute for labor, depending on the occupational context.

## Moderating Factors
The relationship between AI exposure and wages is moderated by other factors, such as task churn and worker mobility. In high-churn occupations where tasks evolve rapidly, AI exposure is negatively associated with wages for immobile workers who remain in the same firm and occupation. This is attributed to potential skill mismatches, where workers cannot adapt through job transitions and are increasingly substituted by AI, putting downward pressure on their wages.
