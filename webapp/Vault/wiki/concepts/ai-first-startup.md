# AI-First Startup

## Definition
An AI-first startup is a new company that, unlike legacy organizations, builds its capabilities, processes, and business model around artificial intelligence from the very beginning. The startup 'Well' is presented as a case study of this approach.

## Advantages over Legacy Companies
It is generally much easier for a startup to build up its AI capabilities from scratch. They do not have to contend with existing legacy technologies, established processes, and entrenched strategies that can make AI adoption in large, older organizations an extremely challenging and slow process.

## Strategic Implications for Legacy Companies
The experience of an AI-first startup offers an instructive contrast for legacy companies. It highlights potential strategies for them, such as setting up independent AI-focused business units that can be scaled up later, or acquiring successful AI startups to integrate new capabilities and business models more rapidly.
