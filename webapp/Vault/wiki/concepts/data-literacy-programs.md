# Data Literacy Programs

## Definition
Data literacy or data fluency programs are formal education offerings that companies use to build a culture supportive of AI. These programs aim to train large numbers of employees—sometimes all of them—on how data can be used effectively within the organization, thereby sowing the seeds for widespread success with AI.

## Core Curriculum
The training typically covers key topics such as the different types of data, how data is used in analytics and AI programs, what kinds of decisions are best supported by data, and how data contributes to the organization's overall success. The ultimate goal is to make it "everyone's job to propose, develop, and implement analytics and AI tools throughout the enterprise."

## Program Structure
The text suggests that leading programs often have multiple components to be effective. This includes conceptual learning about key data and AI topics, experiential learning through activities like simulations or case study discussions, and sustainment learning to reinforce key lessons and address new topics over time.
