# Ethical AI and FAT ML

## Definition
Ethical AI is an emerging field concerned with embedding ethical notions and principles into artificial intelligence systems. A key sub-field within machine learning is known as FAT ML, which stands for Fairness, Accountability, and Transparency in Machine Learning, and has dedicated conferences focused on its development.

## Purpose and Goals
The primary goal of FAT ML and the broader Ethical AI movement is to address issues of bias and subjectivity in algorithms. It seeks to find ways to incorporate concepts of fairness and ethics directly into machine learning models to make them more humanized and objective, moving beyond purely technical performance metrics.

## Significance
This area of study is a response to the growing deployment of AI in critical applications where biases can have significant negative impacts. By focusing on fairness, accountability, and transparency, researchers and practitioners aim to build more trustworthy and equitable AI systems that are more palatable and beneficial to society.
