# Stages of Operating Model Transformation

## Overview
Research on how firms develop AI-centered capabilities shows that they follow a natural sequence of stages in their journey to become a state-of-the-art "AI factory." This transformation involves not just technological investment but also substantial cultural shifts to fully embrace the opportunities and challenges presented by AI.

## The Four Stages
The model consists of four distinct stages. Stage 1 is "siloed data," the typical starting point for most organizations. Stage 2 is the "pilot" stage, where the value of analytics is demonstrated, often with the help of vendors, without requiring major organizational change. Stage 3 is the "data hub" stage, which involves rearchitecting the organization to aggregate data for companywide use. The final stage is the "AI factory," representing a mature, AI-centered operating model.

## Key Challenges
The transition between stages presents different challenges. While moving to the pilot stage (Stage 2) has few barriers, the shift to the data hub stage (Stage 3) is a critical point requiring substantial investment. At this stage, an organization must overcome data silos to aggregate information from many sources and use it to identify companywide opportunities, a significant organizational and architectural challenge.
