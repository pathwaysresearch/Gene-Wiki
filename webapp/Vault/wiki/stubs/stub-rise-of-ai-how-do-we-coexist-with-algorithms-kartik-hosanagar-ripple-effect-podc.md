# Rise of AI: How Do We Coexist with Algorithms? | Kartik Hosanagar — Ripple Effect Podcast

**Type**: RAG stub — full content in vector index, not in wiki

## Abstract

Wharton professor Kartik Hosanagar discusses the rapid acceleration of AI development and its transformative implications for business, society, and the labor market on the Knowledge at Wharton Ripple Effect podcast (May 2023). He frames AI as a general-purpose technology comparable to electricity, the steam engine, and computers—one that will fundamentally reshape humanity. The conversation covers whether AI development should be slowed, how businesses can differentiate by combining AI with proprietary data and user experience, potential legal issues, and AI's net impact on employment. Hosanagar argues that AI will likely cause net job loss but will also significantly augment existing jobs rather than purely replacing them.

## Key Claims

- AI is a general-purpose technology on par with electricity, the steam engine, and computers, poised to change the world forever
- ChatGPT's breakthrough was not fundamentally new capability but rather a seamless user experience layered on top of existing GPT models
- Businesses can differentiate with AI by combining off-the-shelf models with proprietary data sets or superior user experiences
- AI will likely result in net job loss rather than net job creation, unlike most previous technological revolutions
- AI will also augment many jobs by automating routine tasks, meaning the full picture is more nuanced than simple job replacement
