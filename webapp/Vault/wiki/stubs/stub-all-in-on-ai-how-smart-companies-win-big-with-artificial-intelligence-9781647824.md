# All In on AI: How Smart Companies Win Big with Artificial Intelligence

**Type**: RAG stub — full content in vector index, not in wiki

## Abstract

Written by Thomas H. Davenport and Nitin Mittal and published by Harvard Business Review Press, this book examines organizations that have gone "all-in" on artificial intelligence — adopting AI at the most aggressive levels and achieving the highest business value through deep integration with strategy, operations, technology, culture, and talent. The authors profile leading companies across multiple industries (including Airbus, Walmart, Capital One, Pfizer, Cleveland Clinic, Disney, and others) to illustrate what distinguishes the most extreme and successful AI adopters from average organizations. The book provides a practical framework covering use cases, implementation paths, and the organizational transformations required to become "AI fueled."

## Key Claims

- The most successful AI-adopting companies go "all-in," integrating AI deeply across strategy, processes, technology, culture, and talent rather than pursuing isolated projects
- AI is most commonly applied to making business processes more efficient, improving decisions, and enhancing existing products and services, and all-in companies eventually develop use cases across a wide variety of functions
- Companies outside Silicon Valley, such as Airbus, have successfully embarked on broad AI-driven digital transformations including employee retraining and operational efficiency improvements
- Individual AI applications may not transform a company on their own, but collectively across functions and processes they create substantial competitive advantage
- There are multiple alternative paths to becoming AI fueled, including transitioning from people-focused operations to people-and-AI-integrated models (illustrated by Deloitte and other examples)

## Topics Covered

AI strategy and integration, enterprise AI adoption maturity, industry-specific AI use cases, AI-driven business process improvement, organizational culture and AI transformation, AI talent and workforce retraining, AI in aviation (Airbus), AI in financial services (Capital One, mortgage lending), AI in healthcare and pharma (Cleveland Clinic, Pfizer, Novartis, AstraZeneca, Eli Lilly), AI in retail (Walmart), AI in government (US and Singapore), AI in manufacturing (Seagate), AI in entertainment (Disney), paths to becoming AI fueled, AI and automation for productivity
