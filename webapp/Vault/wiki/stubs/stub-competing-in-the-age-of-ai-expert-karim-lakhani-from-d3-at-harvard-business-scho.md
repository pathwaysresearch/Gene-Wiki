# Competing in the Age of AI — Karim Lakhani, D³ at Harvard Business School

**Type**: RAG stub — full content in vector index, not in wiki

## Abstract

Harvard Business School professor Karim Lakhani presents a talk on competing in the age of AI, drawing on his research to illustrate how AI is transforming business strategy and value creation. He traces the evolution from the early 2000s internet era (AOL–Time Warner merger) to the present day of trillion-dollar companies serving billions of users, emphasizing how digital and AI-native firms have reshaped competition. The talk includes live demonstrations of generative AI tools like Midjourney, a case study on using AI to recreate Rembrandt-style paintings through statistical feature extraction, and an introduction to the newly launched Harvard Business School AI Institute.

## Key Claims

- Companies today are worth trillions of dollars and effortlessly serve billions of users, a reality that would have been unimaginable in the early 2000s
- Business not only survived but thrived during the pandemic, underscoring the power of digitally-enabled operating models
- AI can extract features that define an artist's style (e.g., Rembrandt) using statistical analysis and algorithms to generate new works, demonstrating creative AI capabilities
- Generative AI tools like Midjourney are accessible and can produce sophisticated outputs from natural language prompts in real time
- Harvard Business School has launched a new AI Institute to advance research and engagement on AI's impact on business
