# Understanding AI | Kartik Hosanagar

**Type**: RAG stub — full content in vector index, not in wiki

## Abstract

This is a CGTN America interview (2024) with Kartik Hosanagar, Professor and Co-Director of AI at Wharton (University of Pennsylvania) and author of *A Human's Guide to Machine Intelligence*. The conversation explores how AI is already pervasive in everyday life, the ways algorithms can manipulate user behavior through business models like advertising, and why AI regulation and transparency are essential. Hosanagar also reflects on his personal journey from studying engineering in India to encountering AI during his PhD at Carnegie Mellon University.

## Key Claims

- AI is not an esoteric, distant technology — it is already shaping people's lives in ways many do not realize
- Online platforms' advertising-driven business models incentivize algorithms to create digital addiction by maximizing user time on site
- There is a gray area between algorithms being helpful to users and algorithms exploiting users' inability to control their own behavior
- AI needs regulation, including requirements for companies to disclose when AI is used in socially consequential decisions like loan approvals, recruiting, and criminal sentencing
- Generative AI's growing sophistication in creating original works (art, videos) is driving renewed attention to the technology beyond earlier applications in big data and robotics
