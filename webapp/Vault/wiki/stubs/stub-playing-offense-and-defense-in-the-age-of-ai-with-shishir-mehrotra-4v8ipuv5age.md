## Abstract

In this episode of Creative Intelligence, host Kartik Hosanagar (Wharton professor) interviews Shishir Mehrotra — CEO of Grammarly, founder of Coda, and former Head of Product at YouTube — about how AI is transforming work. The conversation explores Grammarly's vision of building an "AI superhighway" that brings intelligent agents to where people already work, the strategic acquisition of Superhuman, and the integration of Coda Brain into a reimagined productivity suite. The discussion frames AI strategy through the lens of playing both offense (leveraging AI for new capabilities) and defense (protecting against disruption), applicable to both individuals and organizations.

## Key Claims

- Grammarly's vision has evolved beyond grammar correction to building an "AI superhighway" that brings AI agents to every surface where people work
- The concept of an "army of agents" accompanies users everywhere they work — support agents, product marketing agents, and others providing contextual assistance in real-time
- Grammarly is expanding its suite of surfaces from documents, spreadsheets, and presentations (via Coda) into email (motivating the Superhuman acquisition)
- Both individuals and organizations need to think about playing offense and defense with AI as a new productivity platform
- Shishir holds a positive bias regarding the role of humans in AI, suggesting humans remain central even as agents proliferate across work surfaces
