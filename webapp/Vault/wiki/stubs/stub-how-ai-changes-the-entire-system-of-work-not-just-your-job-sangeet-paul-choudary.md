# How AI Changes the Entire System of Work (Not Just Your Job) – Sangeet Paul Choudary

**Type**: RAG stub — full content in vector index, not in wiki

## Abstract

In this episode of the Creative Intelligence podcast, Sangeet Paul Choudary joins host Kartik Hosanagar to argue that AI's impact on work goes far beyond individual jobs and tasks — it transforms the entire "system of work," including organizational structures, workflows, and competitive ecosystems. Choudary critiques the popular saying "AI won't take your job, but someone using AI will" as a dangerous oversimplification, contending that AI reshapes competitiveness at the ecosystem level, which in turn restructures organizations and renders certain roles irrelevant. The conversation introduces frameworks for understanding how tasks carry economic value, intrinsic value, and contextual value, and how individuals can identify where new constraints emerge as AI commoditizes previously scarce skills.

## Key Claims

- The phrase "AI won't take your job, but someone using AI will" is a dangerous oversimplification that misses systemic changes
- AI is not just changing individual tasks or jobs — it is transforming the entire system of work including organizations, workflows, and competitive ecosystems
- Changes in ecosystem-level competition drive organizational restructuring, which in turn makes certain jobs irrelevant
- Tasks have three distinct types of value: intrinsic value, economic value, and contextual value — and these must be understood separately to navigate AI's impact
- When AI commoditizes a skill (e.g., coding), the constraint shifts to adjacent higher-order activities (e.g., validating code, integrating it into larger systems), and workers must identify and move toward the new constraint
