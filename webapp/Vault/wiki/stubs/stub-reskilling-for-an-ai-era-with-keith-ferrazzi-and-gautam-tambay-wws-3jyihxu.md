# Reskilling for an AI Era with Keith Ferrazzi and Gautam Tambay

**Type**: RAG stub — full content in vector index, not in wiki

## Abstract

This episode of the Creative Intelligence podcast, hosted by Wharton professor Kartik Hosanagar, features a conversation with leadership author Keith Ferrazzi and Springboard CEO Gautam Tambay about how AI is transforming the workplace and what reskilling strategies are needed. The discussion covers how AI changes team collaboration, the distinction between durable skills (trust-building, strategic thinking) and perishable technical skills, and the challenge of redesigning workflows when existing workers lack deep AI familiarity. The conversation explores which jobs may be replaced, augmented, or created by AI, and how business leaders can serve as change agents for workforce transformation.

## Key Claims

- Companies, especially Fortune 500s, are demanding training in "durable skills" (trust, team building, strategic thinking) that humans can uniquely do, alongside AI-specific technical upskilling
- AI is changing how teams collaborate, but not enough research has been done on how teams function when working alongside AI agents
- Workflow redesign needs to be led by people intimately familiar with current workflows, yet many of these workers have only tinkered with ChatGPT and lack deep understanding of AI's potential
- There is a distinction between reskilling knowledge workers and frontline workers for an AI-enabled world, each requiring different approaches
- The market for training people to work with AI-integrated collaborative suites (e.g., Microsoft's tools with AI agents) represents a significant and sometimes counterintuitive revenue opportunity
