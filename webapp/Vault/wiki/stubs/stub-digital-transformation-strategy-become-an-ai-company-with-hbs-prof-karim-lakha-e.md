## Abstract

HBS Professor Karim Lakhani discusses how established organizations can develop enterprise AI and digital transformation strategies to compete in a cloud-based world. He argues that digital transformation requires a total rewiring of the organization—not just technology adoption—and that 70% of the effort is change management while only 30% is the data science and technology stack. Lakhani emphasizes that C-suite leaders cannot outsource this transformation and must personally become experts in driving organizational change. The conversation covers evolving business models, breaking down enterprise silos, and leveraging digital footprints across operations, customers, and suppliers.

## Key Claims

- Digital transformation is a "total rewiring" of the organization, not merely a technology upgrade
- 70% of digital transformation effort is change management and organizational change; only 30% is data science and technology stack
- C-suite executives cannot outsource transformation leadership—they must become change management leaders and even HR specialists themselves
- Business models must be analyzed along two separate dimensions: value creation (why customers interact with you) and value capture (how the company makes money), both of which can be enhanced through AI and digital
- Traditional enterprise silos must be dismantled ("de-silofied") to take advantage of digital footprints and data across company operations, customer interactions, and supplier relationships
