# Wharton Professor Stefano Puntoni Offers Insights On Generative Ai And Customer Experience

**Type**: RAG stub — full content in vector index, not in wiki

## Abstract

Wharton marketing professor Stefano Puntoni discusses the impact of generative AI on business and customer experience (CX) in a podcast interview with Blake Morgan. He outlines specific use cases for generative AI including data analysis, customer service augmentation, and backend operations optimization, drawing on survey data and real-world company examples. The conversation addresses both the current state of enterprise AI adoption—noting that over 50% of U.S. senior executives have already embraced generative AI—and the rapidly evolving landscape of capabilities and deployments. This source is valuable for understanding practical generative AI applications in CX and marketing from an academic and research-informed perspective.

## Key Claims

- Data analysis was the use case with the highest level of endorsement in their survey, including using tools like ChatGPT with advanced analysis plugins to work with datasets from market research, website visitor data, and product features
- Customer service will be impacted by generative AI in many ways, from frontline chatbots to augmenting human customer service representatives' skills in more complex interactions
- AI can reduce customer service request volume by making better predictions about customer needs and anticipating where service might go awry, preventing issues before they occur
- Over 50% of U.S. senior executives have already embraced generative AI according to a GBK Collective study, with adoption expected to accelerate
- Some form of AI has already been used for a decade to improve efficiency of backend business operations through learning algorithms and predictive capabilities
