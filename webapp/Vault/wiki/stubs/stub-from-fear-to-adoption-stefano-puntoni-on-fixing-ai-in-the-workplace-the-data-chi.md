## Abstract

Wharton Professor Stefano Puntoni joins Cindi Howson on The Data Chief to discuss why AI adoption in the workplace is failing and how to fix it. He argues that employees enter "self-preservation mode" when AI is introduced without transparency or empowerment, which kills adoption. Puntoni advocates for a "meet in the middle" approach where leaders provide vision and resources while workers co-create the implementation roadmap. The conversation frames AI not as a strategy in itself but as a tool that requires deliberate organizational and cultural management to succeed.

## Key Claims

- Employees enter "self-preservation mode" when threatened by AI, which is a primary barrier killing workplace AI adoption
- AI is not a strategy — it is a tool that requires a broader strategic framework and human-centered implementation
- A "meet in the middle" approach is essential: top-down mandates alone are insufficient; leaders must provide vision and resources while empowering workers to co-create the roadmap
- Transparency is non-negotiable — honest and even uncomfortable conversations about AI's impact are better than corporate silence
- Successful AI adoption requires co-creation between leadership and frontline workers rather than purely top-down or bottom-up approaches
