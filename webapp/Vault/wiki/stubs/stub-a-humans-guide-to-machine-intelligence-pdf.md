# A Human's Guide to Machine Intelligence PDF

**Type**: RAG stub — full content in vector index, not in wiki

## Abstract

Written by Kartik Hosanagar, this book examines how algorithms are shaping human lives and decision-making, and how individuals can stay in control. Through real-world examples—from Google's autocomplete feature to AlphaGo's mastery of Go—the book explores the unanticipated consequences of algorithmic systems, the tension between predictability and resilience in AI, and the erosion of free will in an algorithm-driven world. It serves as an accessible guide for understanding the pervasive influence of machine intelligence on everyday choices, social connections, and society at large.

## Key Claims

- Algorithms subtly dictate daily decision-making, from sleep-tracking apps to social media notifications, undermining personal autonomy and free will
- The "Law of Unanticipated Consequences" means that algorithmic tools like Google's autocomplete, while enhancing efficiency, can promote harmful content in unforeseen ways
- Recommendation systems on platforms like Facebook and Tinder rely on interconnected social data rather than solely on shared preferences
- AlphaGo's use of machine learning over traditional rule-based systems demonstrates unexpected creativity that surpasses human expert play
- The "Predictability-Resilience Paradox" describes the fundamental trade-off between creating predictable but rigid algorithms and resilient systems that adapt but are harder to control
