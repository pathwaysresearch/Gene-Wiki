# Karthik Hosanagar Knowledge Base

**Type**: RAG stub — full content in vector index, not in wiki

## Abstract

This knowledge base compiles articles, publications, podcast episodes, and social media posts by Kartik Hosanagar, the John C. Hower Professor at the Wharton School and Faculty Co-Director of Wharton Human-AI Research. It covers his work exploring the intersection of AI, creativity, and human potential, including his "Creative Intelligence" podcast (launched May 2025) and his earlier book "A Human's Guide to Machine Intelligence." The collection also captures his reflections on the recent history of AI breakthroughs, particularly the evolution from GPT-2 and BERT-era transformer models to modern generative AI, as well as guest conversations on topics ranging from AI consciousness to the future of the creative industry.

## Key Claims

- AI's recent explosive growth can be traced to the emergence of transformer models like GPT-2 and BERT in 2018-2019, which represented a step change from earlier capabilities
- Hosanagar attempted to co-write a book chapter with AI in 2017-18 but found the technology too early; subsequent transformer models changed the feasibility
- AI is reshaping the creative industry, and creativity and technology must now move hand in hand according to leaders like David Droga (CEO of Accenture Song)
- Not all creativity is worth saving — AI can elevate originality rather than merely drive efficiency
- The podcast "Creative Intelligence" explores questions such as whether AI can be conscious, featuring researchers like Anil Seth

AI and creativity, transformer models (GPT-2 and BERT), history of recent AI breakthroughs, AI consciousness, creative industry disruption, Wharton Human-AI research, A Human's Guide to Machine Intelligence, advertising and CMO evolution, serial entrepreneurship, comfort zone psychology, human potential and emotional mastery
