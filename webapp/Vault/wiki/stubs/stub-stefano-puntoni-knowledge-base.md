# Stefano Puntoni Knowledge Base

**Type**: RAG stub — full content in vector index, not in wiki

## Abstract

This knowledge base compiles articles, publications, and podcast appearances by Stefano Puntoni, a Wharton professor focused on the intersection of AI, marketing, and consumer behavior. It covers how AI is reshaping marketing through changes in consumer search behavior and automated purchasing decisions, the organizational adoption of generative AI, and the impact of IoT on identity-driven consumption. The collection spans peer-reviewed articles, Harvard Business Review pieces, and podcast conversations, providing both academic and practitioner-oriented perspectives on technology's role in marketing strategy.

## Key Claims

- AI is upending marketing on two fronts simultaneously: how consumers search for information and who makes purchasing decisions
- Generative AI will drive significant changes in marketing over the next five years, with automation taking over tactical execution while human judgment remains crucial for strategic decision-making
- Prompt engineering strategies are important for effective use of generative AI tools
- Responsible and ethical use of AI tools is essential for organizational adoption
- IoT adoption is more likely when associated tasks are less relevant for identity signaling, and less likely when tasks are central to one's identity
