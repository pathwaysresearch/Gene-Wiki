**Type**: RAG stub — full content in vector index, not in wiki

## Abstract

This is a conversation with Stefano Puntoni, AI at Wharton faculty co-director, discussing how to reframe the dominant "human or AI" narrative away from human replacement toward "human flourishing." Puntoni argues that current AI discourse is heavily flavored by a replacement mindset originating from the tech sector and advocates for incorporating AI into organizational workflows in a complementary way. The conversation was filmed at a Wharton Magazine alumni event and moderated by editor-in-chief Richard Rys.

## Key Claims

- Much of current AI conversation is framed around human replacement
- The human replacement framing largely originates from the tech sector
- There is a need to shift from a "human or AI" mindset to a more integrative approach
- AI should be incorporated into organizational workflows to achieve "human flourishing"
- Organizations can benefit from viewing AI as complementary to human work rather than a substitute
