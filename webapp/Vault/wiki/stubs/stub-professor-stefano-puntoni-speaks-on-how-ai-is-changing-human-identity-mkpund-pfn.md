# Professor Stefano Puntoni Speaks On How Ai Is Changing Human Identity

**Type**: RAG stub — full content in vector index, not in wiki

## Abstract

Professor Stefano Puntoni delivers a talk at Ahmedabad University exploring how rapid advances in artificial intelligence and robotics are transforming the economy and, more fundamentally, reshaping human identity. He reviews a research programme investigating the human relationship with technology in the age of smart machines, examining how human identity shapes responses to autonomous machines and how autonomous machines in turn affect human identity. The talk addresses the broader implications of AI-driven automation on organizational and production processes, as well as the development of new products and services.

## Key Claims

- Rapid advances in AI and robotics are enabling new products and services while triggering a new wave of automation in organizational and production processes
- Human identity shapes how people respond to autonomous machines
- Autonomous machines in turn reshape human identity
- The relationship between humans and technology is becoming increasingly complex in the age of smart machines
- Understanding the human-AI relationship requires investigating identity as a central factor
