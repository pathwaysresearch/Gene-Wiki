# AI Won't Replace Humans—But Humans With AI Will Replace Humans Without AI

**Type**: RAG stub — full content in vector index, not in wiki

## Abstract

This is an interview from Harvard Business Review's "The New World of Work" video series (August 2023), in which HBR editor-in-chief Adi Ignatius speaks with Harvard Business School professor Karim Lakhani about AI adoption in business. Lakhani argues that most companies will have no choice but to adopt AI and digital technologies at their core functions, and that the real competitive divide will be between humans who leverage AI and those who don't. The conversation covers practical steps for business leaders—experimentation, sandboxes, internal bootcamps—and emphasizes continuous learning and change management as essential organizational capabilities in the AI era.

## Key Claims

- Most companies will not have a choice but to adopt AI and digital at their core functions
- People are already living in an AI age through their interactions with consumer technology (Uber, Ola, DiDi, smartphones)
- Business leaders must experiment, create sandboxes, run internal bootcamps, and develop AI use cases for all employees—not just technology workers
- Continuous personal and organizational learning is essential; companies must invest in learning for their employees
- Change management is a critically underrated skill for managers, leaders, and executives navigating AI adoption
- The emergence of AI is compared to the early days of the internet browser—initially dismissed as "cute applications" but ultimately transformative
