# Ideation With Generative AI: Insights from Wharton's Dr. Stefano Puntoni

**Type**: RAG stub — full content in vector index, not in wiki

## Abstract

Dr. Stefano Puntoni of The Wharton School discusses his research on using generative AI for creative ideation in consumer research and beyond. He argues that GenAI enhances ideation through two pathways—productivity and semantic breadth—which mirror the human creativity mechanisms of persistence and flexibility. The interview explores metaphorical roles for AI in ideation (Designer, Writer, Interviewer, Actor), the complementarity between human and AI strengths, and the risks of over-reliance on AI outputs. The discussion provides a framework for researchers and practitioners seeking to leverage human-AI collaboration for both incremental and radical creative insights.

## Key Claims

- GenAI enhances ideation through productivity and semantic breadth, analogous to human persistence and flexibility in creative thinking
- Human-AI collaboration should be structured around complementarity, leveraging the distinct strengths of both humans and AI
- Four metaphorical ideation roles for AI are proposed: Designer, Writer (AI as lead generator) and Interviewer, Actor (AI pulling ideas out of the human)
- Over-reliance on GenAI is a significant danger because its outputs are optimized for plausibility rather than truth, making uncritical acceptance tempting
- Effective use of GenAI for ideation requires expertise in understanding model capabilities and training data to craft prompts that direct the model toward productive creative spaces
