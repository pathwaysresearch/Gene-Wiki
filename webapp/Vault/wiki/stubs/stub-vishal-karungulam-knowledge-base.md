# Vishal Karungulam Knowledge Base

**Type**: RAG stub — full content in vector index, not in wiki

## Abstract

This knowledge base compiles articles, publications, podcast appearances, and LinkedIn posts by or about Vishal Karungulam, a Clinical Assistant Professor of Information Systems and Academic Director of The LAB – Office of Experiential Learning at ISB. A central theme is the environmental and economic costs of AI, arguing that AI's carbon and water footprint is already significant and growing, and that responsible management of these costs is critical. The collection also covers his contributions to CHRO Dialogues on AI's impact on workforce skills and intelligence at work.

## Key Claims

- Digital technologies and data centres now produce more CO₂ emissions than commercial aviation, accounting for an estimated 3.7% of global emissions versus ~2.5% for aviation
- Large Language Models require immense computational capacity and the data centres powering them consume vast amounts of water for evaporative cooling
- AI's environmental footprint is currently "the smallest it will ever be," meaning the real challenge lies in managing its escalating costs
- The "cloud" metaphor obscures the energy-hungry, carbon-intensive physical infrastructure that underpins AI systems
- AI is redefining intelligence at work while creating an emerging skills gap, as discussed in CHRO Dialogues at ISB Executive Education
