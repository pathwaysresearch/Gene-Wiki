## Abstract

This is a recorded session from the Harvard Business School AI Institute's "Leading with AI" series (June 2024) featuring Grammy Award-winning sound engineer Derek Ali (CEO of EngineEars), Grammy-nominated artist Bas, and HBS professor Karim Lakhani. The session explores how AI is transforming music creation and production through live demonstrations of AI-generated music, a collaborative studio session between Derek Ali and Bas, and a panel discussion on the opportunities and challenges AI presents for artists, producers, and engineers. The conversation addresses practical use cases like AI-generated beats, voice cloning, sample clearance workarounds, and the democratization of music production.

## Key Claims

- AI can generate music from text prompts, demonstrated live by creating songs in various genres (classical, R&B, jazz hip-hop) about HBS faculty members, though generation takes significant time (~60 minutes)
- Technology has always made music creation more accessible (e.g., laptops replacing expensive studio sessions), and AI continues this trend by enabling songwriters who lack production skills to explore melodies and production ideas
- AI-generated beats could help producers avoid sound clearance issues by creating original compositions in the style of older records without needing to sample and clear copyrighted material
- Voice cloning technology could allow artists like Bas to test how their voice sounds over sequences of drums or lay down samples when they are on the road or vocally strained
- Creatives need to be at the forefront of AI conversations to maintain their voice and protect their revenue as the technology evolves
