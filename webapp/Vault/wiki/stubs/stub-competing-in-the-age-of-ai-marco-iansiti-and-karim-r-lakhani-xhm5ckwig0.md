## Abstract

This is a presentation by Harvard Business School Professors Marco Iansiti and Karim R. Lakhani discussing their book "Competing in the Age of AI: Strategy and Leadership When Algorithms and Networks Run the World." The authors argue that reinventing the firm around data, analytics, and AI removes traditional constraints on scale, scope, and learning that have restricted business growth for hundreds of years. They contend that when these operating constraints are removed, strategy fundamentally transforms into a new competitive game with new rules and outcomes. The talk was hosted by the HBS Digital Initiative in early 2020.

## Key Claims

- Reinventing firms around data, analytics, and AI removes traditional constraints on scale, scope, and learning
- Traditional operating constraints have restricted business growth for hundreds of years
- When traditional constraints are removed, strategy becomes a fundamentally different game
- Algorithms and networks are now running significant portions of the business world
- The book provides a framework for understanding the new rules and likely outcomes of AI-driven competition
