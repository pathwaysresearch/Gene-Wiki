## Abstract

This is a comprehensive knowledge base compiling the articles, publications, and interviews of Karim Lakhani, a Harvard Business School professor who studies AI and machine learning in the workplace. The collection includes his influential work on how AI is transforming business and society, with a central thesis that AI will drastically lower the cost of cognition much as the internet lowered the cost of information transmission. The resource catalogs his body of work with links, dates, and topic tags, serving as a reference for his research output and public thought leadership.

## Key Claims

- AI will not replace humans, but humans augmented with AI will replace humans who do not use AI
- AI will lower the cost of cognition in a manner analogous to how the internet lowered the cost of information transmission
- Companies are increasingly expected to deliver AI-augmented experiences and capabilities
- Lakhani has been studying AI and machine learning in the workplace for years, grounding his claims in sustained research
- The knowledge base organizes his work by topic, including "business and society" as a key subject area
