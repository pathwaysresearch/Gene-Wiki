# How AI Is Rewriting the Rules of Business (Feat. Prof. Karim Lakhani)

**Type**: RAG stub — full content in vector index, not in wiki

## Abstract

In this episode of Brew Markets (Episode 50, aired October 24, 2025), host Ann Barry interviews Harvard Professor Karim Lakhani about his research on AI productivity and measuring the return on the projected $1.5 trillion worldwide AI spend. Lakhani discusses a clinical-trial-style study conducted with Procter & Gamble that tested AI's impact on R&D and commercial teams, finding that AI is transforming from a tool to a teammate. The conversation also covers the J-curve of AI adoption, the need to redesign work processes, and how AI fundamentally reshapes business architecture — including business models and operating models. The episode closes with market commentary on Target's turnaround and Procter & Gamble earnings.

## Key Claims

- AI improves productivity — it functions as a teammate, enabling individuals and teams to get more work done
- Corporations should conduct "clinical trials" of AI (controlled studies) before scaling investment, similar to FDA drug approval processes
- AI adoption follows a J-curve: performance may initially dip before improving, and organizations must figure out where in workflows to integrate the technology
- AI is not fundamentally about technology — it is about business, impacting business architecture, value creation, value capture, business models, and operating models
- Organizations must change their work processes to capture AI's benefits; without process redesign, the gains from AI tools will be limited
