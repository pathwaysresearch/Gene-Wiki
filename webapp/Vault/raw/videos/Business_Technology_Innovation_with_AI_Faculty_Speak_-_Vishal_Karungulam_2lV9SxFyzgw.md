## Description

Effective business management today goes beyond traditional principles, it requires a deep understanding of how emerging technologies are reshaping industries and influencing decision-making at every level. As businesses navigate digital transformation, leaders must be equipped to connect technological advancements with real-world business impact.
 
The Business Technology & Innovation with AI programme addresses this need. Featuring insights from Vishal Karungulam, Clinical Assistant Professor at ISB, the programme explores the practical applications of technologies like Blockchain, Internet of Things, Cybersecurity, Artificial Intelligence, and Machine Learning. Each module is structured to help professionals translate technological knowledge into strategic action.
