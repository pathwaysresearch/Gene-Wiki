# The Rise of AI: Creating a Dominant Design

**Channel:** Wharton Magazine   |   **Date:** 2024-03-21   |   **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FKe5FZr5fag

## Description

Rahul Kapoor, Wharton management department chairperson, and Stefano Puntoni, AI at Wharton faculty co-director, discuss what can be learned from previous technologies which competed for dominance. This conversation was filmed during Inside Wharton Magazine, an event for alumni held in Wharton’s WAVE classroom to mark the launch of the Fall/Winter 2023 issue. Moderated by Wharton Magazine editor-in-chief Richard Rys.
