## Description

Discover how organisations can turn the challenges of AI and digital transformation into opportunities for growth in Stefano Puntoni’s keynote, “AI, Platforms, and the Future of Work.”

Key takeaways include why AI should be viewed not just as a technology, but as a driver of organisational change, and how platforms are reshaping the way value is created and shared. Stefano also highlights the leadership mindset and capabilities needed to thrive in an era where human decision-making and machine intelligence increasingly intersect.

Learn how to lead responsibly, embrace innovation, and prepare for the future of work in the age of AI!

Held online, the Reshaping Work 2025 Conference connected global thought leaders, policymakers, academics, and innovators to discuss the transformative impact of AI and platforms on the workplace and society.
