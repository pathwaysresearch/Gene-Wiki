## Description

Artificial intelligence is rapidly reshaping how organizations operate, compete, and create value. From automation and data analysis to strategy and decision-making, AI adoption is no longer optional—it’s becoming essential.

In this faculty Q&A, Harvard Business School Professor Karim Lakhani explores a critical question for today’s leaders: What happens if you ignore AI adoption in your organization?

While some companies may feel comfortable waiting, history shows that organizations that resist technological change risk being left behind. Leaders who fail to understand AI tools—and what they can unlock—miss opportunities to improve how their teams work, collaborate, and innovate. Over time, that gap widens, limiting competitiveness and long-term growth.

Discover why delaying AI adoption can stall innovation, weaken your competitive advantage, and prevent your organization from unlocking the full potential of emerging technologies. Learn why it’s important to experiment, build AI fluency, and take action now.
