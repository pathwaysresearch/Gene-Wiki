## Description

In their latest work "Competing in the Age of AI: Strategy and Leadership When Algorithms and Networks Run the World," Harvard Business School Professors Marco Iansiti and Karim R. Lakhani show how reinventing the firm around data, analytics, and AI removes traditional constraints on scale, scope, and learning that have restricted business growth for hundreds of years. When traditional operating constraints are removed, strategy becomes a whole new game; one whose rules and likely outcomes this book will make clear.
