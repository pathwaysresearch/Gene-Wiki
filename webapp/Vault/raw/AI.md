__📚  RECOMMENDED TEXTBOOKS__

- __Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach — Russell & Norvig \(4th ed\., 2020\) — __The definitive university AI textbook, used in virtually every top\-tier AI course globally\. Covers problem\-solving, knowledge representation, ML, NLP, and robotics\. Technical but accessible to analytically minded business professionals\.
- __Deep Learning — Goodfellow, Bengio & Courville \(2016, free online\) — __Endorsed by Elon Musk as 'the only comprehensive book\.' The technical bible for neural networks\. For practitioners and data engineers who need to understand what's under the hood\. Available free at deeplearningbook\.org\.
- __Competing in the Age of AI — Iansiti & Lakhani, HBS \(2020\) — __The best business\-school text on AI strategy\. Covers how AI\-first operating architectures remove traditional scale constraints\. Winner of Financial Times Business Book of the Year nomination\. Ideal for the Analytics skill in a business context\.
- __Hands\-On Machine Learning with Scikit\-Learn, Keras & TensorFlow — Aurélien Géron \(3rd ed\., 2022\) — __The most practical ML textbook for professionals who want to build, not just understand\. Clear, well\-structured, and Python\-based\. Widely used in applied ML courses\.
- __The Book of Why — Judea Pearl & Dana Mackenzie \(2018\) — __The foundational text on causal inference in AI/ML — critical for data engineering and analytics\. Pearl's contribution underlies modern AI reasoning\. Accessible to non\-mathematicians\.
- __All\-In on AI — Tom Davenport & Nitin Mittal, Deloitte \(2023\) — __The practitioner's companion to Iansiti & Lakhani\. Profiles companies going all\-in on AI transformation\. Excellent for Agentic AI and Emerging Technologies skills\.
